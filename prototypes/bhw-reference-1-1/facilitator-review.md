# Facilitator review: 1.1

Separate review material. Do not include in the learner payload. Competency: Participate in workplace communication. Original source: Facilitator Guide PDF 19–20; Day 1 presentation PDF 5–13; Reference Manual PDF 11–12. All numbering is one-based PDF numbering.

The guide's table on PDF 19 allocates six hours, while its narrative on PDF 20 says at least three hours. This discrepancy is retained, not resolved by adding digital lesson estimates. Independent study is initially 3–7 minutes per lesson. Facilitated activity duration remains facilitator-planned and unvalidated. Viewing or marking a lesson reviewed is not practical competency evidence.

Each lesson's observable objective appears in the preview. Use its objective as the observation target below. Activities, evidence and feedback remain separate from course tests.

Keep the deck's stages distinct: learning activity (recall actual work), explanation (role model), demonstration (facilitator example), practical application (learner rehearsal). Learner copy uses direct actions and never displays “the trainer may…” as teaching content.

Self-check rationale is intentionally learner-visible only after an answer: 1.1.1 organizing links people and action; 1.1.2 an adapted discussion reaches youth; 1.1.3 participation connects observations to planning; 1.1.4 seek appropriate guidance within scope; 1.1.5 registry identifies service recipients; 1.1.6 report observations and remaining needs without a diagnosis. These checks do not feed pre/post-test scores.
