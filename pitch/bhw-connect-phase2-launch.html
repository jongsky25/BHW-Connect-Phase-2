The start of the fulfillment of the promise

BHW Connect Phase 2.0

The BHW-facing application: courses, coaching and certification, delivered to every registered BHW individually — not cascaded through a representative who was in the room.

Beat before the mockups. No numbers here, just the reframe.

Feature 1 · Courses

Self-paced courses, in her own pocket.

Not a parallel curriculum — the same reference material, authored as text, video and quiz modules, and completed individually rather than cascaded through a representative.

  • 1Text, video and quiz modules, authored by admins — not one-size-fits-all.
  • 280% passing score, three attempts by default — both are policy settings, configurable per course.
  • 3Finishing every module queues an in-person skills demonstration automatically.

Feature 2 · Assessment

On-the-ground assessment, scored live.

Finishing the course doesn't certify anyone. It queues a real, in-person return-demonstration — graded on the spot, not re-keyed from a paper form later.

  • 1A new assessor role — anyone qualified can claim an open assessment from a queue.
  • 2The same rubric structure as any DOH skills checklist, digitized, not reinvented.
  • 3Scored as the assessor grades — the result is a record, not a form in a drawer.

Feature 3 · Certification

A QR-verified certificate, the moment she passes.

Competency stops being a piece of paper in a filing cabinet and becomes a record DOH can query — at any tier of the hierarchy.

  • 1A QR-verifiable certificate, issued the moment she passes — no waiting for a batch to close.
  • 2Publicly verifiable without a login — an LGU can check a credential in seconds.
  • 3Written to the same registry Phase 1 built — the answer to "which BHWs here are certified?"
