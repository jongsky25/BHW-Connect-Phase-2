Reaching 300,000 people this way is not a budget problem. It is a design problem.

02 The direction

From one-off events to one standard system that reaches every BHW.

Systematic and deliberate: one material, many functions, a real person confirming real skill.

One material Written once. Approved once. Used everywhere.
Self-paced coursesStandard lessons any BHW can take on their own time.
The appTraining that lives on the phone, in Filipino and English.
Knowledge baseThe approved answer, searchable at the doorstep.
Chat guideAsk in plain words, get the standard answer.
The anchorOn-site assessment at the RHUNurses and midwives watch the skill performed. Competence is confirmed in person.
Every old problem closed
CostLearn locally, not in a hall

No venues, travel or per diem. Assessment happens at the RHU the BHW already uses.

Stale materialFix once, everyone updated

One source of truth. A correction reaches every BHW.

Never goes nationalStandard from day one

Built as a national system, not a project that ends.

RetentionSupport that stays

Third-hand informationStraight from the source

No cascade. Every BHW gets the approved content directly.

Not a pipe dream. A real, working, piloted tool, ready to launch the first materials.

03 The opportunity

Co-create more than the technology: the framework and standards for taking any piloted material nationwide.

So that every primary-care and BHW material that proves itself can be mainstreamed, with HHP+ as the prime example.

What the framework defines
A content standardHow any material is written, structured, updated and kept current.
A validation and approval pathClinical review and DOH approval before a material reaches a BHW.
An assessment and certification standardHow RHU nurses and midwives confirm skill, and when it is renewed.
What we propose
Ask 1 A joint WHO–BLHSD working group To co-author the mainstreaming framework and standards, not just adopt a tool.
Ask 2 Co-sponsor HHP+ as the first national-grade material The worked example that proves the framework, and the model every later module follows.

Notes · slide 1

One three-day training costs ₱1.8 billion. There are 300,000 BHWs. Say it, then pause. We are not saying anyone is running training badly. Even well run, the model has five structural limits: cost repeats every round; material is frozen the day it is made; partner-supported efforts stay pilots; three days in a hall fade; and by the time it reaches a BHW it is third-hand.

Notes · slide 2

One material, many functions. The same approved content is a self-paced course, lives in the app, is a searchable knowledge base, and answers through the chat guide. The anchor is physical: an RHU nurse or midwife confirms the skill in person. Walk the right column and let each old problem close. Then the line that matters: this is a real, working, piloted tool, ready to launch the first materials. We claim no field results yet. The demo follows.

Notes · slide 3

The opportunity is bigger than the software. If WHO and BLHSD co-create the framework and the standards, then any material that proves itself in a pilot has a defined route to national: a content standard, a validation and approval path, and an assessment and certification standard. HHP+ is the first and best example. Two asks: stand up a joint working group, and co-sponsor HHP+ as the first national-grade material. Then go to the demo.
