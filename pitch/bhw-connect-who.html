02 What BHW Connect Is

Three things a training event cannot be.

Support at the doorstep

A bilingual Knowledge Base and Chat Guide answering a BHW's question in Filipino, English or Taglish — at the moment the question arises, not the moment the training was held.

Training that doesn't decay

Every BHW takes the same modules directly — no cascade, no retelling. Assessment is per person, against the same rubric, and content updates for everyone at once.

A record that can be queried

Competency is written to the national registry. DOH can ask “which BHWs in this municipality are certified for CBG screening?” and get an answer, at any level of the hierarchy.

All three run on the same platform, on a workforce that is already registered in it.

04 What a Training Programme Cannot Do

Six gaps that survive a perfectly executed contract.6 open

At a doorstep in month nine, a Barangay Health Worker has a question and no one to ask. That is the shape of all six.

G1Open

Materials go stale

Video is cut once. When guidance moves there is no mechanism to re-cut it and get the correction to people already trained.

G2Open

Cascade decay

Representatives cascade to peers. What each BHW receives depends on who retold it — and nothing measures the difference.

G3Open

48.4% is the map. Delivery is 2–5%.

Iloilo and Guimaras hold 11,533 of the region's 23,813 BHWs. But training begins only after inception, package development and validation — and a contractor running sessions for the weeks that remain reaches 360–1,200 people directly. The rest cascade. Aklan, Antique and Capiz — 12,280 — are outside the package entirely.

Modelled at 6–10 weeks of delivery, 2–3 sessions a week, 30–40 participants each. Assumptions shown so they can be checked.

G4Open

No route to national

Output 5 delivers scale-up recommendations. A recommendation is not a mechanism.

G5Open

One competency, once

Nothing is left standing to hold the next programme. Immunization, nutrition, MNCH each start again from zero.

G6Open

Paper competence

The return-demonstration checklist is hand-scored and filed locally. No queryable record of who is competent, no re-certification trigger.

Technology is not the end-all.
The standard we hold it to

A better app doesn't fix a health system — people do. BHW Connect earns its place only by making the human frontline stronger. The tool serves the worker, never the other way around.

05 The Whole System

Not a chat app. A platform the whole workforce runs on.

Answer

  • Knowledge Base — bilingual, versioned, every entry with a named owner and review-due date
  • Chat Guide — Filipino / English / Taglish, typo-tolerant, no cost per matched answer
  • Gap detection — every unanswered question logged, themed and counted
  • Flip-chart builder — client-facing image beside the BHW's script, approved before publish

Train & certify

  • E-learning — text, video and quizzes, with DOH-set pass marks and retake limits
  • Skills assessment — assessor queue, digitized return-demonstration rubrics
  • Certification — QR-verified, checkable by anyone without a login
  • Re-certification — refresher pathways designed in for workforce turnover

Reach & listen

  • Announcements — cascade from national down to a single barangay
  • Surveys — four question types, per-survey anonymity
  • Forum — cross-cutting practice sharing, moderated after posting
  • Notifications and offline access for low-connectivity barangays

See & govern

  • Roll-up dashboards at every tier — barangay to national
  • Reports & exports — CSV, Excel, PDF with a column picker
  • Plain-language audit trail, bilingual, for every administrative action
  • Accessibility & data privacy — font scale, contrast, dark mode; consent, retention and data-rights actions

Sixteen capabilities, one platform, one login — designed against a workforce that is already registered in it.

08 Designed to Last

HHP+ is module one, not the boundary.

One engine, many curricula
  • 1Community NCD screening — HHP+ / PhilPEN 2025. Year 1 flagship, Region VI. Proven end to end, and the template every later module inherits.
  • +Immunization · Nutrition · Family Planning · Communicable Disease Surveillance · First Aid · BHW Administrative Procedures — each on the roadmap, each a content and approval exercise.

Adding a programme is authoring and DOH approval, not a new build. The second module and the third cost a fraction of the first, because the engine, the workflow and the workforce are already there.

No per-question licensing

The rule-based engine answers matched questions at no marginal cost. The optional AI fallback for unmatched questions is modelled at ₱4,000–43,000 a year.

Portable by design

An open, non-proprietary Postgres core intended for migration onto government infrastructure, subject to a hosting agreement with DOH KMITS.

Built for turnover

BHW and local-official turnover is expected, not exceptional. Self-service onboarding, refreshers and re-certification are designed in, so a new cohort needs no new project.

Year 2, and every year after

What you buy again

15 The Impact

The output is 300,000 workers. The outcome is a country.

Every Filipino lives in a barangay, and every barangay has Barangay Health Workers. No other health workforce in the country can say that — which is why this is the only training investment whose denominator is the whole population.

≈380Filipinos per BHW

Present in every barangay

BHWs are the one cadre with a mandated presence in all 42,000+ barangays. Physicians, nurses and midwives are posted to facilities — and facilities are not everywhere.

Universal by construction: accredited barangay by barangay under RA 7883.

They go to the door

Facility care reaches the people who come. BHWs reach the people who never present — in the household, unprompted, before anything has become an emergency.

The only cadre whose default mode of delivery is the home visit.

Scale no facility network matches

Roughly 300,000 Barangay Health Workers. The whole public clinical workforce — physicians, nurses and midwives together — is a fraction of that.

≈1 BHW per 380 Filipinos, before a peso of new hiring. Comparator headcount [TO CONFIRM].

The last mile, where there is no one else

In geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas — island, upland and conflict-affected barangays — there is frequently no resident clinician at all.

Where the health system thins to nothing, the BHW is the health system.

Train the 300,000 and you have not trained 300,000 people. You have raised the floor of primary care for 114 million — by the only route that reaches all of them.

Population ≈114M (PSA population projections, 2024). 42,000+ barangays nationwide. ~300,000 BHWs from the Phase 1 registry. BHW mandate, accreditation and barangay assignment under RA 7883 (1995). Per-BHW ratio is 300,000 ÷ 114M and is a coverage denominator, not a caseload.
