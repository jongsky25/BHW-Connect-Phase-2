## Source register and wording rules

Every draft statement below uses an ID. The same IDs should travel into the final course, flipcharts, KB answers, test rationales, and review record. Before pilot publication, recheck each source for later issuances and the actual accredited provider list. The dates below identify an issuance or source, not a promise that a benefit or facility remains unchanged.

| Claim | BHW wording to preserve in both languages | Official source | Change sensitivity |

**Do not hard-code in lesson narration:** a medicine list, annual peso cap, clinic roster, pharmacy stock, fees, or a form-by-form membership application. A dated reference panel may show a currently verified figure or list later. These details are especially likely to change and must not become a generic Chat Guide promise.

## Course-level performance task

Given a household question, the BHW listens, sorts the request into the right pathway, explains the next action accurately in Filipino or English, offers the official channel and a low-connectivity fallback, and checks understanding. Passing the course does not grant a new permission or PhilHealth credential.

## Module 1 — Tungkulin ng BHW / The BHW's role

**Scene:** Aling Liza asks, “BHW ka, ikaw na ba ang mag-aapprove ng PhilHealth ko?” She also wants to know whether a medicine will be free.

**Observable objectives:** The BHW (1) names two types of help they can provide; (2) directs an individual record or clinical question to its proper decision maker; and (3) states one next action without promising eligibility or supply. **Claim IDs:** PH-02, PH-06, PH-07, BHW-01, BHW-02.

**Filipino draft:** “Matutulungan kitang alamin kung aling hakbang ang kailangan at kung saan ito opisyal na gagawin. Kung tungkol sa PIN o rekord mo, PhilHealth ang titingin niyan. Kung tungkol sa reseta o angkop na gamot, doktor at parmasyutiko ang magpapasya. Piliin natin ang una mong gagawin ngayon.” Ask what she already has: a PIN, a chosen YAKAP clinic, or a prescription. Refer to the matching path. Do not ask her to send a password or one-time code.

**English draft:** “I can help identify your next step and the official place to do it. PhilHealth checks your PIN or member record. A clinician and pharmacist handle prescription and medicine questions. Let us choose your first action today.” Ask whether she already has a PIN, a selected YAKAP clinic, or a prescription. Direct her to the matching path. Do not ask for a password or one-time code.

**Practice:** Sort three cards—“no PIN,” “which medicine should I take,” and “where is the accredited clinic”—to PhilHealth, clinician/pharmacist, or current provider listing. **Recall prompt:** “What is the first question you ask before giving directions?”

## Module 2 — Gabay sa YAKAP / Guide through YAKAP

**Scene:** Mang Nilo has a PhilHealth PIN, but says he has never chosen a YAKAP clinic. He believes choosing one in the app means all tests will happen immediately.

**Observable objectives:** The BHW (1) explains clinic selection and first encounter in sequence; (2) identifies who decides follow-up tests and referrals; and (3) helps locate a current accredited clinic without promising a specific service. **Claim IDs:** PH-01, PH-03, PH-04, PH-05, PH-06.

**Filipino draft:** “May PIN na kayo. Ang susunod ay pumili ng accredited YAKAP clinic sa kasalukuyang PhilHealth channel. Pagkatapos, pumunta sa napiling clinic para sa unang konsultasyon at assessment. Doon pag-uusapan ng clinician kung anong follow-up, test, referral, o gamot ang kailangan.” Show where to check the current clinic listing. If the app or signal is unavailable, offer the PhilHealth office route. Ask Mang Nilo to repeat where he will go first.

**English draft:** “You already have a PIN. Next, choose an accredited YAKAP clinic through a current PhilHealth channel. Then visit the selected clinic for the first consultation and assessment. The clinician will discuss any needed follow-up, test, referral, or medicine.” Show where to verify the current clinic list. If the app or signal is unavailable, offer the PhilHealth office route. Ask Mang Nilo to repeat where he will go first.

**Practice:** Put four cards in order: PIN or record check; clinic selection; first patient encounter; clinician-directed follow-up. **Misconception check:** “Does clinic selection by itself guarantee a laboratory test?” Expected answer: no; clinical assessment and the applicable benefit rules determine the next care step.

## Module 3 — Gabay sa GAMOT / Guide through GAMOT

**Scene:** Aling Tess brings a photo of a medicine box and asks whether the BHW can tell her the dose and which pharmacy has it today.

**Observable objectives:** The BHW (1) explains the consultation-to-prescription-to-dispensing pathway; (2) distinguishes current accredited facility information from live stock information; and (3) redirects dose, suitability, and prescription questions to the clinician or pharmacist. **Claim IDs:** PH-05, PH-06, PH-07, BHW-01.

**Filipino draft:** “Maaaring may benepisyo sa outpatient na gamot sa ilalim ng GAMOT, pero kailangan munang masuri kayo at mabigyan ng angkop na reseta ayon sa kasalukuyang tuntunin. Titingnan natin ang listahan ng accredited na pasilidad; ang doktor o parmasyutiko ang sasagot sa dosis at kung ang gamot na ito ay para sa inyo. Tatawagan o tatanungin ang pasilidad para sa kasalukuyang availability.” Do not infer coverage or stock from the box photo.

**English draft:** “GAMOT may cover outpatient medicines, but you first need an assessment and an appropriate prescription under the current rules. We can check the accredited facility list; the clinician or pharmacist answers the dose and whether this medicine is right for you. Ask the facility about current availability.” Do not infer coverage or stock from the box photo.

**Practice:** Respond to “Can I get this medicine free today?” in two sentences: one explains the path, one gives the next action. **Misconception check:** An accredited pharmacy listing is not proof that a particular medicine is in stock today.

## Module 4 — Tamang rehistrasyon / The right registration path

**Scene:** Three neighbors all say, “Magpaparehistro ako sa PhilHealth,” but mean different things: one has no PIN, one has a PIN but no YAKAP clinic, and one is asking about a dependent.

**Observable objectives:** The BHW (1) asks which registration task is meant; (2) sorts the three common pathways; and (3) gives an official next step that works when the phone or signal is unavailable. **Claim IDs:** PH-02, PH-03, PH-04, PH-05, PH-08, BHW-02.

**Filipino draft:** “Ang ‘rehistro’ ay maaaring tungkol sa PhilHealth PIN o rekord, o tungkol sa pagpili ng YAKAP clinic. Alin dito ang kailangan ninyo?” If there is no PIN or the record is uncertain, direct the resident to the official PhilHealth membership channel; do not fill out the full application inside BHW Connect. If the PIN exists but no clinic is selected, show the current clinic-selection channels and accredited list. For a dependent or transfer, verify the current PhilHealth process before giving a step. If internet is unavailable, name the nearest appropriate PhilHealth office or advise how to find it when connected. Let the resident enter their own credentials.

**English draft:** “Registration can mean a PhilHealth PIN or record, or selecting a YAKAP clinic. Which one do you need?” With no PIN or an uncertain record, direct the resident to the official PhilHealth membership channel; do not complete the detailed application in BHW Connect. With a PIN but no selected clinic, show the current selection channels and accredited list. For a dependent or transfer, verify the current PhilHealth process first. If the internet is unavailable, offer the appropriate PhilHealth office route. Let the resident enter their own credentials.

**Practice:** Classify four short cases into PIN/record, clinic selection, dependent/transfer, or unclear. For an unclear case, ask a clarifying question rather than choosing a route. **Misconception check:** Having a PIN and having a selected YAKAP clinic are separate conditions.

## Module 5 — Usapang pangbahay / The household conversation

**Scene:** Mang Nilo has little mobile data and is frustrated after two unsuccessful attempts to use an app. He asks the BHW to “just do everything” for him.

**Observable objectives:** The BHW (1) asks and confirms the need; (2) presents the correct patient-facing flipchart while retaining separate BHW notes; (3) gives one feasible next action; and (4) uses teach-back to confirm understanding. **Claim IDs:** PH-04, PH-05, BHW-01, BHW-02.

**Filipino draft:** “Tingnan muna natin kung PIN/rekord ba ang kailangan o pagpili ng YAKAP clinic. Ipapakita ko ang hakbang sa chart. Kung walang signal, puwede nating hanapin ang opisyal na tanggapan o magplano kung kailan makakakonekta. Kayo ang maglalagay ng inyong sariling login at code. Para matiyak kong malinaw ang paliwanag ko, ano ang una ninyong gagawin?” Correct one misunderstanding calmly and agree on a follow-up if needed.

**English draft:** “First let us check whether you need help with your PIN or record, or with choosing a YAKAP clinic. I will show the step on the chart. If there is no signal, we can find the official office or plan when to reconnect. You enter your own login and code. To make sure I explained it clearly, what will you do first?” Correct one misunderstanding calmly and agree on a follow-up if needed.

**Practice:** A short rehearsal with a partner or self-study recording. The final observed role-play uses a different household card, the same job aid, and the six-indicator rubric.
