# Gabay sa PhilHealth — P1 assessment draft

**Status:** Draft for admin and assessor review. These items are not in the live course.
**Pass contract:** Ten-item quiz, at least 80%, up to three attempts; then an assessor-observed role-play. A certificate follows both passes and changes no permissions. The opening diagnostic is unscored.

## Opening diagnostic (unscored)

Ask before lessons, then show the correct reasoning without recording a pass/fail: (D1) “Does a PIN mean a YAKAP clinic has already been selected?” (No.) (D2) “Can a BHW promise that a named medicine is available today?” (No.) (D3) “When someone says ‘register,’ what should the BHW ask first?” (Whether they mean PIN/member record or YAKAP clinic selection.) Claim IDs: PH-02, PH-03, PH-05, PH-07.

## Ten-item knowledge assessment

Each item has one correct option. The text below is a content draft; check translation, distractors, and current PhilHealth rules before loading a question bank. The rationales should appear after a completed attempt, not expose answers during the attempt.

### Q1 — BHW scope (PH-02, PH-06)

**Filipino:** Sabi ni Aling Liza, “Ikaw na ang mag-aapprove ng PhilHealth ko?” Ano ang pinakamainam na sagot?
**English:** Aling Liza asks, “Will you approve my PhilHealth?” What is the best reply?

A. “Oo, maaaprubahan kita kapag natapos ang kurso ko.” / “Yes, I can approve you once I finish my course.”
B. “Matutulungan kitang hanapin ang tamang hakbang; PhilHealth ang titingin sa iyong rekord.” / “I can help identify the right step; PhilHealth checks your record.” **(Correct)**
C. “Hindi mo na kailangan ng PIN.” / “You no longer need a PIN.”

**Rationale:** The BHW guides; PhilHealth resolves an individual member record.

### Q2 — Credentials (BHW-02)

**Filipino:** Walang signal sa bahay. Gusto ng residente na itext sa BHW ang password at one-time code. Ano ang gagawin?
**English:** There is no signal at home. The resident wants to text the BHW a password and one-time code. What should the BHW do?

A. Tanggapin at itago para sa susunod na pagbisita. / Accept and save them for the next visit.
B. Ilagay sa Chat Guide para maalala. / Put them in Chat Guide for later.
C. Sabihing ang residente mismo ang gagamit ng kaniyang credentials sa opisyal na channel kapag maaari na. / Explain that the resident should use their own credentials in an official channel when possible. **(Correct)**

**Rationale:** The course does not authorize a BHW to collect or retain credentials.

### Q3 — YAKAP sequence (PH-02, PH-03)

**Filipino:** May PIN na si Mang Nilo ngunit wala pang napiling YAKAP clinic. Ano ang susunod?
**English:** Mang Nilo has a PIN but has not selected a YAKAP clinic. What is next?

A. Pumili ng accredited YAKAP clinic sa kasalukuyang opisyal na channel, pagkatapos ay pumunta roon para sa FPE; itanong kung paano susunod ang konsultasyon. / Select an accredited YAKAP clinic through a current official channel, then go there for the FPE; ask how consultation follows. **(Correct)**
B. Pumunta agad sa kahit anong botika para sa GAMOT. / Go straight to any pharmacy for GAMOT.
C. Mag-apply muli para sa bagong PIN. / Apply for a new PIN.

**Rationale:** PIN and clinic selection are separate steps.

### Q4 — Clinical decision (PH-06)

**Filipino:** Pagkapili ng clinic, hinihingi ni Mang Nilo ang isang partikular na laboratory test. Ano ang sasabihin?
**English:** After selecting a clinic, Mang Nilo asks for a particular laboratory test. What should the BHW say?

A. “Siguradong kasama iyan bukas.” / “That test is guaranteed tomorrow.”
B. “Pagkatapos ng FPE, clinician ang magpapasya sa konsultasyon kung anong test o referral ang kailangan.” / “After the FPE, a clinician decides at the consultation which test or referral is needed.” **(Correct)**
C. “Ako ang mag-oorder ng test para sa inyo.” / “I will order the test.”

**Rationale:** Selection does not substitute for clinical assessment or the applicable benefit rules.

### Q5 — GAMOT pathway (PH-07)

**Filipino:** Nagtatanong si Aling Tess kung paano magsisimula sa GAMOT. Alin ang tamang daloy?
**English:** Aling Tess asks how to start with GAMOT. Which sequence is appropriate?

A. Pumili ng gamot → humingi ng bayad sa BHW → kumuha sa botika. / Choose a medicine → pay the BHW → collect it from a pharmacy.
B. Konsultasyon at assessment → angkop na reseta ayon sa tuntunin → accredited dispensing facility. / Consultation and assessment → appropriate prescription under current rules → accredited dispensing facility. **(Correct)**
C. Ipakita ang litrato ng kahon sa BHW → pumili ng dosis. / Show a box photo to the BHW → choose a dose.

**Rationale:** A clinician determines the appropriate medicine; dispensing follows current PhilHealth requirements.

### Q6 — Stock and coverage claim (PH-05, PH-07)

**Filipino:** Nasa accredited facility list ang isang botika. Ano ang maaari mong sabihin tungkol sa gamot na gusto ng residente?
**English:** A pharmacy is in the accredited facility list. What can you say about the resident's requested medicine?

A. “Tiyak na may stock at libre iyan ngayon.” / “It is definitely in stock and free today.”
B. “Accredited ang pasilidad sa kasalukuyang listahan; kumpirmahin sa clinician, PhilHealth, at botika ang reseta, sakop, at availability.” / “The facility appears in the current accredited list; confirm the prescription, coverage, and availability with the clinician, PhilHealth, and pharmacy.” **(Correct)**
C. “Puwede kang magpalit ng dosis kung walang stock.” / “You can change the dose if it is out of stock.”

**Rationale:** A listing does not establish a particular medicine's suitability, coverage, or live stock.

### Q7 — No PIN (PH-02)

**Filipino:** Hindi makumpirma ng residente kung mayroon siyang PIN. Ano ang unang ruta?
**English:** A resident cannot confirm whether they have a PIN. What is the first route?

A. Tulungan siyang magtanong sa opisyal na PhilHealth membership channel tungkol sa PIN o rekord. / Help them contact an official PhilHealth membership channel about the PIN or record. **(Correct)**
B. Pumili ka ng YAKAP clinic gamit ang sarili mong account. / Select a YAKAP clinic using your own account.
C. Magbigay ng reseta. / Issue a prescription.

**Rationale:** Resolve the record through PhilHealth; the detailed application remains there.

### Q8 — Ambiguous registration (PH-02, PH-03)

**Filipino:** Sinabi ng residente, “Paano magparehistro?” Ano ang unang tanong?
**English:** A resident says, “How do I register?” What do you ask first?

A. “PIN o rekord ba sa PhilHealth, o pagpili ng YAKAP clinic?” / “Do you mean your PhilHealth PIN or record, or selecting a YAKAP clinic?” **(Correct)**
B. “Anong gamot ang gusto mo?” / “Which medicine do you want?”
C. “Ibigay ang password mo.” / “Give me your password.”

**Rationale:** Clarify the task before giving a pathway.

### Q9 — Dependent/transfer (PH-08)

**Filipino:** Sabi ng magulang, “Napili ko na ang clinic ko; tiyak bang iyon din ang sa anak ko?” Ano ang sagot?
**English:** A parent asks, “I selected my clinic; is my child automatically at the same clinic?” What is the reply?

A. “Oo, laging awtomatiko.” / “Yes, always automatically.”
B. “Hindi na maaaring iparehistro ang dependent.” / “Dependents cannot be registered.”
C. “Tingnan natin ang kasalukuyang proseso at selection record ng dependent sa opisyal na PhilHealth channel.” / “Let us verify the current dependent process and selection record through an official PhilHealth channel.” **(Correct)**

**Rationale:** Do not infer an individual dependent's selection from the parent's record.

### Q10 — Teach-back and low connectivity (PH-04, BHW-01)

**Filipino:** Walang mobile data si Mang Nilo. Pagkatapos ipakita ang chart, ano ang pinakamainam na pagtatapos?
**English:** Mang Nilo has no mobile data. After showing the chart, what is the best ending?

A. “Balik ka na lang kapag may data; wala na akong ibang magagawa.” / “Come back with data; there is nothing else I can do.”
B. Magbigay ng kasalukuyang in-person PhilHealth option at itanong kung ano ang una niyang gagawin. / Give a current in-person PhilHealth option and ask him to repeat his first step. **(Correct)**
C. Kunin ang telepono at itago ang password. / Take his phone and save the password.

**Rationale:** A feasible official route plus teach-back shows whether the resident can act.

## Observed role-play cards

**Conditions for every card:** 8–10 minutes; BHW may use the one-page pathway aid and relevant flipchart. The assessor may ask one neutral follow-up. No real resident identifiers. The assessor records the scenario ID, six indicators, observed evidence, any prompt used, pass/fail, and practice advice. A retry uses a different card and preserves the earlier record and passing quiz.

| Card | Resident brief | Assessor follow-up | Essential response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 — No PIN** | “I want YAKAP but do not know if I have a PIN.” | “Can you use your phone and keep my login?” | Identify PIN/record path, direct to official PhilHealth channel, refuse credential retention, confirm resident's next step. |
| **R2 — PIN, no clinic** | “I have a PIN but never chose a clinic.” | “Will choosing one immediately give me a laboratory test?” | Identify clinic-selection path, verify accredited clinic/current channel, explain first encounter and clinician decision, teach back. |
| **R3 — Dependent or change** | “I selected a clinic; I want to check my child or move clinics.” | “Our old clinic says we are assigned there.” | Distinguish dependent/transfer, direct to current official verification, avoid promising an immediate change, teach back. |
| **R4 — Medicine request** | “Can I get this named maintenance medicine through GAMOT today?” | “My old prescription says a different dose; which should I take?” | Explain assessment/prescription/accredited dispensing path, refuse dose advice and stock guarantee, refer to clinician/pharmacist, teach back. |

## Six-indicator observation rubric

Mark **Observed**, **Needs practice**, or **Not seen** and note the words or action seen. All six must be Observed for a pass. A neutral prompt is permitted, but if the prompt supplies a critical missing step, score that step Needs practice and arrange a retry.

1. **Ask and sort:** The BHW asks what the resident needs and chooses PIN/record, clinic selection, dependent/transfer, GAMOT, or “need to clarify” correctly.
2. **Explain:** The BHW states the relevant sequence in plain language without adding an unsupported guarantee.
3. **Stay in role:** The BHW neither decides eligibility nor gives dosing/prescribing advice, and does not collect credentials.
4. **Direct:** The BHW gives one feasible official next action and an offline option if the card requires it.
5. **Use the flipchart:** The patient sees the patient view; the BHW uses separate notes and does not expose another person's data.
6. **Check understanding:** The BHW asks the resident to repeat the next step and corrects a misunderstanding respectfully.

**Feedback template:** “Observed: [specific action]. Practice: [specific missed step]. Before retry, rehearse [lesson/flipchart page].” The assessor should not give a bare fail with no reason. A failed observation does not invalidate a passed knowledge quiz and does not issue a certificate.
