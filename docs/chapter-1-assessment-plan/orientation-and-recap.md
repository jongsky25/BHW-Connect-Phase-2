# Chapter 1 orientation major lesson introductions and recap

Before learners start Chapter 1, establish what to expect and what they will work toward. Give each of the nine major lessons one complete short introduction, then bring the chapter together in **What we have learned in Chapter 1** before the post-test. These briefers give the learning journey a clear beginning, direction and ending.

**Scope: one whole introduction for Major Lesson 1, one for Major Lesson 2, and so on through Major Lesson 9.** Each introduction covers the full major lesson's purpose, scope and learning goals and appears once before its content begins. Author and review all nine introductions as one complete set. Do not split an introduction into increments or add introductions at smaller lesson or checkpoint boundaries.

For repository mapping only, the nine major lessons correspond to modules/subchapters 1.1–1.9, which contain 42 smaller lessons. The separate 12-group reinforcement plan does not create additional introductions.

The bilingual copy below is a concrete editorial draft based on the chapter assessment map. Review it against the approved lesson revisions before publication; it does not replace the detailed lesson objectives or existing observation rubrics.

The [Sol handoff](SOL-HANDOFF.md) specifies routes, baseline gating, returning-learner behavior and acceptance checks. All nine introductions are part of the same complete delivery.

## Placement and pacing

| Moment | Learner experience | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Expectation setting | What participation looks like, how feedback works, and how to ask for support | First entry to Chapter 1 |
| Chapter overview | Why this chapter matters and the broad capabilities it develops | After expectations, before the pre-test |
| Nine major lesson introductions | The whole major lesson's purpose, scope and two concrete learning goals | After the pre-test, once at the start of each major lesson |
| Chapter recap | Retrieve and connect the main ideas; choose any review needed | After chapter study, before the post-test |
| Answer review | Inspect actual answers, explanations and next steps | After confirmed post-test submission, including after certification |

Keep expectations and the chapter overview as two short steps within one chapter welcome, not two new course modules. Aim for roughly 60–100 words per language for each opening step, 50–90 words for each complete major lesson introduction, and a scannable recap of about 250–350 words per language. These are editorial budgets, not mandatory timers. Optional narration reads the same content; a video is not required.

Preview topics and broad goals before the pre-test, without explaining factual distinctions or walking through questions. Put the nine complete major lesson introductions after the baseline, each at the start of its major lesson. A recap before the post-test is instructional review: the result measures understanding after review, not delayed retention. Do not conceal the recap merely to make the test harder.

## Expectation setting draft

### English

**Before we begin**

Chapter 1 connects the BHW role with everyday community work. You will study short lessons, think through situations, practise decisions and receive explanations. You can pause and return.

The pre-test helps identify your starting point; you do not need to pass it. During study, quick practice and learning notes help you identify questions. The post-test shows what you understand and what to revisit. Your assessor checks practical performance separately and can respond to insights you choose to share. Ask for support when you need it; you are not expected to know everything at the start.

### Filipino

**Bago tayo magsimula**

Iniuugnay ng Kabanata 1 ang tungkulin ng BHW sa pang-araw-araw na gawain sa komunidad. Mag-aaral ka ng maiikling aralin, susuri ng mga sitwasyon, magsasanay sa pagpapasya at makatatanggap ng paliwanag. Maaari kang huminto at bumalik.

Tutukuyin ng pre-test ang panimulang kaalaman mo; hindi mo ito kailangang ipasa. Sa pag-aaral, makatutulong ang maikling pagsasanay at mga tala upang matukoy ang mga tanong mo. Ipapakita ng post-test ang nauunawaan mo at dapat balikan. Hiwalay na oobserbahan ng assessor ang praktikal na pagganap at maaari niyang sagutin ang mga insight na ibabahagi mo. Humingi ng tulong kapag kailangan; hindi inaasahang alam mo na ang lahat.

## Chapter overview draft

### English

**What you will learn in Chapter 1**

This chapter builds the foundations for your work as a BHW. Across nine areas, you will explore your role, UHC, health-station policies, BHW rights and benefits, local partners, communication, problem solving, safe work and responsible use of resources.

By the end, you will work toward being able to:

- Explain your role and navigate the policies and people relevant to your work.
- Communicate clearly and handle information carefully.
- Examine a community concern and propose a practical next step.
- Make reasoned choices about safety and resources.

You will practise these through situations, discussion and activities. Begin with the pre-test to identify your starting point.

### Filipino

**Ano ang matututuhan sa Kabanata 1**

Binubuo ng kabanatang ito ang pundasyon ng gawain mo bilang BHW. Sa siyam na paksa, pag-aaralan mo ang iyong tungkulin, UHC, mga polisiya sa health station, karapatan at benepisyo ng BHW, mga katuwang, komunikasyon, paglutas ng problema, ligtas na paggawa at wastong paggamit ng rekurso.

Sa pagtatapos, pagsisikapan mong magawa ang mga sumusunod:

- Ipaliwanag ang iyong tungkulin at tukuyin ang mga polisiya at taong kaugnay ng gawain.
- Makipag-usap nang malinaw at maingat na humawak ng impormasyon.
- Suriin ang suliranin sa komunidad at magmungkahi ng praktikal na hakbang.
- Magpasya nang may batayan tungkol sa kaligtasan at rekurso.

Magsasanay ka sa mga sitwasyon, talakayan at gawain. Magsimula sa pre-test upang matukoy ang panimulang kaalaman mo.

## Complete set of nine major lesson introductions

Show the relevant whole introduction once on entry to each major lesson, with Start lesson and Back to chapter actions. Keep it available through Overview in the major lesson header. Returning learners get Resume without having to replay the introduction. Major Lessons 1–9 below map in order to the existing 1.1–1.9 subject areas; no smaller lesson has a new introduction under this plan.

### Major lesson 1 The roles of a BHW

**English:** A household concern can involve several parts of your BHW role. This section follows how you help people learn, participate and connect with services, and how accurate records support that work.

By the end, practise explaining how the roles work together and what is within your training. Choose an appropriate record and give a clear account of what happened, what you did and what still needs attention.

**Filipino:** Maaaring mangailangan ang isang suliranin ng kabahayan ng iba't ibang bahagi ng tungkulin ng BHW. Susundan dito kung paano ka tumutulong sa pagkatuto, pakikilahok at paglapit sa serbisyo, at kung paano sinusuportahan ng tamang tala ang gawain.

Sa pagtatapos, magsanay sa pagpapaliwanag kung paano nagtutulungan ang mga papel at ano ang saklaw ng pagsasanay mo. Pumili ng angkop na tala at malinaw na iulat ang nangyari, ginawa at kailangan pang asikasuhin.

### Major lesson 2 The UHC Act of 2019

**English:** Residents may ask what UHC means for the care they need. This section connects the purpose of UHC with primary care, local health-system roles and small improvements in how people find help.

By the end, practise explaining what needs to be checked about a particular service. Identify an appropriate local verification step and propose one feasible improvement with the people responsible for it.

**Filipino:** Maaaring magtanong ang mga residente kung ano ang ibig sabihin ng UHC para sa pangangalagang kailangan nila. Iniuugnay ng bahaging ito ang layunin ng UHC sa primary care, mga tungkulin sa lokal na sistemang pangkalusugan at maliliit na pagpapabuti sa paglapit sa tulong.

Sa pagtatapos, magsanay sa pagpapaliwanag kung ano ang dapat tiyakin tungkol sa isang serbisyo. Tukuyin ang angkop na lokal na hakbang sa beripikasyon at magmungkahi ng kayang pagpapabuti kasama ang mga responsable rito.

### Major lesson 3 Policies at the barangay health station

**English:** Offers, requests and proposed changes at the BHS need careful judgment. This section explores company promotions, breastfeeding support, respectful refusals and resource policies through practical situations.

By the end, identify the policy questions and responsible contact for a specific situation. Practise a respectful response or proposal that protects service needs and includes the checks required before action.

**Filipino:** Nangangailangan ng maingat na pagpapasya ang mga alok, kahilingan at mungkahing pagbabago sa BHS. Susuriin dito ang promosyon ng kumpanya, suporta sa pagpapasuso, magalang na pagtanggi at mga polisiya sa rekurso gamit ang praktikal na sitwasyon.

Sa pagtatapos, tukuyin ang mga tanong tungkol sa polisiya at ang responsableng contact sa isang sitwasyon. Magsanay ng magalang na tugon o mungkahing nangangalaga sa serbisyo at may kinakailangang pagtiyak bago kumilos.

### Major lesson 4 RA 7883 benefits rights and accreditation

**English:** Questions about BHW status, benefits and eligibility often involve different conditions and offices. This section helps you read a situation carefully and ask a useful follow-up question.

By the end, distinguish accreditation, registration, benefit conditions and civil service eligibility. Practise checking the available evidence and asking the appropriate office about what remains unclear, without promising an outcome that has not been confirmed.

**Filipino:** Kadalasang may magkakaibang kondisyon at tanggapan ang mga tanong tungkol sa katayuan, benepisyo at eligibility ng BHW. Tutulong ang bahaging ito upang maingat mong maunawaan ang sitwasyon at makapagtanong ng kapaki-pakinabang na follow-up.

Sa pagtatapos, maihiwalay ang akreditasyon, pagpaparehistro, kondisyon ng benepisyo at civil service eligibility. Magsanay sa pagsusuri ng ebidensya at pagtatanong sa angkop na tanggapan tungkol sa hindi pa malinaw, nang walang pangakong hindi pa kumpirmado.

### Major lesson 5 The BHW and their barangay

**English:** Community work depends on relationships, clear responsibilities and realistic commitments. This section introduces barangay and city or municipal partners, teamwork and management of your own work.

By the end, explain why a particular contact fits a stated need and how to verify the local arrangement. Practise adapting to a changed task, communicating early and agreeing on a respectful handoff and follow-up.

**Filipino:** Nakasalalay ang gawaing pangkomunidad sa ugnayan, malinaw na pananagutan at makatotohanang pangako. Ipakikilala rito ang mga katuwang sa barangay at lungsod o munisipyo, pagtutulungan at pamamahala sa sariling gawain.

Sa pagtatapos, ipaliwanag kung bakit angkop ang isang contact sa pangangailangan at paano titiyakin ang lokal na kaayusan. Magsanay sa pag-angkop sa nagbagong gawain, maagang pakikipag-usap at pagbuo ng magalang na handoff at follow-up.

### Major lesson 6 Effective communication

**English:** A useful conversation helps people express their concern and understand the next step. This section follows listening, clarification, explanation, recording and sharing relevant information.

By the end, practise asking neutral questions and checking what the other person understood. Produce a short record or handoff that identifies sources, preserves uncertainty where necessary and respects the person and the intended recipient.

**Filipino:** Tinutulungan ng mabuting usapan ang tao na maipahayag ang concern at maunawaan ang susunod na hakbang. Susundan dito ang pakikinig, paglilinaw, pagpapaliwanag, pagtatala at pagbabahagi ng mahalagang impormasyon.

Sa pagtatapos, magsanay sa pagtatanong nang hindi isinusubo ang sagot at sa pagsuri sa naunawaan ng kausap. Gumawa ng maikling tala o handoff na may pinagmulan ng impormasyon, tapat sa hindi pa tiyak at may paggalang sa tao at angkop na tatanggap.

### Major lesson 7 Identifying problems and planning solutions

**English:** A workable solution starts with a clear account of the problem and evidence about possible causes. This section moves from describing a concern to examining causes, discussing priorities and reviewing an action plan.

By the end, practise separating evidence from assumptions and explaining a proposed priority. Build a small plan with partners and identify what result would tell you whether it needs to change.

**Filipino:** Nagsisimula ang maisasagawang solusyon sa malinaw na paglalarawan ng problema at ebidensya tungkol sa posibleng sanhi. Mula sa concern, susuriin dito ang mga sanhi, tatalakayin ang prayoridad at babalikan ang plano ng aksyon.

Sa pagtatapos, magsanay sa paghihiwalay ng ebidensya at hinala at sa pagpapaliwanag ng mungkahing prayoridad. Bumuo ng maliit na plano kasama ang mga katuwang at tukuyin ang resultang magsasabi kung kailangan itong baguhin.

### Major lesson 8 Occupational safety and health

**English:** Safe work begins before an activity starts and continues when conditions change. This section covers hazards, appropriate controls, preparation for station and field work, and response to an exposure.

By the end, identify a hazard, a suitable control and the condition that must be checked before proceeding. Practise the approved response and reporting steps in a safe simulation; a trainer observes the practical demonstration separately.

**Filipino:** Nagsisimula ang ligtas na paggawa bago ang gawain at nagpapatuloy kapag nagbabago ang kondisyon. Saklaw dito ang mga panganib, angkop na kontrol, paghahanda sa health station at field work, at pagtugon sa exposure.

Sa pagtatapos, tukuyin ang panganib, angkop na kontrol at kondisyong dapat tiyakin bago magpatuloy. Magsanay ng aprubadong pagtugon at pag-uulat sa ligtas na simulation; hiwalay na oobserbahan ng trainer ang praktikal na demonstrasyon.

### Major lesson 9 Efficient and sustainable practices at work

**English:** Using resources well means keeping services correct, safe and timely while reducing avoidable waste. This section examines stock and materials, possible changes and how to judge their results.

By the end, practise verifying usable supplies and proposing an authorized change with safeguards. Compare resource use under comparable workloads and explain what the information does, and does not, show about service quality.

**Filipino:** Ang mahusay na paggamit ng rekurso ay pagpapanatili ng tama, ligtas at napapanahong serbisyo habang binabawasan ang maiiwasang aksaya. Susuriin dito ang stock at materyales, posibleng pagbabago at resulta nito.

Sa pagtatapos, magsanay sa pagtiyak ng magagamit na supplies at pagmungkahi ng awtorisadong pagbabagong may proteksyon. Ihambing ang paggamit ng rekurso sa maihahambing na dami ng gawain at ipaliwanag kung ano ang ipinapakita, at hindi ipinapakita, ng impormasyon tungkol sa kalidad ng serbisyo.

## Chapter recap before the post test

Present this after the last required lesson, with the normal optional checkpoint/reflection invitation still available. An unfinished optional checkpoint or reflection does not block the recap or post-test. A returning learner with completed content can open it from the chapter outline.

Start with an optional mental-retrieval prompt: “Before reading the summary, think of one idea you would use differently in your work.” / “Bago basahin ang buod, isipin ang isang ideyang gagamitin mo nang iba sa iyong gawain.” The learner can reveal the summary immediately; no typed answer or correctness gate is required.

### English recap

**What we have learned in Chapter 1**

Chapter 1 brought together the foundations of BHW work. Use these reminders to connect the ideas and identify what you want to revisit:

1. **Our roles:** Education, organizing and service support can work together. Stay within your training and explain the next step clearly.
2. **UHC:** Help residents navigate care. Check the particular benefit, provider and local process before making a promise about a service.
3. **BHS policies:** Understand the actual offer or proposed change, respect residents and confirm the applicable policy and authority.
4. **Rights and benefits:** Accreditation, registration, benefit conditions and civil service eligibility have distinct requirements. Verify evidence and unresolved questions with the appropriate office.
5. **Working with others:** Choose contacts by the need, clarify responsibilities and communicate changes early. A clear handoff respects private reasons and confirms who will act.
6. **Communication:** Listen, clarify and check understanding. Keep reports, observations and unknown details distinct, and share relevant information through an appropriate route.
7. **Problem solving:** Describe the concern without blame, test possible causes and agree a feasible plan. Review the outcome; a completed activity does not by itself prove improvement.
8. **Safety:** Match hazards to controls, check readiness and pause affected unsafe work. Follow the taught reporting and evaluation steps promptly after an exposure.
9. **Resources:** Verify usable stock and approved materials. Protect essential services when proposing savings, and compare resource use alongside workload and quality.

These are reminders of what we studied, not a declaration that every skill has been demonstrated. Your practical assessment remains separate.

Revisit an area if you need to, or continue to the post-test to see what you understand and what needs more work. After submitting, you can review your answers and their explanations.

### Filipino recap

**Mga natutuhan natin sa Kabanata 1**

Pinagsama ng Kabanata 1 ang mga pundasyon ng gawain ng BHW. Gamitin ang mga paalalang ito upang iugnay ang mga ideya at matukoy ang nais mong balikan:

1. **Mga tungkulin:** Maaaring magtulungan ang pagtuturo, pag-oorganisa at suporta sa serbisyo. Manatili sa saklaw ng pagsasanay at malinaw na ipaliwanag ang susunod na hakbang.
2. **UHC:** Tulungan ang residente sa paglapit sa pangangalaga. Tiyakin ang partikular na benepisyo, provider at lokal na proseso bago mangako tungkol sa serbisyo.
3. **Mga polisiya sa BHS:** Unawain ang aktuwal na alok o mungkahing pagbabago, igalang ang residente at tiyakin ang naaangkop na polisiya at awtoridad.
4. **Karapatan at benepisyo:** May magkakaibang kinakailangan ang akreditasyon, pagpaparehistro, mga benepisyo at civil service eligibility. Beripikahin ang ebidensya at hindi pa malinaw sa angkop na tanggapan.
5. **Pakikipagtulungan:** Pumili ng contact ayon sa pangangailangan, linawin ang pananagutan at maagang ipaalam ang pagbabago. Igalang ang pribadong dahilan at kumpirmahin kung sino ang kikilos sa handoff.
6. **Komunikasyon:** Makinig, maglinaw at suriin ang pagkaunawa. Ihiwalay ang sinabi, naobserbahan at hindi pa tiyak; ibahagi ang mahalagang impormasyon sa angkop na paraan.
7. **Paglutas ng problema:** Ilarawan ang concern nang walang sisi, beripikahin ang posibleng sanhi at magkasundo sa kayang plano. Suriin ang resulta; hindi sapat na patunay ng pagbuti ang natapos na aktibidad.
8. **Kaligtasan:** Itugma ang panganib sa kontrol, tiyakin ang kahandaan at ihinto muna ang apektadong hindi ligtas na gawain. Sundin agad ang itinurong pag-uulat at pagpapatingin pagkatapos ng exposure.
9. **Rekurso:** Tiyakin ang magagamit na stock at aprubadong materyales. Protektahan ang mahalagang serbisyo sa pagtitipid at ihambing ang paggamit ayon sa dami at kalidad ng gawain.

Mga paalala ito ng ating pinag-aralan, hindi pahayag na naipakita na ang lahat ng kasanayan. Hiwalay pa rin ang praktikal na assessment.

Balikan ang kailangan, o magpatuloy sa post-test upang makita ang nauunawaan mo at dapat pang pag-aralan. Pagkatapos magsumite, maaari mong balikan ang mga sagot at paliwanag.
