# Chapter 1 sample paired assessment questions

These four draft pairs demonstrate the proposed tasks for records, teamwork, prioritization and resource monitoring. Each has one scored response, three substantive alternatives and the shared unscored-choice label “Hindi ko pa alam / I don't know yet.” That explicit unknown earns zero points, but is recorded separately from a wrong answer. Each form therefore contains four scored questions in this sample, not a complete chapter test.

All cases and figures are fictional. The forms target matching constructs and intended demand; equivalence still needs review and pilot evidence. Filipino and English text below are authoring drafts requiring bilingual review. The keys and explanations are reviewer material and, in the proposed product, post-submission feedback; they do not appear beside an unanswered question or after individual pre-test responses.

## Pair 1 Choosing and checking a record

Blueprint T04; primary lesson 1.1.5, supporting lesson 1.6.4. Evidence: select the record suited to a service question and handle an uncertain entry without inventing an answer.

### Pre test in English

The midwife asks which households received sanitation teaching last week. The household profile lists household members. The service registry records teaching visits, but one entry has no confirmed date. What should the BHW do?

A. Use the household profile, because it lists every household that could receive teaching.

B. Use the service registry and verify the undated entry before counting it for last week.

C. Use the service registry and count the undated entry because it appears beside last week's entries.

### Pre test in Filipino

Itinanong ng midwife kung aling mga kabahayan ang nabigyan ng pagtuturo tungkol sa sanitasyon noong nakaraang linggo. Nasa household profile ang mga miyembro ng kabahayan. Nakatala sa service registry ang mga pagbisita para sa pagtuturo, ngunit hindi pa tiyak ang petsa ng isang entry. Ano ang dapat gawin ng BHW?

A. Gamitin ang household profile dahil nakalista rito ang lahat ng kabahayang maaaring mabigyan ng pagtuturo.

B. Gamitin ang service registry at beripikahin ang entry na walang tiyak na petsa bago ito isama sa bilang para sa nakaraang linggo.

C. Gamitin ang service registry at isama ang entry na walang petsa dahil katabi ito ng mga entry noong nakaraang linggo.

### Post test in English

The nurse asks which older residents received healthy-food teaching this month. The master list identifies older residents. The service registry records teaching sessions, but one entry has no confirmed date. What should the BHW do?

A. Use the service registry and verify the undated entry before counting it for this month.

B. Use the master list, because everyone in that group was intended to receive teaching.

C. Use the service registry and count the undated entry because it appears beside this month's entries.

### Post test in Filipino

Itinanong ng nurse kung sinong mga nakatatandang residente ang nabigyan ng pagtuturo tungkol sa masustansiyang pagkain ngayong buwan. Nasa master list ang mga nakatatandang residente. Nakatala sa service registry ang mga pagtuturo, ngunit hindi pa tiyak ang petsa ng isang entry. Ano ang dapat gawin ng BHW?

A. Gamitin ang service registry at beripikahin ang entry na walang tiyak na petsa bago ito isama sa bilang para sa buwang ito.

B. Gamitin ang master list dahil nilayong mabigyan ng pagtuturo ang lahat ng kabilang sa grupong iyon.

C. Gamitin ang service registry at isama ang entry na walang petsa dahil katabi ito ng mga entry ngayong buwan.

### Key and feedback

Pre B; post A. The registry addresses receipt of a service. A household profile or group list does not establish service delivery. Position beside dated entries does not verify an undated entry. The choices distinguish wrong record selection from an unsupported inference within the right record.

Filipino: Ang registry ang tumutugon sa tanong kung sino ang nabigyan ng serbisyo. Hindi patunay ng naibigay na serbisyo ang household profile o master list. Hindi rin pinatutunayan ng katabing entry ang petsa ng isang entry na hindi pa tiyak. Beripikahin ang kulang bago ito isama sa bilang para sa takdang panahon.

Review links: [records](../../content/training/day1-basic-competencies/modules/01-tungkulin-ng-bhw/lessons/bhw-records/read.en.md), [recording uncertain information](../../content/training/day1-basic-competencies/modules/06-komunikasyon/lessons/communication-record/read.en.md). A correct choice does not demonstrate the ability to complete or securely hand over a real record; use fictional record practice for that evidence.

## Pair 2 Respectful teamwork without private disclosure

Blueprint T15; primary lesson 1.5.4. Evidence: identify the work impact, preserve personal privacy and obtain the required permission and acceptance for a handoff.

### Pre test in English

A colleague says she cannot finish preparing the invitations by the agreed time. She does not want to discuss her personal reason. There is no immediate danger. Under the stated local arrangement, the supervisor must confirm a change of assignment. What is the best response?

A. Ask her to explain the private reason to the team so everyone can decide whether a change is justified.

B. Divide the unfinished invitations among the team now and tell the supervisor after the task is complete.

C. Ask what is done and what remains, seek confirmation for the change, and agree who accepts the task and reports completion.

### Pre test in Filipino

Sinabi ng kasama na hindi niya matatapos ang paghahanda ng mga imbitasyon sa napagkasunduang oras. Ayaw niyang pag-usapan ang personal na dahilan. Walang agarang panganib. Sa lokal na kaayusang ibinigay sa sitwasyong ito, kailangang kumpirmahin ng supervisor ang pagbabago ng atas. Ano ang pinakamainam na tugon?

A. Hilinging ipaliwanag niya sa grupo ang personal na dahilan upang mapagpasiyahan ng lahat kung makatwiran ang pagbabago.

B. Hatiin agad sa grupo ang natitirang mga imbitasyon at ipaalam sa supervisor pagkatapos matapos ang gawain.

C. Itanong ang tapos at natitira, hingin ang kumpirmasyon sa pagbabago, at magkasundo kung sino ang tatanggap ng gawain at mag-uulat ng pagkumpleto.

### Post test in English

A team message proposes that you take over a colleague's unfinished teaching-kit checks. The colleague's personal reason is private. There is no immediate danger. The supervisor has not yet confirmed the reassignment, as required by the stated local arrangement. Which reply best supports the handoff?

A. “Please clarify what remains and the deadline. Let us confirm the change with the supervisor, then agree who accepts and reports completion.”

B. “Please share the personal reason in this group so we can decide whether the change is justified before asking the supervisor.”

C. “I will take the unchecked kits now and report completion. We can tell the supervisor about the change when the work is finished.”

### Post test in Filipino

Iminungkahi sa mensahe ng grupo na ikaw ang tumapos sa pagsuri sa mga teaching kit ng isang kasama. Pribado ang personal niyang dahilan. Walang agarang panganib. Hindi pa nakukumpirma ng supervisor ang pagbabago ng atas, na kailangan sa lokal na kaayusang ibinigay. Aling sagot ang pinakamainam para sa handoff?

A. “Pakilinaw ang natitira at deadline. Kumpirmahin natin ang pagbabago sa supervisor, saka magkasundo kung sino ang tatanggap at mag-uulat ng pagkumpleto.”

B. “Pakibahagi sa grupong ito ang personal na dahilan upang mapagpasiyahan natin kung makatwiran ang pagbabago bago lumapit sa supervisor.”

C. “Ako na ang kukuha ng mga kit na hindi pa nasusuri at mag-uulat ng pagkumpleto. Ipaalam na lang natin sa supervisor ang pagbabago kapag tapos na ang gawain.”

### Key and feedback

Pre C; post A. The task impact can be discussed without requiring private reasons. Reassignment needs the permission specified in this case; a message after completion would not meet that condition. Acceptance and a completion update make the handoff clear. The scenario supplies its local rule and does not assert a universal process for every barangay.

Filipino: Maaaring pag-usapan ang epekto sa gawain nang hindi hinihingi ang personal na dahilan. Kailangan ang pahintulot na nakasaad sa sitwasyong ito; hindi sapat ang pagpapaalam pagkatapos matapos ang gawain. Nililinaw ng pagtanggap sa atas at pag-uulat ng pagkumpleto ang handoff.

Review link: [teamwork](../../content/training/day1-basic-competencies/modules/05-bhw-at-barangay/lessons/bhw-teamwork/read.en.md). Follow with an actual role-play and observation of respectful language, acceptance and confirmation. The post-test changes the perspective to the person receiving the proposed handoff; check whether that shift changes difficulty in the pilot.

## Pair 3 Interpreting tied priorities

Blueprint T23; primary lesson 1.7.3. Evidence: distinguish a planning score from evidence of feasibility and from an already-directed urgent referral. The question introduces no clinical triage rule.

### Pre test in English

Two community plans each total 17 across the four planning criteria. For the first plan, transport is still unconfirmed. For the second, staff time is confirmed. Separately, a health professional has already directed an urgent referral for a resident. What should the BHW and team do?

A. Proceed with the directed referral and choose the first plan because it appears first among equal totals.

B. Settle which plan comes first, then proceed with the directed referral so the planning order stays clear.

C. Proceed with the directed referral; review evidence and unconfirmed resources with partners before agreeing the planning priority.

### Pre test in Filipino

Parehong 17 ang kabuuan ng dalawang community plan sa apat na pamantayan sa pagpaplano. Hindi pa kumpirmado ang transportasyon para sa unang plano. Kumpirmado ang oras ng staff para sa ikalawa. Sa hiwalay na pangyayari, nagbigay na ang health professional ng tagubilin para sa agarang referral ng isang residente. Ano ang dapat gawin ng BHW at grupo?

A. Ituloy ang itinagubiling referral at piliin ang unang plano dahil ito ang unang nakalista sa magkapantay na kabuuan.

B. Magpasya muna kung aling plano ang mauuna, saka ituloy ang itinagubiling referral upang malinaw ang pagkakasunod ng plano.

C. Ituloy ang itinagubiling referral; suriin kasama ang mga katuwang ang ebidensya at hindi pa kumpirmadong rekurso bago magkasundo sa prayoridad ng plano.

### Post test in English

Two community plans each total 16 across the four planning criteria. For the first plan, supplies are still unconfirmed. For the second, suitable materials are confirmed. Separately, a health professional has already directed an urgent referral for a resident. What should the BHW and team do?

A. Settle which plan comes first, then proceed with the directed referral so the planning order stays clear.

B. Proceed with the directed referral; review evidence and unconfirmed resources with partners before agreeing the planning priority.

C. Proceed with the directed referral and choose the first plan because it appears first among equal totals.

### Post test in Filipino

Parehong 16 ang kabuuan ng dalawang community plan sa apat na pamantayan sa pagpaplano. Hindi pa kumpirmado ang supplies para sa unang plano. Kumpirmado ang angkop na materyales para sa ikalawa. Sa hiwalay na pangyayari, nagbigay na ang health professional ng tagubilin para sa agarang referral ng isang residente. Ano ang dapat gawin ng BHW at grupo?

A. Magpasya muna kung aling plano ang mauuna, saka ituloy ang itinagubiling referral upang malinaw ang pagkakasunod ng plano.

B. Ituloy ang itinagubiling referral; suriin kasama ang mga katuwang ang ebidensya at hindi pa kumpirmadong rekurso bago magkasundo sa prayoridad ng plano.

C. Ituloy ang itinagubiling referral at piliin ang unang plano dahil ito ang unang nakalista sa magkapantay na kabuuan.

### Key and feedback

Pre C; post B. A tie does not settle priority. Verify evidence and resources, consider the criteria and seek agreement; a confirmed resource is relevant but does not automatically settle every criterion. The already-directed urgent referral proceeds without waiting for planning. One alternative tests list-order reasoning; the other tests an inappropriate delay.

Filipino: Hindi pa napagpapasyahan ang prayoridad dahil lamang magkapantay ang kabuuan. Beripikahin ang ebidensya at rekurso, balikan ang mga pamantayan at humingi ng pagsang-ayon. Mahalaga ang kumpirmadong rekurso, ngunit hindi nito awtomatikong sinasagot ang lahat ng pamantayan. Ituloy ang itinagubiling agarang referral nang hindi hinihintay ang pagpaplano.

Review link: [prioritization](../../content/training/day1-basic-competencies/modules/07-problema/lessons/problem-prioritize/read.en.md). This item intentionally supplies totals. Calculation, explanation of all four criteria and an original tie discussion remain practice/observation evidence. Review whether the two decisions in this item create excessive reading load; do not infer separate proficiency scores from the single response.

## Pair 4 Interpreting resource use and service quality

Blueprint T30; primary lesson 1.9.3. Evidence: use a comparable denominator and check service quality before claiming improvement. Both forms provide the per-session calculation so the main construct is interpretation rather than arithmetic.

### Pre test in English

The teaching sessions were comparable in type and group size. Before a printing change, 10 sessions used 100 pages: 10 pages per session. After the change, 20 sessions used 160 pages: 8 pages per session. Sessions starting late because materials were not ready increased from none to three. What is the best conclusion?

A. The change increased paper waste because the total number of pages rose.

B. Pages per session fell, but the new delays need investigation before calling the change an overall improvement.

C. Pages per session fell, so the change improved the service and reduced its total cost.

### Pre test in Filipino

Maihahambing ang uri ng mga session at laki ng mga grupo. Bago ang pagbabago sa pagpi-print, gumamit ng 100 pahina ang 10 session: 10 pahina bawat session. Pagkatapos, gumamit ng 160 pahina ang 20 session: 8 pahina bawat session. Dumami mula wala tungong tatlo ang mga session na nahuli dahil hindi handa ang materyales. Ano ang pinakamainam na konklusyon?

A. Nadagdagan ng pagbabago ang nasasayang na papel dahil tumaas ang kabuuang bilang ng pahina.

B. Bumaba ang pahina bawat session, ngunit kailangang siyasatin ang mga bagong pagkaantala bago sabihing bumuti ang kabuuang proseso.

C. Bumaba ang pahina bawat session, kaya bumuti ang serbisyo at bumaba ang kabuuang gastos nito.

### Post test in English

The teaching sessions were comparable in type and group size. Before a printing change, 5 sessions used 60 pages: 12 pages per session. After the change, 10 sessions used 100 pages: 10 pages per session. Sessions starting late because materials were not ready increased from none to two. What is the best conclusion?

A. Pages per session fell, so the change improved the service and reduced its total cost.

B. The change increased paper waste because the total number of pages rose.

C. Pages per session fell, but the new delays need investigation before calling the change an overall improvement.

### Post test in Filipino

Maihahambing ang uri ng mga session at laki ng mga grupo. Bago ang pagbabago sa pagpi-print, gumamit ng 60 pahina ang 5 session: 12 pahina bawat session. Pagkatapos, gumamit ng 100 pahina ang 10 session: 10 pahina bawat session. Dumami mula wala tungong dalawa ang mga session na nahuli dahil hindi handa ang materyales. Ano ang pinakamainam na konklusyon?

A. Bumaba ang pahina bawat session, kaya bumuti ang serbisyo at bumaba ang kabuuang gastos nito.

B. Nadagdagan ng pagbabago ang nasasayang na papel dahil tumaas ang kabuuang bilang ng pahina.

C. Bumaba ang pahina bawat session, ngunit kailangang siyasatin ang mga bagong pagkaantala bago sabihing bumuti ang kabuuang proseso.

### Key and feedback

Pre B; post C. The per-session use fell in both cases while session count doubled. That supports a narrow statement about paper use, not a claim that total cost fell or the whole service improved. The new delays need investigation; these figures alone do not establish their cause. No cost figures are supplied.

Filipino: Sa dalawang sitwasyon, bumaba ang gamit bawat session habang dumoble ang bilang ng session. Sinusuportahan nito ang tiyak na pahayag tungkol sa paggamit ng papel, hindi ang konklusyong bumaba ang kabuuang gastos o bumuti ang buong serbisyo. Kailangang siyasatin ang mga bagong pagkaantala; hindi pa pinatutunayan ng mga bilang ang sanhi nito. Walang ibinigay na halaga ng gastos.

Review link: [resource monitoring](../../content/training/day1-basic-competencies/modules/09-sustainable-practices/lessons/resources-monitor/read.en.md). Separately ask learners to create a trial card with owner, baseline, quality measure, review date and handover. Recognizing the correct conclusion does not demonstrate that planning skill.

## Review before authoring the full forms

For each pair, confirm the intended evidence, one defensible key, plausible alternatives and language equivalence. Check the source lesson's approval and publication version. The controlled parallels here make the intended match visible to reviewers; use the pilot to determine how much scenario variation is needed to reduce recognition without changing difficulty. Never describe a change between these unvalidated forms as a validated measure of learning gain.
