# Research and gap review

Reviewed 11 October 2026. This is a desk review of primary documentation and the repository, not a usability study of those products or proof that this design improves learning. The [Sol handoff](SOL-HANDOFF.md) turns the findings into build requirements.

## What comparable platforms and learning guidance support

| Source | Relevant practice or finding | Decision for Chapter 1 |
|---|---|---|
| [Moodle: Quiz settings](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/Quiz_settings) | Pagination, navigation, feedback timing and answer-review visibility are separate controls. Deferred feedback and interactive practice serve different purposes. | One question at a time, free backward navigation and a review list. Defer diagnostic feedback until submission; give immediate practice feedback. Passing is not a condition for post-test review. |
| [Canvas: question feedback in New Quizzes](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/661066-unknown) | Supports correct-answer, incorrect-answer, general and individual-choice feedback, including links. | Every item needs an explanation and useful next step, not just a red/green result. Include feedback for correct guesses and explicit unknown responses. |
| [Open edX: problem settings](https://docs.openedx.org/en/latest/educators/references/course_development/problem_settings.html) | Answer visibility, attempts and explanations are configurable independently; different answer-release policies have different consequences. | Make our release rule explicit and enforce it on the server. A diagnostic attempt stays immutable after submission; later practice is labeled separately. |
| [Moodle: Feedback settings](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/Feedback_settings) | Identified feedback and anonymous feedback are distinct settings, as are repeat submissions and notifications. | Clearly label assessor-shared reflections as identifiable. Use explicit sharing, private drafts and an in-app reply queue; do not imply anonymity or promise a response time. |
| [CDC: Quality Training Standards](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/qts/index.html) | Emphasizes measurable objectives, content expertise, engagement, accessible delivery, learner evaluation, aligned assessments and follow-up support. | Tie questions to revised objectives; check bilingual content and policy/clinical accuracy; collect learner feedback; make review and support available after completion. This is guidance, not a claim of CDC approval. |
| [Carnegie Mellon: assessment alignment](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/basics/alignment.html) | A recall task cannot adequately assess an application objective. Assessment and instruction must address the intended kind of learning. | Use fresh decisions and explanations for chapter understanding. Keep practical observation separate. Map each scored item to a primary target; do not claim one item proves all associated lessons. |
| [IES: Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) | The guide supports retrieval quizzes, explanatory questions and distributed study, with different evidence ratings for different recommendations. | Use short retrieval/application practice with correction. Revisiting an earlier concept is useful design intent; checkpoints completed in one sitting do not establish spaced learning or long-term retention. The 2007 guide does not validate this BHW program's exact counts. |
| [W3C WAI: Multi-page Forms](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/) | Break long forms into logical steps, explain progress, preserve entered data on return, and avoid unnecessary time limits. | Show question and section progress, save/resume, keyboard-accessible navigation and an unanswered-items review. One-question screens still need an overview so users do not become lost. |
| [Supabase: Row Level Security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) | Grants, table policies and view behavior all affect exposure; views need explicit scrutiny. | Keep keys in a private schema, enforce ownership/assignment in RPCs, and test direct API access. Removing a key from a React prop alone does not protect it. |

These sources support the design principles. **Thirty questions, three sections, twelve two-question checkpoints, nine introductions and optional reflections are local design decisions.** Thirty is a pilot budget, not an evidence-based optimum. Nine introductions is the user's explicit curriculum requirement.

## Gaps found and how the refined plan closes them

P0 means resolve before enabling the redesigned learner flow; P1 means required for the complete feature and its evaluation. Neither means production changes are authorized by this planning task.

| P0 | The [assessor exam migration](../../supabase/migrations/20260928093110_assessor_candidate_exams.sql) consumes the same bank and requires at least 38 Chapter 1 questions. | P0 | “Responsible assessor” is not a defined reflection assignment. | P0 | Current [course detail](../../src/components/elearning/course-detail.tsx) and pending-assessment logic are insufficient for a certified learner's persistent review. | Add a history/review route independent of pending status, with ownership checks and a legacy adapter. |
| P0 | The [training route](../../src/app/training/[programId]/[[...path]]/page.tsx) interprets path segments as module/lesson IDs. | P1 | [Cohort summaries](../../src/lib/elearning/session-summary.ts) pair by learner without form metadata; [facilitator item analysis](../../src/lib/elearning/load-facilitator-guide.ts) uses today's bank. | Partition by curriculum/form version and use frozen item identities. Suppress comparable-change claims until evidence supports comparability. Show denominators and paired sample counts. |
| P1 | “Fresh retry” had no authoring budget. | Author 24 first-pass checkpoint questions plus 24 alternate questions, in addition to the 60 pre/post questions. Repeats thereafter are labeled exposed-item practice. No generated clinical answers. |
| P1 | Shorter tests can hide increased total workload. | Keep 130 first-pass response opportunities visible: 60 tests + 46 existing checks + 24 optional checkpoint questions. Compare with the current 122; measure time and fatigue, including orientation. |

## Measurement limits and pilot decisions

The post-test measures sampled understanding **after instruction and the requested recap**. It does not independently establish practical competence, unaided retention, or a causal training effect. Three or four questions per major lesson support specific review suggestions, not a reliable mastery classification.

Run a usability pilot before making claims about manageable length. Include English/Filipino use, low reading confidence, narrow phones, interrupted connections and assistive technology. Observe whether people can find the right/wrong explanation after certification, resume a saved response, skip optional practice and understand who sees a reflection. Prompted think-aloud attempts are usability evidence, not efficacy scores.

Before activation, the content owner reviews the full paired forms and rationales against the actually published curriculum. Then gather item response patterns, explicit unknowns, completion/drop-off, response time and feedback usefulness. Report sample sizes and language/form versions. No numerical reliability or mastery threshold is justified yet; do not invent one to complete a dashboard.

The first release labels the two form results separately and withholds a headline learning-gain calculation. A subsequent comparability decision must record its evidence and reviewer; equal item counts or matching blueprint slots alone are insufficient. The code can support that future decision without assuming it now.

The remaining human work is explicit: policy/clinical review where relevant, bilingual review, learner pilot and a decision to activate. Sol can build the complete feature and validation fixtures now; those reviews are release evidence, not missing architectural decisions.
