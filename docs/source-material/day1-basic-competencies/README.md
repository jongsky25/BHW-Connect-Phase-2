# Day 1 — Basic Competencies: source material

Transcriptions of the three DOH documents the Day 1 training course is built from, shared with the
project from Google Drive (owner: rayjustinventura@gmail.com). Extracted **19 September 2026**.

**Why they are here.** `docs/training-modules-plan.md` names these as the source material for the
Day 1 course but never checked them in, so every session had to re-fetch them from someone else's
Drive — and two of them are too large to hold in context. These are durable, diffable, greppable
copies. The Drive originals remain the source of truth; if the two disagree, Drive wins.

**How they relate to each other.** The presentation is what the trainer actually delivers. The
Facilitator's Manual says how to deliver it and what the TESDA requirements are. The Reference
Manual is the BHW's own book and holds the substance.

## The competency table — read this before planning any module

Facilitator's Manual p.12 (PDF page 19) carries the authoritative TESDA Barangay Health Services
NC II mapping. It is an image in the original and is transcribed in
[facilitator-guide.md](./facilitator-guide.md#page-19):

| Basic competency | Topic | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Participate in workplace communication | Ang mga Tungkulin ng mga BHW | 6 |
| Contribute to workplace innovation | Ang UHC Law of 2019 | 3 |
| Practice entrepreneurial skills in the workplace | Mga polisiya sa barangay health station | 4 |
| Develop life and career decisions | The BHW Benefits and Incentives Act of 1995 | 3 |
| Work in a team environment | Ang BHW at ang Kanilang Barangay; Team Work; Self-management skills | 3 |
| Present relevant information | Epektibong komunikasyon | 8 |
| Solve or address general workplace problems | Pagkilala sa mga problema at pagpaplano ng mga solusyon | 3 |
| Practice occupational safety and health policies and procedures | Occupational safety and health in the barangay | 4 |
| Exercise efficient and effective sustainable practices in the workplaces | Occupational safety and health in the barangay | 3 |
| **Total** | | **37 (4.5–5 full days)** |

**Nine competencies, not eight.** `docs/training-modules-plan.md` §C maps the chapter to 8 modules
against 8 competencies; this table has nine. The differences are real and each one changes the
module boundaries — they are worked through in that plan, not here.

**Training hours are the weighting signal.** Komunikasyon is 8 hours and Tungkulin is 6; several
others are 3. A module map that gives all nine equal weight contradicts the source.

## What the deck alone does not give you

Content the two manuals add that the 78 slides omit, and that a "full breadth" course needs:

- **Per-topic TESDA learning outcomes and training hours** (Facilitator's Manual, each topic page).
- **The local health board's eight accreditation responsibilities** under the RA 7883 IRR
  (Facilitator's Manual p.18 / PDF 25) — the deck says only "register with the local health board".
- **The BHW's role in the Local Investment Plan for Health (LIPH)** under UHC (Reference Manual
  PDF 12) — absent from the deck.
- **The initial medical services a BHW actually performs** — interview, vital signs, recording,
  household profile, first aid (Reference Manual PDF 12).
- **The worked Five Why's example** — the child who died of diarrhoea, traced to BHW training gaps
  (Reference Manual PDF 20–21). The deck shows only the resulting table.
- **The full occupational hazards table** (Reference Manual PDF 22).
- **Suggested review questions** per topic (Facilitator's Manual pp. 17, 24, 29, 32, 36).
- **Team-building activity instructions** — sinking vessel and marshmallow tower, with actual rules
  (Facilitator's Manual PDF 27). The deck names them only.
- **Suggested three-day and five-day training designs** (Facilitator's Manual PDF 16–17).

## Known gaps and cautions

- **Three pages were image-only** and are transcribed by visual reading, flagged inline: the two
  training designs (PDF 16–17) and the competency table (PDF 19). Everything else is extracted text.
- **The Facilitator's Manual contradicts itself on Tungkulin's hours** — the competency table says
  6, the narrative on its p.13 (PDF 20) says "at least 3 hours". Don't cite either without noting
  the other.
- **"Kwento ni Rosario" is not in any of these files.** It is an external Zuellig Family Foundation
  workbook, cited at PDF 28 of the Facilitator's Manual:
  https://zuelligfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/HLMP-Module-1-Workbook.compressed.pdf —
  it is referenced by both the Komunikasyon and Problem-solving topics.
- **Two "Health Facts" slides in the deck are unrelated filler** (slides 45 and 73 — high heels and
  shopping, hydration and brain function), sourced from posthood.com and healthline.com. They are
  not DOH content and are not part of any competency.
- **Images and diagrams are not reproduced.** Photographs, the HEPO infographic, the open-vs-closed
  questions graphic, the Five Why's diagram and the sharps/PPE photos exist only in the originals.
- **The deck and the manuals disagree on where self-management belongs.** The competency table puts
  it under *Work in a team environment*; the Facilitator's Manual p.16 (PDF 23) lists it under
  *Develop life and career decisions*. Both are transcribed as printed.

## Re-extracting these files

The Drive read tool returns the text of all three. For the two large PDFs (26 MB and 41 MB) it is
faster to download and parse locally:

```python
import pymupdf
doc = pymupdf.open("file.pdf")
for i, page in enumerate(doc):
    print(page.get_text())              # 0 chars => image-only page
    page.get_pixmap(dpi=150).save(f"p{i+1:02d}.png")   # then read it visually
```

Download with `curl -sSL -o out.pdf "https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=<fileId>"`.

## Provenance and handling

The Reference Manual and Facilitator's Manual are DOH publications (Bureau of Local Health Systems
Development, with WHO Philippines) and name their contributors and pilot-test participants. The
presentation is an unpublished working deck. Treat all three as internal project reference material:
do not republish, and do not lift text verbatim into BHW-facing content — the whole point of
`docs/training-content-style-guide.md` is that this material gets rewritten, not copied.
