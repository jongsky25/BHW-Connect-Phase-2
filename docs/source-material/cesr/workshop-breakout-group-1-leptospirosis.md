### 2. Best practices

| Best Practice | Impact |
| :-- | :-- |
| 1. DM 2023-03-09 Guidelines on the Prevention, Control Management, and Surveillance of Leptospirosis | Structured prevention guidelines on Leptospirosis. |
| 2. Augmentation of Doxycycline from HEMB → HEMS (Provision to LGUs and Health Offices BARMM process) | Readily available resources. Timely provision of commodities. Hence, no stock out. |
| 3. IEC → SBC Health Promotion Leptospirosis | Increase public awareness |

### 3. Gaps and challenges

| Gap / Challenge | Impact |
| :-- | :-- |
| 1. Climate Change | Year round cases of Leptospirosis |
| 2. Geographical Factor — Islands: underserved and unserved areas | Delayed delivery and replenishment of medicines |
| 3. Limited fund for Leptospirosis | Limited resources and technical assistance for Leptospirosis |
| 4. Adopted comment from the group: Poor sanitation and poor waste management (environmental concerns), no pet/rodent control measures, and **behavior** as a challenging component regardless how much of promotion materials are available for the public. | Actual change in behavior is not visible because there's no governing policy or strict guidelines or repercussions to those who deliberately perform risky behaviors. |

## Documentation of Session 1 Output

### Comments/Suggestions from Group 3

- Clarification on the augmentation as best practice yet there is the existence of limited fund for
  leptospirosis
- Remove "climate change" under gaps and challenges because this is not actionable
- Under delayed delivery and replenishment of medicines add "access or provision of social behavior
  change materials is an additional challenge"
- Language barrier in IEC → SBC materials on Leptospirosis (assumed challenge if materials were not
  localized)

### Comments/Suggestions from Group 4

- Climate change → adapt to the concerning condition (as way forward)
- Add in the best practice → interagency and inter-regional coordination mechanisms (one health
  approach)
- Add in the gaps and challenges → Poor sanitation and poor waste management (environmental
  concerns), no pet/rodent control measures, and behavior as a challenging component regardless how
  much of promotion materials are available for the public.

### Comments/Suggestions from Group 2

- Add "interconnectedness of islands (creation of bridge)" under the geographical factor.

as empty templates — the root-cause ("5 whys"), multisectoral-actions and priority-setting tables
contain no rows of content, and blank rows are reproduced above rather than invented. Two cells in
