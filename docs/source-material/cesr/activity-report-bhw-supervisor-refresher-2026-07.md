## What the document is

A signed activity report for a **Refresher Training for BHW Supervisors** on Community Event-Based
Surveillance and Response, run 19–25 July 2026 under EpiC / Meeting Targets and Maintaining Epidemic
Control.

This training is the direct follow-through on the recommendation that recurs across the May–June
2026 M&SS findings: *"Conduct refresher training on CESR signal definitions, detection, recording,
and reporting procedures"* and *"Conduct targeted training and refresher activities for midwives,
BHWs, and key informants"* — the M&SS visits found that **BHWs demonstrated gaps in recalling CESR
signal definitions**, and this is the remediation.
