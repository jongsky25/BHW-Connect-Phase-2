**This is the paper instrument the M&SS visits are conducted with.** It is the closest thing in the
folder to a form specification: every question below is a field, with its answer type already
decided. If BHW Connect ever digitizes supervisory supervision, this is the form to digitize, and
Sections 3 and 4 are the indicator definitions that a dashboard would have to compute.

**Instructions (verbatim).** This tool is designed for the use of upper-level ESUs (P/RESUs)
performing the role of supervisors to conduct supportive supervision visits to local ESUs
(C/MESUs). It is also for the use of C/MESUs to conduct supportive supervision visits to Barangay
Health Stations (BHS) or Barangay Health Centers (BHC) for the implementation of CESR in the
community. At the local and facility level, it can also be used for **self-assessment** purposes, to
improve performance.

- Go through each section with the appropriate staff, check available resources, and review records
  (logbooks, reports, ESR-IS, etc.).
- Document findings clearly. Include any relevant observations or additional information that could
  help improve ESR/CESR implementation. If no applicable answer, provide comments and details.
- Review findings with the supervisee and discuss both good practices and gaps, and note them in
  Section 5.
- Agree on actions at the end of the visit and record them in Section 5, with signatures from both
  supervisor and supervisee.
