## Extraction note

This is a text extraction of a Power BI PDF export, so chart geometry is lost: bar and donut values
arrive as a stream of numbers separated from their category labels. Where a value could not be
matched to a specific category with confidence, the values are listed as rendered and the category
list is given alongside, rather than guessed at — this affects the Phase I "Trainers Trained by
Organizational Unit" chart (only Gasan = 5, 12.2% is unambiguous), the policy-document,
data-management-tool and ICT-tool charts, the "Events by Source of Information" and "Events by
Disease Classification" donuts, and the per-area percentages on the Phase III signal-quality pages.
One percentage is truncated in the source as "1 (14…)" — most likely 14.29%.

Detail tables are paginated in the dashboard and each shows only the first ~9–12 rows; the trainer
name list likewise shows 22 of 42 names. The Phase II (Barangay Rollout Trainings) page is
effectively empty in this export — one visual is unpopulated and only the year 2025 and the values
9 and 10 are present. The National Capital Region slicer appears on every Phase III page but no NCR
rows are rendered. Repeated "Power BI Desktop" header text has been dropped.
