## Cause and evidence cards

CLASSROOM FICTION · Three households, no actual records.

A — Resident: “I had no one to look after my child at that time.” Later: “I received one time; I do not know whether there were others.”
B — Resident: “I did not have enough fare that day.” Costs, route and cause are not yet checked.
C — No reply yet. Do not invent a reason.
Staff correction — Authorized fictional staff show a blank practice schedule with TWO offered times. This confirms the schedule detail only; it does not confirm which message reached A or why.

Cut-out status tokens: REPORTED (person said it) / VERIFIED (specific fact checked against named evidence) / TO CHECK (unsupported or unknown). Apply to each arrow, not the whole household.

Remove “only one time offered.” Retain “one received, resident report.” Why only one was received stays unknown. The fare branch stays open.

## Rotating role cards

BHW / Carole: Draw two possible branches. Ask a non-blaming evidence question for each; name a person or authorized record. Attribute statements and keep C unknown. Revise after the correction card; do not promise a service.

Resident / staff: Read A or B exactly; do not invent motivation or agreement. When asked about offered times, reveal the staff correction card. As A say: “That is what I meant about the message.” This confirms your statement only, not everybody’s cause.

Observer: Identify one unsupported arrow. Say which source/status/check is missing, then watch the learner correct and retry. Record what you saw, not a guess about ability.

Three rounds of 6 minutes: 2 first attempt + 1 feedback + 2 correction/retry + 1 record/rotate. All three practise BHW. Peer feedback is not facilitator assessment.
