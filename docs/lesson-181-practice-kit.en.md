## Role cards and observer checklist

BHW: name five groups and examples. Give a report on one scene: hazard, harm, safe action, contact/verification and follow-up. Do not invent a correction.

CONTACT / PARTNER: use only the printed facts. If contact or local procedure is absent, answer “unknown—confirm with authorized staff.” No promise that the floor is fixed.

OBSERVER: check five groups/examples and four fields. Record exact words and prompting. Ask a specific question about a missing field, then listen to the revised report. Mark trainer / peer / self / not observed. Peer feedback remains practice.

Each six-minute round: 2 attempt + 1 partner + 1 feedback + 1 retry + 1 record/swap. Three rounds, one BHW turn per learner.
Trainer: one triad/round throughout attempt and retry. Observe at most three named learners: three initial attempts + three retries = six performances. Arrange later observation for the rest.

## One-page reporting aid

REPORT A HAZARD — BEFORE WORK

1. Observe from a safe position. Do not touch sharps or lift a heavy obstruction alone.
2. State the location and hazard.
3. Explain who could be harmed and how. Possible harm is not a diagnosis.
4. Name a safe immediate action: keep people away safely and request correction by responsible staff.
5. Confirm contact, contact method and local alternative. If unknown, say how you will verify; never invent a number.
6. Follow up on the condition. A report does not prove correction.

Example: “Wet entrance; visitors could slip. Keep people away from a safe dry position and request correction. I need to confirm the designated staff/contact and recheck the floor.”

Pause related work beyond training or missing essential protection; seek an authorized decision. Real exposure/injury: report and seek immediate medical evaluation. Paperwork must not delay care. Local trainer must verify the actual route.

Rehearse → specific feedback → retry. No real patient data. No certification or observed pass from a quiz click.
