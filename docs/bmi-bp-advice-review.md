# BMI and blood-pressure advice: clinical review sheet

Draft wording for the "What should I tell them / what do I do next" guidance a BHW sees after the Chat Guide computes a BMI or blood-pressure category. **Status: approved by the clinical reviewer on 1 Oct 2026**, as written below; the entries are `tier: cited` in `content/kb/hhp-ncd/entries/module-5.json`. They were drafted as `pending` first, so no BHW saw them before approval. Any change to the wording after this point is an ordinary content edit and should come back to the reviewer.

For each entry please mark **Approve / Change (write the change) / Remove**, in both English and Filipino. Please look especially at:

- the emergency questions in `adv-bp-raised` and `adv-bp-grade-3` (chest pain, trouble breathing, severe headache, one-sided weakness, confusion, fainting);
- the lifestyle lines ("one small step the person chooses", asking about salty food and movement) in `adv-bp-high-normal`, `adv-bmi-overweight` and `adv-bmi-obese`: is this within the BHW scope?;
- the underweight advice (`adv-bmi-underweight`): when should a BHW refer?;
- that nothing here fixes a referral time: grade 3 timing and the numeric low-BP cut-off still wait on the PhilPEN protocol (`m3-bp-grade-3`, `m3-bp-low-numbers`).

Every entry also tells the BHW to give no medicine and to record the result and date.

## `adv-bp-normal`

**Question (EN):** The blood pressure is optimal or normal. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Give the number and say it is a good reading today: 'Ang presyon ninyo ngayon ay 118/76, maayos po.' Praise what they are already doing well, and be honest that normal today does not mean normal for good, which is why re-checking is part of the programme. Avoid 'wala kayong sakit', which people hear as permission to stop being checked. Record the reading, and re-check at the interval your PhilPEN protocol sets. If they have other risk factors, record those too, because the facility may still want to see them. Do not give or advise any medicine, and write the reading and date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Optimal o normal ang presyon. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Sabihin ang numero at na maayos ang resulta ngayon: 'Ang presyon ninyo ngayon ay 118/76, maayos po.' Purihin ang mga ginagawa na nilang mabuti, at maging tapat na ang normal ngayon ay hindi nangangahulugang normal na habambuhay, kaya may muling pagsusuri sa programa. Iwasan ang 'wala kayong sakit', na naririnig ng tao bilang pahintulot na huminto sa pagpapasuri. Itala ang resulta, at suriin ulit ayon sa agwat na itinakda ng inyong PhilPEN protocol. Kung may iba pa silang risk factor, itala rin ang mga iyon, dahil maaaring gustuhin pa rin ng pasilidad na makita sila. Huwag magbigay o magpayo ng anumang gamot, at isulat ang resulta at petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO HEARTS technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020)

## `adv-bp-high-normal`

**Question (EN):** The blood pressure is high-normal. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Say it is a little above the ideal and worth keeping an eye on, not a disease: 'Medyo mataas na po ito sa mainam, kaya babantayan natin.' Ask about the usual things in plain questions (salty food and seasonings, how many days they move enough to breathe harder) and agree on one small step the person chooses themselves, stated concretely. Record the reading, agree a re-check date and write it on both copies, and re-check at the interval your PhilPEN protocol sets. Do not give or advise any medicine, and write the reading and date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** High-normal ang presyon. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Sabihin na medyo mas mataas ito sa mainam at dapat bantayan, hindi ito sakit: 'Medyo mataas na po ito sa mainam, kaya babantayan natin.' Magtanong ng karaniwang bagay sa simpleng tanong (maaalat na pagkain at pampalasa, ilang araw sila kumikilos nang sapat para humingal) at magkasundo sa isang maliit na hakbang na sila mismo ang pipili, malinaw ang pagkakasabi. Itala ang resulta, magkasundo sa petsa ng susunod na pagsusuri at isulat ito sa dalawang kopya, at suriin ulit ayon sa agwat na itinakda ng inyong PhilPEN protocol. Huwag magbigay o magpayo ng anumang gamot, at isulat ang resulta at petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO HEARTS technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); 2018 ESC/ESH Guidelines for the management of arterial hypertension (European Society of Cardiology / European Society of Hypertension, 2018)

## `adv-bp-raised`

**Question (EN):** The blood pressure is in the grade 1 or grade 2 range. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** First ask whether they have chest pain, trouble breathing, a severe headache, weakness or confusion; if they do, treat it as an emergency instead. Otherwise give the number, say what it means in one plain sentence, and name the next step: 'Ang presyon ninyo ngayon ay 152/96. Mas mataas ito sa dapat, kaya kailangan itong kumpirmahin sa health center.' One reading is not a diagnosis, so do not say 'may altapresyon na kayo' and do not say it is nothing. Refer or repeat as your PhilPEN protocol directs and give the referral instructions: where to go, when, who to look for, and what to bring. Agree a follow-up date and write it down. If they say they feel fine, agree that most people with high blood pressure feel nothing, which is why it is checked. Do not give or advise any medicine, and write the reading and date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Nasa grade 1 o grade 2 ang presyon. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Una, itanong kung may sakit sa dibdib, hirap huminga, matinding sakit ng ulo, panghihina o pagkalito; kung mayroon, ituring itong emergency. Kung wala, sabihin ang numero, ipaliwanag sa isang simpleng pangungusap, at pangalanan ang susunod na hakbang: 'Ang presyon ninyo ngayon ay 152/96. Mas mataas ito sa dapat, kaya kailangan itong kumpirmahin sa health center.' Hindi diagnosis ang isang resulta, kaya huwag sabihing 'may altapresyon na kayo' at huwag ding sabihing walang kaso ito. Mag-refer o ulitin ayon sa inyong PhilPEN protocol at ibigay ang tagubilin sa referral: saan pupunta, kailan, sino ang hahanapin, at ano ang dadalhin. Magkasundo sa petsa ng follow-up at isulat ito. Kung sasabihin nilang maayos ang pakiramdam nila, sang-ayunan na karamihan ng may mataas na presyon ay walang nararamdaman, kaya nga ito sinusuri. Huwag magbigay o magpayo ng anumang gamot, at isulat ang resulta at petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO HEARTS technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); 2018 ESC/ESH Guidelines for the management of arterial hypertension (European Society of Cardiology / European Society of Hypertension, 2018); DOH Lifestyle-Related Diseases programme (PhilPEN) (Department of Health, Philippines, 2025)

## `adv-bp-grade-3`

> **Changed 2 Oct 2026:** referral timing is now "seen at the health facility today" (emergency now if any warning sign), the warning-sign list gained trouble speaking and changes in vision, and the LGU's PhilPEN protocol overrides the timing. See `docs/bp-thresholds-evidence.md`. **Needs the reviewer's read.**

**Question (EN):** The blood pressure is 180/110 or higher. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Stay calm and stay with them. Ask right away about chest pain, trouble breathing, severe headache, weakness on one side, trouble speaking, changes in vision, confusion or fainting; if there is any, follow the emergency steps and arrange transport now. If there is none, tell them plainly and kindly that the reading is very high and needs to be assessed at the health facility, and arrange for them to be seen there today (or at the time your LGU's PhilPEN protocol sets for this level). Do not let them go home alone to 'see how it goes', and do not promise that it is nothing serious. Do not give or advise any medicine, and write the reading and date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** 180/110 o mas mataas ang presyon. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Manatiling kalmado at samahan sila. Itanong agad kung may sakit sa dibdib, hirap huminga, matinding sakit ng ulo, panghihina sa isang bahagi, hirap magsalita, pagbabago ng paningin, pagkalito o pagkahimatay; kung mayroon, sundin ang mga hakbang sa emergency at ayusin ang transport ngayon din. Kung wala, sabihin nang malinaw at mahinahon na napakataas ng resulta at kailangan itong suriin sa pasilidad, at ayusin na masuri sila roon ngayong araw (o sa oras na itinakda ng PhilPEN protocol ng inyong LGU para sa antas na ito). Huwag silang hayaang umuwi nang mag-isa para 'tingnan muna', at huwag mangakong walang malubha. Huwag magbigay o magpayo ng anumang gamot, at isulat ang resulta at petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); WHO HEARTS technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); 2018 ESC/ESH Guidelines for the management of arterial hypertension (European Society of Cardiology / European Society of Hypertension, 2018); DOH Lifestyle-Related Diseases programme (PhilPEN) (Department of Health, Philippines, 2025)

## `adv-bp-low`

**Question (EN):** The blood pressure reading is low. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Ask how they feel. If they are dizzy on standing, weak, cold and clammy, confused or have fainted, have them lie down, do not let them walk off alone, and refer. If they feel well, say that a low number in someone who feels fine is usually not a problem, check the cuff size and position, measure again, and record both readings. Tell them to report dizziness or fainting. Do not give or advise any medicine, and write the reading and date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Mababa ang resulta ng presyon. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Tanungin kung ano ang pakiramdam nila. Kung nahihilo sa pagtayo, nanghihina, malamig at basa ang balat, nalilito o nahimatay, pahigain sila, huwag silang hayaang umalis nang mag-isa, at i-refer. Kung maayos ang pakiramdam, sabihing ang mababang numero sa taong maayos ang pakiramdam ay karaniwang hindi problema, suriin ang laki at pagkakalagay ng cuff, sukatin ulit, at itala ang dalawang resulta. Sabihan silang ipaalam kung mahihilo o mahihimatay. Huwag magbigay o magpayo ng anumang gamot, at isulat ang resulta at petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); HEARTS in the Americas: Blood Pressure Measurement (Pan American Health Organization / WHO, 2024)

## `adv-bmi-underweight`

**Question (EN):** The BMI is underweight. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Say gently that the weight is below the healthy range for their height, and that this does not by itself mean they are sick. Ask without judging about appetite, any recent weight loss they did not intend, ongoing illness, and whether there is enough food at home or cost is the barrier. Record the height, weight and BMI. Refer to the midwife or nurse for assessment, and give the referral instructions (where, when, who, what to bring). Do not recommend supplements, vitamins or herbal products. Write the date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Underweight ang BMI. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Sabihin nang mahinahon na mas mababa ang timbang sa mainam para sa taas nila, at hindi ito nangangahulugang may sakit sila. Magtanong nang walang panghuhusga tungkol sa gana sa pagkain, anumang pagbaba ng timbang na hindi nila sinadya, kasalukuyang karamdaman, at kung sapat ang pagkain sa bahay o gastos ang hadlang. Itala ang taas, timbang at BMI. I-refer sa midwife o nurse para masuri, at ibigay ang tagubilin sa referral (saan, kailan, sino, ano ang dadalhin). Huwag magrekomenda ng supplement, bitamina o halamang gamot. Isulat ang petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO Technical Report Series 894: Obesity — preventing and managing the global epidemic (World Health Organization, 2000); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020)

## `adv-bmi-normal`

**Question (EN):** The BMI is normal. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Say the weight is in the healthy range for their height and praise the habits that help, such as staying active and eating vegetables. Be honest that a normal BMI does not rule out high blood pressure or high blood sugar, so those checks still matter. Record the height, weight and BMI, and re-measure at your routine re-screening. Write the date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Normal ang BMI. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Sabihin na nasa mainam na saklaw ang timbang para sa taas nila at purihin ang mga ugaling nakakatulong, gaya ng pananatiling aktibo at pagkain ng gulay. Maging tapat na hindi inaalis ng normal na BMI ang posibilidad ng mataas na presyon o mataas na asukal, kaya mahalaga pa rin ang mga pagsusuring iyon. Itala ang taas, timbang at BMI, at sukatin ulit sa inyong regular na muling pagsusuri. Isulat ang petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO Technical Report Series 894: Obesity — preventing and managing the global epidemic (World Health Organization, 2000); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020)

## `adv-bmi-overweight`

**Question (EN):** The BMI is overweight. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Explain without blame that a higher weight raises the chance of high blood pressure, diabetes and heart disease, and that small changes help. Ask about food and movement in concrete questions, and agree on one small step the person chooses themselves, such as 'hindi na ako iinom ng softdrinks sa umaga'. Check blood pressure, and the other measurements your assessment form asks for. Record the height, weight and BMI, agree a follow-up date and write it down. Do not recommend slimming products, supplements, herbal remedies or crash diets, and do not comment on their body in front of others. Write the date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Overweight ang BMI. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Ipaliwanag nang walang sisihan na ang mas mataas na timbang ay nagpapataas ng tsansa ng mataas na presyon, diabetes at sakit sa puso, at nakakatulong ang maliliit na pagbabago. Magtanong tungkol sa pagkain at pagkilos sa mga tiyak na tanong, at magkasundo sa isang maliit na hakbang na sila mismo ang pipili, gaya ng 'hindi na ako iinom ng softdrinks sa umaga'. Sukatin ang presyon, at ang iba pang sukat na hinihingi ng inyong assessment form. Itala ang taas, timbang at BMI, magkasundo sa petsa ng follow-up at isulat ito. Huwag magrekomenda ng pampapayat, supplement, halamang gamot o crash diet, at huwag pag-usapan ang katawan nila sa harap ng ibang tao. Isulat ang petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO Technical Report Series 894: Obesity — preventing and managing the global epidemic (World Health Organization, 2000); WHO Expert Consultation: appropriate body-mass index for Asian populations and its implications for policy and intervention strategies (The Lancet / World Health Organization, 2004); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); WHO HEARTS technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020)

## `adv-bmi-obese`

**Question (EN):** The BMI is in the obese range. What do I tell the client and what do I do next?

**Answer (EN):** Use respectful words and a private place: say the weight is well above the healthy range for their height and that this raises their risk of diabetes, high blood pressure and heart disease, which is why you want them checked. Measure blood pressure, and the other measurements your assessment form asks for, and record the height, weight and BMI. Refer for assessment following your PhilPEN protocol and give the referral instructions (where, when, who, what to bring). Agree on one small step the person chooses and a follow-up date. Do not recommend slimming products, supplements, herbal remedies or crash diets, and keep the result confidential. Write the date on your record.

**Question (FIL):** Nasa obese range ang BMI. Ano ang sasabihin ko sa kliyente at ano ang susunod kong gagawin?

**Answer (FIL):** Gumamit ng magalang na salita at pribadong lugar: sabihin na mas mataas nang malaki ang timbang sa mainam para sa taas nila at pinapataas nito ang panganib ng diabetes, mataas na presyon at sakit sa puso, kaya nais ninyo silang masuri. Sukatin ang presyon, at ang iba pang sukat na hinihingi ng inyong assessment form, at itala ang taas, timbang at BMI. Mag-refer para masuri ayon sa inyong PhilPEN protocol at ibigay ang tagubilin sa referral (saan, kailan, sino, ano ang dadalhin). Magkasundo sa isang maliit na hakbang na sila ang pipili at sa petsa ng follow-up. Huwag magrekomenda ng pampapayat, supplement, halamang gamot o crash diet, at panatilihing kompidensyal ang resulta. Isulat ang petsa sa inyong record.

**Sources:** WHO Technical Report Series 894: Obesity — preventing and managing the global epidemic (World Health Organization, 2000); WHO Expert Consultation: appropriate body-mass index for Asian populations and its implications for policy and intervention strategies (The Lancet / World Health Organization, 2004); WHO package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020); WHO HEARTS technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care (World Health Organization, 2020)
