# PSGC org hierarchy, assessor catchments and BHW barangays

## The tree

`org_units` is the Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC):
national (Department of Health) → 18 regions → 118 provinces/HUCs →
1,651 cities/municipalities → 41,965 barangays. Each unit carries its code in
`org_units.psgc_code` (2-digit region, 5-digit province, 7-digit city/municipality,
10-digit barangay).

- **Vintage.** The codes match the BHW Connect Dashboard's `dim_geo` exactly
  (2023 series, ≥2024 release, includes NIR; Sulu still under BARMM). When BHW
  Connect records are later used to populate legitimate BHWs, barangays join on
  identical codes with no crosswalk.
- **Source.** The seed is generated by `scripts/psgc/build-org-units-migration.py`
  from a checkout of `jongsky25/BHW-Connect-Dashboard`. The script reads only the
  geography columns of the parquet, plus the StepZero patch and NHFR barangays.
  To refresh, re-run the script and ship the output as a new migration. Never
  hand-edit `20261002000000_psgc_org_units.sql`.
- **IDs.** The pilot units (Region IV-A, Laguna, Los Baños, Batong Malake, Anos)
  keep their original UUIDs. Every other unit's UUID is `md5('psgc:' || code)`, so
  every environment agrees on IDs.
- **Loading in the UI.** The tree is far too big to load whole. Screens show
  `OrgUnitPicker` (`src/components/org-unit-picker.tsx`), which loads one unit's
  children at a time and starts at the signed-in user's own unit. Never load
  `org_units` unfiltered: PostgREST truncates the result at 1,000 rows.

## Where each role sits

Enforced by the `users_enforce_role_org_level` trigger on every insert, and on
every change of role or org unit. The rule lives in
`public.org_level_allowed_for_role`, mirrored in `ROLE_LEVELS` in
`src/lib/org-units.ts`.

| Role | Allowed org unit levels |
|---|---|
| assessor | region, province or city/municipality: their **catchment**, set when the account is created |
| bhw | barangay; or city/municipality, in which case the BHW chooses their barangay on first sign-in |
| admin, designer | any level |

## What a catchment sees

An assessor sees everything inside their catchment:
- BHWs, progress, test attempts and observations (these rules already existed)
- the assessment queue: `assessments_assessor_scope_read` and
  `rpc_assessment_claim` compared org paths in the opposite direction until
  `20261002000100`
- published courses and training programs from anywhere inside the catchment,
  as well as ones that cascade down to them from above

The rule is `org_visible_to_actor()` / `assessor_catchment_includes()`.

## BHW barangay selection

A BHW placed at a city/municipality (or at a wider unit, for legacy rows) is sent
to `/select-barangay` by the middleware before anything else. The BHW picks a
barangay inside their unit, and `rpc_bhw_select_barangay` saves it. The RPC is
one-time and writes an audit event. After that, only an admin transfer can move
the BHW.
