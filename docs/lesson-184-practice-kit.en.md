## 4. Reporting roles and retry record

Three roles: DEMONSTRATOR / SIMULATED CONTACT / OBSERVER.
Contact receives the simulated report, repeats the verified pathway and identifies unknowns; a qualified provider decides actual assessment/treatment.
Observer records exact actions, calls “Pause” for unsafe practice or delay, and gives one specific correction.

Three rotations of 8 minutes each:
0:00–2:00 attempt; 2:00–4:00 feedback;
4:00–6:00 safe retry; 6:00–8:00 record and swap.
Ten triads = 30 learners. Final 11 minutes: one trainer observes up to three selected learners, 3 minutes each plus 2 minutes movement/logging. Other 27 are not observed unless another qualified trainer actually sees them. Incomplete observation is not passed.

## 5. One-page exposure-response aid

SIMULATED PRACTICE — dummy equipment only.
For an actual used-needle injury:

1. Wash the wound with soap and water.
2. Report immediately through the verified local process.
3. Obtain immediate evaluation from a qualified medical provider.

Do not wait for symptoms, shift end, visits or forms.
Washing alone is insufficient. No symptoms do not establish safety.
Do not choose medication from supplies; a qualified provider decides assessment and treatment.

IMMEDIATE CARE → LATER DOCUMENTATION
Record relevant incident details afterward through the local process.
A near miss without injury/exposure uses the local near-miss process; it does not replace exposure care.
