# Chapter 2.4 First Aid review package

Eighteen bilingual First Aid drafts bring Chapter 2 to 37 of 55 authored lessons; 18 remain outlines. These are supervised training cases under the Chapter 1 teaching and review pattern. Chapter 2.3 remains a draft awaiting owner and independent clinical review; “do it” authorized this next authoring batch, not clinical approval of any earlier or new lesson.

The facilitator guide F38 allocates seven hours to common emergency cases and one hour to first-aid-kit contents. The proposed 18 case stations total 420 minutes, and the kit station adds 60 minutes. Their individual durations are an authoring adaptation; the guide does not prescribe time for each case. Case stations introduce decisions and observation. CPR, choking and other hands-on skills require qualified instruction and further observation before any independent clinical role.

Every lesson has seven bilingual Read sections, seven separately authored Slides, two checks with option feedback, a distinct original diagram, a participant worksheet and cards, a private facilitator guide and a rubric. The module also has a job aid and transfer prompts. Practice uses fictional cases and safe props; CPR/choking use training manikins.

## Source decisions

The BHW Reference Manual R38–48 and Facilitator Guide F38–40 establish scope; presentation P67–92 mainly supplies first-aid orientation and childhood-case material. The author checked current primary guidance on 25 September 2026. Full URLs, lesson coverage and remaining questions are in `drafts/04-first-aid/evidence-review.json`.

- [WHO IMCI](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/integrated-management-of-childhood-illness---chart-booklet-%28march-2014%29) informs childhood danger signs and age-specific assessment. The lessons do not copy a single old respiratory or temperature threshold into BHW referral decisions.
- [WHO diarrhoea guidance](https://www.who.int/health-topics/diarrhoea) supports ORS and continued breastfeeding when safe. The worksheet uses the actual sachet instructions; the older homemade recipe is excluded.
- [CDC seizure guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/epilepsy/first-aid-for-seizures/index.html) supports clearing hazards, timing and avoiding restraint or objects in the mouth. Routine fever wiping is not taught as seizure control.
- [AHA adult](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/adult-basic-life-support) and [pediatric](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/pediatric-basic-life-support) 2025 BLS guidance separates age-specific CPR and choking pathways. Drowning uses the [special-circumstances guidance](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/adult-and-pediatric-special-circumstances-of-resuscitation) and trained water rescue.
- [AHA/Red Cross first-aid guidance](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/2024-first-aid-guidelines) and [IFRC guidance](https://www.ifrc.org/document/ifrc-international-first-aid-resuscitation-and-education-guidelines-2025) inform bleeding, fractures, burns, bites and referral boundaries. [WHO snakebite guidance](https://www.who.int/health-topics/snakebite/snakebite) supports minimal movement and prompt transport.
- Poisoning and chest-pain cases exclude the source's routine large drink, fixed chemical-spill distance and repeated aspirin schedule. The kit lesson treats its medicines as inventory requiring current local authority, not a standing prescription.
