## Source review

R48–51 of the BHW Reference Manual and P6–16 of the presentation identify the ten legacy plant topics and include recipes and doses. The [PITAHC Directory of Herbs](https://pitahc.gov.ph/herbs-directory/) gives botanical names, parts, uses and consultation reminders. Its heading says “9 herbs” although the page contains ten named plant sections; the discrepancy is recorded in `drafts/05-medicinal-plants/evidence-review.json`. The ten BHW source topics remain mapped pending confirmation of the current official list and local product status.

The [WHO traditional medicine guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/traditional-medicine) calls for evidence, quality controls and competent practice. The learner lessons distinguish a source-listed use from treatment authorization. They do not reproduce age-based plant doses, brewing recipes or directions to replace prescribed care. The cases call for clinical assessment for breathing difficulty, infected wounds, severe urinary pain and other concerning symptoms. The evidence file maps each plant to its botanical name, part and source pages and records remaining clinical, botanical and local questions.
