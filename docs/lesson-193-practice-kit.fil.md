## Measure–check–review: one-page aid

1. MUNGKAHI: problema, pagbabago, owner/kapalit, baseline, kalidad, pahintulot, review date. Pending permission? Huwag simulan.
2. SUKAT: magkatulad na denominator. 20/100 → 5/100 = 20% → 5%; 15 points; 75% relative reprint reduction lamang.
3. CHECK: magkatulad bang workload at tama/nababasa ang materyal? Delay, reklamo, naibigay na serbisyo? Hindi pa alam? Isulat.
4. HANDOVER: controlled approved master, version check, owner/kapalit, stock-gap contact, review.
5. REVIEW: continue / revise / stop sa awtorisadong proseso. Walang cost/causation claim kung walang ebidensya.
