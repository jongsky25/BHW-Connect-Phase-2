## Tatlong waste card

1. Tubig: may patak sa gripo; volume, bill at sanhi hindi pa alam. Iulat, walang repair.
2. Papel/printing energy/oras: printout A, dummy master B. Walang cost data.
3. Oras/possible duplicate order: teaching cards hindi makita sa assigned slot. Stock count hindi pa verified.

## Master cards: practice lamang

Card A: LUMANG PRACTICE VERSION. Apat na papel na A. Huwag ipamigay.

Task card: anim na B ang kailangan. Gap: tatlo. Walang delivery confirmation.
Hiwalay sa quiz: inventory quiz = apat na luma, walang current sa kit.

## Gabay sa hanap–tiyak–bilang–balik

1. HANAPIN ang may-label na puwesto.
2. TIYAKIN ang current master sa awtorisadong supervisor.
3. BILANGIN ang usable current stock; ibukod/iulat ang luma o sira.
4. IBALIK ang reusable teaching materials. Itala ang responsible role at next check.
5. IULAT ang verified gap at unknowns; request hindi delivery.
Protektahan: hand-hygiene water, clinical single-use rules, cold chain, ventilation, accessible teaching at urgent services.
Walang utility repair, medicine handling o clinical reuse experiment.
Audit/demo ≠ savings, owner approval o certification.
