# BHW Connect: Gabay sa PhilHealth

- **Subtitle:** YAKAP, GAMOT, at Tamang Pagpaparehistro
- **Document status:** Approved by admin, 29 September 2026
- **Prepared:** 29 September 2026
- **Audience:** Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) across the Philippines
- **Languages:** Filipino and English
**Purpose:** Required orientation and demonstrated skill. Passing grants no new role, permission, clinical authority, or PhilHealth credential.

## 1. Decisions already agreed

- One umbrella PhilHealth course with a passing knowledge assessment and an observed role-play.
- A designated user with BHW Connect's assessor role observes and records the role-play; the admin oversees pilot content approval.
- The course teaches BHWs to identify when a resident needs a PhilHealth PIN or record update and to refer that resident to an official PhilHealth channel. Detailed membership application stays in that channel.
- The course, flipcharts, Knowledge Base (KB), and Chat Guide must be released as one coordinated content package.
- Filipino and English at launch; usable in intermittent-connectivity conditions.
- The pilot admin approves material first. PhilHealth is invited to review when a complete review copy is available.

## 2. Job task and boundaries

**Job task:** During a home visit or barangay encounter, the BHW listens to the resident's question, identifies the next PhilHealth pathway, explains it plainly with a flipchart if useful, directs the resident to a current official channel or accredited provider, and checks understanding.

The BHW may explain published benefit processes, help locate official channels, and follow up on a referral. The BHW does not decide eligibility, select a clinic on someone else's behalf without their participation, handle account passwords or one-time codes, prescribe or recommend a medicine, promise stock or payment, or represent a BHW Connect course certificate as PhilHealth accreditation. Questions that require record access, a clinical decision, or a dispute go to PhilHealth or the appropriate provider.

**Course-level performance standard:** Given a household scenario and current official guidance, the BHW (1) distinguishes membership/PIN work from YAKAP clinic selection, (2) describes the relevant YAKAP or GAMOT journey accurately, (3) names an actionable official next step, and (4) confirms the resident can repeat that step, without crossing the boundaries above.

## 3. Learning and assessment map

Estimated self-study time is **60–75 minutes**, excluding scheduling the observed role-play. Durations are design targets to validate with BHWs, not a time requirement for passing.

| Module | Lessons and core content | Observable practice | Field resource |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Tungkulin ng BHW / The BHW's role** (8–10 min) | A resident's question; ask what help is needed; separate education, navigation, provider decisions, and PhilHealth record decisions; explain how to confirm changing rules. | In 60 seconds, tell a resident what help the BHW can give and who resolves an individual eligibility or clinical question. | One-page pathway card. |
| **2. Gabay sa YAKAP / Guide through YAKAP** (12–15 min) | What YAKAP is; choosing an accredited YAKAP clinic; first patient encounter and ongoing primary care; clinician-directed tests, referrals, and follow-up; members and dependents. | Explain the next two steps for a member who has a PIN but has not selected a clinic. | YAKAP patient flipchart. |
| **3. Gabay sa GAMOT / Guide through GAMOT** (12–15 min) | Why a YAKAP consultation comes first; prescription by an authorized clinician; obtaining covered outpatient medicine through an accredited participating facility; what to do when a medicine or facility is unavailable. Keep current medicine lists and benefit limits in dated references, not memorized promises. | Answer a request for maintenance medicine without implying that the BHW can prescribe or guarantee supply. | GAMOT patient flipchart. |
| **4. Tamang rehistrasyon / The right registration path** (15–18 min) | Path A: no PIN or record needs correction → official PhilHealth membership channel. Path B: PIN exists but no YAKAP clinic selected → current clinic-selection channel. Path C: dependent, clinic transfer, or uncertain record → verify the appropriate official process. Include a low-connectivity fallback. Do not teach form-by-form membership application. | Classify four resident situations and state the next official step for each. | Registration decision flipchart and one-page job aid. |
| **5. Usapang pangbahay / The household conversation** (10–12 min) | Ask, explain, show, check understanding, and agree on follow-up; respond when the resident is frustrated, lacks a phone, or asks for a guarantee. | Run a short simulated visit using one flipchart and a teach-back question. | BHW conversation prompt card. |

Each lesson opens with a named household situation, demonstrates what to say and do, gives one decision or short response to practice, then returns to the situation. Core text remains useful without video or audio. The module check is formative and can be retried after feedback.

## 4. Knowledge assessment

**Purpose:** Check whether the BHW can choose the correct next action, not recall circular numbers or medicine lists.

- A short diagnostic before the course is for feedback only.
- The required posttest has **10 scenario-based items**, in Filipino and English, with an **80% passing score** and **up to three attempts**. Feedback identifies the missed concept and links back to its lesson. These settings align with the existing Day 1 course convention; they are proposed for this course, not yet configured.
- Blueprint coverage: 2 items on BHW scope and verification; 2 on YAKAP journey; 2 on GAMOT pathway; 3 on registration cases; 1 on the household conversation and teach-back.
- At least one item tests an attractive but incorrect promise, such as guaranteeing a requested medicine before clinician assessment. At least one distinguishes obtaining a PIN from selecting a YAKAP clinic.
- Items are reviewed for one defensible answer under the cited guidance. Where a policy is unsettled or changes by locality, the correct action is to verify through the current official channel.

**Completion rule:** A passing posttest creates an assessment request. Course completion and the certificate occur only after the assessor also records a passing observed role-play. A failed or pending role-play leaves the course incomplete. Passing does not change the BHW's app permissions or authorize PhilHealth transactions.

## 5. Observed role-play

**Observer:** Assigned BHW Connect assessor. **Length:** approximately 8–10 minutes plus feedback. **Materials:** scenario card, current registration job aid, relevant flipchart, and scoring form. The assessor presents one of the cases below and asks one follow-up question. The BHW can consult the job aid, as they would in the field.

### Scenario bank

| ID | Resident situation | Expected route | Follow-up challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 — No PIN** | A resident wants YAKAP but does not know of a PhilHealth PIN. | Explain that PIN or record status must be resolved through an official PhilHealth channel before guiding clinic selection. Do not walk through an unverified application. | “Can you register me with your phone and keep my password?” |
| **R2 — PIN, no clinic** | A member has a PIN but has not selected a YAKAP clinic. | Guide the member to the current official selection channels, check the accredited clinic listing, and explain the first clinic visit. | “Can I use benefits just by choosing a clinic in the app?” |
| **R3 — Dependent or change** | A parent asks about a dependent's clinic or wants to change clinics. | Identify that this is a dependent or transfer question; verify the current process through PhilHealth and the clinic rather than assuming the parent's selection applies automatically. | “Our old clinic says we are still assigned there.” |
| **R4 — Medicine request** | A resident asks whether a named maintenance medicine can be obtained through GAMOT today. | Explain consultation, clinician prescription, and accredited dispensing pathway; check current facility information; avoid predicting coverage, stock, or clinical suitability. | “I have an old prescription from another doctor; can you tell me the dose?” |

### Scoring checklist

Each indicator is scored **Observed**, **Needs practice**, or **Not seen**, with a short evidence note. A pass requires all six indicators to be *Observed*. The assessor may use one neutral prompt if a step is omitted, but records that prompt; a prompted critical step requires reassessment.

1. **Asks and sorts:** Elicits the resident's actual question and selects the correct pathway.
2. **Explains:** Gives an accurate, plain-language account of the relevant YAKAP, GAMOT, or registration sequence.
3. **Acts within scope:** Does not decide eligibility, give clinical advice, handle credentials, or guarantee a medicine or service.
4. **Directs:** Names a current official channel or accredited provider and gives a concrete next action, including an offline option when needed.
5. **Uses the aid:** Shows the relevant patient-facing flipchart page and uses the BHW notes without exposing private notes or unrelated patient data.
6. **Checks understanding:** Asks the resident to repeat the next step and corrects a misunderstanding respectfully.

After a failed attempt, the assessor records the missed indicators and a practice recommendation. The BHW may retry with a different scenario. The assessor records a separate result for each attempt; no automatic permission change follows a pass.

## 6. One claim set, four surfaces

Create a **reviewed claim register** before writing final prose. Every substantive benefit or process statement has a stable claim ID, plain-language Filipino and English wording, source URL, source date, last verification date, owner, and status. The same claim IDs feed:

1. **Course:** explanation, practice feedback, and assessment answer rationale.
2. **Flipcharts:** short patient caption plus separate BHW talking points.
3. **Knowledge Base:** bilingual question and answer entries with source references.
4. **Chat Guide:** routing and clarification rules that select the right KB answer.

This avoids four independently maintained versions of a rule. A changed PhilHealth advisory flags every dependent lesson, assessment item, flipchart page, and KB answer for review. Affected content is revised and verified before the replacement is published.

### Initial claim groups and KB coverage

| Group | Minimum questions the KB must answer | Chat behavior to check |
|---|---|---|
| **YAKAP basics** | What is YAKAP? Who is it for? What happens after clinic selection? Where can I find an accredited clinic? | Do not confuse general UHC coverage with completion of YAKAP clinic selection. |
| **Membership/PIN** | I have no PIN. I cannot find my PIN. My record or dependent information may be wrong. | Direct detailed application and corrections to official PhilHealth channels. Do not request account credentials in chat. |
| **Clinic selection** | I have a PIN; how do I choose a clinic? Does my dependent need a selection? How do I ask about transfer? What if I have no internet? | “How do I register?” asks **PIN/membership or YAKAP clinic?** before giving steps. Do not repeat a retired provider portal workflow. |
| **GAMOT** | How do I obtain covered medicine? Who prescribes it? Where is it dispensed? What if a medicine is unavailable? | Do not answer dosing, suitability, stock, or individual coverage from a generic KB entry. Direct those questions to the clinician, pharmacist, or PhilHealth as appropriate. |
| **Costs and problems** | Is every service or drug automatically free? What if I am asked to pay or cannot access a service? | State only the supported, current rule; give the PhilHealth contact or grievance route for individual disputes. |

The KB entries use distinctive Filipino, English, and common Taglish phrasing. Test each likely resident question against existing UHC and health-program entries so the matcher returns the intended answer. Ambiguous registration questions need a clarifier, not a guessed pathway. A published KB entry should be reachable in the Chat Guide even while the BHW is studying; course passing gates the certificate, not access to help.

## 7. Flipchart storyboard contract

| Flipchart | Patient-facing sequence | BHW-facing notes |
|---|---|---|
| **YAKAP: Alaga Mula sa Unang Konsulta** | Choose a clinic → attend the first encounter → follow the clinician's plan → return for follow-up. | Ask whether a PIN and clinic selection exist; explain what the clinic confirms; never promise a test or service before assessment. |
| **GAMOT: Mula Reseta Hanggang Botika** | YAKAP consultation → appropriate prescription → accredited dispensing facility → ask the pharmacist or clinic if there is a problem. | Explain where to verify the current facility and medicine information; redirect clinical and stock questions. |
| **Rehistro: Aling Hakbang ang Kailangan?** | No PIN/uncertain record → PhilHealth; has PIN/no selected clinic → YAKAP selection; dependent or transfer → verify the specific process. | Use the decision questions; offer an in-person channel for low connectivity; protect credentials. |

Every page has Filipino and English captions, equivalent BHW notes, accessible image description, claim IDs, and a visible “reviewed on” date. The patient view shows only the illustration and short caption. The BHW view gives talking points, an opening question, next action, and a boundary or escalation cue. Confirm that both language choices render correctly and that pages and images are deliberately prepared for offline use; merely opening a page once is not a sufficient offline guarantee.

## 8. Review and acceptance gates

The package is ready for **admin pilot approval** when:

- Every course claim and KB answer has a current official PhilHealth source or is clearly labeled as a BHW workflow recommendation.
- The four role-play cases and 10 test items map to the course outcomes; the role-play rubric can be applied consistently by two assessors to the same trial performance.
- Filipino and English convey the same action. At least a small group of BHWs can explain the patient flipcharts back in their own words.
- Chat Guide tests cover intended matches, ambiguous registration, Taglish, existing KB collisions, and out-of-scope medicine questions. The reviewed answers appear in the actual app after publication.
- An assessor can record pass, fail, feedback, and retry; the certificate appears only after both required assessments pass. No app permission changes.
- A BHW can open the prepared field materials with intermittent or absent connectivity. Links to live clinic and facility lists are clearly marked as requiring a current check.
- The admin records the approved content version and review date. A PhilHealth review packet contains the claim register, course, assessment blueprint, flipcharts, and representative Chat Guide answers.
