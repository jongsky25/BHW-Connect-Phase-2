# 1.6.5 · Gibs · Fictional practice kit

## BHW · Gibs

Fictional facts: Liza, 29, says finding childcare makes attendance difficult. Last attendance date is unverified. She asks to talk with the midwife about scheduling. No diagnosis or promised appointment/childcare.
Training procedure only: the named role-card midwife receives the individual report face to face in a private corner; she is the authorized recipient in this simulation. This does not approve a real local channel.
Give the concern, source/uncertainty and requested action. Verify the recipient/method. Answer her clarification honestly, then confirm receipt, owner/action/time-or-method. Keep request and agreement separate.

## Authorized recipient · midwife

You are authorized only within this fictional exercise. Ask: “Is the last attendance date certain?” Wait for an honest correction. Say: “I received your report. I will check scheduling options and speak with you tomorrow through our agreed private method.” Ask Gibs to repeat the agreement. Do not promise an appointment or childcare.
In the retry, ask: “What exactly did Liza request?” If Gibs says a visit is already booked, ask him to correct that certainty. If asked about an unavailable recipient, the answer is to verify the local backup procedure; this card supplies no universal rule.

## One-page handoff aid

1. Recipient and purpose: Who is authorized? What relevant detail is needed?
2. Concern: What did the person report?
3. Source and uncertainty: According to whom? What is verified or still unknown?
4. Request: What action within the BHW role is requested?
5. Secure method: Verify the actual recipient and locally approved channel; no assumed personal-phone or public-chat approval.
6. Clarify and correct: Answer honestly; do not fill gaps with guesses.
7. Confirm: Was it received? Who agrees to do what? When/how will you check again? Record request, acknowledgement and agreement separately.
Meeting: use only an appropriate minimal/de-identified pattern and coordination question. Removing names may be insufficient.
Solo: say/write the report, read the midwife card, answer and confirm. Arrange direct observation later.
Urgent help: do not delay it for a routine report; follow verified local procedure. No real records, upload, diagnosis or guaranteed service.
