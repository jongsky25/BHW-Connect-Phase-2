## Source review

The BHW reference manual R31–37, facilitator guide F35–37 and presentation P46–63 guide the scope. Author source checking on 25 September 2026 is documented in `drafts/03-infection-control/evidence-review.json`, including twelve primary sources and remaining clinical/local questions.

- Respiratory transmission uses current human-health terminology from [WHO](https://www.who.int/news/item/18-04-2024-leading-health-agencies-outline-updated-terminology-for-pathogens-that-transmit-through-the-air); misleading disease-route pairings are excluded.
- Healthcare handwashing uses the whole-procedure 40–60 seconds in the [WHO poster](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/patient-safety/how-to-handwash-poster.pdf). Handrub is separately taught as 20–30 seconds until dry using the [WHO handrub poster](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/patient-safety/how-to-handrub-poster.pdf).
- Cough practice uses a tissue or bent elbow, disposal and hand hygiene, with local reporting and masking procedures left for facility confirmation.
- Water and sanitation lessons do not prescribe unverified chlorine doses, fixed boiling times or septic distances. Local water advisories and sanitary guidance must be confirmed.
- The DOH 2021 primary-care IPC manual supports the ten policy areas, ten training topics and monitoring tasks. Current local adoption and procedures remain to be verified.
