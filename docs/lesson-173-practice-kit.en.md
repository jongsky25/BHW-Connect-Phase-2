## Role cards

Rotate all three roles. Three rounds: each 3-minute attempt + 1-minute feedback + 1-minute retry; 1 minute transitions = 16 total.
BHW/facilitator: calculate all rows, explain feasibility, ask evidence questions, facilitate the separate tie decision and state the referral boundary.
Resident-or-partner/recorder: ask “What supports the score?” Explain the tie facts in your own words. Record reasons, unknowns and corrected sums. Do not invent findings or commitments.
Observer: mark only actions you witness. Give one specific correction, then watch the retry. Silence/nodding is not agreement. Peer feedback is practice, not staff assessment.
Solo: cover the key, write calculations and reasons, uncover and correct; arrange later direct staff observation.
