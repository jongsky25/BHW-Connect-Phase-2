# Assessor candidate path: Chapter II

Chapter II, *The BHW as First Responder*, uses the seven subchapters and 55 required lessons already published for the Los Baños pilot. The assessor candidate takes a diagnostic pre-test before study, then a qualifying post-test after all current lesson revisions are complete. Both phases draw from the published 14-question Chapter II bank recorded in the 2026-09-25 release manifest. The pre-test is diagnostic; the post-test requires at least 80% (12 of 14 correct). Attempts are assessor records and do not create BHW test attempts or certificates.

After the post-test passes, the candidate completes six Chapter II orientation lessons and rates eight fictional observations against the versioned Chapter II rubric. At least seven ratings must be correct, including both safety cases: visibly dirty hands in the infection prevention simulation and simulated traffic in the first aid scene. Each attempt retains feedback. Passing the orientation triggers the existing chapter-scoped qualification check; it does not grant Chapter I qualification or certify a BHW.

The authored orientation spans all seven subchapters and avoids patient data and treatment instructions. The application reads the current published course questions at attempt creation, so a bank below 14 active questions blocks an attempt. The migration installs Chapter II requirements; pilot application waits for PR review and merge. Reviewers should confirm the clinical and local suitability of the two safety cases and whether the 14-question bank gives sufficient coverage for assessor qualification before pilot activation.
