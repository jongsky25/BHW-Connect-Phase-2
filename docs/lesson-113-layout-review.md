# Lesson 1.1.3 build and review

Lesson: `bhw-community-organizer` — **Bilang Community Organizer / As a Community Organizer**.

## Source and learning target

The [BHW Reference Manual](source-material/day1-basic-competencies/bhw-reference-manual.md) (PDF p. 12) and [Day 1 presentation](source-material/day1-basic-competencies/day1-part1-presentation.md) (slide 10) cover relationships with residents, local leaders and health staff; encouraging participation; and contributing to the barangay planning team for health. The manual also links barangay and municipal input to the Local Investment Plan for Health (LIPH). The lesson applies these ideas to one situation: Marites notices standing water, invites residents into a discussion, distinguishes observation from an unverified cause, and brings the concern into local planning.

| Screen | Learner action | Source concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| From observation to conversation | Name whom Marites listens to and connects | `m1.role.organizer` |
| Invite people to take part | Give an invitation and adapt to a resident's reply | `m1.organizer.participation` |
| Report what was seen | Distinguish a specific observation from an assumed cause | `m1.organizer.planningteam` |
| Bring it into local planning | Identify who brings the concern forward and reports back | `m1.organizer.planningteam` |
| Contribute to wider planning | Explain the LIPH link without a funding promise | `m1.organizer.liph` |
| Apply your learning | Choose the next response in Marites's situation | All four |

## Review points

- Confirm the barangay planning team and LIPH wording with a local health planning reviewer. The actual route for carrying a concern and reporting back varies by location.
- Review the invitation and the observation-versus-cause distinction with BHWs. The final check should measure the decision in the story, not recall of an acronym.
- Read every Read and Slides screen in Filipino and English, on narrow phone portrait and landscape layouts. Check the full-screen presenter, image crops, choice buttons, and feedback.
- Listen to both languages of narration after the final copy is rendered. The narration manifest must match the lesson text exactly.
- Keep the learner-facing copy free of source and review notes; retain sources and review status in private authoring material.
