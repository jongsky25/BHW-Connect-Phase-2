# Lesson 1.1.4 build and review

Lesson: `bhw-service-provider` — **Bilang Health Service Provider / As a Health Service Provider**.

## Source and learning target

The [BHW Reference Manual](source-material/day1-basic-competencies/bhw-reference-manual.md) (PDF p. 12) and [Day 1 presentation](source-material/day1-basic-competencies/day1-part1-presentation.md) (slides 11–12) describe the BHW as a member of the primary care team who assists the midwife, may be a resident's first contact, guides people to appropriate services, performs initial tasks within training, and helps monitor health. This lesson applies those ideas to Aling Nena approaching Marites about her child. It teaches the sequence of listening, staying within scope, giving a clear next step, and following the health team's instructions.

| Screen | Learner action | Source concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marites is the first contact | Recognize the BHW's place in the team | `m1.role.provider`, `m1.provider.firstcontact` |
| Listen before guiding | Ask about the need without diagnosing | `m1.provider.firstcontact` |
| Know your limits | Recognize examples of initial services as dependent on training and supervision | `m1.provider.initialservices` |
| Explain the next step | Identify the appropriate contact using local arrangements | `m1.provider.guide` |
| Follow through with the team | Confirm what, when, and to whom to report | `m1.provider.monitoring` |
| Apply your learning | Respond to a beyond-training request through the full sequence | All five |
