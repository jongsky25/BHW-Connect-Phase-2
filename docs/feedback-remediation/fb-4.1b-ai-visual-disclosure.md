# FB-4.1b — visible AI illustration disclosure

The owner directed implementation, testing, merge and live publication of the
AI visual disclosure increment. Report `09d07323-30b4-4024-81f1-9cea997f1511`
was checked read-only on 24 October 2026 and remains `new`. It concerns UHC
purpose and coverage, published revision `5d28c239-c4a2-4cda-a719-e8959a19ae91`.
Its `vlanche-ernesto` provenance explicitly identifies an AI-generated
illustration; its separate narrated story explicitly uses that illustration.

## Delivered behavior

Known AI illustrations display **AI-generated illustration** / **Ilustrasyong
ginawa gamit ang AI** in their visible captions. Verified animations containing
such artwork display **Includes AI-generated illustrations** / **May mga
ilustrasyong ginawa gamit ang AI**. This describes the imagery, not the whole
animation or its voice track. Existing captions, image alternatives, controls,
posters, language selection and text alternatives remain intact.

The shared figure handles regular Read/Slides artwork and optional stories.
The illustrated reader also labels its separate built-in scene, including the
reported UHC scene, in Read, Slides and the full-screen presenter. The labels
use existing canvas/ink tokens on an opaque background so an image or gradient
does not determine their text contrast. Their language is marked explicitly.

## Evidence and classification limits

`src/lib/elearning/ai-visual-registry.json` contains 155 exact asset identities:
122 static illustrations and 33 video compositions with verified illustration
provenance. The source-asset records and two additional historical authoring
records are documented in `fb-4.1b-visual-provenance.json`. Source records must
explicitly identify imagegen, the built-in image tool or AI-generated artwork;
Gemini/TTS narration alone is not evidence that an image was generated by AI.

Each registered static path has its complete SHA-256. Revision assets must
match that hash, and a selected video must also match an exact registered video
path/hash. Built-in story scenes use exact registered paths, whose immutable
bytes are verified by tests. Unknown paths, changed assets, unregistered video
versions, code-authored SVGs and photographs receive no inferred label. This
registry does not grant content or clinical approval to a draft asset.

The 1.1.2 built-in scene is confirmed in `docs/lesson-112-layout-review.md`.
The 1.1.1 scene is confirmed by the introducing PR #196, which explicitly
records built-in Imagegen generation. Earlier community-organizer, records and
service-provider scene notes describe generated fictional illustrations but do
not identify their generation tool unambiguously; those three hardcoded scenes
remain unclassified pending stronger provenance. Their voice-only provenance
also does not justify labeling their whole stories. This is an evidence-bound
disclosure increment, not a claim that every historical asset has been classified.

No image is replaced, no metadata is rewritten, and no authored lesson text,
narration, timing, revision, assessment, grade, completion rule, database schema
or pilot record changes. The shared-reader edit has a new exact successor
receipt; historical guards reconstruct the complete prior reader through the
existing chain without altering old approval hashes.
