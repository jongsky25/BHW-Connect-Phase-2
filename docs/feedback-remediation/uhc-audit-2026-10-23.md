## Official-source checks

| Source | Relevant conclusion for this review |
| --- | --- |
| [RA 11223, §§4(s), 5 and 6(c–d)](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/about_us/RA11223_UHC.pdf) | Automatic NHIP inclusion and choosing/registering with a primary care provider are distinct. The statutory provider definition includes a qualifying worker or institution; an RHU-only definition is too narrow. |
| [PhilHealth Circular 2025-0017, IV and V](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2025/PC2025-0017.pdf) | Clinic choice, registration, FPE and empanelment need distinct treatment. Preserve the beneficiary's choice and check applicable processes. |
| [PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0003](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0003.pdf) | Verification language must preserve the rights attached to covered YAKAP services, including the public-clinic no-fee rule in the advisory's context. |
| [PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0029](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0029.pdf) | A detailed empanelment item must account for the digital YES process and beneficiary consent. |
| [PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0023](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0023.pdf) | Clinic accreditation and empanelment conditions matter; an unaccredited outreach activity is not a substitute. |

The project-provided [Reference Manual transcription, PDF p.65](../source-material/day1-basic-competencies/bhw-reference-manual.md#page-65)
also distinguishes BHW coordination from comprehensive patient guidance by a
doctor, nurse or midwife. Its original-document provenance is recorded in the
source-material README; this audit did not retrieve the Drive original anew.

## Concrete next content increment

Prepare FB-2.2a as a versioned assessment proposal with these acceptance criteria:

1. Use a self-contained resident scenario to distinguish NHIP inclusion from
   clinic selection and empanelment. Name YAKAP where that is the intended
   assessment target. Confirm which blueprint slot is appropriate before
   replacing an item; do not overload T06's referral objective.
2. Use §4(s) when explaining provider terminology; allow the legally described
   worker/institution distinction and public/private choice.
3. Review both languages, the keyed answer and every distractor rationale with
   the responsible assessment/health reviewer. Keep diagnostic and formal
   assessment purposes separate.
4. Use new item identity/version and a new controlled release. Preserve pinned
   draft forms, submitted answers, scores and historical review. Do not rewrite
   existing attempts when improving the bank.
