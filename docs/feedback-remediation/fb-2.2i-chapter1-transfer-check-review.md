**English prompt:** During a visit, BHW Lani sees that the handwashing station has no soap. The midwife asks her to record what she sees, ask residents what help they need, check with the agreed supply contact, and report what still needs follow-up. Which handover follows the instruction?

Aling ulat ang sumusunod sa tagubilin?

Magbigay ng sarili mong ulat at ipaliwanag ang mga tungkuling ginamit; hiwalay ang praktikal na assessment.

This changes the facts, person, and required decisions while retaining observation versus assumption, listening, coordination, accurate records, and an unresolved next step. It does not assert a universal restocking contact or instruct clinical treatment. The proposed choice still uses a three-option check, so it cannot by itself prove applied competence; the separate observed task remains necessary.
