# Lesson 1.1.2 layout review

Lesson: `bhw-health-educator` — **Bilang Health Educator / As a Health Educator**.

## Source and learning target

The [BHW Reference Manual](source-material/day1-basic-competencies/bhw-reference-manual.md) (printed pp. 11–12) describes three connected duties: teach people how to care for their bodies and surroundings, continue education about illness, accidents, and risks through life, and reach every community sector. The [Day 1 presentation](source-material/day1-basic-competencies/day1-part1-presentation.md) repeats these points on slide 9. The authored lesson's objective is to identify education suited to different groups and life stages.

| Existing lesson scene | Manual point | Visual focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start with your audience | Education is a conversation that helps people learn | BHW listening to a resident's question |
| Body and environment | Teach care for the body and surroundings | BHW with group in a clean barangay space |
| Needs change through life | Discuss illness, accidents, and risks through life | Infant's caregiver, young person, and older adult |
| Reach every community group | Share information, knowledge, and training with all sectors | Full group and the question of who is absent |
| Apply your learning | Choose an appropriate approach | Decision about reaching young people |
