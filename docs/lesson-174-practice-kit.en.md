# Lesson 1.7.4 · Carole · Fictional practice kit

## Scenario and partner cards

BHW: You are Carole. Three households were invited; one attended. This exercise supplies confirmed work-hour and childcare barriers. Preferred times, staff availability, costs and agreement to a new trial are unknown. Compare a poster alone with discussion of a small schedule trial. Recommend consultation before implementation; complete the seven fields on page 3.

Resident: “We knew about the invitation, but had work and a child to care for.” Do not invent an agreed time or childcare provider. Ask: “How will you include households who miss meetings? What still needs asking?” You agree only to discuss preferences in this fictional exercise, not to a service change.

Midwife/partner: You can discuss the proposal; no trial date, staff allocation or funding is approved. Ask: “Who decides? Which resources are confirmed? Who agreed? What will you measure if it fails?” Reply: “Staff time and a trial date still require confirmation.” Silence or a nod does not establish agreement.

Each six-minute round: 1 minute plan, 1 minute recommendation, 1 minute questions, 1 minute feedback, 1 minute revision, 1 minute retry. Rotate BHW, partner and observer for three rounds. Use no real household records.
