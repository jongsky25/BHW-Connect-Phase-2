## Roles and observation record

Ten triads for 30 learners. Rotate BHW / contact-or-partner / observer.

Read/allocate: 2 minutes. Three rounds: 4-minute attempt + 2-minute feedback + 1-minute retry each = 21 minutes. Transitions/collection: 2 minutes. Total 25 minutes.

BHW: fill five fields, brief pause/alternative/contact and verify follow-up.
Contact: read only the supplied follow-up; do not improvise permissions.
Observer: name one exact gap, ask for correction and record the retry.

One trainer can observe at most three full 7-minute sequences, one per round in different triads. Others: not observed by trainer. Peer feedback is practice. Stop unsafe proposals, prompt the pause/contact step and retry.

## One-page pause and contact aid

BEFORE WORK: scope → supplies/protection → route/weather → contact/return time → pause conditions.

PAUSE affected work if essential protection is missing, work exceeds training, the route is threatened or required conditions/contact remain unconfirmed.

CONTACT the authorized supervisor under current local procedures. State task, gap/threat, affected work, proposed alternative and unknowns. Do not invent a number or permission.

VERIFY the actual correction and agreement. Record evidence, responsible contact and ready/not-ready. Requested does not mean corrected. No response does not mean agreement.

REPEAT the agreed plan and unresolved conditions. Resume only under authorized local procedure when relevant conditions are verified. Recheck if conditions change.

EXPOSURE, BITE, ACCIDENT OR ILLNESS: report promptly and obtain appropriate immediate medical evaluation/referral. Paperwork and visit targets must not delay it. A qualified provider decides care; no symptoms does not clear exposure.

Dummy exercise only. Hand hygiene/PPE, containers, heat/vector precautions, reporting/referral, route/buddy and pause/resume procedures await authorized local clinical review. No real sharps, chemicals or body fluids.
