# 1.6.4 · Fictional recording practice

## BHW role card · Gibs

Task: an authorized household profile and access-barrier rehearsal. Ask permission; explain relevant health-team use under local policy. Ask Liza what “canceled” means. Do not supply her clarification yourself. No real identifiers, diagnosis or official form is needed.

Source cards: (A) Liza first reports “The schedule was canceled.” (B) Neighbor reports “She was not interested.” (C) You requested the card; no card was shown in the conversation. (D) You have not checked the authorized attendance record; date and status are unconfirmed. (E) Liza reports difficulty finding childcare for two children. (F) Neighbor volunteers gossip about another household’s debt: exclude it.

Produce an attributed short note and a proportionate verification step. Ask Liza to check your summary. Correct the first note without concealing the first statement’s source. Request an authorized midwife’s help with the relevant attendance record under local access procedure. Do not invent the result, a date, childcare or a new appointment.

## Resident role card · Liza

Fictional, age 29; yellow blouse and blue skirt. You choose Filipino and a quiet place. First say “The schedule was canceled.” When asked what you mean, clarify: “I could not go when my sister was away. I am not sure whether the center canceled it.” You have two children and difficulty finding childcare when your sister is away. You cannot show the card today. You do not remember the exact date.

If Gibs summarizes your clarification accurately, confirm that this is what you said. Agree to clarify the date you remember when possible. This confirms your account and willingness to clarify, not attendance or cancellation. Correct any suggestion that the center definitely canceled every appointment. Do not invent a date or agreement to a service. No participant must disclose personal experience. The neighbor’s initial report is printed on the BHW card; no fourth actor is needed.

## Observer role card

Observe one attempt, then give specific feedback. Do not grade or certify. Record the exact words/action beside each item as independent / prompted / not yet / not observed.

□ Explains purpose and relevant authorized use; asks permission.
□ Attributes Liza’s report and the neighbor’s report separately.
□ Records “no card shown” without inferring nonattendance or motive.
□ Asks for clarification and retains its source in the corrected note.
□ Marks date and status unconfirmed; excludes irrelevant debt gossip.
□ Names the relevant record and authorized person/procedure to verify it.
□ Does not invent an appointment, diagnosis, secrecy or deletion rule.

Helpful exact phrase: __________. One step to repeat: __________.
Give feedback and retry that step before rotating. Round 1: A BHW / B resident / C observer. Round 2: B BHW / C resident / A observer. Round 3: C BHW / A resident / B observer. Each ten-minute round: 5 attempt + 3 feedback + 2 retry. A facilitator records only directly observed performances; unsampled evidence remains not observed.
