## One-page listening aid

Permission: “May we talk?”
Choices: “Where and in which language are you comfortable? Would you like a support person?”
Listen: “What would you like to tell me?” Wait; put the phone away.
Summarize: “Have I understood correctly…?”
Correct: offer the more accurate summary and check again.
No internet/projector: printed cards and spoken demonstration. Alone: say both roles, check, repeat and later obtain direct observation. No actual records or upload of private accounts.
