## 4. Why BLHSD is well placed for this assignment

BLHSD is not approaching this as a first engagement with the subject matter. Its Equity in Health and Special Concerns Section has already designed BHW Connect Phase 2 — the DOH digital platform whose Year-1 flagship content module is this same HHP+ community NCD screening curriculum (see the separate WHO concept note). That means the competency framework, the seven competency domains, and the DOH return-demonstration instrument (29 criteria, 87-point maximum) are already mapped before this contract even starts.

## 5. BHW Connect Phase 2 Development — Plan & Budget for This Training

This is the one piece specific to this training assignment: what it takes to make sure the curriculum developed under this contract (Output 2) is authored in a form that plugs directly into BHW Connect Phase 2, rather than needing to be redone later.

### 5.1 Plan

- **Author once, to platform-ready form.** The facilitator guides, participant manuals, quiz banks and the digitized return-demonstration instrument (Output 2) are drafted directly as BHW Connect Knowledge Base entries and e-learning modules, not as stand-alone training PDFs that would need a separate digitization pass later.
- **Embed the people doing that work at BLHSD.** Three (3) staff are assigned to BLHSD's Equity in Health and Special Concerns Section office for the training's development period, doing this contract's content-authoring work (Outputs 1–3) in platform-ready form.
- **Give that office the tools to do it.** An AI/productivity subscription for the BLHSD office supports drafting, translation checking (Filipino/English) and first-pass content review during the same period.
- **Duration matches the content-development half of the contract.** 6 months — covering inception through validation (Outputs 1–3) — not the full 12-month platform build the separate concept note proposes; this is the training-specific slice of that larger effort.
