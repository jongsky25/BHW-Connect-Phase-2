## One-page safe-change decision aid
1. Name the waste; do not guess its cause or cost.
2. Choose a proposal preserving water, infection prevention, clinical single-use rules, critical equipment, ventilation and urgent referral.
3. Verify the approved version and accessible instructions. A reusable teaching visual must be approved and suitable.
4. Explain the rejected unsafe cut and safeguard.
5. Record unknowns: cause, authorization, repair and measured savings. Obtain authorized verification before workplace action.
6. Practice: attempt → specific feedback → observable retry. Trainer observation is separate from the quiz and peer/solo rehearsal.
“I choose __. __ stays. I reject __ because __. First verify __.”
The ending is a proposal; it claims no successful repair, financial savings or certification.
