## Event/source/gap cards

Fictional classroom facts only. Cut the cards apart.

A — First resident: “I missed Monday.” No calendar date or record supplied.
B — Second resident: “I had work.” Exact visit day and other barriers unknown.
C — Third resident: “The day was unclear.” No card shown.
D — Correction, reveal after the first attempt: first resident says “Tuesday, not Monday. I do not know whether the center canceled it.”
E — Event: three households report missed visits.
F — Guess: “They did not want to go.”
G — Proposed solution: “We need a poster.”
Task: sort E/F/G; identify sources and one gap. Do not turn B into everyone’s cause.

## Resident / interviewer / observer

Ten triads, three four-minute cycles. Rotate A/B/C → B/C/A → C/A/B.
Interviewer: 1 minute write; 1 minute neutral question and correction; 45 seconds receive feedback; 1 minute revise; 15 seconds rotate.
Resident: use A/B/C; reveal D only after the first attempt. Never invent a cause, exact date or record finding.
Observer: check event/scope, source, gap and no blame. Ask “Who said it?” or “What remains unknown?” Retain the correction’s source. Peer feedback is not certification.
Solo: write from A/B/C, reveal D, revise, compare with the answer page, retry. Unwitnessed work is not observed.

## Answer and retry

E is event; F is guess; G is solution.
Sample: “According to three households’ direct reports to Carole, they missed agreed visits. The first resident corrected the reported day to Tuesday. Attendance records, exact dates and each barrier remain unchecked.”
The resident confirms the summary of their report only. No record was checked; no cancellation or common cause was confirmed.
Quiz: poster = untested solution; unwillingness = judgment; three missed visits/check each barrier = useful starting point. Add source and gap.
Retry: underline event/scope once, source twice; circle an unknown. Revise any blame or promised solution.
