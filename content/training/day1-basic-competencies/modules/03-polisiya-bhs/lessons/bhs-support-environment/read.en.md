## [section-3] Promoting breastfeeding, and the ban on plastic

RA 10028 strengthens breastfeeding support, including lactation stations under its requirements. A BHW can direct a parent to trained support and help maintain a respectful, supportive environment. DOH DC 2021-0486 concerns phased restrictions on unnecessary single-use plastics in health facilities and DOH offices. Follow facility guidance on safe alternatives. Do not remove sterile or otherwise necessary clinical supplies on your own; reducing unnecessary waste must not compromise safe care.
