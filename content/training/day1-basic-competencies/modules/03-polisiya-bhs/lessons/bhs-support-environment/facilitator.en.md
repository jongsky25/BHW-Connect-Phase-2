## [purpose] Purpose

By the end of this short lesson the BHW can explain that RA 10028 and DOH DC 2021-0486 are not only prohibitions. They also expect active follow-through at the BHS: support breastfeeding, and reduce unnecessary single-use plastic. In a situation, the BHW can name the first action and what to check with the supervisor, without removing supplies that safe care needs.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** about 30 minutes in a face-to-face session: the second half of the module's 60-minute "four policies" block (the breastfeeding act and the plastics ban), from the timing table in the subchapter guide. The first half goes to "Recognizing a company offer". Self-study of the lesson alone takes about 5 minutes (one short section and one self-check).
- **Materials:** manila paper and markers; the lesson's slide ("Promoting breastfeeding, and the ban on plastic"); three situation cards for the practice activity (below); if your facility has written guidance on single-use plastics or on its breastfeeding space, bring a copy.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson in both Read and Slides so you know exactly what the BHW has seen.
- Find out what your participants' facilities already have: a breastfeeding or lactation space, trained breastfeeding support people to refer parents to, and any guidance on plastics and safe alternatives. Know who the supervisor is (usually the midwife or RHU staff).
- Know the lesson's wording by heart: RA 10028 strengthens breastfeeding support, including lactation stations under its requirements. DOH DC 2021-0486 concerns phased restrictions on unnecessary single-use plastics in health facilities and DOH offices.
- This lesson is about **what these two policies expect at the BHS**. Do not teach breastfeeding counselling or technique here; the lesson's step is to direct the parent to trained support. Do not list which clinical supplies may or may not be replaced; that is the facility's guidance, not this lesson.

## [opening] Opening

Put two headings on manila paper: **"Bawal"** (not allowed) and **"Dapat gawin"** (must be done). Ask: *"In the last lesson we saw two policies that say what is not allowed at the BHS. Can a policy also tell us what we should actively do? Give one example."* Write their answers under the right heading. Keep the paper up for the whole lesson.

## [steps] Steps

1. **Two "active expectation" policies.** As the module script suggests, present the breastfeeding act and the plastics ban as the second group of two, after the two bans on promotion. Ask: *"What is the difference between a policy that bans and a policy that asks you to act?"*
2. **RA 10028 (Read "Promoting breastfeeding, and the ban on plastic", the slide's first line).** Say: RA 10028 strengthens breastfeeding support, including lactation stations under its requirements. A BHW can direct a parent to trained support and help keep a respectful, supportive environment. Ask: *"A mother waiting at your BHS needs to breastfeed. What can you do?"* Point out that a lactation station is set up with the LGU and the health team, never with support from a milk company; the next lesson shows Corazon declining exactly that kind of offer.
3. **DOH DC 2021-0486 (same Read section, the slide's second line).** Say: DC 2021-0486 concerns phased restrictions on unnecessary single-use plastics in health facilities and DOH offices. Follow the facility's guidance on safe alternatives. Ask: *"What single-use plastic do you see at your BHS that isn't needed?"* If they need examples, the BHW Reference Manual lists thin plastic cups, drinking straws, coffee stirrers, plastic spoons, forks and knives, and plastic "labo" and sando bags.
4. **Safe care comes first (the slide's third line).** Ask: *"Is every plastic item at the BHS unnecessary?"* Make the point clearly: do not remove sterile or otherwise necessary clinical supplies on your own. Reducing unnecessary waste must not compromise safe care.
5. **Self-check.** Read the check question aloud and let the group discuss before you reveal the answer. Ask one BHW to explain the answer using the lesson situation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"RA 10028 is the ban on milk formula."** This mixes it up with the Milk Code, which is common. Respond gently: *"The Milk Code limits the promotion of milk substitutes. What does RA 10028 ask us to actively do?"* Point to the "Dapat gawin" column.
- **"We don't have space for a lactation station."** This is honest and often true. The lesson says lactation stations are "under its requirements". The BHW's part is to direct the parent to trained support and help keep a respectful, supportive environment. The question of space goes to the supervisor. The BHW does not promise anything about it.
- **"Let's get rid of all plastic — even gloves and syringes are plastic."** Acknowledge the energy for reducing waste, then point to the slide's third line: supplies necessary for safe clinical care stay. Only unnecessary single-use plastic is reduced, and the facility's guidance decides the alternatives.
- **They start giving breastfeeding advice** (for example, about technique). Acknowledge what they know, then bring it back: *"In this lesson, what is our first step?"* The answer is to direct the parent to trained support.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"These are just two more bans."** A BHW who sees RA 10028 and DC 2021-0486 only as "things that are not allowed" may wait for a problem instead of acting. Correct it without embarrassing anyone: point to the "Dapat gawin" column from the opening and ask the group to add one thing each BHW could actively do this week at their BHS. Both policies are about something done, not only something avoided: promote breastfeeding, and cut down on plastic.

## [practice] Practice activity

In pairs, each BHW draws one situation card and has about one minute to say, in their own words: the main point, the first action, and what needs checking with the midwife, RHU or supervisor.

- **Card A:** A mother waiting at the BHS needs to breastfeed her baby and looks uncomfortable in the crowded waiting area.
- **Card B:** The BHS is preparing snacks for a mothers' class, and someone brings a pack of plastic cups and straws.
- **Card C:** A fellow BHW says, *"Let's throw out all the plastic, including the packaged sterile supplies."*

Expected direction: for A, help keep a respectful, supportive space, direct her to trained support, and check with the supervisor what breastfeeding space the facility has. For B, suggest alternatives according to the facility's guidance and check that guidance with the supervisor. For C, do not remove sterile or necessary supplies, and bring the idea to the supervisor. Walk around and listen. This is where you observe.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

The lesson has one check. It appears in the Read section "Promoting breastfeeding, and the ban on plastic" and again on the slide of the same name with the same wording and options, so it is covered once here.

- **"Which facility action is appropriate?"** → **Support breastfeeding and reduce unnecessary single-use plastic under facility policy.** This is exactly what the two policies expect: active support for breastfeeding under RA 10028, and reducing *unnecessary* single-use plastic under DC 2021-0486, following facility guidance.
  - **"Remove all sterile supplies even when necessary for safe care"** is wrong. The lesson says not to remove sterile or otherwise necessary clinical supplies on your own; reducing unnecessary waste must not compromise safe care. The circular is about *unnecessary* single-use plastic, not "all" supplies.

The feedback asks the BHW to explain why using the lesson situation. Card C from the practice activity is a good way to do this.

## [observe] What to observe

Use the lesson's observation indicator during the practice activity: the BHW explains the main point and an appropriate first action in the situation, including what must be checked with the supervisor.

- **Kaya na:** without prompting, the BHW gives the right main point for the card (active support for breastfeeding, reducing only unnecessary plastic, keeping necessary supplies), a first action from the lesson, and what to check with the supervisor.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** the direction is correct, but you have to ask follow-up questions for the details. For example, they say "support breastfeeding" but cannot say what the BHW actually does, or they forget to check facility guidance.
- **Hindi pa:** the BHW agrees to throw out necessary supplies, gives breastfeeding advice instead of directing to trained support, promises a lactation space without checking with the supervisor, or cannot say the main point.

Observing this in the room is separate from the BHW finishing the online self-check. A correct self-check answer is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- Pair them with a BHW rated **Kaya na** and let them answer the same card second.
- Narrow the task: give Card C first, since the safety line ("retain supplies necessary for safe clinical care") is the one most important not to miss. Then give Card A.
- Re-observe at the next session with the same card, not a new one.
