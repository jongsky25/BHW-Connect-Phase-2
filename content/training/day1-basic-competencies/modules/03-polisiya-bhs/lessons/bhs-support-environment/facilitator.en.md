## [purpose] Purpose

Help a BHW demonstrate respectful parent support and safe reduction of unnecessary non-clinical waste. The BHW confirms the trained contact and suitable space, names the institution’s facility duties and the supervisor’s policy role, and protects supplies needed for safe clinical care.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

**Suggested time: 40 minutes** within the whole workplace-policy competency, for which the Facilitator Manual recommends at least four hours. This is an authored lesson allocation, not a new official requirement. Self-study takes about 8 minutes.

Use the six Read screens or Slides, optional bilingual narrated story, two scenario cards, and a board with **Confirmed / To check / Who can confirm**. Use only non-clinical props such as a lunch container; do not handle or substitute real medical supplies. Obtain current facility guidance before teaching.

## [prepare] Prepare

Confirm with the midwife or supervisor who is trained to give breastfeeding support, the available private space, how a parent can reach that support and whom to contact if it is unavailable. Check the facility’s current directive on unnecessary single-use items, permitted non-clinical alternatives, cleaning responsibility and staff-led infection-control and waste protocols. Do not invent a facility or contact.

Review RA 10028 and the original DC 2021-0486. Institutional requirements are distinct from actual local availability. The circular considered infection control and introduced phased coverage; this is not permission for a BHW to remove or reuse clinical supplies. Set a respectful discussion rule: no parent shaming, imposed feeding choices, identifiable patient stories or clinical feeding demonstrations.

## [opening] Opening

Read Mimi’s parent question from screen 1, or play the optional story. Ask participants to name the two distinct topics and one unknown local detail for each. Expected: breastfeeding support/contact/space, and unnecessary plastic/facility directive/safe alternative. Place unknowns under **To check**.

## [steps] Steps

1. **Welcome and distinguish topics (5 minutes).** Ask why listening matters before promising help. Keep breastfeeding support distinct from avoidable waste.
2. **Facilities and responsibilities (6 minutes).** Read screen 2. Explain private, clean, ventilated lactation space outside a toilet under the law’s requirements and formal exemption process. Institution: compliance; BHW: confirm locally and raise gaps.
3. **Parent connection (7 minutes).** Read screen 3. Model a respectful question, ask permission and protect privacy. Confirm the trained contact and suitable space. Clinical advice belongs to trained staff under local guidance.
4. **Circular and safe alternative (7 minutes).** Read screens 4–5. Use the listed items and phased coverage as policy context. Ask about a reusable lunch container in the staff break area; the supervisor must confirm it. Protect sterile and necessary clinical supplies and staff-led waste protocols.
5. **Paired practice (10 minutes).** Run both role-plays below and swap roles. Observe actual words and decisions.
6. **Application check and debrief (5 minutes).** Answer screen 6 individually and explain all three options. Ask each participant to name one local fact still to check.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

- **“The law means I can promise a full station here.”** A requirement does not prove local availability. Check the space, contact and guidance, and raise gaps with the supervisor.
- **“I should tell the parent what feeding choice to make.”** Listen respectfully, ask permission, protect privacy and connect to trained support. Do not impose a choice.
- **“All plastic is illegal everywhere.”** The circular names unnecessary items, settings, groups and phases. Check current facility directives.
- **“Reusable means we should reuse clinical supplies.”** The example is a non-clinical lunch container. Protect sterile and necessary supplies; follow trained staff protocols.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Breastfeeding support is not a reason to shame a parent, and waste reduction is not a reason to override safe care. A policy requirement also does not guarantee that a specific service or worker is available right now. Demonstrate how to say, “I will check with our midwife or supervisor,” while helping the person get a confirmed next step.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Scenario A — parent support.** A parent carrying an infant asks for breastfeeding support. The BHW has not confirmed the contact or space. One participant plays the parent, one Mimi, and one the observer. The BHW listens respectfully, asks permission, avoids public disclosure, states what must be checked, and names the midwife or supervisor who can confirm trained support. No diagnosis or feeding technique demonstration.

**Scenario B — avoidable waste.** Staff propose reusable lunch containers in the break area; another person says to remove all plastic medical supplies too. Mimi asks the supervisor whether the non-clinical alternative is safe and permitted and explicitly stops the proposed independent clinical-supply change. Infection-control and healthcare-waste decisions follow trained staff guidance. Swap roles and repeat both scenarios.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

The first option is correct: listen privately and respectfully; confirm trained support and space; ask the supervisor about reusable containers in the break area while protecting clinical supplies. The second option promises an unverified facility and always-available worker. The third imposes a feeding choice and independently changes sterile supplies. Both violate the practical boundaries taught here. Ask for a spoken reason, not just the option number.

## [observe] What to observe

- **Kaya na:** independently demonstrates respectful listening/privacy and permission, names the trained contact/space to confirm and the institution’s responsibility, asks the supervisor about the non-clinical alternative, and protects necessary/sterile clinical supplies in both scenarios.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** uses respectful language and recognizes the safety boundary but needs prompting about privacy, permission, contact, space or supervisor confirmation.
- **Hindi pa:** promises an unconfirmed facility/contact, imposes a feeding choice, or removes/reuses clinical supplies independently. Correct the action before repeating the practice.

Record role-play observation separately. Online completion or a correct quiz response is not evidence of demonstrated parent-support or safety practice.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

Use two cue cards: **Listen / privacy / permission / confirmed trained support**, and **Non-clinical option / supervisor / protect clinical supplies**. Model one response, then repeat the same scenarios without the cards. Ask the participant to bring one locally confirmed support contact and one approved non-clinical practice to a later session; do not record personal parent information.
