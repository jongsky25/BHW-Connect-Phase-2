## [purpose] Purpose

By the end of this lesson, a BHW can choose among a household profile, master list, registry, and assigned form for a stated information need; check an uncertain detail; and explain who receives the information and how the local team wants it submitted. This introduces record purpose and handoff, not completion of every field.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** about 45 minutes of face-to-face practice. The six learner screens take about 10 minutes for self-study.
- **Materials:** six lesson slides; four fictional cards (household HH-014 with four members, group master list for older residents, service registry for health teaching, assigned form addressed to the midwife); blank local forms approved for teaching; and an observation checklist.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the six Read sections and Slides in Filipino and English. Both modes follow the same Marites and midwife situation.
- Ask the supervising midwife or health team which local forms are used, who assigns and receives them, and how to store and submit them. “Recipient: midwife” in the fictional example is not a universal route.
- Bring **blank** local forms only. Never use completed forms with real household or patient details, and do not ask learners to use real names. The illustration and HH-014 example are fictional, not official forms.
- Confirm how to resolve an unclear entry locally. This lesson teaches checking and asking, not changing an official record from memory.

## [opening] Opening

Marites has returned from speaking with Aling Nena. Ask: *“If the midwife asks who already received health teaching, which record would you check first, and why?”* Keep the initial answers visible and return to them after practice.

## [steps] Steps

1. **After the visit.** Ask what information the team needs and why record purpose matters. Explain that the examples are fictional and blank.
2. **Household profile.** Show HH-014 and ask what it describes. Check that learners do not treat a household description as proof of service delivery.
3. **Master list.** Show the older-resident group card. Ask what it answers and whether group membership proves health teaching occurred.
4. **Registry.** Present the midwife's service question. Ask why the registry is relevant and what Marites should do about one unclear entry.
5. **Assigned form.** Point to “recipient: midwife.” Ask what was requested, what has been verified, and which locally confirmed handoff route applies.
6. **Self-check.** Let each learner choose before showing feedback. Discuss why a group list is not a service record and why missing details must not be guessed.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **“The master list has the names, so it is enough.”** Ask whether the question is who belongs to a group or who received a service. Return to the two cards.
- **“I will complete the missing entry to submit on time.”** Acknowledge the pressure, then ask whom the local team says to consult. Keep the entry unresolved until checked.
- **“Every form goes to the midwife.”** The example has that recipient; actual recipients and routes depend on local forms and instructions.
- **“I do not know where it goes.”** Treat this as a useful question. Name the confirmed local contact or ask the health team; do not invent an answer.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**“If a person appears on a master list, they must have received the service.”** A master list groups people; the manual describes a registry as recording who received services. Ask which record answers the midwife's exact question. Even a registry entry must be checked if unclear.

## [practice] Practice activity

In groups of three, match each fictional card to one question: *What describes this household? Who belongs to the group? Who received a service? What information is requested, and who should receive it?* Give each group an uncertain registry entry. Each BHW explains which record to consult, what to clarify, and how to hand verified information to the locally confirmed recipient. Use blank local forms for comparison only; enter no real data. Observe each person's explanation.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

The same check appears in Read and the final Slide. The best response is to **use the registry, clarify the uncertain entry, and give verified information to the midwife through the approved local route**.

- The master list identifies group membership, not who received health teaching.
- Guessing a missing detail can make the record inaccurate. An assigned form does not remove the need to check the source information and recipient.

## [observe] What to observe

- **Kaya na:** matches all four purposes, chooses the registry for the service question, flags uncertainty, and names the confirmed local handoff route without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** chooses the right record but needs a prompt to check the uncertain entry or recipient.
- **Hindi pa:** confuses master list and registry or guesses a missing detail even after prompting.

Completing the online self-check is separate from observing this skill in practice.

## [support] If more support is needed

Start with master list and registry cards only. Ask the exact question each answers, then add the other two. Demonstrate a handoff using a blank local form and the same fictional scenario. Let the BHW repeat the decision before moving to a new example.
