## [record-purpose] What is the record used for?

BHWs help collect and safeguard information from households in their area. Accurate records inform program planning and identification of needs. Every document shown here is a fictional learning example, not an official form or an actual patient record.

## [household-profile] Household profile: one household

A household profile describes a household. This example shows code HH-014 and four members to illustrate the kind of information brought together. Actual fields and storage arrangements must follow the approved local form and instructions. Never use the fictional example as a real record.

## [list-registry] Master list or registry?

In the manual’s description, a master list groups people, such as children, pregnant residents, or older adults. A registry records people who received services. In this fictional example, the master list says “group: older adults”; the registry says “service provided: health teaching.” They answer different questions.

## [assigned-form] Follow the form instructions

A nurse, midwife, or doctor may assign government, DOH, or local-government forms. Clarify the required information and recipient. The example form highlights “recipient: midwife.” Check entries against the information collected; do not guess missing details. Deliver and safeguard the record using approved local arrangements.
