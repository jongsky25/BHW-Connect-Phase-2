# Facilitator notes — Module 1: The Roles of a BHW

## Timing

**15-20 minutes** at Normal density (core + standard). Add 5-8 minutes
for Long density (includes the contrast section).

## Delivery script (follows the lesson's sections)

1. **Open with the question, not the list.** Read Aling Nena's
   situation aloud before showing any slide or list. Ask the group: "If
   you were BHW Marites, who would you think of first?" — let a few
   answer before revealing the "Four Relationships" section.
2. **On the hub-spoke visual (§concept/core "Four Relationships"),**
   point to each spoke as you name it — don't just read the on-screen
   text straight through; the visual is there to help them remember the
   shape, not to be read literally.
3. **On the RA 7883 section,** emphasize that the basis for the work is
   not just "goodwill" — it is law. This is the detail most easily
   missed in a rush: remind them that accreditation from the local
   health board is what makes them official, not simply attending the
   training.
4. **On "Who is the right person to approach?",** use the check as a
   short group discussion before revealing the answer — ask openly
   "What do you think?" first.
5. **On "Why each relationship matters",** connect it back to
   participants' own experience — ask: "Has there been a time you
   weren't sure who to actually approach?"
6. **If running at Long density,** use the contrast section as a
   closing role-play: split the group in two, one side acting out
   "wrong," the other "right."

## Named misconception

Many new BHWs assume the midwife is the first person to approach for
**everything** — even matters that aren't clinical at all, like a
documentation question or transport support. The effect: genuine
clinical needs get delayed because the midwife's time fills up with
things that were never hers to handle. When teaching this, don't just
say "that's wrong" — instead show the question "what kind of support
does this actually need?" as the easier way to choose.

## Discussion prompts

- "Have you ever not been sure who to actually approach? What did you
  do?"
- "Of the four relationships, which do you think is least used in your
  barangay? Why might that be?"
- "How would you explain to a brand-new BHW why an accreditation
  process exists before they can start?"

## Answer key to the retrieval checks

1. **"Who accredits a BHW..."** → The local health board under RA 7883
   (not the midwife or a fellow BHW).
2. **"A resident has had a long-standing cough..."** → The midwife or
   RHU (a clinical matter, not an administrative one).
