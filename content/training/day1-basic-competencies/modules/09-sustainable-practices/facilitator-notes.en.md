# Facilitator guide — Efficient and Sustainable Practices

## Timing and preparation
Weighting: 3 hours, 180 minutes—25 resource walk-through, 30 safe-savings scenarios, 30 inventory/7S practice, 35 routine/communication, 35 before-after comparison, 25 demonstration/retrieval. Add breaks. Prepare a fictional print/stock log, reusable teaching kit, local policies and rubric. This competency is distinct from Module 8 and does not repeat clinical OSH training.

## Delivery script
1. **Supplies / Water and electricity:** Ask, “Where is real waste, and what does safe care require?” List leaks, duplicate printing and searching time. Never switch off actual critical equipment in a demonstration.
2. **Find/count / Reduce unnecessary use:** Organize dummy stock and an expired-item card. Distinguish reusable teaching materials from clinical single-use items. Do not demonstrate reuse of clinical consumables.
3. **Flow / One change:** Write the agreed checklist, owner, resources, date and quality measure. Use the chain from baseline to review.
4. **7S:** Assign kit storage, check-in/out and cleaning routines. Teach the seven practices through the kit rather than memorizing labels alone.
5. **Comparison / Share:** Calculate 20/100 and 5/100. The change is 15 percentage points, not automatically a 15% relative reduction. Ask whether workloads were comparable and services still delivered. Role-play a proposal to a colleague without internet.
6. **Deep sections:** Compare weeks with five and ten sessions, then have Elena’s replacement use the routine. Apply the four observation indicators.

## Misconception: “Every cost cut is good”
Use shutting down cold-chain equipment or reusing a lancet as counterexamples. Identify the harm and choose a safe alternative such as fixing a leak.

## Discussion and assessment
What quality check is needed before claiming savings? Who acts when the owner is absent? How do we ensure patients retain services? Collect baseline, routine and review sheets; repeat any plan involving an unsafe cut.


## Retrieval-check answer key

- **Water and electricity with safeguards:** Repair the leaking tap while maintaining handwashing water. — The measure removes a leak rather than an essential safety practice.

- **Reduce unnecessary use, preserve safety:** Use a durable reusable teaching visual under facility policy. — A teaching visual can be reusable; clinical waste and single-use devices require different handling.

- **Try a simple comparison:** No; compare use per session and whether service was delivered. — Consumption may fall because service fell, not because the process became more efficient.
