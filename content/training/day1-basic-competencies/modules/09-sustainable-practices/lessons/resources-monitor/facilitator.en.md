## [purpose] Purpose

Propose a small trial with an owner and review date; compare resource use at comparable workloads with quality. Charlaine and all practice records are fictional. No patient identifiers. A quiz answer, attendance or peer rehearsal is not trainer-observed competence.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

60 minutes: 5 opening + 12 modelling + 25 practice/feedback/retry + 10 fair-comparison debrief + 8 transfer. This is separate from audio or optional story duration. Module total: 60 + 60 + 60 = 180 minutes. Prepare the five A4 sheets in lesson-193-practice-kit.en.md, dummy master and outdated copy, pens, calculator and timer. Ten triads for 30 learners; one kit per triad. No actual clinical or utility work.

## [prepare] Prepare

Print the kit. Dummy master: PRACTICE v2, not a clinical handout. Outdated dummy copy: PRACTICE v1. Version 2 is approved only within the fictional role card for simulation; this is not authorisation for a real trial. Show blank approval and local-contact fields. Assign triads T1–T10 and seats A/B/C. Explain trainer sampling and follow-up observation. Protect hand-hygiene water, single-use rules, critical/cold-chain systems, ventilation and timely service. If clinical reuse or equipment shutdown is proposed, stop the rehearsal and return to dummy paperwork.

## [opening] Opening — 5 minutes

0–2: Ask, “Paper use halved because half the sessions were canceled. What can be concluded?” Withhold the answer key. 2–4: ask learners to identify services delivered and missing evidence; accept spoken or written answers. 4–5: explain the objective and fictional-only boundary. The card supplies no bill or cost data; do not add quiz facts.

## [steps] Modelling — 12 minutes

5–9: Model the trial card: proposed owner/replacement, approval pending, baseline 20/100, quality unknown, review two weeks after an authorised start. 9–12: calculate 20% and 5%, 15 percentage points and (20−5)/20 = 75% relative reprint reduction. Do not call it cost savings or causation. 12–15: role-play no internet; use the dummy v2 controlled master and seek feedback. 15–17: ask the replacement to use the routine; model specific feedback and an observable retry. Present question facts before asking for an answer; give rationales only after the response.

## [expected-answers] Expected products

A trial card with all fields and explicit pending approval; a 20% → 5% comparison, 15 percentage points and 75% relative reprint reduction, without a savings claim; a list of missing workload/quality/cost/causal evidence; a controlled dummy master and colleague feedback for the no-internet barrier; a handover with owner/replacement, version check, stock-gap contact and review; specific feedback and an observed retry. Five versus ten sessions supply no reprint counts, so no reprint rate can be calculated.

## [misconception] Common misconception

“Lower totals mean success.” Ask, “Were workload and services delivered comparable?” “Costs are 75% lower.” Ask for, “75% relative reduction in reprints only; costs unknown.” “The card means approval.” Point to pending approval. “A peer observer means a pass.” Explain that evidence covers only actual trainer observations; attendance does not establish a pass.

## [practice] Practice — 25 minutes

17–35: three six-minute role rounds in ten triads. Round 1: A BHW, B colleague, C observer; round 2: B BHW, C colleague, A observer; round 3: C BHW, A colleague, B observer. Each round: 2 minutes context/setup, 2 attempt, 1 feedback, 1 retry. The attempt includes the trial card, comparison and short handover; the peer observer identifies one gap. 35–42: seven-minute shared debrief. Ask, “Which denominator did you use? What is unknown? Can the replacement follow the routine?” Record the retry on sheet 4. Stop an unsafe proposal, explain the boundary and repeat using dummy paperwork.

## [support] Support and a weaker attempt

Accept spoken or written responses in the preferred language. Read cards aloud and ask learners to point to numerator and denominator. Scaffold: “More paper means failure.” Prompt: “Five sessions before, ten now—what is missing for a rate?” Expected retry: “Reprint/page counts and quality; totals are insufficient.” Second scaffold: “Just download it.” Prompt: “There is no internet—which master is verified?” Retry: show dummy v2 and seek feedback. Solo: write or speak the same trial/comparison/handover and record a self-retry; this is not an observed pass. Keep the same rubric.
