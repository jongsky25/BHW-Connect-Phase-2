## [scenario/core] Supplies run out before the week ends {m9.competency, m9.resources}

BHW Elena, 38, is preparing health teaching at the BHS. A tap leaks, incorrect handouts are repeatedly printed, and missing supplies are bought again. She does not immediately conclude that the budget is too small. She records where water, paper, electricity and time are being wasted. Effective workplace practice delivers needed services while reducing wasted resources.

:::takeaway
Identify wasted resources before choosing a saving measure.
:::

## [concept/core] Water and electricity with safeguards {m9.resources, m9.safe-savings}

Report the leaking tap and follow up on its repair. Switch off unnecessary lights or equipment according to facility instructions. Do not turn off cold-chain equipment, required ventilation or other critical systems simply to lower a bill. Do not reduce handwashing water to save money. Target leaks and unnecessary consumption while preserving safe care.

:::takeaway
Safe conservation removes waste while preserving what care requires.
:::
:::check
? Which conservation measure is suitable at the BHS?
- Turn off the vaccine refrigerator at night.
+ Repair the leaking tap while maintaining handwashing water.
- Reduce hand hygiene to make soap last longer.
> The measure removes a leak rather than an essential safety practice.
:::

## [concept/core] Find it, count it, return it {m9.organize}

Assign labeled places for approved supplies. Count stock before requesting more and check expiry under the local inventory procedure. Separate and report expired or damaged items; do not distribute them simply to use them up. Return reusable teaching materials to their place. Orderly storage reduces duplicate ordering and searching time while making real shortages visible.

:::takeaway
Orderly storage supports safe use and accurate resource requests.
:::

## [contrast/core] Reduce unnecessary use, preserve safety {m9.plastics, m9.safe-savings}

For health teaching, a durable reusable visual can replace repeated disposable decorations. Follow BHS policy on unnecessary single-use plastics and proper segregation. Clinical single-use items are different: needles, lancets and other single-use items must not be made reusable. When uncertain, ask the supervisor rather than inventing cleaning or reuse methods.

:::takeaway
Waste reduction does not authorize reuse of clinical single-use items.
:::
:::check
? The team wants less waste in a teaching session. Which is safe?
- Reuse a disposable lancet after cleaning.
- Mix sharps and paper for one collection.
+ Use a durable reusable teaching visual under facility policy.
> A teaching visual can be reusable; clinical waste and single-use devices require different handling.
:::

## [concept/core] Improve the flow, not just the speed {m9.productivity}

Before printing, Elena checks the date and approved version of the handout. She assembles session materials together to avoid repeated trips to the cabinet. Supply delivery can be combined with a planned safe route, but urgent referral must not be delayed to save travel. Measure quality service delivered and time saved; the number of rushed clients is not a measure of good work.

:::takeaway
Productivity means quality service with less waste and rework.
:::

## [practice/core] One change, an owner and a measure {m9.communicate, m9.review}

Elena proposes checking the approved handout before each print run. She prepares the checklist, the midwife confirms the version, and after two weeks they review incorrect printouts and any delayed sessions. Record session counts to compare similar workloads. Share results and revise the process if quality suffers. Agree on the change rather than issuing a new rule to everyone without discussion.

:::visual 1
:::takeaway
A sustainable practice has an owner, measured results and regular review.
:::

## [concept/standard] 7S as a daily routine {m9.organize}

Use 7S to maintain order: remove unnecessary items, arrange what is needed, clean the area, standardize the method, ensure safety, practice self-discipline and sustain the routine. For example, give the teaching kit a label, contents checklist, cleaning schedule and a person checking it before and after use. A single cleanup is insufficient if disorder returns the following week.

:::takeaway
A simple routine and clear responsibility sustain order.
:::

## [practice/standard] Try a simple comparison

Fictional data: previously, 20 of 100 printed pages had to be reprinted because the version was wrong; after the checklist, 5 of 100. Reprints decreased by 15 per 100 pages, from 20% to 5%. Before declaring success, check whether session types were comparable and participants still received correct materials. Patient names are unnecessary for measuring print waste.

:::takeaway
Compare resources across similar work and check quality too.
:::

- Yes, provided the bill is smaller.
> Consumption may fall because service fell, not because the process became more efficient.
:::

## [practice/standard] Share the proposal with colleagues

Describe waste without blame: “There are two versions in the drawer, which causes reprinting.” Present the proposal, responsibilities and review date. Ask colleagues about overlooked barriers, such as lack of internet for the updated file. A controlled paper master may be needed. Resourcefulness finds safe, approved alternatives rather than bypassing policy.

:::takeaway
A change lasts when colleagues understand it and can carry it out.
:::

## [practice/deep] When workloads differ

One week has five teaching sessions and the next has ten. Total paper use can rise even when the process improves. Compare correct materials and reprints per session or per 100 pages, alongside complaints or delays. Do not claim savings from a raw total alone. Distinguish workload changes from process changes.

:::takeaway
A fair comparison accounts for the amount of work performed.
:::

## [practice/deep] Keep the routine when people change

When Elena is absent, a colleague should still be able to use the teaching kit and checklist. Keep a short routine at the work area, orient new colleagues and identify who receives stock-gap reports. During review, remove unhelpful steps and preserve necessary safeguards. A sustainable system does not depend on one diligent person’s memory.

:::takeaway
A routine that colleagues can inherit lasts longer than an individual workaround.
:::
