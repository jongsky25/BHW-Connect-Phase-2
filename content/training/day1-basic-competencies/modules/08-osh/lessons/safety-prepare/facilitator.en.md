## [purpose] Purpose

Build a before-work checklist for station work and field visits covering supplies, route, reporting contact and explicit pause conditions.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

60 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 4 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare local incident contacts, route reporting and buddy-system rules. Use a fictional route.

## [opening] Opening

There is a threat on the route but many visits remain. What is safe?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The second response secures a safe approach and coordination. Visit targets do not justify entering danger; the route and return time still need to be known.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Continues an unsafe route or unprotected task simply to finish the target. A checklist must support a decision about safe starting conditions, not just list equipment.

## [practice] Practice activity

Fill a card: task and scope; supplies and protection; route and weather; contact and return time; pause conditions. Tell your partner: “There is no soap and a threat on the route.” Explain what pauses and whose decision is needed. Alone, write the response. Before the next visit, confirm the route and contact.

## [answer-key] Answer key

2. Avoid the threat and arrange an alternative with the supervisor.

## [observe] Observe

Completes the five checklist fields, identifies the unsafe task to pause and supervisor to contact, and includes heat, route and return-time planning.

Ready: Completes the five checklist fields, identifies the unsafe task to pause and supervisor to contact, and includes heat, route and return-time planning. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Lists supplies and route but misses a contact or pause condition; complete these before repeating the briefing.

Not yet: Continues an unsafe route or unprotected task simply to finish the target.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Lists supplies and route but misses a contact or pause condition; complete these before repeating the briefing. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
