## [station-preparation] Prepare the area and equipment

Ana, 33, is preparing for station work and home visits on a hot day. She needs to check supplies and the route with the midwife.

Arrange for the slippery entrance to be corrected and keep people away from the hazard. Check for the correct sharps container, hand-hygiene supplies and PPE required for the task. Know the safe route and incident contact. If essential protection is missing or a task exceeds training, pause and escalate to the supervisor. Gloves or a mask do not replace removing a hazard when removal is possible.

## [field-preparation] Plan a safe field visit

Before leaving, check weather, slippery paths, dogs and possible threats. Share the route and expected return time; use a buddy system when local protocols call for one. Carry drinking water and plan shade and rest during heat. Do not enter an unsafe situation; arrange an alternative. Report accidents, bites or illness promptly and follow the appropriate referral rather than finishing the household list first.

## [combined-hazards] When hazards combine

Clients arrive in a surge, the room is hot and some PPE is unavailable. As a group, identify tasks to pause, tasks that can be modified and who must decide. Consider staffing capacity and client flow; extra BHW hours should not be the only answer. Propose one safer alternative for supervisor approval.

## [practice] Try it, then repeat

Fill a card: task and scope; supplies and protection; route and weather; contact and return time; pause conditions. Tell your partner: “There is no soap and a threat on the route.” Explain what pauses and whose decision is needed. Alone, write the response. Before the next visit, confirm the route and contact.
