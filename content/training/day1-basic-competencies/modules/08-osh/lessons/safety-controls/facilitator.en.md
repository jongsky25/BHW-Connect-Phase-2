## [purpose] Purpose

Select controls for five hazard groups and explain the pause, verified contact and authorized correction needed before resuming. Apple is a fictional adult BHW (she/her). Dialogue is an adaptation, not a DOH quotation. This lesson selects controls; preparation and physical return demonstrations belong to later lessons.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

60 guided minutes: 5 opening + 10 demonstration + 25 matching/feedback/retry + 12 pause/escalation debrief + 8 transfer. The four lessons retain 45 + 60 + 60 + 75 = 240 minutes. This is facilitated practice, not a reading-time estimate or certification. Actual self-study audio duration is pending generation and measurement; optional story/replay adds time.

For 30 learners, prepare ten five-pair card sets, unsafe-alternative cards, ten role/checklist sheets and a control/pause aid per learner. Use paper, pens, an empty needle-free dummy container with a marked fill line, a lightweight bag and stable chair. No real sharps, fluids, chemicals, painful exercise or live injury.

## [opening] Opening · minutes 0–5

Say: “Apple sees a nearly full sharps box. The contact and replacement are unknown. What pauses, and what must be verified before the task resumes?” Give one minute to think, two to hear alternatives, one to identify unsafe suggestions and one to state the objective. Keep the answer key covered until each attempt. Say: “Gloves alone do not make this container safe.”

## [steps] Steps · minutes 5–15

Demonstrate only with an empty dummy: point to the fill line without opening or compressing contents. Say: “I pause the task that needs the container. I verify who is authorized to arrange replacement. The task resumes only after the correction is verified under local procedure.” Use 3 minutes for this case, 4 to model infection/body/workload/route controls and 3 for questions. Ask: “Which route or condition does the control change? What remains unknown?” Show why gloves for a heavy bag and extra hours for exhaustion are mismatches. This is a control-selection demonstration, not a clinical PPE sequence or physical competency assessment.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

Sharps: proper container, fill line and trained handling; pause if essential containment is missing. Infection: route-/task-appropriate controls, including hand hygiene, ventilation, safe waste and bite prevention; gloves do not replace hand hygiene. Body: arrange supplies, reduce carrying, rest and obtain help; report pain, avoid painful stretches. Stress: agreed workload, rest and coordinator support. Accidents: verify a safe route, avoid threats and plan heat avoidance. Vector control remains within infection for the five-group exercise. Unknown contact/replacement/route must be verified, not assumed. There is no promised replacement, partner agreement or medical outcome.

## [misconception] Common misconception

PPE is one control, not a replacement for safe containment, work design, workload limits or route correction. More volunteer hours are not the default control. A clicked answer or peer checklist does not establish demonstrated competence. If someone proposes recapping, bending, hand-passing, compression, ordinary-bin disposal or continuing without essential protection, stop the simulated task, explain the risk and model a safe pause before retry. Do not demonstrate the unsafe act physically.

## [practice] Practice · minutes 15–40

Ten triads work simultaneously. Use 1 minute to assign roles; then three 8-minute rounds: 3-minute BHW attempt, 2-minute contact questions, 1-minute observer feedback, 2-minute BHW retry. Rotate BHW/contact/observer after each round. Each learner explains all five pairs and corrects the two unsafe mismatches. Contact asks: “What stops? Who must you verify? What permits resuming?” Observer records exact words, including honest unknowns.

One trainer can sample six complete 3-minute initial performances across the rounds (18 minutes), leaving six minutes for selected retries and one for setup. This does not cover all 30 initial performances or all retries. Identify sampled learners beforehand; mark everyone else and any unseen retry “not observed.” Peer comments support practice only. Arrange a later staff observation when required, without claiming it occurred.

Minutes 40–52: 4 minutes compare five pairs, 4 rehearse the essential-protection pause, 4 debrief: “Why is PPE alone insufficient? What evidence of correction is needed? If the contact is unknown, what happens?” Minutes 52–60: 3 minutes each writes one next-duty control/contact to verify, 3 compare the check and rationales, 2 record feedback and any unobserved retry.

## [answer-key] Answer key

Keep the original three options and correct index 2 (third option). First: compressing contents can puncture or expose the next handler. Second: an ordinary bin is improper sharps containment. Third: replace according to the fill line and local protocol before overfilling. Corrected fictional ending: Apple matches the hazard to the control and requests authorized replacement. The affected task resumes only after verified correction under local procedure; if contact or replacement remains unknown, keep it paused and seek help. No actual correction is claimed.

Two retry cards: gloves for a heavy bag → adjust load/work and obtain help; extra hours for an exhausted BHW → agreed workload, rest and authorized reassignment. Accept equivalent safe wording in either language.

## [observe] Observe

Use the single objective_index 0 indicator in competency.json. Record five pairs, reason PPE alone is insufficient, affected task paused, unknown contact identified, and correction verified before resuming. Record date, observer role, exact response, feedback and retry result; no personal health data. Mark each attempt independent / prompted / unsafe / not observed. “Not observed” is a recording state, not a new rubric level. Only staff-witnessed performances support staff ratings.

Ready: all five matches and a safe pause/contact/correction explanation without prompting, including correction of both mismatches. Needs practice: safe pause but two matches or explanations need prompts; record the specific retry. Not yet: unsafe sharps alternative or resuming with missing essential protection; stop, scaffold and retry. Attendance, watching the story and quiz clicks do not count as observation or certification.

## [support] Support

Offer spoken, written or card-pointing responses with the same criteria. Read the cards aloud, use the learner’s preferred language and allow a reader. For a weaker attempt, ask “What hazard? Which condition must change? What pauses?” Model one pair, then ask for an observable independent retry on both mismatches. Solo: cover the key, speak/write five pairs and the pause, compare, revise and retain both attempts. Solo practice is not a staff-observed pass or a substitute for later physical return demonstration.
