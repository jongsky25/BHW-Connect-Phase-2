## [purpose] Purpose

Match five hazard groups with appropriate controls and explain when to pause work and obtain correction.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

60 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 4 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare the five practice cards. Confirm PPE and sharps procedures with the local trainer; no real used sharps in the session.

## [opening] Opening

The sharps box is nearly full. What should be arranged before continuing?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The third response maintains proper containment. Compression and ordinary bins endanger the next handler. Extra caution alone is not a solution.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Suggests recapping, compression, ordinary bins or continuing without essential protection. Choose controls for the hazard; PPE cannot replace every change needed in the work.

## [practice] Practice activity

Make five hazard-control cards: sharps–proper container; infection–task-appropriate prevention; body pain–work adjustment and breaks; stress–agreed workload and support; accidents–safe route and avoiding threats. Match them and explain why PPE alone is insufficient. In pairs, ask what must pause when protection is missing. Alone, explain each card aloud. Check one control before the next duty.

## [observe] Observe

Matches all five hazard-control pairs, pauses and escalates when essential protection is missing, and does not use ordinary bins or reuse for sharps.

Ready: Matches all five hazard-control pairs, pauses and escalates when essential protection is missing, and does not use ordinary bins or reuse for sharps. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Makes a safe decision but mixes up controls for two hazards; rematch and explain.

Not yet: Suggests recapping, compression, ordinary bins or continuing without essential protection.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Makes a safe decision but mixes up controls for two hazards; rematch and explain. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
