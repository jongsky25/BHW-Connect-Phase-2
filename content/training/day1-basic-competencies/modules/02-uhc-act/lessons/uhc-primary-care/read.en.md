## [bridge] Four changes, one journey

Last time, Mang Ernesto and BHW Vlanche learned that every Filipino citizen is automatically included in the National Health Insurance Program. That is the first box in the UHC guide’s selected four-change chain: **PhilHealth inclusion, outpatient consultation, choosing a primary care provider, and a better referral system**. The BHW Reference Manual lists more changes; this story follows these four. Now Mang Ernesto asks, ‘Where do I start, and what if I need another service?’ Vlanche will not guess the answer for their barangay.

## [outpatient] What is an outpatient benefit?

Outpatient care does not require hospital admission; a primary care consultation is one example. PhilHealth’s current YAKAP primary care package covers specified services under its rules. That does not mean every test, medicine, or consultation is available at any facility. Vlanche asks which service Mang Ernesto is seeking, then checks with PhilHealth or the health team whether it is covered, at which accredited clinic, and what steps apply. A health professional decides the appropriate clinical care.

## [provider] Which primary care clinic was chosen?

Under the UHC Act, every Filipino is to choose and register with a public or private primary care provider. PhilHealth’s current rules provide a process for selecting an accredited YAKAP primary care clinic and completing a first patient encounter. Automatic PhilHealth inclusion does not mean a clinic has already been selected. Vlanche asks whether Mang Ernesto has chosen one; if he is unsure, she helps him ask the RHU, PhilHealth, or health team about current choices and how to select. Mang Ernesto chooses. Vlanche does not choose for him or name a clinic she has not confirmed.

## [referral] Who decides on a referral?

UHC aims to connect services better, including reorganizing previously separate health programs and improving movement between levels of care. Local governments may have their own referral protocols. A doctor, nurse, or midwife guides the patient and decides the clinical need and appropriate referral. Vlanche’s role is to ask them for the current local contact, communication method, and instructions for the resident. She does not decide which condition needs hospital care or which facility will receive the patient.
