## [section-6] Propose it, and talk it through first

Now try connecting it all. Have you noticed something in your own barangay that does not yet fully follow UHC's direction — for example, it is still unclear who to approach before a referral, or a resident does not yet know they are already a PhilHealth member?

Before acting on your own, talk it through with a fellow BHW or the midwife first. There may be a reason things are still done that way, or there may be another approach you had not thought of. The point is not to immediately change everything — the point is to start the conversation openly.

## [section-7] The first small step

Think again about the gap you noticed. Instead of waiting for a big change to come from above, what is one small, concrete thing you could do starting next week?

For example: if you noticed residents don't yet know they're automatically covered by PhilHealth, you could start with a short reminder on your next household visit — it doesn't need to be a full campaign right away, just a consistent mention until it becomes something everyone knows.

Big changes in a system usually start with a small change in habit — and the BHW who makes that change first, within their own reach, is the one who speeds it up for everyone else.
