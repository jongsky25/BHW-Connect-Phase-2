## [section-1] Mang Ernesto's question

“Are consultations at the health center free now?” Mang Ernesto asks BHW Vlanche while they wait at the barangay health station. He heard this on the radio, but he does not know whether a consultation is covered at the facility he plans to visit.

Vlanche listens. “I can explain what UHC says. Then we can check which benefit and process apply to you.” She does not promise a price or a free service yet.

## [section-2] What is UHC trying to achieve?

Republic Act 11223, the Universal Health Care (UHC) Act of 2019, aims to improve access to affordable, quality health services for every Filipino and protect people from financial hardship when they need care. This is the law's direction for the health system; it does not say that the same services are already available everywhere today.

Vlanche explains to Mang Ernesto: “UHC aims to make appropriate care easier to reach without the cost becoming a heavy burden.”

## [included] What does automatic inclusion mean?

Under the UHC Act, every Filipino citizen is automatically included in PhilHealth's National Health Insurance Program. This describes who is included in the program. Automatic inclusion alone does not answer whether a particular service is covered at a particular facility.

Vlanche says, “You are included in PhilHealth under UHC. Let us still check the rules for the consultation you are asking about.”

## [benefit] Included, but which service is covered?

Automatic inclusion does not mean every service is free at every facility. Specific benefits have rules about the service, provider, and how to use the benefit. Services currently available can also differ by location. Keep **included in the program** separate from **this benefit is available here today**.

Vlanche neither dismisses Mang Ernesto's question nor promises that the visit is free. She first checks which consultation and facility he means.
