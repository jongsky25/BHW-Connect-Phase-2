## [purpose] Purpose

By the end of this short lesson the BHW can answer a resident's question like Mang Ernesto's: say the goal of the UHC Act in their own words, explain that every Filipino is automatically included in the National Health Insurance Program but that this does not make every service at every facility free, and name what to verify with PhilHealth or the RHU — the benefit, the provider and the registration process — before promising anything.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** about 60 minutes in a face-to-face session. This is this lesson's share of the 3-hour plan in the subchapter guide: the 15-minute opening, the "UHC's goal" half (about 15 minutes) of the 30-minute "UHC's goal and the four changes" row, and the 30-minute mini-quiz / video. The other half of that 30-minute row goes to the next lesson, *Primary care and referral*. Self-study of the lesson alone takes about 5 minutes.
- **Materials:** manila paper and markers; the lesson's slides (including the "understand, verify, practice" visual, still marked draft); for the mini-quiz or video, one of the Learning Activity options on the Day 1 deck (mini-quiz on the UHC Act, asking the significant changes they have heard of, or a video on UHC); a UHC primer if your RHU has one.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson in both Read and Slides so you know exactly what the BHW has seen. There are only two sections: "Mang Ernesto's question" and "The goal of the UHC Act".
- Before the session, ask your midwife or RHU how a resident in your area would check a PhilHealth benefit for a consultation: which benefit applies, who the provider is, and how registration works. If you cannot confirm it, say so in the room — that is the lesson's point.
- Keep the legal wording exactly as the lesson states it: RA 11223 "aims to improve access to quality health services and protect people from financial hardship" and "automatically includes every Filipino in the National Health Insurance Program."
- Scope: this lesson is the law's goal and coverage only. The four concrete changes come in *Primary care and referral*; the health board comes in *Health boards and health promotion*. Do not quote benefit amounts, contribution rates or membership rules — they are not in the lesson. Put those questions on a "check with PhilHealth or the RHU" list.

## [opening] Opening

Before showing any content, ask the group: *"Has something like this happened to you — being unsure about a new policy?"* Let two or three BHWs share. Then read Mang Ernesto's question aloud: *"Is it true consultations at the health center are free now? I only heard it on the radio."* Ask: *"What would you have answered him?"* Write their answers on manila paper without correcting them. Leave the sheet up.

## [steps] Steps

1. **The scene (Read "Mang Ernesto's question", slide "Mang Ernesto's question").** Point to Beth's answer: "Let me check and I'll get back to you." Ask: *"Why did Beth not want to give that answer again?"* Draw out the takeaway: when a law is not clear to the BHW, it is not clear either to the person counting on them.
2. **The goal (Read "The goal of the UHC Act", slide "The goal of the UHC Act").** Read the goal sentence exactly as written. Ask: *"In your own words, what is the law trying to do?"* Accept any answer that covers both parts: access to quality services, and protection from financial hardship.
3. **Included — but not free everywhere.** Read: "It automatically includes every Filipino in the National Health Insurance Program. This does not mean every service at every facility is free." Draw two columns on manila paper: **"Already true"** (every Filipino is included) and **"Still to check"** (the benefit, the provider, the registration process, contribution and benefit-access rules). Ask: *"What does 'automatically included' mean for Mang Ernesto? What does it not mean?"*
4. **Mini-quiz or video (Learning Activity).** Run one of the Learning Activity options from the deck as a warm-up after the goal — a short mini-quiz on the UHC Act, asking what changes they have heard of, or a video if you have one. If a BHW raises one of the four changes, write it down and say it will come in the next lesson.
5. **Back to the opening sheet.** Return to the answers from the opening. Ask: *"Which of these answers promised something before checking? How would you say it now?"*
6. **Self-check.** Ask the check question, *"What should you explain about PhilHealth?"*, and let the group discuss before revealing the answer.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Yes, it's free now."** Do not say "that's wrong." Ask: *"Free for which service, at which facility? How would we find out?"* Then read the lesson line again: this does not mean every service at every facility is free.
- **"No, nothing has changed."** Point to what has changed: every Filipino is now automatically included in the National Health Insurance Program. That is real — and still, the benefit and process must be checked.
- **"I don't know, I'll ask."** Acknowledge it is honest, and it is exactly what Beth said. Then push one step further: *"Good — who exactly will you ask, and what will you ask about?"* The answer the lesson wants: the benefit, the provider and the registration process, with PhilHealth or the RHU.
- **Questions about specific amounts, contributions or who qualifies.** Do not answer from memory. Write them on the "check with PhilHealth or the RHU" list and say you will bring the answer back.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Everything is free now because of UHC."** This usually comes from a half-heard piece of news, like Mang Ernesto's radio. Correct it without embarrassing anyone: ask the group, *"Where did we first hear that?"* — most will say the radio, a neighbour or a meeting, which shows it is nobody's fault. Then go back to the specifics in the lesson: every Filipino is automatically included — a major step — but not every service at every facility is free, and contribution and benefit-access rules still apply. The accurate explanation is more persuasive than any correction.

## [practice] Practice activity

In pairs, one BHW plays Mang Ernesto and asks his question word for word. The other BHW is the BHW in line and has 2 minutes to answer. The answer should include: (1) the goal of the law in their own words, (2) what "automatically included" means and does not mean, and (3) one first action — who they will check with (PhilHealth or the RHU) and what they will check (the benefit, the provider, the registration process) — without promising that the consultation is free. Then swap roles. Two or three pairs perform for the group; the group gives one strength and one suggestion.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

The lesson has one check. It appears both at the end of Read section "The goal of the UHC Act" and on the slide "The goal of the UHC Act"; it is the same question, so it is covered once here.

- **"What should you explain about PhilHealth?"** → **All Filipinos are included, but the covered benefit and process must be checked.** This matches the lesson: the law automatically includes every Filipino, and the resident still needs to verify the benefit, provider and registration process.
  - **"Every service is free at every facility."** is wrong because the lesson says plainly that inclusion "does not mean every service at every facility is free", and contribution and benefit-access rules still apply. Promising this could leave Mang Ernesto facing a cost he did not expect.
  - As the feedback says, ask the BHW to explain why using Mang Ernesto's situation.

## [observe] What to observe

Use the lesson's observation indicator — *explains the main point and an appropriate first action in the situation, including what must be checked with the supervisor* — during the Mang Ernesto role-play:

- **Kaya na:** without prompting, the BHW gives the goal of the law, says Mang Ernesto is included but not every service is free, and names the first action: checking the benefit, provider and registration process with PhilHealth, the RHU or the midwife.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** the BHW has the right direction ("not everything is free, let's check") but can only say what to check, or with whom, after you ask a follow-up question.
- **Hindi pa:** the BHW tells Mang Ernesto "yes, it's free now" or "nothing has changed", or promises there will be no charge without checking.

Observing this in the room is separate from the BHW finishing the online self-check. A correct self-check answer is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- Give them a three-line cue card: *goal of the law / included, but check / who and what to check*. Let them repeat the role-play using the card, then once without it.
- Ask them to rewrite Beth's answer ("Let me check and I'll get back to you") into a better one that says what she will check and with whom.
- Pair them with a BHW rated **Kaya na** and let them answer second.
- Re-observe at the next session using the same Mang Ernesto role-play, not a new scenario.
