## [purpose] Purpose

By the end, the BHW can answer a question like Mang Ernesto's: explain UHC's aim, every Filipino citizen's automatic inclusion in the NHIP, and what to check before saying that a consultation is covered or has no charge.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

About 60 minutes face to face within the subchapter's three-hour plan; about five minutes for self-study. Prepare the six Read or Slides screens, paper, and a current contact list for PhilHealth, the RHU, or the health team. Do not put unverified amounts or benefits on a handout.

## [prepare] Prepare

Confirm the current local way to ask about a benefit, provider, and steps for a primary care consultation with the RHU or PhilHealth. Separate automatic NHIP inclusion from selecting or registering with a primary care clinic for a particular benefit. Detailed primary care and referral belong in 1.2.2.

## [opening] Opening

Read Mang Ernesto's question: “Are consultations at the health center free now?” Ask each learner for one sentence they would say. Write the answers down and revisit them at the end.

## [steps] Steps

1. **Question:** show how Vlanche listens before answering.
2. **Purpose:** ask learners to describe UHC's two aims in their own words: access to affordable, quality care and protection from financial hardship.
3. **Included:** explain automatic NHIP inclusion. Ask whether it settles coverage for a consultation at one facility; it does not.
4. **Benefit:** make two columns, “included in the program” and “this benefit is covered here.” Ask learners to explain the difference.
5. **Verify:** name the three things to check: benefit, provider/facility, and required steps. Use a confirmed local contact, not a remembered assumption.
6. **Answer:** do the self-check, then revisit the opening answers.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

“You are included in PhilHealth” is true but incomplete. “Everything is free” does not establish coverage for this consultation. Seek the fuller response: Mang Ernesto is included in the NHIP; they will check whether this consultation is covered with this provider and what steps apply.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Automatic inclusion is different from an automatic promise of free services everywhere. Do not say a person must first register to be included in the NHIP; choosing a primary care clinic is a separate step for using a particular benefit.

## [practice] Practice activity

In pairs, one learner plays Mang Ernesto and the other Vlanche. In two minutes, explain UHC's aim, what automatic inclusion means, and the three things to check. Swap roles. Listen to each BHW using the observation indicator; a correct online check does not replace a spoken explanation.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

The first response is correct: Mang Ernesto is included in PhilHealth, and the benefit, provider, and steps for this consultation still need checking. A promise that everything is free is wrong; so is a claim that no consultation could be covered.

## [observe] What to observe

**Kaya na:** explains UHC's aim and NHIP inclusion, then names what to check without prompting. **Needs practice:** has the right direction but needs detail prompts. **Not yet:** promises everything is free or cannot name what to check.

## [support] If more support is needed

Give a three-line cue card: “aim / included in NHIP / check benefit, provider, and steps.” Repeat the role-play without the card. Confirm local details with a supervisor before using them with a real resident.
