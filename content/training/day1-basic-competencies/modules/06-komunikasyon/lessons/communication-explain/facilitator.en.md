## [purpose] Purpose

In a sensitive conversation, acknowledge the concern, give one next step within the BHW role and ask the person to explain it in their own words. Demonstrate repair and recheck; do not diagnose or treat.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

120 guided minutes: 10 opening + 20 demonstration + 45 three rounds + 25 repair/recheck + 15 debrief + 5 exit check = 120. The 90 + 90 + 120 + 90 + 90 = 480 minutes form one combined program; the shared module outline is another view of the same time, not an additional eight hours.

30 participants in ten triads. Paper, pens, bilingual role cards, observer checklist and one-page task aid. No real records. Projector and internet are optional.

Authored independent estimate: 13–16 minutes, using the longer language track. Actual six-screen narration is 529.66 seconds in English and 584.93 seconds in Filipino; allow 3–6 minutes for the check, brief rehearsal and reflection. Optional story or replay is additional.

## [prepare] Prepare

Before delivery, verify authorized information, contacts for family planning, distress and smoking support, hours, private conversation arrangements and emergency procedures with the local health team. Review approved information use and access under local policy and NPC transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality. Human/SME review is pending. Until verified, use only fictional cards, state what is unconfirmed and make no referral promise.

Prepare ten triads and role rotation. Explain that no personal disclosure, eye contact or upload is required; a private pair or solo alternative is available. No real resident names.

## [opening] Opening · 10 minutes

Ask: “Does a nod tell us our explanation was clear? What else could we ask?” Accept different reasons for nodding; do not guess intelligence or feelings. Say: “We are checking our explanation. You may change how you participate and use your preferred language.”

## [steps] Demonstration · 20 minutes

Read the entire teach-back screen as Gibs and Liza. Show the misunderstanding, “Do I have an appointment tomorrow?” Pause: “What was unclear in Gibs’s explanation?” Model the repair: “Not yet. First we will ask the midwife about scheduling.” Then ask again: “What will we do first?” The resident replies, “Ask about the schedule; I am not booked yet.”

Ask: “Who is responsible for clarity? Which phrase clarifies what is unconfirmed?” Show the illustrated sequence. Do not reveal check feedback before a learner chooses.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The BHW repairs the explanation without shaming the person. Give one step in plain words and ask for an explanation in their own words. In the Jo/Ben/Ramon cards, first ask the health team about a qualified contact; this guarantees no appointment, method or medication. End with the resident’s own explanation, a supportive correction when needed and recheck. People may decline discussion or details.

## [misconception] Common errors

“Do you understand?” and a nod alone: model “To check that I explained clearly, how would you describe the first step?” Faster repetition: use different plain words. A lecture or blame: acknowledge the concern. Diagnosis from silence: ask about a comfortable setting, do not infer. A service/appointment promise: state what is unconfirmed. No medication, dose, forced disclosure or absolute secrecy. Do not delay urgent help for an ordinary interview/report.

## [practice] Three rounds · 45 minutes; repair · 25 minutes

Round 1, 15 minutes: Jo. Round 2, 15 minutes: Ben. Round 3, 15 minutes: Ramon. Each round: 3 minutes reading cards, 5 conversation, 3 observer feedback and 4 repair/recheck. Rotate roles A/B/C: BHW/resident/observer → observer/BHW/resident → resident/observer/BHW. Everyone plays BHW once.

Then 25 minutes: choose a confusing explanation; repeat in pairs or alone, including repair, the person’s own answer and recheck. Observe scheduled retries; do not grade from a peer report.

Debrief, 15 minutes: “Which concern was acknowledged? Which step is within the BHW role? What remains unknown? Which question revealed misunderstanding? How did you repair and check again?” No personal experience needs to be disclosed.

## [answer-key] Exit check · 5 minutes

Show the unresolved check scene, ask learners to choose and explain why. Correct index 2 / third option.

First: Blame and ending the conversation do not repair an unclear message and may silence Liza. Second: The same explanation delivered faster does not address the misunderstanding. Third: Explaining another way and checking again is appropriate; Gibs takes responsibility for clarity. In the ending, Gibs says, “This is not an appointment yet. We will ask about scheduling first. What will we do first?” Liza replies, “Ask about the schedule; there is no confirmed appointment.” “Yes, that is the first step,” Gibs confirms.

Ask for the repaired next step in their own words. Check/completion and viewing do not establish certification.

## [observe] Direct observation

Acknowledges the concern without blame; gives one correct step within the BHW role; invites an explanation in the person’s own words; repairs misunderstanding and checks again; states what is unconfirmed.

Facilitator sampling: triads 1–3 in round 1, 4–6 in round 2, 7–10 in round 3. Sampling a triad does not observe all three learners’ BHW attempts. Record the specific learner attempt actually seen. Mark others not observed and schedule later direct observation. Peer feedback is practice, not a facilitator grade.

Ready: Acknowledges the concern without blame; gives one correct step within the BHW role; invites an explanation in the person’s own words; repairs misunderstanding and checks again; states what is unconfirmed. Performs without prompting in a directly observed attempt.

Needs practice: The step is appropriate but teach-back, repair or recheck needs prompting; for example, initially checks only for a nod. Record support and arrange an independent retry.

Not yet: No direct evidence of an attempt, or gives a diagnosis, treatment or guaranteed service, or delays immediate help. Record the missing or unsafe action; model and retry.

On the checklist record concern, one step, own explanation, repair and recheck as done / prompted / not yet / not observed. Do not upload personal details.

## [support] Accessibility and retry

Allow speech, writing, reading assistance or a preferred language. Offer a quiet pair or solo using the same fictional cards; no internet/projector. Acting and eye contact are optional. Alone, write both sides and repair/recheck, then demonstrate to the facilitator.

For a supported attempt, model the missing step, provide a new fictional misunderstanding and ask for an independent demonstration. Record fresh direct evidence; never automatically convert not observed into ready.
