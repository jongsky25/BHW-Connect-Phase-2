## [purpose] Purpose

Given a missed-visit story, use an open question, clarify one detail and confirm a summary without leading the answer.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

90 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 8 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare two ambiguous replies: “I have nobody with me” and “That day did not work.”

## [opening] Opening

Liza says, “That day did not work.” Which clarification helps?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The first question explores the barrier. The second assumes motivation; the third limits the conversation to reminders. After the response, clarify the date or another needed detail.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Embeds a motive in the question or records an interpretation that was not checked. A clear question elicits information rather than the answer we expect.

## [practice] Practice activity

Replace “Why don’t you follow instructions?” with an open question. Your partner replies, “I have nobody with me.” Clarify whether this means a travel companion or someone to watch the children, then check your summary. Alone, write and read the three lines aloud. At the next visit, clarify one ambiguous answer before recording it.

## [answer-key] Answer key

1. “What got in the way that day?”

## [observe] Observe

Uses a neutral open question, one focused follow-up and a summary that invites correction.

Ready: Uses a neutral open question, one focused follow-up and a summary that invites correction. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Uses an open question but the follow-up remains unclear; repeat using “What do you mean by…?”

Not yet: Embeds a motive in the question or records an interpretation that was not checked.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Uses an open question but the follow-up remains unclear; repeat using “What do you mean by…?” Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
