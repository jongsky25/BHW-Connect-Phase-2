## [purpose] Purpose

From conflicting accounts, produce a record separating reports, observations and unconfirmed details, with a way to verify the gap.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

90 minutes of facilitated practice: 10 opening + 15 demonstration + 30 three triad rounds + 20 correction/verification + 10 debrief + 5 exit check = 90. Five lessons allocate 90 + 90 + 120 + 90 + 90 = 480 minutes; the shared module outline is another view of this same program, not an extra eight hours. Paper, pens, bilingual role cards, observer checklist and recording aid. No actual resident records. Authored independent estimate: 11–14 minutes, using the longer language track. Actual six-screen narration is 400.61 seconds in English and 446.30 seconds in Filipino; allow 3–6 minutes for the check, brief rehearsal and reflection. Optional story or replay is additional.

## [prepare] Prepare

For 30 participants, form ten triads numbered 1–10. Print four A4 pages per language: BHW, resident, observer with checklist, and recording aid. Show the picture before the check; withhold its ending and correct answer. Prepare source cards A–F. Have the authorized local custodian review purpose, access, correction and retention procedure before real use. Until then, use this as fictional rehearsal only; do not invent policy.

## [opening] Opening · 10 minutes

Ask: “When no card is shown, what did we directly observe? What do we still not know?” Expected: no card was shown in the conversation; this does not establish no appointment or nonattendance. Introduce Gibs, Liza and the four columns. Nobody must disclose personal experience. Distinguish information from diagnosis, judgment or investigation.

## [steps] Demonstration and sequence

15 minutes: read sources and relevant-detail. As Gibs: “This is for an authorized household profile. Only relevant information will be used by the authorized health team under local policy. May we clarify?” As Liza: “The schedule was canceled.” Gibs: “What do you mean by canceled?” Liza: “I could not go when my sister was away. I am not sure whether the center canceled it.” Gibs: “You could not go when your sister was away; the status is uncertain. Is that right?” Liza: “Yes.” Show the incorrect note and attributed correction from verify-gap. Say: “This confirms the summary of the report, not attendance.” This dialogue is fictional adaptation, not a DOH quotation. Continue with 30 triads, 20 revision, 10 debrief and 5 exit check.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

A and E: Liza reports. B: neighbor reports; opinion about interest, not verified motive. C: direct observation only. D: unconfirmed. F: irrelevant, exclude it. Liza’s clarification is an attributed reported correction. Suitable verification: the relevant attendance record with the authorized midwife under the access procedure. The actual date is not expected because it is absent from the cards.

## [misconception] Common errors

Turning “reports” into fact; assuming no card means nonattendance; choosing the loudest voice; writing a diagnosis or “liar”; guessing a date; including gossip; treating a nod as proof of everything; erasing the correction’s source. Ask: “Which card is your evidence? Is it reported or observed? What remains uncertain?” Do not turn peer feedback into a grade.

## [practice] Practice and debrief

30 minutes: three ten-minute rounds (5 attempt + 3 feedback + 2 retry). A/B/C roles: BHW/resident/observer → observer/BHW/resident → resident/observer/BHW. Everyone attempts the BHW role. The neighbor is a printed source card, not a fourth actor. 20 minutes: review and correct the note, underline sources and write one suitable verification step. 10 debrief: “What changed after clarification? What is still uncertain? Why exclude F? What did Liza confirm and what did she not?” Expected: attributed correction; date/status still uncertain; debt is irrelevant; only her summary is confirmed. 5 exit check: choose, explain and compare with the practice note.

## [answer-key] Check answer key

2. “Liza reports a canceled schedule; date to be verified in the record.”

First: even with “according to the neighbor,” “not interested” is a judgment about motive, not an established fact. Second: the report is clearly attributed to Liza and identifies a gap for verification; this is the appropriate initial note. Third: one report does not establish that every appointment was canceled. In the ending, Liza clarifies that she could not attend when her sister was away and is unsure whether the center canceled it. Corrected note: Liza first reported cancellation; she clarified the barrier and uncertainty. No card was shown. Date/status remain unconfirmed; ask the authorized midwife to check the relevant record under the local access procedure. Liza confirms the summary of her own report, not attendance or a new appointment.

## [observe] Direct evidence

Attributes each report, separates direct observation from inference, marks the gap honestly, selects only relevant details and names a suitable verification step.

Ready: Attributes each report, separates direct observation from inference, marks the gap honestly, selects only relevant details and names a suitable verification step. Performs independently and retains the correction’s source.
Needs practice: Attempts the task and separates some details but needs prompts for attribution, uncertainty, relevance or verification. Repeat that part.
Not yet: No attempt is observed, or guesses/opinions are still recorded as fact despite support. If not witnessed, mark not observed and arrange direct observation.

Facilitator sampling: triads 1–3 in round 1, 4–6 in round 2, 7–10 in round 3. Record the role/attempt actually witnessed and exact words/actions. This does not cover all 30 BHW attempts. Mark other attempts not observed and arrange direct follow-up. One indicator only, objective_index: 0. Completion and story viewing are not competence or certification.

## [support] Support and retry

No internet/projector: use printed cards and a read-aloud demonstration. Solo: paper sorting, attributed note and verification plan; arrange later direct observation. Reading/language/accessibility: read aloud, dictation, large print or preferred language; keep the same standard. Do not force eye contact or personal disclosure. For prompted work, choose one observed gap, give specific feedback and retry. Record support or independence in the new attempt; never replace not observed with a guess. Immediate help is not delayed by a routine interview.
