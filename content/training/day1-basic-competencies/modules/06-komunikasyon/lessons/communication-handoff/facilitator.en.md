## [purpose] Purpose

Prepare a short handoff for the midwife containing the concern, sources and uncertainty, requested action and a secure delivery method.

Observable evidence is a secure, attributed handoff with clarification, acknowledged receipt and an agreed next step. Lesson completion, peer feedback and watching the optional story are not competency assessment.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

90 guided minutes: 10 opening + 15 demonstration + 30 three-round triads + 20 report/reception retry + 10 debrief + 5 exit check. The allocations 90 + 90 + 120 + 90 + 90 = 480 are one combined program; the shared module outline describes the same 480 minutes.

30 participants in ten triads. Print the three role cards, observer checklist and one-page task aid per triad; use paper, pens and an unmarked folder. No real resident records or uploads. Independent Read/audio time: Authored independent estimate: 12–15 minutes, using the longer language track. Actual six-screen narration is 459.02 seconds in English and 522.84 seconds in Filipino; allow 3–6 minutes for the check, brief rehearsal and reflection. Optional story or replay is additional.

## [prepare] Prepare

Verify the locally approved form, purpose, authorized recipient, secure communication method, storage, backup contact process and urgent-assistance procedure with the responsible health team. Record pending policy/SME review; do not fill gaps with assumed personal-device approval, a universal channel or a referral threshold. If unavailable, use only the explicit fictional face-to-face training method. This does not approve it for real work.

Arrange ten triads numbered 1–10. Share the rubric. Invite preferred language and reading/accessibility support without requiring personal disclosure. Keep each learner’s direct observation status distinct from role participation.

## [opening] Opening · 10 minutes

Ask: “What can a planning meeting use without receiving a private household form?” Give two minutes to think or write, four to discuss, four to compare a request with a confirmed agreement. Use fictional examples only. Do not reveal the check answer yet.

## [steps] Demonstration · 15 minutes

Model the handoff, audience and confirm-next-step dialogue exactly as in the Read screens. Use a volunteer as the designated midwife. Ask: “Which statement comes from Liza? Which date remains unknown? What did Gibs request? What did the midwife actually agree to do?” Pause before the acknowledgement and invite one clarification. Correct the date uncertainty, then repeat owner/action/time-or-method.

The fictional midwife agrees to check scheduling options and speak privately tomorrow; Gibs agrees to confirm the response and explain only verified information to Liza. Do not promise an appointment, childcare, diagnosis or treatment. Contrast this private report with the meeting’s minimal aggregated summary. Dialogue, timing and tomorrow’s agreement are authored adaptations, not official rules.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

Source: Liza’s own report of difficulty finding childcare. Unknown: last attendance date and available appointment/childcare arrangements. Request: a conversation about scheduling. Agreement: midwife checks options; Gibs confirms tomorrow through their agreed private method and then explains verified information. A sent message alone proves neither receipt nor agreement.

The planning group can discuss an appropriately de-identified pattern and a scheduling question; it does not need personal forms, names or a neighbor’s opinion. Removing names alone may not prevent identification.

## [misconception] Common errors

Unattributed “difficult family”; claiming an unverified date is certain; promising a booked visit or childcare; assuming everyone in the meeting is authorized; sending a record to a public chat or unverified account; treating “sent” as “received”; leaving follow-up without an owner; grading peers as facilitator observations. Ask for the exact corrected sentence or action, then retry it. Do not delay urgent help to finish a routine report.

## [practice] Practice and retry · 50 minutes

30-minute triads: three rounds of ten minutes, each with two minutes to read cards, three for the report/clarification/confirmation, three for specific observer feedback, and two to retry and rotate. Rotate BHW → midwife → observer so every participant gives a handoff.

Facilitator samples triads 1–3 in round one, 4–6 in round two and 7–10 in round three. Record only the observed BHW performance in each sampled triad; sampling all triads does not observe all 30 learners. Mark other performances “not observed” and arrange later direct observation. Peer notes remain practice evidence.

20-minute report/reception retry: five to choose one gap; ten to repeat the handoff including a new clarification; five to check the correction. Directly observe additional learners as time permits without claiming blanket coverage. Provide paper/solo versions from the cards when needed.

## [answer-key] Check and debrief · 15 minutes

First: The aggregated childcare barrier and scheduling question are sufficient for planning if no details identify a household. Second: Photographs of household forms expose unnecessary private details; the group does not need them. Third: Names and a neighbor’s opinion are inappropriate; an opinion is not evidence of the cause. In the ending, the chair acknowledges the aggregated summary and agrees to ask the designated health team member how to clarify scheduling at the next coordination meeting. Gibs confirms who will act and how to obtain the response. Separately, he confirms that the authorized midwife received the individual report and checks their follow-up agreement. No appointment or childcare is promised.

10-minute debrief prompts: “What is the difference between a request and an agreement?” “How did you correct an unknown honestly?” “What would you do if the intended recipient could not be reached?” Expected backup answer: follow only the verified local process. Five-minute exit check: present the unresolved picture and all three choices, ask for a selection and explanation, then reveal all three rationales and ending. Allow spoken or written responses.

## [observe] Direct observation

Identifies the authorized recipient; reports the concern, source and uncertainty clearly; requests action within the BHW role; selects a verified secure method; confirms receipt and who will do what and when or how to follow up.

Ready: Identifies the authorized recipient; reports the concern, source and uncertainty clearly; requests action within the BHW role; selects a verified secure method; confirms receipt and who will do what and when or how to follow up. Does this independently and honestly corrects missing details.

Needs practice: Gives a relevant report with prompts but omits a source, secure method or follow-up owner and time/method. Retry the missing part before another observation.

Not yet: No direct evidence yet, or exposes a private form, invents certainty or promises a service; does not demonstrate a secure handoff and confirmation.

Record exact words/actions, date, support given and the part actually observed. Distinguish “not observed” from a failed attempt. Re-observe after supported rehearsal. One indicator at objective_index 0; no new certification or scoring rule.

## [support] Access and support

Without internet/projector: print cards, aid and the dilemma; read the dialogue aloud. Alone: prepare a report, use the midwife card to answer the clarification, and write or say the confirmation; arrange later direct observation. Offer large print, reading assistance, breaks and spoken/written responses in the preferred language. Do not force a personal story or upload. Keep the same task standard and describe support honestly.
