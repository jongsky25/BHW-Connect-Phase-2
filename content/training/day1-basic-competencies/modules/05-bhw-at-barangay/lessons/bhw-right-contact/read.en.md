## [contact-professional] A health concern

For a clinical, uncertain or urgent concern, contact the appropriate authorized health professional and follow the local referral or emergency process. The contact may be a midwife, nurse, doctor or RHU according to the assigned service and need; no single profession is sufficient for every case. Malou does not diagnose or prescribe medicines. If immediate help is needed, act promptly under the local process while arranging coordination.

## [section-12] Wrong and right choices

In a fictional example, Aling Nena asks about her child’s cough. Malou should not choose the barangay captain for a clinical decision just because they often speak. The appropriate choice is guidance from the authorized health professional or RHU under the local process, without delaying immediate help when needed. Transport support may be coordinated with the responsible local contact at the same time. Support is separate from clinical decisions; Malou does not guarantee a vehicle or funding.
