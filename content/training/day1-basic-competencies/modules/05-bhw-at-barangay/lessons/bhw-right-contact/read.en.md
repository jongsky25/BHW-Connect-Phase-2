## [section-11] Try it: one more situation

Now try it yourself. A newly accredited BHW in your barangay is confused about how to file their first report. Who is the most fitting person to approach — not the midwife, not barangay officials, but someone with the same experience as a BHW?

Think of the answer before moving on: a more experienced fellow BHW is the easiest person to go to for this — not because it's a lesser concern, but because that is exactly where the everyday, practical know-how of the work actually lives.

## [section-12] Wrong versus right: approaching the right person

**Wrong**: BHW Marites went straight to the barangay captain about Aling Nena's child's cough, because "he's the one I usually talk to anyway." The barangay captain's time was wasted on a clinical matter outside his role, and the proper referral was delayed.

**Right**: BHW Marites first recognized the clinical nature of the concern, approached the midwife/RHU, and only involved barangay officials once transport support to the facility was actually needed.
