## [section-8] What good teamwork looks like

A barangay usually has more than one BHW, and they need to work as a team. Five recommendations guide this.

First, everyone should be clear on why they volunteered — to improve the community's health. Second, each member's role should be clear — who's assigned to which purok, who leads which programme. Third, be open about issues, even personal ones — talk about them right away before they deepen. Fourth, be open to feedback and opinions from teammates or the community — treat it as help, not an attack. Fifth, build relationships with teammates outside of work too — it helps team bonding.
