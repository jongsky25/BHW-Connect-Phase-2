## [purpose] Purpose

The learner identifies suitable city or municipal contacts for a stated need, checks local assignments and consent, and explains a safe first action without funding or clinical promises.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

The combined contacts segment remains **30 minutes** within the six-segment **180-minute** module: released lesson 1.5.2 uses an abbreviated **10-minute** barangay core, leaving a selected **20-minute** city/municipal core. The full standalone lesson practice may take about 25–30 minutes; this is not additive to the combined segment. SELF_STUDY_TIMING_PENDING. Finalize from encoded Filipino and English narration plus at least 3–5 minutes of choice and reflection. Use six slide visuals, blank local contact cards and an accessible written or oral response route.

## [prepare] Prepare

Verify the actual CHO/MHO, midwife, PHN, HEPO, BHW president, social welfare/development and sanitary contacts with the local RHU. Check the local emergency and social referral procedures. Use blank fictional examples and seek consent before discussing any family. Do not infer a universal supervisory chain.

## [opening] Opening

Ask: “A family asks about help with medicine. Which question must we answer before naming an office?” Record the type of need and the current verified first contact. No participant must disclose a real family’s health details.

## [steps] Steps

1. Clarify clinical, health-promotion, social-support or sanitation need. 2. Map CHO/MHO, locally assigned midwife/PHN and city HEPO; place individual clinical choices with authorized health professionals. 3. Name mayor, local health board, BHW president, social welfare/development and sanitary inspector; distinguish budget proposal/advice from individual decisions. 4. Walk through Malou’s fictional medicine-support request with consent and a midwife/RHU contact check. 5. Have pairs state the verified next step and follow-up. 6. Invite each learner to choose and explain one of three application choices.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and responses

A learner may say “the board will pay”; ask who assesses the request and note that the board proposes allocations to the sanggunian. “Ask any municipal worker” needs a local contact check. “Send the full record” needs consent, an authorized route and minimum necessary information. If the learner names a plausible alternative local role, ask them to verify that assignment rather than mark the title alone wrong.

## [misconception] Common misconception

A planning body or elected official does not make a particular family’s clinical decision or guarantee assistance. The mayor chairs the board and the CHO/MHO is vice-chair, but local referral arrangements must still be confirmed.

## [practice] Practice

In pairs, role-play Malou and the RHU contact using a fictional family request. State the need, seek consent, ask who currently handles it, limit shared information, and set a follow-up. Offer an equivalent written three-step response or a spoken response without a live role-play. Observe the explanation and the action, not confidence, accent or personal connections.

## [answer-key] Answer key

**First:** Correct: obtain consent, verify the current contact and process through the midwife/RHU, and share only necessary information. **Second:** Incorrect: the complete health record to an unverified official exceeds the need and skips the local route. **Third:** Incorrect: the board proposes and advises on budget matters; the BHW cannot promise funding. Ask learners to explain why before showing the rationale.

## [observe] What to observe

Use the one objective-0 indicator: **Kaya na** names a suitable partner and explains consent, verification and limited disclosure without prompting. **Kailangan pa ng practice** has a sound route but needs a reminder about consent or verification. **Hindi pa** chooses an unsuitable contact, makes a guarantee, or discloses without checking. Online completion alone does not demonstrate this skill.

## [support] If more support is needed

Give a locally verified role card, allow a second try with a simpler sanitation example, and reassess with a fresh fictional case. Permit oral, written or partner-supported practice with the same decision standard.
