## [purpose] Purpose

By the end of this short lesson the BHW can say what the city or municipal partners in the lesson do — CHO/MHO, midwife, PHN, city or municipal HEPO office, local health board, mayor, BHW President, municipal social work and development officer, sanitary inspector — and, when unsure of the current local contact, confirm it with the midwife or RHU before acting.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** about 25 minutes in a face-to-face session. This is this lesson's share of the 40-minute "Additional contacts at the barangay, city, municipal level" part of the plan in the subchapter guide; the other 15 minutes go to Barangay partners. Self-study of the lesson alone takes about 5 minutes.
- **Materials:** manila paper and markers; the lesson's slides; the module's stack visual of the network levels (`02-antas-ng-network.svg`) printed or projected; if available, the names and photos of the actual officials in your city or municipality; four situation cards for the practice activity (see below).

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson in both Read and Slides so you know exactly what the BHW has seen.
- Collect the real names (and photos if you can) of your CHO/MHO, PHN, HEPO office, BHW President, social welfare office and sanitary inspector. A real face is easier to remember than a title.
- Know how your midwife or RHU wants BHWs to confirm a local referral contact, since that is the self-check's answer.
- This lesson is about **knowing who does what**, not about applying for benefits. Do not explain AICS eligibility or amounts, or local health board procedures beyond what the lesson says.

## [opening] Opening

Ask the group: *"Outside our barangay, which health office or official have you actually talked to? What was it about?"* Write each name or office on manila paper. Circle the ones nobody could put a face to.

## [steps] Steps

1. **Additional contacts at the city or municipal level (Read "Additional contacts at the city or municipal level", first slide).** Show the stack visual. Introduce the CHO/MHO, the midwife and the PHN, using your local names. Ask: *"Who sets your tasks and is your technical supervisor? Who supervises the midwives?"*
2. **The HEPO office and the local health board.** Point out that the city or municipal HEPO office is not the same as the BHW's own HEPO role from Module 1. Ask: *"What does the local health board do, and who chairs it?"* (It gives health recommendations to the LGU and manages the budget; the mayor chairs it and the CHO/MHO is vice-chair.)
3. **Other partners (Read "Other partners", second slide).** Introduce the mayor, BHW President, municipal social work and development officer, and sanitary inspector. Go back to the circled names from the opening and fill in who they are.
4. **Not every partner works directly in health.** Ask: *"Why would a BHW need the social welfare officer or the sanitary inspector?"* Link them to the lesson: financial assistance for people needing medical help (AICS), and finding households that need environmental sanitation help.
5. **Self-check.** Give the check (what to do if unsure of the local referral contact) and let the group discuss before revealing the answer.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"I have never talked to anyone outside the barangay."** That is fine and common. Say: *"Then today we put names to faces."* Show the local names you collected.
- **They mix up the HEPO office with the BHW's own HEPO role.** Do not make it a mistake. Say: *"Good that you remember HEPO from Module 1. This one is the office that plans health promotion for the whole locality and monitors BHWs' skill at it."*
- **"I'll just tell the family to go to the social welfare office and they'll get money."** Acknowledge the helpful intent, then ask: *"Do we know the current contact and process? Who can we confirm it with before we send them?"*

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Only people who work in health are my partners."** BHWs often think of the RHU and the midwife only. The lesson's takeaway is that the barangay's leader, the one who provides financial aid, and the one who promotes cleanliness all matter too. Correct it without embarrassing anyone: ask the group for one family situation from their own work where money or sanitation, not a clinical question, was the real problem.

## [practice] Practice activity

Prepare four situation cards from the lesson: (1) a family needs financial help for a medical need; (2) several households have no safe water or sanitary toilet; (3) a BHW's name needs updating in the BHW registry; (4) you are not sure who the current referral contact at the city or municipal level is. In pairs, a BHW draws a card, retells it in their own words, then says: which partner's role fits, their first action, and what they would confirm with the midwife or RHU before telling the family anything. Listen for promises made before checking.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

The section check in Read "Other partners" and the check on the second slide are the same question; it is covered once here.

- **"What if you are unsure of the local referral contact?"** → **Confirm the current contact and procedure with the midwife or RHU.** The midwife is the BHW's technical supervisor, and contacts and procedures differ between localities, so checking first keeps the referral on the right path.
  - *"Invent a procedure without coordination"* is wrong because a made-up procedure can send the family to the wrong office or promise help that is not available, and it bypasses the people who are responsible for referral.

## [observe] What to observe

Use the lesson's observation indicator (explains the main point and an appropriate first action, including what must be checked with the supervisor) during the practice activity:

- **Kaya na:** the BHW retells the card, names the partner whose role fits and why, gives a first action, and says what to confirm with the midwife or RHU — without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** the BHW names the right partner but needs your follow-up question to describe what that partner does or what to confirm first.
- **Hindi pa:** the explanation or action does not fit — for example, the BHW names a title but cannot say what that person does, or promises the family assistance without checking.

Observing this in the room is separate from the BHW finishing the online self-check. A correct self-check answer is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- Give them a one-page list of your local names beside each title and let them use it in a second try; then take it away for the third.
- Start with two cards only (financial help and sanitation) before adding the registry and referral-contact cards.
- Re-observe with the same cards during the "Exercise and summary" part of the module, or at the next session.
