# Facilitator notes — Module 5: The BHW and Their Barangay

## Timing

TESDA Training Regulations set **3 hours** for the competency *Work in
a Team Environment* — combining the "Ang BHW at ang Kanyang Barangay"
and "Pagganap ng Tungkulin bilang Miyembro ng Team" topics
(facilitator-guide PDF 26-27), together with Self-Management, which the
Reference Manual discusses within the same section.

Suggested split:

| Part | Time |
|---|---|
| Opening: Malou and Nena’s fictional activity questions + the four relationships | 30 min |
| Additional contacts at the barangay, city, municipal level | 30 min |
| What good teamwork looks like | 30 min |
| Optional accessible team activity (seated task cards or oral planning) | 30 min |
| Self-management: skills and how to develop them | 30 min |
| Exercise and summary | 30 min |

## Delivery script (follows the lesson's sections)

1. **Open with the question, not the list.** Read Aling Nena's
   situation aloud before showing any slide or list. Ask the group:
   "If you were BHW Malou, who would you think of first?"
2. **On the hub-spoke visual (four relationships),** point to each
   spoke as you name it.
3. **On the additional contacts,** use the actual names and photos of
   officials in your own city or municipality if available — a real
   face is easier to remember than an abstract title.
4. **On "What Good Teamwork Looks Like",** turn it into a group
   discussion: "Which of the five recommendations is hardest to follow
   in your experience? Why?"
5. **Offer the optional accessible team activity** from the lesson 1.5.4 guide: seated blank task cards or oral planning, followed by a debrief. Participation format is chosen freely; no shrinking-space contact or physical-strength scoring. The 30-minute core and separate 30-minute activity occupy the existing two slots.
6. **On self-management,** don't just present the six skills as a
   checklist — ask each participant: "Which of these is already your
   strength? Which needs work?"
7. **On "How to Improve Self-Management",** make it a personal
   exercise — have each participant write one SMART goal before the
   session ends.
8. **If running at Long density,** use the Wrong/Right section as a
   closing role-play.

## Named misconception

**"Self-management is a natural ability — either you have it or you
don't."**

This is a common belief, especially among new BHWs who take "I'm just
not an organized person" as a permanent truth. Don't correct it by
saying "of course you can do it." Instead, show the "How to Improve
Self-Management" section as proof that this is a skill developed
through specific habits — not a trait you either have or lack.

## Discussion prompts

- "Has it happened to you that you weren't sure who to actually
  approach? What did you do?"
- "Who among you already sits on your barangay's planning team for
  health?"
- "Which of the six self-management skills is already your strength?
  Which needs more work?"

## Answer key to the retrieval checks

1. **"Nena asks about the venue and a personal health question."** → Clarify her preference, confirm a professional contact for health guidance and a responsible coordinator for the venue. Rank or peer experience does not replace assessment.
2. **Teamwork handoff check:** A confirms remaining work, permission, acceptance and completion. B takes over without checking scope or permission. C unnecessarily discloses private reasons. Routine issues call for early respectful discussion; immediate danger or a serious breach must not wait for a peer conversation.
