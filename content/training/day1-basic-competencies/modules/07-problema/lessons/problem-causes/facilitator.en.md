## [purpose] Purpose

Build a cause map with two possible branches and verification steps, without treating five questions alone as proof.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

50 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 3 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare missed-visit cards and cause-map paper. Read the separate Rosario case; do not turn medicines in the historical story into medical advice.

## [opening] Opening

At the fifth “why,” the group says the mother lacks knowledge. What next?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The second response tests the explanation before acting. The question count is not proof. A lecture may miss transport, income or service gaps.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Treats a blaming explanation as proven because five questions were asked. Five Whys structures inquiry with others; evidence supports the proposed causes.

## [practice] Practice activity

Put the missed visit at the center of a page. Draw childcare and transport-cost branches. Mark each cause reported, verified or to check. Name a person to consult or record to check on each branch. Ask a partner to identify one unsupported arrow. Alone, circle it and write a verification question. Bring the resident’s perspective to the next case discussion.

## [observe] Observe

Maps two branches, separates evidence from hypotheses, gives a verification step for each and includes the resident’s perspective.

Ready: Maps two branches, separates evidence from hypotheses, gives a verification step for each and includes the resident’s perspective. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Draws two branches without verification; prompt for the person or record behind each arrow.

Not yet: Treats a blaming explanation as proven because five questions were asked.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Draws two branches without verification; prompt for the person or record behind each arrow. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
