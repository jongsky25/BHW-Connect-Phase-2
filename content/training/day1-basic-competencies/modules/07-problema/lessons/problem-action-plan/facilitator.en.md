## [purpose] Purpose

Recommend a trial addressing a verified cause, with an owner, resources, deadline, measure and review date, subject to partner agreement.

This is an authored Carole adaptation using fictional facts; no source quotation, clinical instruction or local authority approval is implied.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

50 facilitated minutes: 6 opening + 10 demonstration + 18 rotating attempts/feedback/retry + 10 review debrief + 6 transfer. Preserve 35 + 50 + 45 + 50 = 180 minutes for module 1.7. Authored independent estimate: 13–16 minutes, using the longer language track. Actual six-screen narration is 512.34 seconds in English and 569.97 seconds in Filipino; allow 3–6 minutes for the check, brief rehearsal and reflection. Optional story or replay is additional. Optional story viewing is additional and not assessment.

For 30 learners: ten triads, each with scenario/partner cards, observer checklist, seven-field plan and review aid; paper and pens. Use only fictional household counts. Print the four-page A4 kit in both languages; its third page is a one-page blank task aid.

## [prepare] Prepare

Read all six screens and print the cards. Write seven fields and a separate agreement box. Establish triads 1–10 and rotate BHW, partner/resident and observer roles. Identify actual local decision-makers with authorized guidance; the classroom health-team role is fictional. Do not improvise preferred times, staff availability, funding or partner agreement.

## [opening] Opening · 6 minutes

0–2: Say: “A meeting finished, but only one of three invited households attended before and after. What changed?” Expected: activity finished; attendance did not improve.
2–4: Ask: “Do these numbers tell us why the other two did not attend?” Expected: no; ask again and attribute reports.
4–6: State the objective, seven plan fields and pending agreement rule. No inference from silence or a nod.

## [steps] Demonstration · 10 minutes

6–8: Compare poster alone with discussion of a small schedule trial. Ask: “Which addresses verified work/childcare barriers?” Expected: discussion may address access, but requires consultation, resources and agreement.
8–11: Model all seven fields using the Plan screen. Say: “Friday is the discussion deadline, not an approved appointment. Staff time and the trial date are pending.”
11–13: Partner asks: “Who decides? Who agreed? What resources are confirmed?” Model honest pending answers.
13–16: Show unchanged 1/3 before and after. Ask: “What outcome, denominator and review date are missing?” Revise the plan without claiming impact.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

A poster may improve notice but cannot alone address work/childcare. Carole can consult and aggregate preferred times; the responsible health team decides schedules/staff. Resources, contributions and trial date need confirmation. Friday is a discussion deadline; Monday after an agreed trial is a provisional review. Measure attendance among those invited and recheck reasons. 1/3 before and 1/3 after means no attendance increase, not proof of each resident’s motive or a solved problem.

## [misconception] Common errors

Activity is not outcome. A meeting count does not measure access. An attractive score is not clinical triage. Do not invent causes, infer agreement from a facial expression, blame residents or promise funding. An earlier trial’s supplied agreement does not authorize a new trial. Never postpone immediate care for classroom planning; use authorized referral procedures.

## [practice] Rotating practice · 18 minutes

16–34: three six-minute rounds; rotate so each learner acts as BHW once. Each round: 1 minute fill the plan; 1 minute recommendation; 1 minute partner questions; 1 minute observer feedback; 1 minute revision; 1 minute repeat recommendation. Partner switches between resident and midwife questions on the cards.

Assessor samples triads 1–3 in round 1, 4–6 in round 2, 7–10 in round 3, noting only the words/actions actually witnessed. This moving sample does not observe every complete attempt or retry. Peer checklists guide practice; they do not establish readiness. Record unsampled or incomplete performances as not observed; schedule direct observation after practice. If staffing allows, use additional assessors for full attempts.

Solo equivalent: fill the paper plan, give a written/spoken one-minute recommendation, answer all four partner questions, compare with checklist, revise and repeat; assessor observation still required.

## [answer-key] Review debrief · 10 minutes

34–36: Ask the original check before feedback.
36–40: Review each option. First: a completed meeting is an activity, not proof of increased attendance or removal of a barrier. Second: repeating without new questions may repeat a mistaken assumption; new evidence is needed. Third: correct—recheck barriers and compare invitations with attendance. Before: 1 of 3; after: 1 of 3. Two still missed visits. Carole’s revised plan: cause—confirmed work and childcare barriers, but each reason for nonattendance remains unknown; proposal—consult again before discussing a small trial; owner—Carole for consultation and the health team for schedule decisions; resources—paper and time, with staff time pending; deadline—Friday discussion; measure—attendance among those invited and reasons for missing visits; review—Monday after an agreed trial. Trial date and contributions still await partner agreement. No funding or resolution of the whole problem is promised.
40–44: Ask: “What do we know? What remains unknown? What can Carole do? Which decision is pending?” Expected: unchanged attendance, unknown individual reasons, consultation, and responsible partner decisions. Takeaway unlocks after selection; clicking the check does not establish competency.

## [observe] Direct observation

Use only objective_index 0 and the six bilingual rubric entries in competency.json. Observe the comparison, all seven fields, separate agreement status, partner questions and outcome review/revision. Record exact words/actions and done / prompted / not yet for each criterion. Independent: complete task without prompts. Needs practice: supported attempt with a specific gap; retry. Not yet: unsafe promises or meeting-only measure persist despite support. Not observed is an evidence status, not a pass or failure. The 6-minute transfer (44–50) is for completing a paper revision, naming an actual next consultation question and arranging direct observation of unobserved learners; do not certify everyone from a demonstration.

## [support] Support

Offer read-aloud cards, preferred language, a scribe or oral recommendation with the same seven fields. Scaffold one weaker attempt: circle the missing measure and ask “Attendance out of how many invited?” Let the learner fill it and retry. For an unauthorized promise ask “Whose agreement is missing?” and require a pending entry. Paper-only and solo options use the same standard.
