## [purpose] Purpose

Recommend a trial addressing a verified cause, with an owner, resources, deadline, measure and review date, subject to partner agreement.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

50 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 3 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare the seven fields on paper. Clarify who can decide on local schedules and resources.

## [opening] Opening

The meeting happened but attendance did not improve. What review is useful?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The third response examines results. A meeting is an activity, not proof that a barrier disappeared; repetition may preserve the wrong assumption.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Promises a new schedule or funding without agreement, or measures only meetings held. A plan names who acts and how to tell whether the barrier decreased.

## [practice] Practice activity

Fill in: cause; proposal; owner; resources; deadline; measure; review date. Compare a poster and trial schedule before choosing. In pairs, the midwife asks about agreement and resources. Revise and repeat the one-minute recommendation. Alone, answer the same questions. Bring the proposal to the next team meeting before implementation.

## [answer-key] Answer key

3. Recheck barriers and compare invitations with attendance before revising.

## [observe] Observe

Links the proposal to the cause, specifies owner, resources, deadline, measure and review, seeks partner agreement and explains how to revise if it fails.

Ready: Links the proposal to the cause, specifies owner, resources, deadline, measure and review, seeks partner agreement and explains how to revise if it fails. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Addresses the cause but omits an owner or measure; complete the plan card before presenting again.

Not yet: Promises a new schedule or funding without agreement, or measures only meetings held.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Addresses the cause but omits an owner or measure; complete the plan card before presenting again. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
