## [purpose] Purpose

Using the four criteria, calculate and explain the example ranking and state why it does not replace urgent referral.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

45 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 3 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Draw four columns and three rows on paper. Label the example as practice data only.

## [opening] Opening

Two problems both score 17. What should the group do?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The first response is evidence-based and open to the group. List order and a leader’s preference are not additional evidence. Follow referral procedures immediately for someone needing urgent care.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Reverses feasibility or treats ranking as permission to delay urgent care. Totals guide planning; evidence, discussion and urgent referral when needed still matter.

## [practice] Practice activity

Copy the three example rows and calculate totals. Explain why 19 ranks first, then list evidence needed before using those scores locally. Simulate a tie: discuss urgency and resources. Alone, write the explanation. At the next planning meeting, ask where the scores came from.

## [answer-key] Answer key

1. Revisit evidence, urgency and resources before agreeing.

## [observe] Observe

Calculates all three totals correctly, explains the four criteria and feasibility direction, requests local evidence and does not delay urgent care.

Ready: Calculates all three totals correctly, explains the four criteria and feasibility direction, requests local evidence and does not delay urgent care. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Uses the right criteria but makes an addition error; use a row-by-row tally and repeat.

Not yet: Reverses feasibility or treats ranking as permission to delay urgent care.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Uses the right criteria but makes an addition error; use a row-by-row tally and repeat. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
