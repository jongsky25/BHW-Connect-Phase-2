## [purpose] Purpose

Use the unchanged lesson objective: calculate and explain the example ranking with four criteria and explain why it does not replace urgent referral. Observe the learner’s calculation, explanation and justified tie decision. A quiz click, attendance or story completion is not direct assessment.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

45 guided minutes: opening 5 + worked scoring 10 + attempts/feedback/retry 16 + tie/referral debrief 9 + transfer 5. The module remains 35 + 50 + 45 + 50 = 180 minutes. These are authored guided allocations within the source topic’s three hours, not new TESDA requirements. Independent reading, audio and optional story are separate. Narration has not yet been generated or measured; do not assert an audio-based study estimate. Print one bilingual kit per triad, paper and pens; no real personal records.

## [prepare] Prepare

For 30 participants make ten triads. Rotate BHW/facilitator, resident-or-partner/recorder and observer so all three attempt. Identify triads 1–10 without resident identities. Use the supplied fictional cards; do not improvise local counts or infer causes of missed visits. Announce that the facilitator can directly sample at most three full attempts during the 16-minute block; peer feedback is rehearsal. Arrange subsequent observation for anyone not witnessed.

## [opening] Opening · 0–5 minutes

Say: “Three households reportedly missed visits. What do we know? What is unknown?” Expected: report only; causes and barangay totals unknown. Ask: “Does a score let urgent care wait?” Expected: no. Introduce Carole as the fictional BHW adaptation; residents speak for themselves. Silence or a nod is not agreement.

## [steps] Steps · 5–15 minutes

Model the three deck rows separately from the missed-visit report. Ask “What is the column order?” Expect magnitude, severity, feasibility, urgency. Demonstrate 4+5=9, +5=14, +5=19; ask learners to finish 17 and 16. Ask “What does a higher feasibility score mean?” Expect more achievable. Ask one evidence question per criterion. Explain that 1–5 categories need not have equal intervals and this equal-weight sum is a planning aid, not a validated formula or triage tool.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

Totals 19, 17, 16; rank in that order. Evidence questions: count/source/period for magnitude; supported effects for severity; actual staff/time/materials/permissions for feasibility; reason for planning urgency. Local scores are unknown. On the separate tie card, Plan A has fictional staff time; Plan B transport is unconfirmed. A justified provisional Plan A decision with explicit partner reasons is acceptable; retain the transport question. A different proposed next step needs evidence and agreement, not invented facts. Urgent referral proceeds independently.

## [misconception] Misconceptions

Higher feasibility means easier to achieve, not a bigger obstacle. A sum does not validate weak evidence. Three reports do not measure local prevalence. List order and leader preference cannot break a tie. Never add a point without new evidence. Do not label residents lazy or infer motive from missed visits. This exercise supplies no medication, diagnosis or clinical triage rule.

## [practice] Practice · 15–31 minutes

Each round is 3 minutes attempt + 1 minute feedback + 1 minute retry. Three rounds (15 minutes) and 1 minute transitions total 16. In the attempt, calculate all rows, explain feasibility, name evidence gaps and discuss the separate tie card with the partner. Observer says “You wrote 18; tally the first row again,” or “You assumed transport; which fact confirms it?” Learner corrects the error in the retry. The facilitator observes one complete triad/learner per round (round 1 triad 1, round 2 triad 2, round 3 triad 3), recording only what was actually seen, then gives feedback within that round. Peer observers cover the others for practice only. Debrief 31–40: ask “Why Plan A? What stays unknown? What if someone needs urgent care?” Transfer 40–45: each writes one question to bring to a future planning meeting and a verification action. No real meeting or partner commitment is claimed.

## [answer-key] Answer key

First: correct—revisit evidence, urgency and resources and ask each partner for reasons. Second: list order is not evidence. Third: the leader’s preference does not justify an extra point. In the corrected ending, the role-play group chooses Plan A as its starting planning step because staff time is available; transport for Plan B remains unconfirmed. Each partner states a reason; agreement is not inferred from silence. Carole records the agreement and the transport question for verification. This is fictional, not a real schedule or partner commitment. If anyone needs urgent care, proceed with referral immediately under the appropriate procedure while planning continues.

Keep the original three options and correct index 0. Reveal the corrected ending only after an answer and a reason. Give the row-by-row key after the independent attempt: 9→14→19; 7→12→17; 6→11→16.

## [observe] Observe

Calculates the three totals 19/17/16, explains the four criteria and higher feasibility, requests evidence and marks unknowns, justifies a tie decision with a partner, and states that urgent referral does not wait for ranking.

Ready: Calculates the three totals 19/17/16, explains the four criteria and higher feasibility, requests evidence and marks unknowns, justifies a tie decision with a partner, and states that urgent referral does not wait for ranking. Performs without prompts.
Needs practice: Attempts the task but needs a prompt for arithmetic, evidence, feasibility or the tie decision. Record the prompt and observe the retry.
Not yet: Cannot yet perform despite scaffolding, or delays urgent care because of ranking. An unseen attempt is not observed; do not treat it as a pass or failure.
Record done/prompted/not yet for witnessed actions, exact words, correction and retry. Keep “not observed” separate from the three achievement levels. Ten triads do not establish 30 passes. Retain a follow-up observation list; peers do not assign staff assessment outcomes.

## [support] Support

Allow the preferred language, oral or written explanations and reading assistance. Give one row at a time and counters: 4+5, then +5, then +5. Scaffold evidence with “source / known / unknown / verify.” Ask the learner to repeat the corrected part with the same criteria. Solo equivalent: complete the tally and tie reason with the answer key covered, then compare and retry; schedule direct staff observation later. A solo self-check is not a pass.
