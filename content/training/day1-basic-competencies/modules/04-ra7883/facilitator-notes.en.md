## Delivery script (follows the lesson's sections)

1. **Open with Demi’s status question.** Ask what experience and an invitation prove, then distinguish training, voluntary primary health care and local health board accreditation. No status or benefit is assumed.
2. **On the legal-basis section,** emphasize that the basis for the
   work is not just "goodwill" — it is law. This is the detail most
   easily missed in a rush.
3. **On benefits,** group the six categories and use Demi’s fictional Card A/Card B to distinguish validated hazard exposure from service within an isolated station. Explain registration, active regular duties and local-fund review. Ask which benefit, evidence and authority/contact need verification. Personal benefit history is optional; no private records are requested.
4. **On BHWE,** use Demi’s fictional Cards A–C and four teaching groups. Compare total service with qualifying continuous accredited service, check education and voluntary/full-time/satisfactory service evidence, and form a specific CSC question about uncertainty or a JO/COS arrangement. Accreditation is not CSC approval or appointment. No private learner records or personal eligibility assessment are requested.
5. **On accreditation,** compare the board’s decision/record with committee screening/recommendation and registry assistance. Use Demi’s fictional Cards A–C to name known facts, missing information and one specific local process/record question. Confirm the actual local arrangement; recommendation, training, BHWE or a job title does not establish accreditation. No private records or personal status disclosure.
6. **On follow-up,** use fictional Demi cards: facts/unknowns, a specific question, listening and clarification, and a realistic next contact. Rotate learner/contact/observer and give feedback; do not score accent, personality or feelings. Retain 45 minutes.
7. **Optional form portion adapted/omitted:** CS Form101-H is for separate CSC BHWE application, not accreditation. Until official download and trainer guidance are verified, use only fictional practice notes and follow-up; no real ID or submission.

## Named misconception

**"I'm just a volunteer, so I shouldn't bring up benefits or money."**

This is the usual reason BHWs — especially new ones — don't ask for
what they're owed. Don't correct it by saying "you need to be
braver." Instead, go back to the legal basis: these benefits are
written into the law itself, not anyone's personal generosity — so
asking about them isn't greed, it's a right.
