## [benefits-question] Are allowances automatically identical?

In this fictional story, someone tells Demi: “Every BHW gets the same allowance automatically.” She does not know whether this is correct. Her accreditation and registration have not been verified either. Instead of promising money, she asks which benefit they mean and which conditions need checking.

RA 7883 provides six categories of benefits. They have different purposes, conditions and responsible authorities. The story video is optional; you can complete Read or Slides without watching it.

## [section-3] Six benefit categories

Section 6 of RA 7883 includes hazard allowance and subsistence allowance. Each has its own conditions; we will compare them on a later screen.

It also includes training, education and career enrichment programs: opportunities for continuing education, training and other educational support. The Act also provides tuition scholarship benefits in state colleges for one child of a BHW unable to use those programs. Civil service eligibility is a separate category with its own CSC qualifications and application; lesson 1.4.3 covers it.

Free legal representation and consultation through the Public Attorney’s Office cover cases arising from or connected with BHW duties, including coercion or interference. Preferential access to loans concerns organized BHW groups with community-based income-generating projects supporting health programs. It is not a promise of a loan to every individual.

## [registration-and-conditions] Registration and further conditions

Section 4 requires registration with the local health board of the city or municipality where service is rendered to receive the Act’s benefits and incentives. It also provides appropriate proof of accreditation. Accreditation and registration are related but distinct. Section 6 refers to accredited BHWs actively and regularly performing their duties, alongside the conditions of each benefit.

Under section 5, DOH determines the ideal ratio of BHWs to households. The total number of BHWs nationwide must not exceed one percent of the total population. This gives us no basis to invent a barangay quota or decide Demi’s qualification.

Section 7 requires local health board review and approval for benefits using local funds. Not receiving a benefit does not prove that a BHW did something wrong or failed to register. Check the applicable conditions and local implementation without blame.

## [compare-allowances] Different conditions for two allowances

Card A: a fictional volunteer BHW may face risk in the workplace while performing duties. Under section 6(a), hazard allowance concerns foreseeable but unavoidable danger affecting health or life during duty, validated by the proper authorities. The local health board and local peace and order council of the LGU determine the amount. Calling a place “dangerous” does not let us validate the exposure or set payment ourselves.

Card B: a fictional BHW serves within an isolated barangay health station to make services available at any and all times. Under section 6(b), subsistence allowance is equivalent to meals taken during duty, computed according to prevailing circumstances determined by the LGU. A distant home alone is not the statutory condition.

A and B need different verification questions. Their status, validation and approved local amounts are unknown; we promise no receipt of benefits.

## [benefit-verification] Demi’s specific verification question

Demi explains: “RA 7883 has six categories with different conditions. Registration where service is rendered is required, and one credential does not establish qualification for every benefit.”

She asks her supervisor or RHU: “For hazard allowance, who is the right contact to verify accreditation and registration, the required exposure evidence, validation by proper authorities, and local review and approval?” The RHU can help identify the contact; it does not replace the statutory authorities. For legal services, the case must connect with BHW duties and PAO is the named provider; for eligibility, CSC is the relevant office.

Demi uses only a blank question sheet. It is not an official form, application, approval or proof of her status. Use fictional cases in practice; no one needs to share private records or a personal benefit-receipt history.
