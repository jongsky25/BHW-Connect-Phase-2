## [section-5] Who makes up the accreditation process

The local health board does not simply grant accreditation on a whim — it carries a set of responsibilities under Section 5 of the RA 7883 IRR: safeguard the registration and accreditation process, evaluate applications, record decisions in a board resolution or meeting minutes, issue certificates of registration or accreditation, review and approve any benefit that would use local funds, recommend benefits and fund appropriation, provide an official copy of the BHW registry to the DOH, and monitor the service registered BHWs provide.

The local health board may also create a BHW Registration and Accreditation Committee to evaluate applications, interview applicants, recommend action to the board, and keep the BHW registry updated.
