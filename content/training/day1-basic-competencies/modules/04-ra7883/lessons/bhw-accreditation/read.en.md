## [accreditation-question] Demi’s question

Demi prepares a question about registration and accreditation. ‘Who reviews an application, and how can I verify the official decision?’ This is a fictional situation; we have not established that Demi has an application, registration or accreditation. RA 7883 places accreditation with the local health board under DOH guidelines. Registration and accreditation are related but distinct steps. Asking a question, attending training or holding a title does not prove that the process is complete.

## [section-5] Responsibilities in the process

The local health board has specific responsibilities. The joint DILG and DOH circular issued in 2023 lists its duties: safeguard registration and accreditation; evaluate and act on applications; record decisions in a board resolution or meeting minutes; issue certificates to qualified BHWs; review and approve benefits using local funds; recommend benefits and fund allocation; provide a copy of the BHW registry to DOH; and monitor services. An accreditation decision and review of a benefit using local funds are different decisions. The board may also create a BHW Registration and Accreditation Committee. The facilitator guide describes screening, interviews, recommendations to the board and registry preparation as supporting committee tasks. A recommendation alone is not the board’s official decision.

## [roles-and-records] Recommendation or decision?

Compare the two roles. A committee helps screen applications and recommend action; the board is responsible for acting and officially recording its decision. The circular encourages municipal and city health boards to create a committee. If none exists yet or creation is pending, the board remains responsible for facilitating registration and accreditation. Find out the actual local arrangement rather than assume every locality has the same committee and office. The RHU or municipal or city health office can help identify the proper contact. Their assistance, an interview or a training certificate does not replace an official decision.
