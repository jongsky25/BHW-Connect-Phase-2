## [purpose] Purpose

Explain and apply the legal basis of the role using Demi’s fictional case: connect training under an accredited organization, voluntary community primary health care, and local health board accreditation under DOH guidelines; identify the authority and one bounded verification step. This remains the single objective at index 0.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

45 minutes: opening and distinction exercise 15; definition/authority 10; worked example 5; paired practice and feedback 10; check and re-practice 5. This is an authored allocation preserving the existing opening 15 + legal basis 30 within the module’s 180-minute plan. The DOH Facilitator Guide recommends a three-hour minimum for the whole Develop Life and Career Decisions competency (original PDF 25, printed 16), not for this lesson alone. Six-screen self-study: about 8–10 minutes, authored estimate; optional story and replay add time. Materials: six bilingual screens, copies/links of RA 7883, fictional Card A/B, blank cue cards, locally verified office contact.

## [prepare] Prepare

Read sections 3–4 of RA 7883 and both lesson languages. Check current law and local DOH/LGU implementation before delivery. Confirm with the RHU/LGU which office supports the local health board’s process; do not invent an officer’s name, forms, deadlines or eligibility result. Distinguish an accredited training organization from accreditation of a person. New art/media are draft; no credential is depicted. Prepare fictional cases, never private participant records. Benefits, BHWE and detailed applications belong in later lessons.

## [opening] Opening

Use section-1: an acquaintance says Demi’s long service and invitation establish status. Ask: “What is known? What still needs evidence?” Collect anonymous questions about the definition, without requiring personal status disclosures. Sort three cards: willingness/experience, training record, board accreditation. Explain that a reasonable question is welcome; no allowance or status is established in this story.

## [steps] Steps

1. Read section-2 and connect all three elements; it paraphrases section 3, not a statutory quotation. 2. On roles-and-authority, separate participant, training provider, supervisor/RHU support and local health board authority. 3. Briefly distinguish accreditation, section-4 benefit registration and CSC eligibility on status-and-other-processes. No automatic benefit, appointment or professional licence is inferred. 4. Model explain-and-verify. 5. Pairs practise both fictional cards and give feedback. 6. Use legal-basis-check, explain all rationales, then repeat the explanation with fewer prompts. The optional video reinforces learning and is not a completion requirement.

## [expected-answers] Worked example and expected answers

Demi: “RA 7883 connects training under an accredited organization, voluntary community primary health care, and local health board accreditation under DOH guidelines. I will ask the RHU for the right contact to verify my training record and proof of board accreditation.” If a learner says the midwife accredits, acknowledge that the midwife may support the process; return to the statutory board authority. If the contact is unknown, identifying it is a valid first step. If asked about an allowance, record the question for the benefits lesson and verify the applicable conditions rather than promising payment.

## [misconception] Common misconception

“I have helped for years, so experience itself proves accreditation.” Respect the service while explaining the legal elements and evidence. “Accreditation guarantees all benefits and a job.” Return to section 4, individual benefit conditions and separate CSC requirements. This lesson does not decide a participant’s actual status or define universal clinical permission. Do not replace uncertainty with a made-up credential.

## [practice] Practice activity

Card A: Demi has attended a training session and was invited to help; her accreditation evidence is unknown. Explain the three elements, name the local health board and ask one specific verification question through the RHU or locally confirmed contact. Card B: an acquaintance says long service proves accreditation and guarantees an allowance and appointment. Correct that assumption and choose a verification step without promising results. Each partner explains both cases, receives one strength and one correction, swaps roles and repeats. Use fictional records only; no application submission, private records or clinical task.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

Choice 1 is correct: it connects training, voluntary community primary health care and local health board accreditation under DOH guidelines, then identifies the contact and evidence to verify. Choice 2 assumes experience and an invitation prove accreditation. Choice 3 promises an allowance and government appointment before checking distinct registration and eligibility conditions. All three rationales appear in both Read and Slides feedback. Ask for an explanation, not just the Act number.

## [observe] What to observe

One indicator at objective_index 0 groups three observable actions on both cards: explain the connected legal elements; identify the local health board as authority while distinguishing support; choose a reasonable contact/evidence verification step without unsupported status or benefit claims. Kaya na: all three without prompts. Kailangan practice: sound direction but incomplete explanation or contact/evidence needs prompts. Hindi pa: experience alone is treated as accreditation, a benefit or appointment is promised, or the legal basis/authority and verification are missing. Record observed practice separately from online completion/check correctness.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

Sentence frame: “RA 7883 connects ___, ___ and accreditation by ___. To verify the evidence I will ask ___ about ___.” Model once, let the learner repeat in their preferred language, then fade prompts. Feedback: name the missing element, point back to section-2 or roles-and-authority, and repeat Card A and Card B. Re-observe after feedback; a correct click alone cannot establish competence. Confirm unknown local contacts through the health team.
