## [purpose] Purpose

Apply the one objective, index 0: explain and demonstrate constructive follow-up. In fictional practice, separate facts from guesses, ask a specific question through the appropriate contact, listen and clarify, and choose a bounded next step. Self-management and reflection support these actions; they are not personality judgments.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

Authored facilitated share, 45 minutes: opening 5; question demonstration 5; Card A/B/C analysis 5; rotating role-play 15; observer feedback and re-practice 10; application check and reflection 5. Total 45. The optional 30-minute form-and-follow-up activity fits inside the final 30 minutes; it adds no compulsory time. Retain the module’s 180 minutes: 15 + four 30-minute parts + 45. The 45 minutes is not a separate TESDA follow-up minimum. Authored independent study estimate: 10–12 minutes, based on six screens with 324.31 seconds of English or 347.27 seconds of Filipino encoded narration, plus about 4–6 minutes for reading/check/reflection; optional stories and replay are additional. Use six screens, fictional cards, blank notebook and pens; no private documents.

## [prepare] Prepare

Read both languages in Read/Slides and the source audit. Confirm the local contact and accessible channel with the health team; mark unknowns and invent no office or deadline. Prepare three learner cards and three staff cards below. Require no disclosure of accreditation, benefits or personal distress. The packaged activity is adapted to follow-up only: the CSC website references CS Form 101-H revised Dec.2011, but download bytes are not yet verified; this package performs no official-form demonstration or submission.

## [opening] Opening

For 5 minutes, show fictional Demi: she provided an application but has no confirmed update. Ask what is known and unconfirmed. Acknowledge worry without blaming silence. Learners need not share personal delays. They may answer on a card, in their preferred language or with support.

## [steps] Steps

1. Opening and known/unknown facts (5 minutes).
2. Model pausing if helpful, purpose and the section-6 question (5). Show respectful firmness without a guarantee.
3. Compare Cards A/B/C (5).
4. Three role-play rounds: learner, contact and observer; 5 minutes each including brief clarification (15).
5. Observer feedback, reflection and re-practice using a different staff card (10).
6. Application check, every rationale and one realistic next-contact note (5). Optional video is unnecessary for online completion.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

Known: a fictional application was provided. Unknown: receipt, completeness, status, cause, responsibility and date. Card A needs a named application and purpose; a vague question is not a moral failure. Card B has a clear purpose and verification question. Card C guesses a lost document and requirement; ask what needs checking first. If a learner expresses frustration, acknowledge it and help form the question. If the contact is unknown, record that uncertainty and confirm with the health team; do not invent it.

## [misconception] Common misconception

“A long wait means approval.” Time alone proves no decision. “A document must be lost.” The cause remains unknown until confirmed. “You must show no emotion to pass.” Accent, feelings, a quiet personality and assertiveness are not skill scores. Observe the specific question, facts/unknowns, clarification and bounded next step. Calmness guarantees neither a response nor a benefit.

## [practice] Practice activity

Learner cards: A vague question; B specific verification question; C a guessed lost document (full text on screen 3). Staff card 1: “Still under review. No date yet.” Staff card 2: “An item needs verification; let us clarify which item and how.” Staff card 3: “I cannot confirm who is responsible.” These are fictional responses only. Rotate through three rounds. Learner: purpose, facts/unknowns, specific question, repeat the response, clarify the next contact. Observer: record what was actually said and one suggestion. Re-practice after feedback. Worksheet: fictional purpose / confirmed response / remaining question / agreed next contact. Use no real ID or document.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

One three-choice check appears on the final Read and Slide screen. First: correct, it verifies receipt/status, listens and clarifies the next step with the appropriate contact. Second: a long wait does not establish automatic approval. Third: it guesses a lost document and requirement; resubmitting everything first is unsupported. Explain every rationale; accept equivalent wording. An online correct answer is separate from observed skill.

## [observe] What to observe

Use one objective_index 0 indicator. Kaya na: independently distinguishes facts/unknowns, asks specifically, repeats/clarifies the response and identifies a bounded next step on two staff cards. Kailangan practice: sound direction but a prompt is needed for one action. Hindi pa: still guesses cause/approval or cannot form a specific question/clarification after support; model and repeat. Accent, nervousness, anger and using a written question do not establish incompetence. Do not score an administrative outcome.

## [support] If more support is needed

Offer a four-part cue card: facts and unknowns / question / understood response and clarification / next contact. Learners may read, write, listen to or dictate the question. Pair with a supportive partner, allow an observer round first and model staff card 1. Ask about preferred language and accessible method. Fade prompts in the second practice and re-observe. Require no real delay story or personal distress disclosure.
