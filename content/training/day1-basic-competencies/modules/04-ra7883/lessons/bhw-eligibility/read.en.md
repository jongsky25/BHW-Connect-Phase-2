## [section-4] BHW Eligibility (BHWE) in the Civil Service

There is a separate qualification for BHW Eligibility under the Civil Service Commission. Four conditions are required: accredited by the local health board; completed a minimum of two (2) years of college; actively and voluntarily serving continuously for five (5) years or more; and having a satisfactory service record as an accredited BHW in the community.

This is not the same thing as general accreditation — it asks for more, and it is for BHWs who want official eligibility in the civil service.
