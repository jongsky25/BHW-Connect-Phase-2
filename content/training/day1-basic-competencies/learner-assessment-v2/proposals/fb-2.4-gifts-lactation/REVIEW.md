# FB-2.4 — gifts, conflicts and lactation facilities

**Draft for bilingual, policy and assessment review.** No content approval, import or activation is claimed.

Future shared forms only. The retained legacy question keys and all historical attempts remain unchanged. Gift examples are limited to inducements for infant-formula promotion; pharmaceutical promotion needs its own source review.

This draft is based on the approved CSC v3 artifact. Its live activation is verified separately; authoring does not change enrollment assignment. T08 covers infant-formula promotional inducements. T09 covers a suitable lactation facility. It avoids a contrastive question combining different laws.

## pretest T08

Proposed identity: `f900e633-6cb4-5334-a801-d5f36bc7e41c`, version 2.

### en

**Current stem**

A company offers free infant formula samples during a health-station teaching session. What should the BHW do?

**Proposed stem**

An infant-formula company offers BHW Mia a gift if she promotes its formula during a health-station session. What should Mia do?

- **o2:** Accept the gift but keep the company name out of her spoken advice.
  Explanation: Hiding the company name does not remove the inducement or make the promotion permissible.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Decline the gift and promotion, and bring the offer to the responsible local channel.
- **o3:** Accept because a free gift cannot affect advice given at the health station.
  Explanation: A gift can create actual or apparent influence; being free is not permission.

**Rationale:**

A gift or benefit tied to promotion can influence, or appear to influence, health advice and services. The Milk Code also restricts inducements, infant-formula promotion and sample distribution in the health-care setting. Declining this promotional offer protects independent advice; report it through the responsible local channel.


### fil

**Current stem**

Nag-alok ang kompanya ng libreng sample ng infant formula sa pagtuturo sa health station. Ano ang gagawin ng BHW?

**Proposed stem**

Nag-alok ang kompanya ng infant formula ng regalo kay BHW Mia kapalit ng pag-promote nito sa isang session sa health station. Ano ang dapat gawin ni Mia?

- **o2:** Tanggapin ang regalo pero huwag banggitin ang pangalan ng kompanya sa kaniyang payo.
  Explanation: Hindi nawawala ang inducement o nagiging pinahihintulutan ang promotion dahil itinago ang pangalan ng kompanya.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Tanggihan ang regalo at promotion, at ipaalam ang alok sa kinauukulang lokal na contact.
- **o3:** Tanggapin dahil hindi makaaapekto ang libreng regalo sa payo sa health station.
  Explanation: Maaaring magdulot ng aktuwal o nakikitang impluwensiya ang regalo; hindi pahintulot ang pagiging libre.

**Rationale:**

Maaaring maimpluwensiyahan, o magmukhang naimpluwensiyahan, ng regalo o benepisyong kapalit ng promotion ang payo at serbisyong pangkalusugan. May mga pagbabawal din ang Milk Code sa inducement, promotion ng infant formula at pamamahagi ng sample sa health-care setting. Tanggihan ang alok na ito upang mapangalagaan ang malayang payo, at ipaalam ito sa kinauukulang lokal na channel.

## pretest T09

Proposed identity: `e2d1ba8b-2c43-55a2-879d-42f4d38df4c0`, version 2.

### en

**Current stem**

A parent asks for help with feeding but does not want to speak in front of neighbors. What should the BHW offer?

**Proposed stem**

Ella's team is considering places where mothers can breastfeed or express milk at a barangay health station. Which proposal should she bring to the responsible team?

- **o2:** Use the toilet because it already has water and a door for privacy.
  Explanation: The lactation station must not be located in a toilet.
- **o3:** Use the open waiting area without arranging privacy or checking facilities.
  Explanation: An open waiting area does not establish the required privacy or facilities.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Identify a private, clean, ventilated area outside the toilet and verify required facilities with the team.

**Rationale:**

RA 10028 requires lactation stations with the applicable facilities and excludes toilet locations. Privacy, cleanliness and ventilation matter, but they do not replace the full facility requirements. The BHW raises the proposal with the responsible team; she does not certify compliance, authorize construction or commit funds.


### fil

**Current stem**

Humihingi ng tulong ang magulang tungkol sa pagpapakain ngunit ayaw magsalita sa harap ng kapitbahay. Ano ang iaalok ng BHW?

**Proposed stem**

Pinag-iisipan ng pangkat ni Ella ang lugar sa barangay health station kung saan maaaring magpasuso o mag-express ng gatas ang mga ina. Aling mungkahi ang dapat niyang dalhin sa kinauukulang pangkat?

- **o2:** Gamitin ang palikuran dahil may tubig at pinto na para sa privacy.
  Explanation: Hindi dapat nasa palikuran ang lactation station.
- **o3:** Gamitin ang bukas na waiting area nang walang privacy o pagsusuri ng facilities.
  Explanation: Hindi natitiyak ng bukas na waiting area ang kailangang privacy o facilities.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Tukuyin ang pribado, malinis at maaliwalas na lugar sa labas ng palikuran at tiyakin sa pangkat ang kailangang facilities.

**Rationale:**

Itinatakda ng RA 10028 ang lactation station na may angkop na facilities at hindi nasa palikuran. Mahalaga ang privacy, kalinisan at bentilasyon, ngunit hindi kapalit ang mga ito ng buong facility requirements. Inihaharap ng BHW ang mungkahi sa kinauukulang pangkat; hindi siya ang nagse-certify ng compliance, nagpapahintulot ng construction o nangangako ng pondo.
