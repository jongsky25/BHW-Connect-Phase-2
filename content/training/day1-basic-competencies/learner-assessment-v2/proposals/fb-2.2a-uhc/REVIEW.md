## Proposed questions

### pretest — T05, version 2

Replaces `9b6aaa98-885e-43bd-a13f-ec37ffbca982` with `002c7bd2-b75f-5942-a326-d1d39820a74b`.

**English: current question**

A resident heard that UHC makes every service free at every clinic. What should the BHW explain?

**English: proposed question**

Lina is included in PhilHealth under UHC but has not chosen a YAKAP clinic. She asks whether this means any service she requests is free at any clinic. What should the BHW explain?

- **o2:** Being included in PhilHealth makes any requested service free at any clinic.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: NHIP inclusion alone does not establish the scope or availability of a particular service at a particular clinic.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** NHIP inclusion and choosing a YAKAP clinic are different. Help her confirm clinic choices, required steps and whether the requested service is covered.
- **o3:** Choosing a YAKAP clinic is what first makes a Filipino included in the NHIP.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: RA 11223 provides automatic NHIP inclusion; clinic selection is a separate process for accessing the relevant benefit.

**Proposed rationale:** Automatic NHIP inclusion does not establish clinic selection or coverage of every service everywhere. The BHW helps the resident check choices and the applicable benefit and steps with PhilHealth or the health team.

**Filipino: current question**

Narinig ng residente na libre ang bawat serbisyo sa bawat klinika dahil sa UHC. Ano ang dapat ipaliwanag ng BHW?

**Filipino: proposed question**

Kasama si Lina sa PhilHealth sa ilalim ng UHC, pero wala pa siyang napiling YAKAP clinic. Itinanong niya kung ibig sabihin nito ay libre ang anumang serbisyong hihingin niya sa kahit anong clinic. Ano ang dapat ipaliwanag ng BHW?

- **o2:** Dahil kasama siya sa PhilHealth, libre ang anumang serbisyong hihingin niya sa kahit anong clinic.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: Hindi sapat ang pagiging kasama sa NHIP upang matiyak ang sakop o availability ng partikular na serbisyo sa isang clinic.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Magkaiba ang pagiging kasama sa NHIP at pagpili ng YAKAP clinic. Tulungan siyang tiyakin ang mga pagpipiliang clinic, kailangang hakbang at kung sakop ang hinihinging serbisyo.
- **o3:** Ang pagpili ng YAKAP clinic ang unang dahilan upang maging kasama ang isang Pilipino sa NHIP.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: Itinatakda ng RA 11223 ang awtomatikong pagsama sa NHIP; hiwalay na proseso ang pagpili ng clinic para magamit ang kaugnay na benepisyo.

**Proposed rationale:** Hindi patunay ang awtomatikong pagsama sa NHIP na may napili nang clinic o sakop ang bawat serbisyo saanman. Tinutulungan ng BHW ang residente na alamin sa PhilHealth o health team ang mga pagpipilian, angkop na benepisyo at kailangang hakbang.

### posttest — T05, version 2

**English: current question**

A family asks whether automatic coverage means a requested service is available today without payment. What is the careful response?

**English: proposed question**

Ramon selected a YAKAP clinic through the Member Portal but has not completed his First Patient Encounter (FPE). He assumes selection makes every service free at any clinic. What should the BHW help him verify?

- **o3:** Only whether his name appears in the Member Portal; this proves all services are covered everywhere.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: A portal record or clinic selection does not prove every service is covered at every clinic.
- **o2:** Only his clinic-selection confirmation; selection already completes empanelment even without an FPE.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: Clinic selection is separate from the FPE and empanelment process. The selected clinic or PhilHealth should confirm the remaining steps.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** The FPE and empanelment steps with his chosen clinic, and whether the requested service is covered there.

**Proposed rationale:** Clinic selection alone does not complete FPE and empanelment. Confirm the current steps and service coverage with the chosen clinic or PhilHealth. This does not justify charging for covered YAKAP services that must be free in a public YAKAP clinic.

**Filipino: current question**

Nagtanong ang pamilya kung ang awtomatikong coverage ay nangangahulugang makukuha ngayon nang walang bayad ang hinihinging serbisyo. Ano ang maingat na tugon?

Pumili si Ramon ng YAKAP clinic sa Member Portal pero hindi pa niya nakukumpleto ang First Patient Encounter (FPE). Akala niya, dahil nakapili na siya, libre ang bawat serbisyo sa kahit anong clinic. Ano ang dapat tulungan ng BHW na tiyakin?

- **o3:** Kung nasa Member Portal lang ang pangalan niya; patunay na itong sakop ang lahat ng serbisyo saanman.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: Hindi patunay ang tala sa portal o pagpili ng clinic na sakop ang bawat serbisyo sa bawat clinic.
- **o2:** Ang kumpirmasyon lamang ng napiling clinic; kumpleto na ang empanelment dahil nakapili na siya kahit wala pang FPE.
  Reason this choice is incorrect: Hiwalay ang pagpili ng clinic sa proseso ng FPE at empanelment. Dapat tiyakin sa napiling clinic o PhilHealth ang mga hakbang na kailangan pa.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Ang mga hakbang sa FPE at empanelment sa napili niyang clinic, at kung sakop doon ang hinihinging serbisyo.

**Proposed rationale:** Hindi nakukumpleto ng pagpili lamang ng clinic ang FPE at empanelment. Tiyakin sa napiling clinic o PhilHealth ang kasalukuyang hakbang at sakop ng serbisyo. Hindi nito pinahihintulutan ang pagsingil sa mga sakop na serbisyong YAKAP na dapat libre sa pampublikong YAKAP clinic.

## Unscored provider terminology note

In RA 11223, a primary care provider can be a health worker certified in primary care under DOH requirements, or a health institution licensed and certified by the DOH. In YAKAP clinic selection, the resident chooses an accredited public or private primary care clinic. The legal term is broader than an RHU or a place alone; it does not automatically qualify every BHW as a certified primary care provider.

Sa RA 11223, ang primary care provider ay maaaring health worker na may sertipikasyon sa primary care ayon sa mga tuntunin ng DOH, o institusyong pangkalusugan na lisensiyado at sertipikado ng DOH. Sa pagpili ng YAKAP clinic, pumipili ang residente ng accredited na pampubliko o pribadong primary care clinic. Mas malawak ang legal na termino kaysa RHU o lugar lamang; hindi nito awtomatikong ginagawang sertipikadong primary care provider ang bawat BHW.

This note is for the reviewer packet. It is not inserted into a pretest, scored as an extra item or displayed to learners. Confirm the teaching location and wording before a separate lesson/content revision.
