## Paired T11 questions

### pretest

Proposed item: `9336265d-6948-57ee-82f1-097dffd61058`, version 2.

**English — current stem**

A BHW receives a recommendation for accreditation but a required document is missing. What does the recommendation mean?

**English — proposed stem**

In this example, the BHW Registration and Accreditation Committee recommended that the Local Health Board approve Ana's application. The board has not recorded a decision, and a required document is missing. What should Ana do next?

- **o2:** Use the committee recommendation as proof that her accreditation is complete.
  Explanation: A committee recommendation is not the Local Health Board decision.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Ask the proper local contact how to complete the document and verify the board decision.
- **o3:** Use a training certificate instead of the required document and board decision.
  Explanation: A training certificate does not replace the required evidence or the board decision.

**Proposed rationale:**

A recommendation is a proposal from the committee to the board. The committee assists with screening; the Local Health Board makes and records the accreditation decision. Ana should ask the locally confirmed RHU, health office or board contact about the missing document and official decision.

**Filipino — current stem**

May rekomendasyon ang BHW para sa accreditation ngunit kulang ang kinakailangang dokumento. Ano ang kahulugan ng rekomendasyon?

**Filipino — proposed stem**

Sa halimbawang ito, iminungkahi ng BHW Registration and Accreditation Committee sa Local Health Board na aprubahan ang aplikasyon ni Ana. Wala pang naitalang pasya ang board at kulang ang isang kailangang dokumento. Ano ang susunod na gagawin ni Ana?

- **o2:** Gamitin ang rekomendasyon ng committee bilang patunay na tapos na ang kaniyang accreditation.
  Explanation: Hindi pasya ng Local Health Board ang rekomendasyon ng committee.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Itanong sa tamang lokal na contact kung paano kukumpletuhin ang dokumento at titiyakin ang pasya ng board.
- **o3:** Gumamit ng training certificate bilang kapalit ng kailangang dokumento at pasya ng board.
  Explanation: Hindi kapalit ng kailangang ebidensiya o pasya ng board ang training certificate.

**Proposed rationale:**

Ang rekomendasyon ay mungkahi ng committee sa board. Tumutulong ang committee sa screening; ang Local Health Board ang nagpapasya at nagtatala ng pasya sa accreditation. Dapat magtanong si Ana sa kumpirmadong lokal na RHU, health office o contact ng board tungkol sa kulang na dokumento at opisyal na pasya.

### posttest

Proposed item: `c3eab24b-abce-50ab-b4b7-4aecc9cacb44`, version 2.

**English — current stem**

A BHW is invited to a recognition event while an accreditation decision is pending. What should be verified?

**English — proposed stem**

A local committee recommended that the Local Health Board approve Bea's BHW accreditation application. Bea then submitted a missing document. A colleague says she is now accredited. What should Bea do before making that claim?

- **o3:** Treat submission of the additional document as proof of completed accreditation.
  Explanation: Submitting a document does not by itself establish a board decision.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Verify the Local Health Board's recorded accreditation decision through the proper local contact.
- **o2:** Treat the committee recommendation as the final accreditation decision.
  Explanation: The committee recommendation does not replace the board decision.

**Proposed rationale:**

A recommendation and submission of a document do not establish completed accreditation. Bea should verify the official Local Health Board decision through the proper local contact. The BHW does not grant accreditation to herself or another worker.

**Filipino — current stem**

Inanyayahan ang BHW sa pagkilala habang hinihintay pa ang pasya sa accreditation. Ano ang dapat tiyakin?

**Filipino — proposed stem**

Iminungkahi ng lokal na committee na aprubahan ng Local Health Board ang aplikasyon ni Bea para sa BHW accreditation. Pagkatapos, isinumite ni Bea ang kulang na dokumento. Sinabi ng kasama na accredited na siya. Ano ang dapat gawin ni Bea bago sabihin iyon?

- **o3:** Ituring ang pagsumite ng dagdag na dokumento bilang patunay na tapos na ang accreditation.
  Explanation: Hindi patunay ng pasya ng board ang pagsumite lamang ng dokumento.
- **o1 — proposed correct answer:** Tiyakin sa tamang lokal na contact ang naitalang pasya ng Local Health Board sa accreditation.
- **o2:** Ituring ang rekomendasyon ng committee bilang pinal na pasya sa accreditation.
  Explanation: Hindi kapalit ng pasya ng board ang rekomendasyon ng committee.

**Proposed rationale:**

Hindi patunay ng natapos na accreditation ang rekomendasyon at pagsumite ng dokumento. Dapat tiyakin ni Bea sa tamang lokal na contact ang opisyal na pasya ng Local Health Board. Hindi ang BHW ang nagbibigay ng accreditation sa sarili o sa ibang worker.

## New unscored section — filing and certificate receipt

Insert after `bhwe-verification` and before the existing application check. It adds no assessment item. Existing qualification conditions and the application check are retained.

### en

Filing and receiving the certificate

CSC guidance permits filing in person, through an authorized representative, through a conduit CSC office (an intermediary office), or by mail, subject to its requirements. Check current instructions with the CSC office responsible for the barangay of service. Filing is not approval. If approved, the applicant must appear personally before CSC for issuance and acceptance of the Certificate of Eligibility; a representative cannot receive it for the applicant. Completing this lesson grants neither BHWE nor a government appointment.

**Takeaway:**

Filing is not approval; personal certificate receipt follows approval.

**Slide display:**

Filing: in person, authorized representative, conduit CSC office or mail, under CSC requirements.
Approval is separate.
If approved: the applicant personally receives the certificate.

Slide narration uses the Read copy above.

### fil

Pag-file at pagtanggap ng certificate

Ayon sa gabay ng CSC, maaaring mag-file nang personal, sa pamamagitan ng awtorisadong kinatawan, sa pamamagitan ng conduit CSC office (tanggapang tagapamagitan), o sa koreo, alinsunod sa mga kinakailangan nito. Tiyakin ang kasalukuyang gabay sa CSC office na may sakop sa barangay ng serbisyo. Hindi approval ang pag-file. Kung aprubado, kailangang personal na humarap ang aplikante sa CSC para sa pag-isyu at pagtanggap ng Certificate of Eligibility; hindi ito maaaring tanggapin ng kinatawan para sa aplikante. Hindi nagbibigay ng BHWE o government appointment ang pagtatapos ng araling ito.

**Takeaway:**

Hindi approval ang pag-file; personal na pagtanggap ng certificate ang kasunod kung aprubado.

**Slide display:**

Pag-file: personal, awtorisadong kinatawan, conduit CSC office o koreo, ayon sa CSC requirements.
Hiwalay ang approval.
Kung aprubado: aplikante ang personal na tatanggap ng certificate.

Slide narration uses the Read copy above.
