## [purpose] Purpose

From conflicting accounts, produce a record separating reports, observations and unconfirmed details, with a way to verify the gap.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

90 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 8 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare three labeled boxes and the conflicting accounts. Use a sample form only.

## [opening] Opening

The card is missing and Liza’s account differs from her neighbor’s. Which record fits?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The second record identifies the source and the information gap. The first prioritizes a judgment; the third expands an unverified claim.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Records a guess or the neighbor’s opinion as established fact. Clear sources and explicit gaps prevent a guess becoming an official record.

## [practice] Practice activity

Draw three boxes: “Reported,” “Observed,” and “Unconfirmed.” Sort Liza’s details. Add: “Verify the date against the authorized record with the midwife.” Ask a partner to check for guesses or diagnoses. Alone, underline each detail’s source. In your next record, mark what remains unconfirmed.

## [answer-key] Answer key

2. “Liza reports a canceled schedule; date to be verified in the record.”

The second record identifies the source and the information gap. The first prioritizes a judgment; the third expands an unverified claim.

## [observe] Observe

Correctly separates all three kinds of detail, adds no diagnosis or judgment, and names a specific record or person for verification.

Ready: Correctly separates all three kinds of detail, adds no diagnosis or judgment, and names a specific record or person for verification. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Distinguishes reports and observations but gives no verification plan; ask for the relevant record or health team member.

Not yet: Records a guess or the neighbor’s opinion as established fact.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Distinguishes reports and observations but gives no verification plan; ask for the relevant record or health team member. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
