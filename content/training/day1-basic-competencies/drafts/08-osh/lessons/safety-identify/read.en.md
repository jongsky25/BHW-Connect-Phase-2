## [ana] Before Ana starts work

Ana, 33, will assist at the health station before household visits. The entrance floor is wet, the sharps box is nearly full and a hot walk lies ahead. “I will be careful” is not enough. She identifies each hazard, who might be harmed and the control needed. She checks the local protocol and confirms the reporting contact and tasks within her training with the midwife.

## [inspect] Inspect a work area

A fictional station has a wet floor, a blocked exit and no soap at the sink. For each, name the hazard, possible harm, immediate control and reporting contact. First keep people away from danger and seek the right assistance. A checklist that only says “be careful” is incomplete. Check that the problem is corrected before removing the warning.

## [practice] Try it, then repeat

Draw five boxes: needlestick injury; infection exposure; body discomfort; stress from excessive work; work-related accidents. Give a station or home-visit example for each. Choose one and write the harm, control and contact. In pairs, replace a vague “be careful” with a specific action. Alone, use the checklist. Before the next duty, perform a short hazard check.

## [check] Choose the next step

Choose an answer and explain why before viewing the feedback. Then compare it with your own practice attempt.
