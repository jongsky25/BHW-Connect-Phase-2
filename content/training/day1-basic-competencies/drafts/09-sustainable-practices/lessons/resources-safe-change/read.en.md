## [utilities] Water and electricity with safeguards

Elena, 38, wants to reduce waste at the BHS while preserving safe, correct services. She needs to check each proposal with the team.

Report the leaking tap and follow up on its repair. Switch off unnecessary lights or equipment according to facility instructions. Do not turn off cold-chain equipment, required ventilation or other critical systems simply to lower a bill. Do not reduce handwashing water to save money. Target leaks and unnecessary consumption while preserving safe care.

## [materials] Reduce unnecessary use, preserve safety

For health teaching, a durable reusable visual can replace repeated disposable decorations. Follow BHS policy on unnecessary single-use plastics and proper segregation. Clinical single-use items are different: needles, lancets and other single-use items must not be made reusable. When uncertain, ask the supervisor rather than inventing cleaning or reuse methods.

## [workflow] Improve the flow, not just the speed

Before printing, Elena checks the date and approved version of the handout. She assembles session materials together to avoid repeated trips to the cabinet. Supply delivery can be combined with a planned safe route, but urgent referral must not be delayed to save travel. Measure quality service delivered and time saved; the number of rushed clients is not a measure of good work.

## [worked-example] Check why waste occurs

In Elena’s example, rework may come from an outdated drawer copy or an incorrect choice before printing. Inspect sample incorrect printouts, compare them with the approved master and ask the person who printed them. Record which explanation the evidence supports. Confirm with the supervisor using local document-control procedures before changing the routine.

## [practice] Try it, then repeat

Compare “repair the leak” with “reduce handwashing.” List savings, potential harm and safeguards. Choose and explain to a partner. Repeat with a reusable visual versus reusing a clinical single-use item. Alone, write the rejected option and reason. Before trying a workplace change, confirm policy and safety checks with the supervisor.
