## [purpose] Purpose

Propose a small trial with an owner and review date, and compare resource use at comparable workloads alongside service quality.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

60 minutes of facilitated practice within the subchapter's 3 hours; short Read completion does not replace that training. Use the scenario and checklist below, paper and a pen. No real personal records in practice.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare the two fictional 100-page tallies and a blank trial card. Confirm authority for the approved version and trial.

## [opening] Opening

Paper use halved because half the sessions were canceled. What can be concluded?

## [steps] Steps

Read the situation. Model one attempt against the Observe checklist. Run the practice in pairs or triads. Give specific feedback and repeat the part needing improvement. Ask the check before revealing the explanation.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers

The first response accounts for workload and quality. Total bills or paper can fall because service was lost, not because the process improved.

## [misconception] Common misconception

Declares lower totals a success despite reduced or canceled services. Lower consumption is a success only when correct, safe service is still delivered.

## [practice] Practice activity

Fill a trial card: problem, change, owner, baseline, quality measure, review date. Use 20/100 and 5/100 for a fair comparison. In pairs, one colleague has no internet; explain the controlled paper master and handover. Alone, write the replacement colleague’s routine. Ask the team to review the trial before starting and set a review date.

## [answer-key] Answer key

1. This does not yet show improvement; compare use per session and services delivered.

The first response accounts for workload and quality. Total bills or paper can fall because service was lost, not because the process improved.

## [observe] Observe

Specifies owner, baseline, quality measure and review date; correctly compares 20% with 5%; accounts for workload and handover.

Ready: Specifies owner, baseline, quality measure and review date; correctly compares 20% with 5%; accounts for workload and handover. Completes this without prompting.

Needs practice: Completes the plan but compares raw totals only; prompt for per-session or per-100-page measures.

Not yet: Declares lower totals a success despite reduced or canceled services.

Mark each criterion done / prompted / not yet and record the exact words or action. Peer feedback is practice; staff observations cover only performances actually observed. One demonstration does not establish that everyone is ready.

## [support] Support

Completes the plan but compares raw totals only; prompt for per-session or per-100-page measures. Allow spoken or written responses and reading assistance. Use the participant’s preferred language while keeping the same criteria.
