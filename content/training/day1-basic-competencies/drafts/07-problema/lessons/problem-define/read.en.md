## [missed-visits] Three visits, the same barrier

BHW Nestor, 41, revisits three households that missed an agreed health-center visit. Instead of labeling them “uncooperative,” he records dates, missed visits and the residents’ stated reasons. These three households are a training example, not data from your barangay. The problem is inability to reach a service on the agreed day; its causes still need investigation before choosing a solution.

## [worked-example] Separate the problem from a guess

“Three households missed visits” describes the event in the example. “They did not want to go” guesses at motivation. “We need a poster” proposes a solution. Before choosing a solution, find out what each household reports and which records can clarify the situation.

## [practice] Try it, then repeat

Write: “Three households in the example missed agreed visits; they report barriers to attending. Dates and each barrier still need clarification.” Compare with “The families lack discipline.” In pairs, underline the event, source and gap. Alone, use the same checklist. In your next problem report, seek the affected residents’ perspectives too.
