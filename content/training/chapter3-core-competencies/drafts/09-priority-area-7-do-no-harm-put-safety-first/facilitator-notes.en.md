## [purpose] Purpose

Priority area 7: Do No Harm, Put Safety First

## [time] Time

12 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- Violence and safe referral: Avoids blaming or unsafe disclosure; records only authorized facts and escalates per protocol.
- Older-person safety and benefits: Distinguishes confirmed benefits from locally unverified requirements.
- Fireworks injury prevention: States an actionable prevention step and referral route.
- Road safety: Identifies the risk, safer behavior, and relevant partner.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
