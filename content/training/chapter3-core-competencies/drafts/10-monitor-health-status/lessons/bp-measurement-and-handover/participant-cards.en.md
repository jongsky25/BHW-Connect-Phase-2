# BP measurement and handover

The midwife asks BHW Amira to measure a training partner's BP. She has a device and a blank record. A supervisor watches while she follows the current approved procedure.

Task: Check the device and suitable cuff. Ask permission and prepare the person using the approved checklist. Measure and repeat if the protocol requires it. Record the actual reading, units, time and context. Tell the midwife the result and any concern.
