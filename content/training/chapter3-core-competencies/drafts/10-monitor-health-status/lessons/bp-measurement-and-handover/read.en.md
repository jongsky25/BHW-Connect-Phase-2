## [scene] The situation

The midwife asks BHW Amira to measure a training partner's BP. She has a device and a blank record. A supervisor watches while she follows the current approved procedure.

## [action] What to do and say

Check the device and suitable cuff. Ask permission and prepare the person using the approved checklist. Measure and repeat if the protocol requires it. Record the actual reading, units, time and context. Tell the midwife the result and any concern.

## [check-start] Choose a response

Choose an answer first. Then read the reason.

## [example] Worked example

Amira checks the equipment before starting. She explains the procedure and follows the checklist. She records the number she actually reads instead of guessing. She tells the midwife the result without making her own diagnosis.

## [scope] Scope and safety

Do not use a faulty device or unsuitable cuff. Do not diagnose, prescribe or assure someone that one reading is safe. Use the current local protocol for urgent referral.

## [check-transfer] Apply the task

Choose the action with an observable result.

## [next-step] Use this at work

Ask the midwife to observe a full return demonstration. Have the record checked and repeat any step needing correction before measuring during real service.
