## [purpose] Purpose

Monitor health status

## [time] Time

72 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- BP measurement and handover: Completed each critical step on the approved BP checklist; recorded the actual reading and handed it to the midwife under the protocol.
- Pulse: Finds appropriate site, measures for required interval, records units/time and flags concern.
- Respiratory rate and temperature: Uses the reviewed method, avoids contaminating equipment, records value/units and escalates concern.
- Height/length and weight: Selects correct equipment, positions safely, reads and records units accurately.
- Growth chart use: Uses correct chart/axis/date and avoids independent diagnosis.
- MUAC: Finds the approved site, avoids tape error, records value and follows current threshold guidance.
- BMI and age-appropriate interpretation: Selects adult BMI versus child/adolescent chart correctly, shows units and refers for interpretation where required.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
