## [purpose] Purpose

Priority area 6: Practice Safe Sex

## [time] Time

16 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- Adolescent SRH and STI: Does not block access, shame, or disclose private information.
- Consent and safer choices: Centers voluntary decision-making and names a source of further help.
- Family planning: Explains choice without selecting or prescribing a method for the resident.
- Prenatal identification and support: Communicates urgency where indicated and refers through the approved pathway.
- Birth and immediate care: Avoids representing unsupervised clinical procedures as BHW tasks.
- Postnatal follow-up: Uses reviewed timing and referral rules, confirms local service availability.
- Newborn health: Gives a current reviewed message and recognizes a referral trigger.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
