## [purpose] Purpose

Household profiling and target lists

## [time] Time

96 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- Record purposes and privacy: Uses the right record for the purpose and protects identifying data.
- Household profile and updates: Captures required fields, identifies changed information and corrects without inventing data.
- WRA and pregnancy lists: Transfers approved fields accurately, avoids duplicate/missing entries and uses private handover.
- Child and oral-health lists: Classifies age group correctly under current forms and flags due follow-up.
- Adult and senior lists: Applies approved age grouping and records only authorized information.
- Environmental-health list: Completes water, sanitation, waste and facility fields consistently, then reports the issue.
- Reconcile and submit records: Finds all critical discrepancies, marks unresolved facts, and follows the approved storage/submission route.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
