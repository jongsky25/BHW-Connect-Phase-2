# Household profiling and target lists

- Choose ITR, household profile, or target list and identify who maintains it.
- Complete and update the locally approved profile.
- Identify eligible entries for WRA and pregnancy/postpartum lists and hand them to the midwife.
- Update newborn, infant, child/adolescent and oral-health target lists.
- Update NCD and older-person lists and identify a reviewed follow-up service.
- Complete the four-part local sanitation record and hand over a concern.
- Find missing/contradictory entries, correct from source, and prepare a confidential supervisor handover.
