## [purpose] Purpose

Priority area 2: Be Clean, Live Sustainably

## [time] Time

32 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- Environmental health: Describes the observed condition, a feasible reviewed action, and who should handle it.
- Tuberculosis: Uses non-stigmatizing language, refers through the verified route, and records only authorized facts.
- Dengue: Identifies breeding-site action and flags concerning illness per reviewed protocol.
- Rabies: Selects the approved pathway and avoids waiting for animal symptoms or improvised treatment.
- Influenza: Gives a current message and checks the resident's plan.
- Hepatitis A, B, and C: Corrects the misconception and identifies where to confirm testing/vaccination or care.
- Leptospirosis: Distinguishes prevention from clinical prescribing and refers appropriately.
- COVID-19: Uses the latest supplied advisory rather than memorized 2022 isolation or treatment details.
- Typhoid: Gives a correct prevention action and identifies care for concerning illness.
- Malaria: States that local relevance must be confirmed and refers a suspected case.
- Leprosy: Protects privacy and connects the person to the reviewed service.
- Filariasis and schistosomiasis: Keeps the two conditions distinct and checks local program applicability.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
