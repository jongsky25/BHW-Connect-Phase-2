## [purpose] Purpose

BHS equipment, supplies, and records

## [time] Time

72 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- BHS services and standards: Reports a specific gap without promising a service or practicing beyond role.
- Equipment readiness: Records item, condition, discrepancy and escalation; distinguishes standard from local stock.
- Inventory: Counts accurately, records date/condition/owner, and follows reconciliation process.
- BP apparatus care: Completes approved care steps safely and does not attempt unauthorized repair.
- Medicine supplies and stock cards: Balances counts, applies approved stock rotation, and reports damaged/expired stock.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
