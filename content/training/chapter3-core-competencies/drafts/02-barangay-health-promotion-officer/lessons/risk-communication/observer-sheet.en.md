# Risk communication

Separates confirmed facts from unknowns, gives a feasible action, and names where to check updates.

- Competent: Separates confirmed facts from unknowns, gives a feasible action, and names where to check updates.
- Needs practice: Completes the main step but needs a prompt or correction on follow-up.
- Not yet: Present an unconfirmed report as certain to make people act.
