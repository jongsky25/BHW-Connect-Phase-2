## [purpose] Purpose

Barangay health promotion officer

## [time] Time

5 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- HEPO role and determinants: Distinguishes the barrier from a diagnosis and proposes an achievable action with partners.
- Respectful interpersonal communication: Uses an open question, reflects the concern, respects context and privacy, and invites correction.
- Audience and message choice: States the audience, barrier, reviewed message, and why the channel fits.
- Risk communication: Separates confirmed facts from unknowns, gives a feasible action, and names where to check updates.
- Social listening and feedback: Records a theme and source context, escalates accurately, and explains how the community will hear the response.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
