## [purpose] Purpose

Priority area 1: Move More, Eat Right

## [time] Time

40 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- Nutrition and activity conversation: Gives an accurate message and checks the resident's own next step.
- Breastfeeding support: Uses reviewed steps, observes respectfully, and refers when the issue exceeds BHW scope.
- Complementary feeding: Uses the approved age guide, checks comprehension, and avoids unverified quantities.
- Garantisadong Pambata: Explains the action and referral site without independently prescribing supplements or medicine.
- Pinggang Pinoy and oral health: Uses the correct reviewed visual and demonstrates/observes the key action.
- Hypertension: Avoids diagnosis from one reading, communicates concern and follow-up.
- Diabetes: Identifies the appropriate service and role boundary; does not make an unsupported treatment claim.
- Cancer prevention and screening: Selects a relevant reviewed message, avoids unsupported age/interval claims, and links to a provider.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
