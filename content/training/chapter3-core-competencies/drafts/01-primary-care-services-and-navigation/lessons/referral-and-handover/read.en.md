## [scene] The situation

Lira asks whether the BHS can see her mother. The schedule on hand is old. BHW Marga needs to confirm the service before giving directions.

## [action] What to do and say

Ask what Lira's mother needs and whether there is an urgent concern. Use the current local directory. If service availability is uncertain, confirm with the midwife or health centre. Ask permission before sharing details.

## [check-start] Choose a response

Choose an answer first. Then read the reason.

## [example] Worked example

Marga says, “I will confirm the schedule first. May I tell the midwife what your mother needs?” With Lira's permission, Marga shares only the necessary facts. She returns with a confirmed contact and follow-up time.

## [scope] Scope and safety

Do not promise that a service is open or free before confirming it. Do not share private details with others. If an urgent concern is present, use the local emergency pathway.
