## [purpose] Purpose

Primary care services and navigation

## [time] Time

3 hours are allocated to facilitated practice in this plan. This does not equal digital study time.

## [prepare] Prepare

Prepare fictional cases, observer sheets and the current approved local guide. Confirm contacts, schedules and scope before the session.

## [run] Run

For each lesson, give the case first, ask for a decision, show the example, then observe the return demonstration or explanation.

## [observe] Observe

- Services by facility: Names a supported destination and avoids promising an unverified service.
- Life-stage needs: States the need, a plausible service entry point, and a fact requiring confirmation.
- Referral and handover: Checked the need, confirmed the service, asked permission, gave a concrete next step and set a follow-up.

## [follow-up] Follow-up

Give each learner specific feedback and arrange a retry for the step needing correction. Do not use real client records in practice.

## [review] Review status

Draft for staging and review. Clinical and local guidance must be confirmed before publication.
