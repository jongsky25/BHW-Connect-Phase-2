## [purpose] Purpose

In a dry simulation, the BHW explains ORS from the packet label, safe water and continued breastfeeding, and names a referral trigger.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **30 minutes** face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 480-minute subchapter plan): opening 3, model 5, practice 15, feedback 4, self-check 3. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- 2.4.4 participant cards, ORS worksheet and observation sheet; job aid; one empty labelled ORS sachet per triad (dry, no mixing).

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson and the job aid section *Respond to diarrhea and dehydration concerns*.
- Confirm with the midwife or RHU which ORS packets are used locally and the referral route for a child with diarrhea.
- Scope: no water, mixing, homemade recipe, amounts from memory or medicines. Do not force fluids when swallowing is unsafe.

## [opening] Opening

Hold up the empty sachet: *"Baby Mina has loose stools and is still breastfeeding. Her caregiver holds this. Where do the mixing directions come from?"*

## [steps] Steps

1. **The situation (Read and first slide).** *"What does Mina's family already have that helps?"*
2. **What to do and say (matching slide).** *"Which four things do you ask about?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** Read the sachet aloud, pointing: *"This packet says how much safe water and how to mix. Keep breastfeeding. Any danger sign, we go right away."* *"Did I quote any amount from memory?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"A neighbor shares a homemade mix. What do you say?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — pairs first.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"Which packet will you read with the next family?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"One glass of water per sachet."** *"Is that true for every packet? Let us read this label."*
- **"Stop breastfeeding while she has diarrhea."** *"What does the lesson say about breastfeeding when the child can feed?"*
- **"Make her drink even if she vomits everything."** *"What does the lesson say when swallowing is unsafe?"*
- **"Read the label, keep breastfeeding, refer for a danger sign."** Confirm.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Everyone knows the ORS recipe; you don't need the packet."** Many of us learned a homemade recipe and want to share it. But packets differ, and one recipe for all ages can be wrong. Correct the case: *"Whose directions are printed here?"*

## [practice] Practice activity

ORS education station (*Sick-child case stations*), dry. Triads: learner as BHW, partner as caregiver, observer. Two rounds with one strength and one retry action between, then rotate. The observer fills the worksheet from what was actually said. Fictional cases only. No sachet: draw a label copied from a local packet. Solo: do the worksheet; staff observe later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

1. **"What comes first?"** **"Read the ORS packet."** — safety and timely help. "Use the shortcut…": one recipe or dose for all, or forced fluids, can cause harm. "Wait without notifying…": delays assessment.
2. **"What should the practice show?"** **"Continue breastfeeding when safe."** — the task and appropriate referral. "Diagnose from the story…": no grounds for independent diagnosis. "Repeat the shortcut…": unsafe.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Use the packet label in a dry simulation, explain safe water and continued feeding, and identify a referral trigger.

- **Kaya na:** Completes the task without prompting: Use the packet label in a dry simulation, explain safe water and continued feeding, and identify a referral trigger.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Performs the response but needs a prompt about referral or scope.
- **Hindi pa:** Uses the shortcut or fails to activate help: Do not give a fixed homemade recipe or fluid dose across all ages and packet sizes. Do not force oral fluids when swallowing is unsafe.

Watch whether the learner reads the label before giving any amount. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** replay only the closing referral sentence; retry with a different sachet.
- **Hindi pa:** read one label line by line together; retry with a Kaya na partner.
- Re-observe this session or next. Peer feedback is practice, not a rating.
