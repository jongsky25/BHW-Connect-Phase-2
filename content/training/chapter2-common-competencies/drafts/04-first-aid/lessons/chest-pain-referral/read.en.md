## [scene] The situation

An adult has new chest pressure and looks pale. A neighbor offers some of her own tablets.

## [action] What to do and say

Activate urgent medical assessment and keep the person at rest while following responder directions. Report onset, symptoms and known medicines or allergies. Medication decisions follow the current local protocol and qualified advice.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Lea helps the adult rest and calls the urgent contact at once. She reports when the pressure began, the symptoms she sees, and any medicines or allergies the person knows of. When the neighbor offers her tablets, Lea says, "Thank you, but medicine follows local protocol and qualified advice." She follows the responders' directions.

## [scope] Scope and support

Do not use the source manual’s repeated aspirin schedule, give another person’s tablets, or delay the call to find medication.
