# 2.4.16 — Arrange urgent chest-pain referral

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

An adult has new chest pressure and looks pale. A neighbor offers some of her own tablets.

## Learner card

You are the BHW. Call the urgent contact and report onset, symptoms and known medicines or allergies. Decline the neighbor's tablets kindly and keep the person at rest.

## Partner card

Play the neighbor who wants to help. Hold out your own tablets and say: "Here, give him one of mine." If the learner declines, ask once: "Shouldn't we find aspirin first?" Accept a clear answer. Use a prop or nothing, never real tablets, and do not name medicines or doses.

## Observer card

Listen for the time of onset in the call and a kind refusal of the tablets. Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
