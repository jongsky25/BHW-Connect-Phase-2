# 2.4.3 — Respond to cough and breathing concerns

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Four-year-old Aya coughs and appears to struggle for breath. The family asks whether she can wait until tomorrow.

## Learner card

You are the BHW. Describe Aya's breathing in plain words and ask about danger signs. When the family asks to wait, do not promise it can wait. Use the confirmed route for prompt assessment and say what you will report.

## Partner card

Play Aya's family member. Say Aya coughs and seems to struggle for breath. Ask twice: "Can it wait until tomorrow?" Answer only what the BHW asks. Do not add new signs, numbers or clinical details.

## Observer card

Record the actual words and actions: the breathing words, the danger-sign questions and the route used. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
