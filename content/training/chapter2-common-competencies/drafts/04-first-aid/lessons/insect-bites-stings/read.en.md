## [scene] The situation

After a bee sting, an adult develops widespread hives and says breathing is difficult.

## [action] What to do and say

Recognize possible severe allergic reaction and activate emergency help immediately. Follow the person's prescribed emergency plan and trained/local protocol. Continue to observe breathing while help is coming.

Do not treat breathing difficulty as a routine local sting, delay referral for home remedies, or administer a drug outside training and authorization.
