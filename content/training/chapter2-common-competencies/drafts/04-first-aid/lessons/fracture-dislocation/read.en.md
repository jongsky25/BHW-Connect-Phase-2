## [scene] The situation

A cyclist fell and now has a painful, deformed forearm. She can speak and wants the arm pulled straight.

## [action] What to do and say

Avoid unnecessary movement. Support the injured part in the position found and arrange prompt assessment. Check for other urgent concerns and follow trained/local instructions for immobilization and transfer.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Lea asks the cyclist to keep still and supports the forearm in the position she found it. When the cyclist asks her to pull it straight, Lea explains that she will not straighten it and will avoid unnecessary movement. She checks for other urgent concerns. Then she calls the confirmed contact for prompt assessment.

## [scope] Scope and support

Do not straighten a deformity, massage it, use an unpadded improvised splint without training, or move a person with possible spinal injury unless safety demands it.

## [check-transfer] Apply it to the situation

Return to the story. Choose an appropriate next step.
