## [scene] The situation

A cyclist fell and now has a painful, deformed forearm. She can speak and wants the arm pulled straight.

## [action] What to do and say

Avoid unnecessary movement. Support the injured part in the position found and arrange prompt assessment. Check for other urgent concerns and follow trained/local instructions for immobilization and transfer.
