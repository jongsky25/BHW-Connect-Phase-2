## [purpose] Purpose

When an adult collapses and does not respond, the BHW calls for help, checks response and breathing, declines any drink and hands over what they saw.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **20 minutes** face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 480-minute subchapter plan): opening 3, model 3, practice 9, feedback 3, self-check 2. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- 2.4.6 participant cards, worksheet and observation sheet; job aid with the confirmed emergency contact.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson and the job aid section *Recognize shock and loss of consciousness*.
- Confirm with the RHU the emergency contact and whether an AED exists nearby. A chair stands in for the adult.
- Scope: the lesson lists no shock signs or treatment; do not add them. CPR and AED belong to the supervised 2.4.7 station, never on a person.

## [opening] Opening

*"An adult collapses in the waiting area and does not answer you. A relative brings a sweet drink. What do you check, and what do you not give?"*

## [steps] Steps

1. **The situation (Read and first slide).** *"What do we know, and what is a guess?"*
2. **What to do and say (matching slide).** *"What two things do you check, and who do you call?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** Model it: *"Ma'am, can you hear me?"* No answer. *"Kuya, call [confirmed contact]: adult collapsed at the waiting area, not responding. I am checking her breathing."* To the relative: *"Nothing by mouth while she cannot respond."*
5. **Scope and support.** *"If she is not breathing normally, what does the lesson say? Where do we practise that?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — pairs first.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"Who is the confirmed contact for your BHS waiting area?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Give the drink; her sugar is low."** *"Can one clue tell us the cause? Can she swallow?"*
- **"Let her rest and see if she wakes."** *"What happens to help if we wait?"*
- **"Start CPR at once."** *"First check response and normal breathing, then follow your local training."*
- **"Call, check breathing, keep watching."** Confirm.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"A sweet drink will bring her around."** Families have seen a weak person recover after a drink. For someone who cannot respond, it is unsafe and delays the call. Correct the case: *"The relative in the story believes that too. What does the lesson say?"*

## [practice] Practice activity

Urgent response simulation (*Scene, collapse and handover stations*). Triads: learner, partner as the relative offering a sweet drink, observer. Two rounds with one strength and one retry action between, then rotate. The observer records response, breathing, call, declined drink and handover. Fictional cases only. Solo: talk it through with the job aid; staff observe later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

1. **"…What comes first?"** **"Activate help and check her breathing."** — call and check response and breathing. "Help her sip the sweet drink…": nothing by mouth, and one clue cannot tell the cause. "Let her rest…": delays the call.
2. **"…The relative insists on the drink…"** **"Decline the drink and keep watching until help arrives."** "Allow one small sip…": still unresponsive, so nothing by mouth. "Tell help the cause is low sugar…": a guess, not an observation.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Demonstrate safe activation, response/breathing observation and an appropriate handover without oral intake.

- **Kaya na:** Checks the scene, response and breathing, activates help, declines the drink and hands over what was seen, without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Activates help and declines the drink but needs a prompt to check breathing or to give a factual handover.
- **Hindi pa:** Allows the sweet drink, guesses the cause, or waits to see if the person wakes before calling.

Watch whether the call happens before any talk of causes. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** practise one refusal of the drink; retry.
- **Hindi pa:** walk the job aid rows (response, breathing, call) together; retry with a Kaya na partner.
- Re-observe this session or next, new case. Peer feedback is practice, not a rating.
