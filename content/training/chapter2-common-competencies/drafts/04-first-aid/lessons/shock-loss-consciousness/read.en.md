## [scene] The situation

An adult collapses at the waiting area and does not respond when spoken to. A relative offers a sweet drink.

## [action] What to do and say

Check scene safety, response and normal breathing. Activate emergency help and request trained assessment. If not breathing normally, follow the locally trained CPR/AED response; if breathing, keep watching until help arrives.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW checks that the waiting area is safe, then asks, "Can you hear me?" There is no answer. She has someone call the confirmed contact and checks for normal breathing. To the relative she says, "Nothing by mouth while she cannot respond." If breathing is not normal, she follows her local CPR/AED training; if it is, she keeps watching until help arrives.

## [scope] Scope and support

Do not give food, candy or water to an unresponsive person. Do not infer the cause of collapse from a single clue or delay emergency activation.
