## [scene] The situation

A farm worker reports a snakebite to the ankle. Friends want to tie a tight cord and walk him to the road.

## [action] What to do and say

Move away from the snake, keep the person calm and as still as possible, remove tight items if safe, and arrange rapid transport to a health facility. Report time and observations.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Lea helps the worker move away from the snake and keeps him calm and as still as possible. She tells the friends kindly, "No cord, and he should not walk. Transport will come to him." She removes tight items if it is safe, calls for transport, and reports the time of the bite and what she sees.

## [scope] Scope and support

Do not use a tourniquet, cut or suck the wound, apply ice, chase the snake, or make the person walk unnecessarily.

## [check-transfer] Apply it to the situation

Return to the story. Choose an appropriate next step.
