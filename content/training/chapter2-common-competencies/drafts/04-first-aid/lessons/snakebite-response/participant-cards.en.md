# 2.4.13 — Respond to snakebite

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

A farm worker reports a snakebite to the ankle. Friends want to tie a tight cord and walk him to the road.

## Learner card

You are the BHW. Decline the cord and the walk kindly, keep the worker calm and still, and call for transport. Say the time of the bite and what you see.

## Partner card

Play the friend who wants to help. Hold up a cord and say: "Let's tie it tight and walk him to the road." If the learner declines, ask once: "Isn't walking faster?" Accept a clear reason. Do not act out tying, cutting or sucking, and do not add new symptoms.

## Observer card

Listen for a kind refusal of the cord and the bite time in the call. Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
