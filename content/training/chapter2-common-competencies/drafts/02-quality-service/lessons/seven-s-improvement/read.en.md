## [scene] The situation

BHW Tess inspects a simulated desk. There is an old poster, mixed blank forms and dust. Containers have no labels. A box blocks the walkway. No checker or review date is assigned. The team needs order that lasts beyond one cleaning session.

## [action] What to do and say

Sort: identify unneeded items and seek authorization before removal. Systematize: arrange items according to use. Sweep: maintain cleanliness using the assigned procedure. Standardize: agree on labels, arrangement and a checklist. At the desk, inspect the poster, forms, dust and containers.

## [example] Worked example

Safety: report the blocking box and arrange a clear walkway. Self-discipline: carry out assigned checks without repeated reminders. Sustain: repeat and review the routine. Tess and staff agree on a daily checklist and a Friday review. All seven categories matter, not just sweeping.

## [scope] Scope and support

Use blank paper and safe props in practice. Do not discard records, medicines or equipment without authorization. Do not handle sharps or contaminated items as part of this activity. Report hazards to the assigned staff and follow the local procedure.
