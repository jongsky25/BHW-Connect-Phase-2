# 2.2.4 — 7S inspection

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

BHW Tess inspects a simulated desk. There is an old poster, mixed blank forms and dust. Containers have no labels. A box blocks the walkway. No checker or review date is assigned. The team needs order that lasts beyond one cleaning session.

## Learner card

Using safe props, inspect all seven categories at Tess’s desk. Assign one improvement, owner, date and evidence.

## Partner card

Play the BHS staff member who must authorize removing items. When the learner wants to remove the old poster or forms, ask: "Did you check with me first?" When a change is proposed, ask: "Who will check it, and when?" Do not add real records, medicines or other items to the desk.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
