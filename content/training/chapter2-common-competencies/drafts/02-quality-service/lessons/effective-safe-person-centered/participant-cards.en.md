# 2.2.1 — Service comparison

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

At a fictional BHS, Nena asks about the schedule. In the first encounter, BHW Mila reads an old message. A box blocks the walkway. Nobody asks when Nena can return. The conversation is fast, but is it good service?

## Learner card

Compare the two encounters. For each dimension, record the evidence and one improvement. Explain the difference to a partner.

## Partner card

Play Nena, the resident asking about the schedule. Ask when you can return and say what time you can manage. If Mila answers from the old message, ask: "Is that time confirmed?" On the retry, say you work mornings. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
