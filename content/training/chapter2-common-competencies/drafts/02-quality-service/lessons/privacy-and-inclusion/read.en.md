## [scene] The situation

In the queue, Alex tells BHW Lorna that the form is difficult to read. Someone loudly asks about Alex’s condition. Alex does not want others to hear. Help is needed without embarrassment.

## [action] What to do and say

Do not answer the public question about the condition. Offer a private conversation and ask what help is wanted. You can read the form calmly in an agreed setting. Respect religion, culture, identity and circumstances. Difficulty reading calls for support, not judgment.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Lorna: “Would you like to speak over there? What help would you prefer with the form?” Alex: “Please read it slowly.” Lorna follows the agreement. She does not disclose the condition to the queue. She asks whether Alex wants a support person before involving anyone else.

## [scope] Scope and support

Use the authorized process for handling and sharing information. Do not promise absolute secrecy in every situation. If unsure who needs the information, ask the supervisor. Do not send a photograph of the form to a public chat.

Return to the story. Choose an appropriate next step.
