# 2.2.2 — Private and equitable conversation

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

In the queue, Alex tells BHW Lorna that the form is difficult to read. Someone loudly asks about Alex’s condition. Alex does not want others to hear. Help is needed without embarrassment.

## Learner card

Play Lorna, Alex and observer. Offer privacy, ask about preferred support and explain the next step.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
