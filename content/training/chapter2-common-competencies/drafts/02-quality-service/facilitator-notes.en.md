# Facilitator notes — Subchapter 2.2: Quality service

## Timing

**10 hours (600 minutes)**, the source allocation for *Maintain high standard of patient/client services* (F34). The per-lesson split is an authoring proposal within those 10 hours.

| Part | Lesson | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 2.2.1 | Apply the three quality dimensions | 120 |
| 2.2.2 | Protect privacy and include everyone | 180 |
| 2.2.3 | Improve the service pathway | 180 |
| 2.2.4 | Use 7S to improve the work area | 120 |
| **Total** | | **600** |

Run 2.2.1–2.2.2 on day one and 2.2.3–2.2.4 on day two (300 minutes each). Breaks are extra. Every lesson runs in triads; 30 participants means 10 triads, so plan enough staff observers or extra rounds to observe everyone. App reading time is not this face-to-face time, and completing the sessions is not NC II certification.

## Before the session

- **Confirm local facts:** the staff contact for schedule questions, the authorized reporting channel for hazards and barriers, the supervisor or organizer, and the local process for sharing resident information. Write them on the job aid's blank lines.
- **Print one set per triad for each lesson:** the participant cards (*2.2.1 Service comparison*, *2.2.2 Private and equitable conversation*, *2.2.3 Service pathway map*, *2.2.4 7S inspection*), the matching worksheets and observation sheets, and one *Quality service* job aid for each participant.
- **Props:** a simulated desk with an old poster, mixed blank forms, unlabeled empty containers and an empty box to block a walkway. Blank paper and safe props only.
- **Staffing:** enough staff observers for the triads, or scheduled later rounds.
- **Scope limits:** do not discard real records, medicines or equipment. Do not handle sharps or contaminated items. Do not use real forms with resident details. Do not teach changing a clinician's advice, skipping required checks or changing queue priority without authorization.

## Delivery script

1. **Open the subchapter (about 5 minutes, within 2.2.1's opening).** Start from their own experience. Ask: *"Think of a time someone came to you at the BHS. What made that visit good service, or not?"* List answers on manila paper; most will say "fast" or "polite". Keep the list.
2. **2.2.1 Apply the three quality dimensions.** Case: Nena asks about the schedule, Mila reads an old message, a box blocks the walkway and nobody asks when Nena can come back. Ask: *"The conversation was fast. Was it good service?"* Run the **Service comparison**: triads fill in evidence and one improvement for effective, safe and people-centered. Go back to the manila paper: *"Which of our answers were about speed only?"* Link: *"People-centered also means not exposing people. Let's look at that next."*
3. **2.2.2 Protect privacy and include everyone.** Case: in the queue, Alex says the form is hard to read and someone loudly asks about Alex's condition. Ask: *"What would you say first, and where?"* Run the **Private and equitable conversation** role-play (Lorna, Alex, observer), then retry with one changed detail. Link: *"Alex got help. Now, what if the service itself keeps sending people back?"*
4. **2.2.3 Improve the service pathway.** Case: Pilar has come back twice: the staff contact was unclear, then she had to rewrite the same details. Ask: *"Where exactly did Pilar lose time?"* Run the **Service pathway map**: four steps, one barrier, a change with an owner, confirmation, date and measure. Link: *"Some barriers are in the room itself."*
5. **2.2.4 Use 7S to improve the work area.** Case: Tess inspects a cluttered desk with a blocking box and nobody assigned to check it. Ask: *"If we clean it today, will it still be tidy in a month? Why?"* Run the **7S inspection** with safe props, all seven rows.
6. **Close (within 2.2.4's workplace-plan time).** Each person names one dimension, one privacy step and one 7S category to check next week, then writes one improvement proposal on the job aid for the transfer task.

For in-lesson timing, checks and answer keys, use the lesson guide.

## Named misconception

**"Good service means fast and friendly."**

People hold it for good reasons: residents complain about waits, and kind, quick BHWs get praised. Speed and courtesy do matter.

Correct it with the lesson, not a lecture. Mila's fast first encounter used an old message, left a hazard in the walkway and never asked Nena what time suited her. Ask the group: *"Mila was fast and polite. What was still missing?"* Let them find the three dimensions themselves. In 2.2.3, show that speed must not remove checks that staff require. Credit whoever answered "fast" in the opening: *"That's part of it. Now we have the other parts."*

## Discussion prompts

- *"When a resident asks you something you are not sure of, who do you check with before answering?"*
- *"Where in your BHS can a resident talk without the queue hearing?"*
- *"Who in your barangay might find it hardest to use the BHS: someone who cannot read well, someone who works afternoons, someone of a different religion or identity? What gets in their way?"*
- *"Think of a form or step that residents repeat. Who would need to agree before it changes?"*
- *"Which of the seven S's does your station already do well? Which one is nobody assigned to?"*

## Observing competence

The Competency tab has one indicator per lesson objective (four). Each activity card names its indicator.

- **Who observes:** a staff facilitator, using the lesson's observation sheet. The peer observer in each triad gives one strength and one next action. That is practice support, not a rating.
- **Rounds:** rotate so each participant is the learner at least once per lesson; retry with one changed detail.
- **Record only what you saw and heard.** Write the actual words or actions under "Evidence observed". Leave the sheet unrated if you did not observe the learner.
- **Levels:** Ready / Needs practice / Not yet, using the anchors on each sheet.
- **Needs practice:** give the one missing element (for example, *"Ask Alex what help is preferred"* or *"Name the owner and the measure"*), then re-observe in the same session.
- **Not yet:** say what was safe and what to change; give a full retry in a later round or at follow-up. Never announce ratings in front of the group.
- A correct answer on a lesson check or quiz is not evidence of the skill. Only staff-observed performance is rated.

## Where this connects

- **2.1 Responding to difficult situations:** listening, moving to a private setting and agreeing next steps.
- **2.3 Infection prevention and control:** the 7S safety and sweep rows lead into IPC controls. Handling sharps and contaminated items is taught there, not here.
- **Chapter 1, Module 6 (Effective communication):** open questions, teach-back and factual reporting.
- **Transfer task:** within 2–4 weeks, each BHW reviews one improvement proposal with the supervisor using the job aid. The supervisor uses the same indicator: observe, give feedback, retry. Recall checks follow at 2, 14 and 60 days.
