Run one block, or two sessions: Parts 1–6 (50 min) and 7–12 (70 min). Breaks are extra. Work in triads (learner, partner, observer); 30 participants make 10 triads, so plan enough staff observers or later rounds to observe everyone. App reading time is not this face-to-face time, and finishing the subchapter is not certification or authorization to prepare or give any plant remedy.

## Before the session

- **Confirm locally:** the midwife / RHU contact, the referral route for urgent cases, the reporting channel, and who authorizes plant products or preparations in your area. Write them on the job aid's blank lines.
- **Print, one set per triad:** participant cards *2.5.1 — Plant-card consultation*, *2.5.2 to 2.5.11 — Reference-card practice: [plant]*, *2.5.12 — Plant-card consultation*; matching worksheets; staff observation sheets (*2.5.x — Observation sheet*); the job aid *Ten medicinal plants*.
- **Reference cards:** printed current PITAHC cards for all ten plants, plus a second card with the same local name to compare at each station.
- **Props:** dry props only. No tasting, hot water, decoction, dose demonstration or product to hand out.
- **Staffing:** enough staff observers to see every triad; peers practise, staff rate.
- **Scope limits:** teach reference-card identity, listed part, listed use as a consultation topic, and when to refer. Do **not** teach or demonstrate preparation, amounts or doses, even if asked or listed in the old manual. Any preparation training belongs to a qualified, locally authorized trainer, not this session. Say: *"That is a question for the midwife or RHU. Today we practise the card check."*

## Delivery script

1. **Open the subchapter (2 minutes, inside Part 1).** Ask about their own experience: *"What do residents already ask you about plants?"* Listen and thank them; do not write down recipes or amounts. Link to F38's outcomes: assess the situation and communicate the details.
2. **2.5.1 Check scope and plant identity (10 min).** Case: two yard plants share the local name "tsaang gubat". Ask: *"Which one is it — and how would you know?"* Run *Plant-card consultation* with the worksheet. Link: *"Now we do this same check for each plant."*
3. **Rotation A, Parts 2–6 (40 min).** Five tables (two triads each, own card sets), 8 minutes each, then move. Rotate the learner role. Each table runs *Reference-card practice* for its plant:
   - **Akapulko** — Ana has an itchy circular rash. *"What would make you send Ana to the health center?"*
   - **Ampalaya** — Joel asks if Ampalaya can replace his diabetes medicine. *"Who decides about Joel's medicine?"*
   - **Bawang** — Liza wants to stop her cholesterol medicine. *"What do you say, and who decides?"*
   - **Bayabas** — Mara has a red wound with pus. *"What in Mara's wound means assessment comes first?"*
   - **Lagundi** — Ben is coughing and has difficulty breathing. *"What cannot wait here?"*
4. **Rotation B, Parts 7–11 (40 min).** Same routine:
   - **Niyog-niyogan** — a child is suspected of having worms. *"Who confirms it and plans deworming?"*
   - **Sambong** — Paolo has severe side pain and little urine. *"Why does this need prompt assessment?"*
   - **Tsaang gubat** — Rosa's stomach pain is getting worse. *"What do you do about the worsening pain?"*
   - **Ulasimang bato / pansit-pansitan** — Carlo has joint pain; a neighbour says the plant alone treats gout. *"Is joint pain the same as gout? Who checks?"*
   - **Yerba buena** — Eva has tooth pain and a swollen cheek. *"Who should see the swelling?"*
   Link: *"Now three residents at once."*
5. **2.5.12 Use the plant references responsibly (30 min).** Case: three residents ask about Lagundi, Ampalaya and Bayabas; their cards include breathing difficulty, diabetes medicine and a wound with pus. Ask: *"Which of these three do you escalate first, and what do you record?"* Run *Plant-card consultation* on all three case cards.
6. **Close with retrieval (last minutes of Part 12).** Ask: *"What follows the card check?"* (Consult a clinician.) Each person names what they will bring to their supervisor on Monday.

For station timing, checks and expected answers, see the lesson guide.

## Named misconception

**"It's natural, so it's safe. The old manual lists these plants, so I can tell people to use them."**

It is understandable: families used these plants, and the legacy manual promoted them. Respect that; do not call it wrong in front of the group.

Instead, go back to 2.5.1: a local name, picture or "natural" label alone does not establish identity, efficacy or safety, and two plants can share one name. Then return to Ben's breathing, Mara's pus and Joel's medicine: *"If the plant works, what could still go wrong here?"* Let them find it: delayed care, or stopped medicine. The listed use is a topic to take to the clinician, not a prescription.

## Discussion prompts

- *When a neighbour says "it's the same plant", what would you need to check?*
- *How can you respond to Joel without dismissing his wish to use Ampalaya?*
- *In today's cases, which signs meant the plant question had to wait?*
- *Who in your area decides which plant products are authorized, and how do you reach them?*

## Observing competence

Use the Competency tab's 12 indicators (one per lesson) with the activity cards.

- **Who observes:** staff only, with the lesson observation sheet. Triad peer feedback is practice, not a rating.
- **Rounds:** each BHW leads at least one plant station per rotation and the integrated case. Not every plant indicator can be observed for everyone in 80 minutes; leave the rest unrated and observe them at a later round or supervision visit.
- **Record behaviour only:** botanical name, part, listed use, symptoms and medicines asked, referral.
- **Levels:** *Kaya na* (Ready), *Kailangan pa ng practice* (Needs practice), *Hindi pa* (Not yet).
- **Needs practice:** name the missing step (usually the limit or referral); retry the same card right away.
- **Not yet:** the learner guessed identity, gave a dose, or replaced treatment. Stop kindly, restate the scope line, re-observe next session.
- A correct self-check or quiz score is not evidence of the skill.

## Where this connects

- **2.4 First aid and referral:** same F38 competency group; escalate breathing difficulty and infected wounds as taught there. 2.6 and 2.7 complete the group.
- **Chapter 1, Module 1 (The Roles of a BHW)** and **Module 6 (Effective Communication):** referral as service, and clear reporting to the midwife.
- **Transfer task:** within 2–4 weeks, each BHW checks one plant card from the job aid *Ten medicinal plants* with the midwife against the current PITAHC card and local authorization, and names the referral route for urgent cases, with no preparation or product; the supervisor uses the same indicator, gives feedback and has the BHW retry. Recall checks are specified for day 2, 14 and 60 (content only; no reminders are scheduled).
