## [purpose] Purpose

The BHW matches Senna alata, leaves and the listed use on the Akapulko card, and tells Ana when her rash needs health-center assessment.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 8 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 120-minute subchapter plan, rotation A): opening 1, model 1, practice 4, feedback 1, check 1. Read: about 3 minutes in the app.
- **At the table:** participant cards *2.5.2 — Reference-card practice: Akapulko*, worksheet, staff observation sheet; job aid *Ten medicinal plants*; two printed cards labelled Akapulko.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson and both checks. Write the midwife / RHU contact on the job aid.
- **Scope:** do not diagnose the rash. No preparation, extract, amount, application or fresh specimens. If asked: *"That is a question for the midwife or RHU. Today we practise the card check."*

## [opening] Opening

Read the case. Ask: *"What would make you send Ana to the health center?"* Return to it at step 4.

## [steps] Steps

1. **"The situation" (first slide).** Ask: *"What don't we know about the rash?"*
2. **"What to do and say" (matching slide).** Point to Senna alata and leaves: *"Is Ana's yard plant confirmed?"*
3. **"Choose the first response."** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **"Worked example."** Model: point to the card; the yard plant is not yet identified. Ask: *"When does Ana go to the health center?"* (Worsens, spreads, looks infected, diagnosis uncertain.)
5. **"Scope and support."** *"What will you not do, even if Ana asks?"* Run the practice.
6. **"Apply it to the situation."** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **"On Monday, try this."** *"What will you mark on two Akapulko cards?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"It's buni; Akapulko is for buni."** *"Who confirms it is ringworm?"*
- **"Use the leaves in her yard."** *"Is that plant confirmed as Senna alata?"*
- **"Tell her how my mother prepared it."** Respect it; say the scope line.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Every itchy round rash is buni, so Akapulko fits."** BHWs see ringworm often. Ask the group, not the person: *"Can we be sure from looking?"* The listed use is a consultation topic, not a diagnosis.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Reference-card practice: Akapulko** (*Reference-card stations, rotation A*): two triads per table; learner, partner (Ana), observer; one 4-minute round, 1 minute feedback; rotate at the next table. The learner fills the worksheet, states what is known and unknown, asks about medicine and symptoms, and names the referral. The observer records the learner's words, then gives one strength and one next action. Fictional details only. Low-resource: one card set for the whole group.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **"Which Akapulko part is listed on the card?"** Correct: **"Leaves of confirmed Senna alata."** Match the part to the written source; this is not a dose.
  - "Any part of a yard plant even when its identity as Akapulko is unconfirmed." Unconfirmed part and identity are unsafe.
  - "The botanical name need not be checked." Common names may cover different plants.
- **"What is the safe response in the Akapulko case?"** Correct: **"Consult a clinician first."** Confirm identity, symptoms and scope before advice.
  - "Replace prescribed treatment now." Necessary care may be displaced or delayed.
  - "Copy an old recipe without assessment." An old recipe does not replace current clinical guidance.

## [observe] What to observe

Watch for: For Akapulko, match the botanical name, plant part and listed use. Explain the boundary and consultation need.

- **Kaya na:** Correctly identifies Akapulko, Senna alata, leaves, use and referral without prompts.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Identifies the plant but needs a prompt about limits or referral.
- **Hindi pa:** Guesses identity, prescribes a dose or replaces treatment.

Look for: the learner names the referral signs unprompted. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** name the missing step; retry the same card.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model step 4; retry with three items only (botanical name, part, referral), paired with a Kaya na BHW.
- Re-observe next session (Ana's rash now spreading). Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
