## [scene] The situation

Ana has an itchy circular rash. A yard plant is called Akapulko.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Senna alata on the reference card. The listed part is leaves; the use topic is ringworm or another fungal skin concern. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Seek health-center assessment if the rash worsens, spreads, looks infected or the diagnosis is uncertain.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW shows Ana the card: Akapulko is Senna alata, and the listed part is leaves. The BHW says the yard plant is not yet confirmed and the rash is not yet diagnosed. The BHW asks about her medicines and symptoms. Because the diagnosis is uncertain, the BHW asks Ana to have the rash assessed at the health center.

## [scope] Scope and support

A local name or picture alone cannot confirm identity. Do not apply an unverified extract. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
