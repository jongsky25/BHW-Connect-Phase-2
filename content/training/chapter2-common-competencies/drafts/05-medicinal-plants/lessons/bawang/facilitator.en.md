## [purpose] Purpose

The BHW matches Allium sativum, bulb and the listed use on the Bawang card, and returns Liza's medicine decision to her clinician.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 8 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 120-minute subchapter plan, rotation A): opening 1, model 1, practice 4, feedback 1, check 1. Read: about 3 minutes in the app.
- **At the table:** participant cards *2.5.4 — Reference-card practice: Bawang*, worksheet, staff observation sheet; job aid *Ten medicinal plants*; two printed Bawang cards.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Read the lesson and checks. Write on the job aid who follows up cholesterol care (midwife / RHU).
- **Scope:** no advice to stop or change medicine; no amount of garlic. No preparation or tasting. If asked: *"That is a question for the midwife or RHU. Today we practise the card check."*

## [opening] Opening

Read the case. Ask: *"What do you say to Liza, and who decides?"* Return to it at step 4.

## [steps] Steps

1. **"The situation" (first slide).** *"What does Liza want to change?"*
2. **"What to do and say" (matching slide).** Point to Allium sativum and bulb: *"What does 'under clinical advice' mean?"*
3. **"Choose the first response."** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **"Worked example."** Model: point to the card; return the decision to the clinician; name no amount. *"Why no amount?"*
5. **"Scope and support."** *"What else should you ask Liza?"* (Other medicines and possible effects.) Run the practice.
6. **"Apply it to the situation."** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **"On Monday, try this."** *"What will you mark on two Bawang cards?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Tell her how many cloves a day."** Kindly: we set no amount; say the scope line.
- **"She can stop if she has garlic daily."** *"Who prescribed it? Who decides to stop it?"*
- **"Keep the medicine; ask her clinician."** Praise it.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Garlic is ordinary food, so it can replace the medicine."** It is in every kitchen, so it feels safe. Ask the group, not the person: *"If Liza stops her medicine, who would know if something changed?"* Garlic as food does not authorize a medication change.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Reference-card practice: Bawang** (*Reference-card stations, rotation A*): two triads per table; learner, partner (Liza), observer; one 4-minute round, 1 minute feedback; rotate at the next table. The learner fills the worksheet, states what is known and unknown, asks about other medicines and symptoms, and returns the decision to the clinician. The observer records the learner's words, then gives one strength and one next action. Fictional details only. Low-resource: one card set for the whole group.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **"Which Bawang part is listed on the card?"** Correct: **"Bulb of confirmed Allium sativum."**
  - "Any part of the garlic plant, since it is ordinary food." Food use is not the listed part.
  - "The part Liza already eats, without reading the card." Liza’s habit is not the check.
- **"What is the safe response in Liza’s case?"** Correct: **"Ask about her other medicines; return the decision to her clinician."**
  - "Tell Liza she may stop her medicine because she eats garlic daily." Food garlic does not authorize a change.
  - "Tell her how many cloves of garlic to eat instead of the medicine." No amount of garlic as medicine.

## [observe] What to observe

Watch for: For Bawang, match the botanical name, plant part and listed use. Explain the boundary and consultation need.

- **Kaya na:** Matches Allium sativum, bulb and listed use, asks about other medicines, and returns the decision to Liza’s clinician without prompts.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Matches the card but needs a prompt to ask about other medicines or to name who decides.
- **Hindi pa:** Agrees that Liza can stop her medicine, or names an amount of garlic.

Look for: no amount named; other medicines asked. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** name the missing step; retry the same card.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model step 4; retry with three items only (botanical name, part, who decides), paired with a Kaya na BHW.
- Re-observe next session (Liza has already stopped her medicine). Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
