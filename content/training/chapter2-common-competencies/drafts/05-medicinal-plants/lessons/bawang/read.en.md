## [scene] The situation

Liza has high cholesterol. She eats garlic and wants to stop her prescribed medicine.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Allium sativum on the reference card. The listed part is bulb; the use topic is cholesterol management under clinical advice. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Return the decision to the clinician; do not prescribe an amount of garlic as medicine.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW shows Liza the card: Bawang is Allium sativum, bulb, for cholesterol management under clinical advice. The BHW says garlic as food does not authorize a medication change and names no amount. The BHW asks about her other medicines and possible effects, then returns the decision to her clinician.

## [scope] Scope and support

Garlic as food does not authorize a medication change. Ask about other medicines and possible effects. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
