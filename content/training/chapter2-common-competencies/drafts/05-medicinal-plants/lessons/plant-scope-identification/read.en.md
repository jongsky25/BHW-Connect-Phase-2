## [scene] The situation

Two yard plants share the local name “tsaang gubat.” A resident asks which one to use.

## [action] What to do and say

Find the botanical name and listed part in a current PITAHC card. If identity is uncertain, do not prepare or provide it. Ask about symptoms and medicines; consult a clinician.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW reads the current card and listens to the resident. Both plants are called “tsaang gubat,” but the card lists one botanical name and part. The identity of the yard plants is uncertain, so the BHW does not prepare or provide either one. The BHW asks about symptoms and medicines and consults a clinician.

## [scope] Scope and support

A local name, picture or “natural” label alone does not establish identity, efficacy or safety.
