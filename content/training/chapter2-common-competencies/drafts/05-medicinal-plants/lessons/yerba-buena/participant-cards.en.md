# 2.5.11 — Reference-card practice: Yerba buena

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Eva has tooth pain and a swollen cheek. She asks about Yerba buena.

## Learner card

Compare the two Yerba buena cards and fill the worksheet. Tell Eva what is known, what is unknown and who to consult.

## Partner card

Play Eva. Say you have tooth pain and a swollen cheek, ask about Yerba buena, and say the dentist is far away. Do not add symptoms, medicines or other details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
