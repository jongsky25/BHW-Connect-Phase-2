## [scene] The situation

Eva has tooth pain and a swollen cheek. She asks about Yerba buena.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Mentha cordifolia on the reference card. The listed part is leaves; the use topic is tooth pain requiring dental assessment. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Refer the swelling promptly to a dental or health professional and report symptoms.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW notices the swollen cheek, not only the tooth pain. The BHW points to Mentha cordifolia and "Part: leaves" on the card and suggests no preparation. The BHW asks about Eva's current medicine, refers the swelling promptly to a dental or health professional and reports her symptoms.

## [scope] Scope and support

A plant does not replace dental care. Do not prescribe an oral or topical preparation. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
