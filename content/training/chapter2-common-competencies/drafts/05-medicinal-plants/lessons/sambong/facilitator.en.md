## [purpose] Purpose

The BHW matches the card to Blumea balsamifera and leaves, names the listed use as a clinical topic, and refers Paolo for prompt assessment.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **8 minutes face-to-face** (of the 120-minute plan): opening 1, model 1, practice 4, feedback 1, checks 1. Read in the app: about 3 minutes.
- **Per triad:** *2.5.8 — Reference-card practice: Sambong* cards and worksheet; *2.5.8 — Observation sheet*; job aid; two Sambong cards. Dry props only.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Confirm with the midwife or RHU where severe pain and changed urination are assessed.
- **Scope:** card check and referral only. No preparation, decoction, amount or dose: *"That is a question for the midwife or RHU."* Do not guess the cause of the pain.

## [opening] Opening

Read the case aloud. Ask: *"Paolo has severe side pain and little urine. What comes before any talk about Sambong?"*

## [steps] Steps

1. **The situation.** *"What two signs did Paolo describe?"*
2. **What to do and say.** Point to the botanical name and part. *"Whose advice does the listed use need?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** Model reading the card without assuming the yard plant is Sambong. *"What did I not guess?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"What do we lose if Paolo tries a decoction first?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** Start the practice.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Sambong is for kidney stones; he can try it."** *"Do we know it is a stone? Who can tell?"*
- **"It grows in my yard; I know it."** *"What on the card proves it?"*
- **"Send him for assessment now."** Correct; ask what to report: symptoms and medicines.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"He can try Sambong first, then go if it doesn't work."** The plant is familiar and the old manual lists it. Ask the group, not the speaker: *"What do we lose by waiting?"* Symptoms alone do not establish a kidney stone; waiting delays assessment.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Reference-card practice: Sambong** (rotation B), triads, one 4-minute round.

- **Learner:** compares the cards, fills the worksheet, tells Paolo what is known, unknown and who to consult.
- **Partner:** plays Paolo; asks to just try the plant.
- **Observer:** records the learner's words; one strength, one next action.

Fictional details only. Alone: worksheet now, staff observation later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1: "Which Sambong part is listed on the card?"** Correct: **"Leaves of confirmed Blumea balsamifera."** Part matched to the card; not a dose.
  - "Leaves of any plant Paolo calls Sambong." A local name is not identity.
  - "Any part, once the local name matches." Only leaves are listed.
- **Check 2: "Paolo has severe side pain and little urine. What is the safe response?"** Correct: **"Seek prompt facility assessment for Paolo."** Severe pain and changed urination need prompt assessment.
  - "Tell Paolo it is a kidney stone that Sambong can help." Symptoms alone do not establish a kidney stone.
  - "Have him try Sambong first and seek assessment later." Do not delay assessment for a decoction.

## [observe] What to observe

Staff only. Indicator: For Sambong, match the botanical name, plant part and listed use. Explain the boundary and consultation need.

- **Kaya na:** Matches Blumea balsamifera and leaves, does not call it a kidney stone and seeks prompt facility assessment for Paolo without prompts.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Matches the card but needs a prompt to seek prompt assessment for Paolo's pain and urine change.
- **Hindi pa:** Calls it a kidney stone, suggests Sambong first or delays Paolo's assessment.

Look for: reads the botanical name; refers Paolo unprompted. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** name the missing step; retry the same card now.
- **Hindi pa:** restate the scope line, re-model step 4, retry with a Kaya na partner.
- Re-observe next session with one changed detail (Paolo's sister asks). Peer feedback is not a staff rating.
