# 2.5.8 — Reference-card practice: Sambong

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Paolo has severe side pain and little urine. He asks about Sambong.

## Learner card

Compare the two Sambong cards and fill the worksheet. Tell Paolo what is known, what is unknown and who to consult.

## Partner card

Play Paolo. Say you have severe side pain and little urine, and ask to just try Sambong first. Do not add symptoms, medicines or other details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
