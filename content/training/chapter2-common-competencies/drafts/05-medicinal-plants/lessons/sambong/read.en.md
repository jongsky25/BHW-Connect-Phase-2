## [scene] The situation

Paolo has severe side pain and little urine. He asks about Sambong.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Blumea balsamifera on the reference card. The listed part is leaves; the use topic is kidney-stone concern under clinical advice. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Seek prompt facility assessment for severe pain and changed urination.
