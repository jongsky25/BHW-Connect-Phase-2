## [scene] The situation

Rosa has worsening stomach pain and wants to drink Tsaang gubat.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Carmona retusa on the reference card. The listed part is leaves; the use topic is stomach pain requiring assessment. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Refer worsening pain to a health professional; do not copy a dose from the old table.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW notices that Rosa's stomach pain is getting worse. The BHW points to Carmona retusa and "Part: leaves" on the card and names no amount. The BHW asks about her current medicine and refers the worsening pain to a health professional.

## [scope] Scope and support

Stomach pain has many causes. A plant name does not establish that it is safe to drink. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
