## [scene] The situation

Ben is coughing and has difficulty breathing. Someone offers a Lagundi decoction.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Vitex negundo on the reference card. The listed part is leaves; the use topic is cough relief under clinical advice. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Connect Ben promptly to a health professional and report the breathing difficulty.
