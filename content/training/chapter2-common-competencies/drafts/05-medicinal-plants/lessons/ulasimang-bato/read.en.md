## [scene] The situation

Carlo has joint pain. A neighbor says Pansit-pansitan alone will treat gout.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Peperomia pellucida on the reference card. The listed part is leafy tops; the use topic is uric-acid or gout concern under clinical advice. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Ask the health center to assess the pain and current medicines.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW hears the neighbor's gout claim and does not call Carlo's joint pain gout. The BHW points to Peperomia pellucida and "Part: leafy tops" on the card. The BHW asks about Carlo's current medicine, says not to stop it, and asks the health center to assess the pain and his medicines.

## [scope] Scope and support

Joint pain is not a gout diagnosis. Do not stop medicine or promise a cure from the plant. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
