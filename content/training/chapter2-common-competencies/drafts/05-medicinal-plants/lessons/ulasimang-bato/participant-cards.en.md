# 2.5.10 — Reference-card practice: Ulasimang bato / pansit-pansitan

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Carlo has joint pain. A neighbor says Pansit-pansitan alone will treat gout.

## Learner card

Compare the two Ulasimang bato / pansit-pansitan cards and fill the worksheet. Tell Carlo what is known, what is unknown and who to consult.

## Partner card

Play Carlo. Say your joints hurt, and repeat the neighbor's claim that Pansit-pansitan alone will treat gout. Do not add symptoms, medicines or other details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
