## [scene] The situation

A child is suspected of having worms. A neighbor wants to pick Niyog-niyogan seeds.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Quisqualis indica on the reference card. The listed part is seeds; the use topic is intestinal worms requiring confirmation. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Refer the child to the health center for diagnosis and an appropriate deworming plan.

Wrong identity or seed amount can harm. Do not give seeds to a child from the old manual’s table. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
