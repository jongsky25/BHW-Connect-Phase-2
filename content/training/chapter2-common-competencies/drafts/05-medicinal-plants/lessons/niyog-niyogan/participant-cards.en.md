# 2.5.7 — Reference-card practice: Niyog-niyogan

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

A child is suspected of having worms. A neighbor wants to pick Niyog-niyogan seeds.

## Learner card

Compare the two Niyog-niyogan cards and fill the worksheet. Tell the parent what is known, what is unknown and who to consult.

## Partner card

Play the child's parent. Say the neighbor wants to pick Niyog-niyogan seeds now, and ask whether a few seeds are fine. Do not add symptoms, amounts or other details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
