## [scene] The situation

Joel has diabetes and takes prescribed medicine. He asks whether Ampalaya can replace it.

## [action] What to do and say

Find Momordica charantia on the reference card. The listed part is leaves; the use topic is blood-sugar management under clinical advice. State what is known and unknown. Ask about current medicine and symptoms. Refer the question to the clinician monitoring Joel’s blood sugar and other medicines.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW shows Joel the card: Ampalaya is Momordica charantia, leaves, for blood-sugar management under clinical advice. The BHW says Ampalaya is not an automatic substitute for his diabetes plan and asks about his medicines and symptoms. The BHW tells Joel not to stop or change his medicine and refers his question to the clinician monitoring his blood sugar.

## [scope] Scope and support

Food or a plant is not an automatic substitute for a diabetes plan. Do not stop or change medication. This lesson does not authorize preparation or dosing of an herbal remedy.
