## [scene] The situation

In a role-play, Mang Lito is about to sneeze in the waiting area. He has no tissue in his pocket. Tissues and a bin are available at the station. BHW Rina will help without embarrassing him or asking him to cough toward anyone.

## [action] What to do and say

Cover nose and mouth with a tissue when coughing or sneezing. Without tissue, use the inside of a bent elbow rather than hands. Discard used tissue in the designated bin. Clean hands after contact with respiratory secretions. Report symptoms to staff and follow their instructions.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Rina: “Tissues are available here. If none are available, sneeze into your elbow.” She points out the bin and hand-hygiene station. She reports symptoms and asks staff about the waiting area. Follow the facility protocol and staff assistance for a recommended mask. She does not diagnose Mang Lito.

## [scope] Scope and support

Mime the actions; do not actually cough or spit toward a partner. Do not handle someone else’s used tissue outside the appropriate procedure. Covering your own mouth when another person coughs does not replace respiratory controls. Protect privacy when reporting.
