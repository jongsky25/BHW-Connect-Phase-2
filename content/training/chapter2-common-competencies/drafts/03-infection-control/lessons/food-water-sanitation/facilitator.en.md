## [purpose] Purpose

In a household role-play, the BHW names food, water and sanitation risks, agrees on safe changes with the resident, and says who must confirm the water.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 90 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 600-minute subchapter plan): opening 5, model 10, role-play 45, feedback and retry 15, checks and debrief 15. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- **Materials:** a mock household corner or a picture of Mila's home; *2.3.5* cards, worksheet and observation sheet; job aid.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Write the current water advisory or contact and the sanitary inspector on the job aid, or "confirm with the midwife / RHU / local protocol".
- **Scope:** no chlorine amounts, boiling times or septic-tank distances; no hot water or chemicals. Never promise that water is safe.

## [opening] Opening

Read Mila's case. Ask: *"'The water looks clear,' Mila says. What do we say?"*

## [steps] Steps

Each step is a Read section and its matching slide.

1. **The situation.** *"What does the BHW do before speaking?"*
2. **What to do and say.** *"Which food step can Mila manage today?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** *"Who chose the changes — the BHW or Mila?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"Mila asks how much chlorine to add. What do you say?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"When should the household wash hands?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Boil it" or "add bleach."** The lesson gives no amounts or times; follow confirmed local advice.
- **"Clear water is safe."** Not all contamination is visible.
- **"Tell her the house is dirty."** Ask permission; Mila chooses.
- **"Cover the raw and cooked together."** One cover does not stop contamination. Separate them.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"If the water looks clear, it's safe to drink."** Clear water usually seems fine. Ask: *"Can we see every germ?"* Then point to who confirms water safety, not to the person who said it.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Household food, water and sanitation review**, triads (BHW, Mila, observer), rotating, 45 minutes plus feedback.

- **BHW:** asks permission; names risks in food, water and toilet areas; agrees one change per area; writes who confirms water safety.
- **Mila:** the resident; may say "the water looks clear".
- **Observer:** notes permission and any guessed amount, time or promise.
- Stop any guess; give one retry. Fictional home only. Low resource: printed story. Alone: review with the supervisor.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1 (clear water):** **"Confirm safety with the appropriate staff."** A reliable basis and instructions are needed, not appearance alone.
  - "Declare it safe because it has no color or smell": not all contamination is visible.
  - "Add any amount of a household chemical": the wrong product or amount may be dangerous.
- **Check 2 (raw chicken):** **"Separate raw and cooked food."** This reduces transfer from raw food.
  - "Leave them together under one cover in the same container": one cover does not prevent contamination between them.
  - "Taste the cooked food to determine whether germs are present": tasting is neither a safe nor reliable test.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Proposes appropriate food, water and sanitation changes without guessed treatment or appearance-based guarantees.

- **Kaya na:** Completes all three rows and identifies what health or water staff must confirm without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Suggests appropriate changes but needs a prompt on water protection or the treatment contact.
- **Hindi pa:** Guarantees that clear water is safe or guesses chemical amounts.

Look for the change Mila chose herself. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** prompt *"Who confirms the water?"*; retry the water row only.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model asking permission and the water row; retry one area at a time. Re-observe on day two.
- Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
