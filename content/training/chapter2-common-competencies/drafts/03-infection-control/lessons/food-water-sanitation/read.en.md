## [scene] The situation

In Mila’s fictional home, raw chicken sits beside cooked food. Drinking water is uncovered and a cup is dipped into it. “The water looks clear,” she says. There is no soap near the toilet. The BHW asks permission before discussing these observations.

## [action] What to do and say

For food: keep hands, utensils and surfaces clean. Separate raw and cooked food. Cook thoroughly. Keep food at safe temperatures instead of leaving it exposed. Use safe water and ingredients. Wash produce with safe water. Protect food from insects and rodents.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Mila chooses to separate raw and cooked food. She will cover stored water and avoid dipping hands or cups into it. Clear appearance does not establish safety. They will confirm a safe source with health or water staff. The household will use a toilet and provide soap and water for washing.

## [scope] Scope and support

When water safety is uncertain, use current local advice and a confirmed treatment method. Do not guess chlorine amounts or boiling instructions. Ask the sanitary inspector to assess toilet and septic-tank location. One distance alone cannot guarantee water safety. Use no hot water or chemicals in this exercise.
