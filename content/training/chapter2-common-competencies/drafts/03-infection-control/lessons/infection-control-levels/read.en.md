## [scene] The situation

At a simulated BHS, someone is coughing in a crowded queue. A window is closed and the BHW does not know whom to notify. Masks are available, but the process is unclear. Distributing one item will not address every problem.

## [action] What to do and say

Source: report symptoms and follow the source-control protocol. Environment: have ventilation and layout reviewed. Work practices: confirm the reporting route and waiting area. PPE: use what the task, risk and training require. These controls work together.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW informs designated staff about the cough. Staff identify the waiting area and appropriate mask under the protocol. A ventilation concern goes to the responsible person. The contact for future arrivals is clarified. The BHW does not decide diagnosis or treatment.

## [scope] Scope and support

Do not alter ventilation or isolation arrangements without facility guidance. A mask is not sufficient for every exposure. Respirators have different use and fit requirements and need appropriate training. If protection is inadequate for a task, seek guidance and safe support first.
