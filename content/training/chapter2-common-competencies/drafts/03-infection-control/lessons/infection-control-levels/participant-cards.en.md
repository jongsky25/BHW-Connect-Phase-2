# 2.3.2 — Layered control map

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

At a simulated BHS, someone is coughing in a crowded queue. A window is closed and the BHW does not know whom to notify. Masks are available, but the process is unclear. Distributing one item will not address every problem.

## Learner card

Complete four rows: source, environment, work practices and PPE. Add a proposal and the person who must confirm it.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
