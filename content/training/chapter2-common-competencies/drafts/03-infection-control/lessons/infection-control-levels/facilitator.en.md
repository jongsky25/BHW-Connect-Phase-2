## [purpose] Purpose

For a crowded BHS queue, the BHW proposes source, environment, work-practice and PPE controls, each with an owner or confirming person.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 60 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 600-minute subchapter plan): opening 5, model 10, practice 25, feedback and retry 10, checks and debrief 10. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- **Materials:** *2.3.2 Layered control map* cards and worksheet, observation sheet; job aid; manila paper.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Confirm who owns symptom reporting, ventilation, the waiting area and PPE rules at your BHS; write them on the job aid, or "confirm with the midwife / RHU / local protocol".
- **Scope:** no changes to ventilation or isolation, no respirator fitting, no mask choice, no diagnosis.

## [opening] Opening

Read the crowded-queue case. Ask: *"If we only hand out masks, what problems remain?"* List every answer on manila paper.

## [steps] Steps

Each step is a Read section and its matching slide.

1. **The situation.** *"Whom should the BHW notify?"*
2. **What to do and say.** *"Which problem on our list does each control type address?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** *"Whom did the BHW tell, and what did she not decide?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"The window is closed. May we open it or move everyone to another room?"* Listen for "report it to the responsible person".
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"Which row belongs to you at your BHS?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Give everyone a mask."** *"With masks on, what is still unsafe?"*
- **"Open the window or move them."** Noticed the air. The new room may be unsuitable; report it to the responsible person.
- **"Tell the midwife."** Good. *"Which row is that? Who owns the other rows?"*
- **Owner column says "everyone".** *"Who would confirm it if you asked?"*

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"If I wear a mask and use alcohol, I'm protected."** Masks and alcohol were the loudest recent messages, and they do help. When only the PPE row is full, ask *"What is still unsafe?"* Let the group find the closed window and missing reporting route. Correct the idea, never the person.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Layered control map**, rotating triads, 25 minutes plus feedback.

- **Learner:** completes all four rows: type / proposal / owner or confirming person.
- **Partner/observer:** one voices the case; one checks each row has an owner and gives one strength and one retry.
- Stop any row that changes ventilation, isolation or care. Fictional BHS only. Alone: review the map with the supervisor.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1 (masks are available):** **"Check the setting and process too."** Complementary controls are needed.
  - "Symptoms no longer need reporting to clinical staff": the agreed reporting process still applies.
  - "There is no remaining risk because masks are available": one item does not remove every risk.
- **Check 2 (ventilation concern):** **"Report it to the responsible person."** This enables appropriate facility review and action.
  - "Immediately move everyone to a room that has not been assessed": the new location may be unsuitable.
  - "Declare the room safe without any assessment": the reassurance lacks a basis.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Includes all four types, appropriate owners and no unauthorized clinical or engineering changes.

- **Kaya na:** Links all four controls and identifies what needs review without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Suggests several controls but needs a prompt to assign owners.
- **Hindi pa:** Relies only on a mask or changes isolation arrangements without guidance.

PPE row: "as task, risk and training require", not a mask type. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** ask *"Who confirms?"* at each row; re-observe with one changed detail (the mask box is empty).
- **Hindi pa:** model two rows, guide the other two; re-observe on day two.
- Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
