# 2.3.1 — Possible exposure cards

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

BHW Jo has three cards: contaminated hands, a used object and an enclosed room with someone coughing. Nobody’s illness is known. Infection involves germs entering and multiplying in the body. Symptoms may not be obvious. Identify a possible route, rather than naming a disease.

## Learner card

Sort eight exposure cards. For each, state a possible route and one control or person to approach.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
