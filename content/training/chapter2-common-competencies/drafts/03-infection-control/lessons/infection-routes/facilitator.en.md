## [purpose] Purpose

The BHW sorts eight fictional exposure cards, names a possible route and one control or contact for each, and diagnoses no one.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 120 minutes face-to-face (share of the 600-minute plan): subchapter opener 10, opening 5, model 10, practice 70, feedback 10, checks and debrief 15. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- **Materials:** *2.3.1 Possible exposure cards* (eight per triad), worksheet, observation sheet; job aid; manila paper.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Confirm the IPC contact and the process for respiratory symptoms; write them on the job aid, or "confirm with the midwife / RHU / local protocol".
- The needle card is a picture only.
- **Scope:** routes, not diseases. Do not name illnesses or teach testing, treatment or extra precautions.

## [opening] Opening

After the subchapter opener, hold up Jo's three cards: *"How could a germ travel here — without naming anyone's illness?"* List answers under "route" or "guess about a disease".

## [steps] Steps

Each step is a Read section and its matching slide.

1. **The situation.** *"Nobody's illness is known. What can we still say?"*
2. **What to do and say.** *"Which card is direct, indirect, inhalation, or particles landing on the face?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** Model one card: route, then control. *"Why doesn't ordinary social contact explain these routes?"*
5. **Scope and support.** Point to the "guess" list: *"Who decides testing and treatment?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"What will your supervisor review?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"The one coughing has TB."** *"What does the card show, and not show?"*
- **"Wipe the desk and it's fixed."** True for the object. *"And the air in the closed room?"*
- **"Desk–hand–mouth is direct."** Point to the desk: an object in between makes it indirect.
- **"The mosquito bite is contact."** *"What carried the germ?"*

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"If someone is coughing, I know what they have."** Experience teaches us to recognize illness. But a cough alone does not prove infection. Ask *"What exactly does the card show?"* and write only that. Thank whoever noticed the cough.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Transmission card sort**, rotating triads, 70 minutes.

- **Learner:** sorts all eight cards; fills card / route / control or contact.
- **Partner:** reads each card aloud; once, pushes back with a guess ("The one coughing has TB").
- **Observer:** notes actual words and any label; gives one strength and one retry.
- Staff rate. Fictional details only. Alone: worksheet first; observe later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1 (desk, then mouth):** **"Indirect contact."** The possible transfer is through an object and hands.
  - "Mother-to-child transmission only": pregnancy or birth is not involved in this scene.
  - "Proof that the person has tuberculosis": this card cannot establish a diagnosis.
- **Check 2 (enclosed room, cough):** **"Follow the BHS process for respiratory controls."** Possible inhalation calls for the BHS's agreed arrangements for space, reporting and protection.
  - "Say wiping the desk alone addresses every risk": this does not address possible inhalation.
  - "Immediately name the illness and tell the entire queue": this guesses a diagnosis and discloses information inappropriately.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Explains all eight cards, distinguishes exposure from diagnosis and links prevention to the route.

- **Kaya na:** Matches all eight cards appropriately, without labels or diagnosis, and suggests suitable controls without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Most matches are appropriate, but contact and inhalation need clarification; corrects them after feedback.
- **Hindi pa:** Diagnoses from appearance or assumes every germ spreads in the same way.

Look for "possible", not "has". A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** re-model the contact and inhalation cards; re-observe in the retry time.
- **Hindi pa:** name one change (*"Say the route, not the disease"*); guide four cards, then eight; re-observe on day two.
- Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
