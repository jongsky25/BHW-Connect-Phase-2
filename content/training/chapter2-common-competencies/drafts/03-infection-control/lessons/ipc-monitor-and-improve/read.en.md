## [scene] The situation

During a practice inspection, the handwashing station has no soap. The training attendance sheet is complete. A colleague says, “Everyone attended, so we are fine.” BHW Aya recognizes that attendance, supplies and actual practice differ. What should she record?

## [action] What to do and say

Record the specific observation, place and time. Report the gap and follow the agreed safe interim arrangement. Agree on an action, owner and follow-up. Choose an observable measure: is soap available and does the station work? Do not infer complete practice from attendance.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Aya records: “Station A, nine o’clock: no soap.” The nurse arranges a refill and an interim station. At follow-up, soap and running water are available. Aya records the date and evidence. If unresolved, she records that honestly and reports it again.

## [scope] Scope and support

Follow local observation and privacy rules. BHWs are not automatically authorized to conduct clinical audits or investigate outbreaks. Report exposures or immediate hazards promptly rather than waiting for routine follow-up. Quiz completion does not replace observation of a procedure.
