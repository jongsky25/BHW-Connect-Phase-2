## [purpose] Purpose

The BHW records one specific IPC gap, the report, an owner, a date and follow-up evidence, without inventing results.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 60 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 600-minute subchapter plan): opening 5, model 10, practice 20, feedback and retry 5, checks and debrief 10, subchapter close 10. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- **Materials:** blank *2.3.7 IPC improvement card* (worksheet) and observation sheet; job aid for everyone; two follow-up slips written in advance: resolved and unresolved.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Confirm who refills supplies and how gaps are reported; write them on the job aid, or "confirm with the midwife / RHU / local protocol".
- **Scope:** BHWs do not run clinical audits or outbreak investigations. Report exposures and immediate hazards at once. No real colleague names on cards.

## [opening] Opening

Read Aya's case. Ask: *"'Everyone attended, so we are fine.' Is that true?"*

## [steps] Steps

Each step is a Read section and its matching slide.

1. **The situation.** *"Attendance, supplies, practice: which did Aya see?"*
2. **What to do and say.** *"What could we look at to know it is fixed?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** *"What will she write if the soap is still missing?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"An immediate hazard. Do you wait for follow-up?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"What is known, observed and still unverified on Aya's card?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"The staff are lazy."** *"What exactly did you see, where and when?"*
- **"Someone promised a refill, so it's done."** A promise is not evidence.
- **"There is no soap sometimes."** Ask for the place and time.
- **"Everyone was trained."** Training does not replace the needed supply.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Everyone attended the training, so our practice is fine."** Attendance is easy to count. Ask: *"Does the sheet tell us there is soap at the station?"* Only observed practice and supplies can show that.

## [practice] Practice activity

**IPC improvement card**, rotating triads, 20 minutes plus feedback.

- **Learner:** completes every card line, then records both follow-up slips honestly, with a next action if unresolved.
- **Partner:** the colleague who says "everyone attended".
- **Observer:** checks for promises written as results; gives one strength and one retry.
- Fictional station only. Alone: complete a card with the supervisor.

**Close the subchapter (10 minutes):** each person names one route, one control and one person they will report to. Hand out the job aid and explain the transfer task.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1 (observation entry):** **"Station A, nine o'clock: no soap."** This identifies what was seen, where and when.
  - "Everyone is lazy, so their practice is certainly wrong": this is a label rather than a specific observation.
  - "Everyone is competent because the attendance sheet is signed": attendance cannot establish actual practice.
- **Check 2 (soap not refilled at follow-up):** **"Unresolved; report it again."** The record is accurate and prompts further action.
  - "The task is complete because someone promised a refill": a promise is not evidence of completion.
  - "There is no problem because everyone received training": training does not replace the needed supply.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Records a specific gap, report, owner, date and evidence; distinguishes promises from observed results.

- **Kaya na:** Completes the card with accurate resolved and unresolved outcomes and next actions without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** States the gap clearly but needs a prompt to set follow-up or evidence.
- **Hindi pa:** Marks unresolved work complete or assumes everyone is competent from attendance.

Look for an observable measure, such as soap present. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** ask *"How will you know it is fixed?"*; retry the follow-up rows.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model Aya's card; retry with the unresolved slip only. Re-observe in the transfer task.
- Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
