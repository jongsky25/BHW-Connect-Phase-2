# 2.3.7 — IPC improvement card

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

During a practice inspection, the handwashing station has no soap. The training attendance sheet is complete. A colleague says, “Everyone attended, so we are fine.” BHW Aya recognizes that attendance, supplies and actual practice differ. What should she record?

## Learner card

Make an improvement card for missing soap. Record the interim arrangement and two possible follow-up outcomes.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
