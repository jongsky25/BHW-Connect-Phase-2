## [scene] The situation

BHW Lea prepares for simulated care. Her hands are visibly dirty. Soap, water, single-use towels and alcohol-based handrub are available. A quick rinse is insufficient. First choose an appropriate method.

## [action] What to do and say

Wet hands and apply soap. Rub palm against palm, then each hand’s back with fingers interlaced. Clean between fingers and rub their backs against the opposite palm. Rotate around each thumb. Rub fingertips and nails against the opposite palm. Work on both hands.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Rinse and dry with a single-use towel. Use the towel to close a manual tap. The complete healthcare wash takes 40–60 seconds. When handrub is appropriate and hands are not visibly dirty, apply enough to cover all surfaces. Rub the same surfaces until dry: 20–30 seconds for the complete handrub. Do not rinse or wipe it off.

## [scope] Scope and support

Use soap and water for visible dirt and after using the toilet. Gloves do not replace hand hygiene; clean hands after removing them. Follow additional IPC instructions for specific infections. This practice is not surgical hand preparation. A brief count does not replace the complete procedure.
