## [purpose] Purpose

In a return demonstration, the BHW chooses the right method for each card and cleans every surface of both hands, including correct drying.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 120 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 600-minute subchapter plan): opening 5, model 15, station practice 70, feedback and repeat 20, checks and debrief 10. The Read takes about 5 minutes in the app.
- **Materials per station:** running water, soap, single-use towels, alcohol-based handrub, a timer, a bin; *2.3.3* method cards (visibly dirty / not visibly dirty), checklist worksheet and observation sheet; job aid.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Test every station; assign one staff observer each. Ask the IPC contact about local instructions for specific infections.
- **Scope:** not surgical hand preparation. No hot water or chemicals. Use the lesson's timing, not a shorter count.

## [opening] Opening

Read Lea's case: *"Lea's hands are visibly dirty. Which method first — and why?"*

## [steps] Steps

Each step is a Read section and its matching slide.

1. **The situation.** *"Why is a quick rinse not enough?"*
2. **What to do and say.** *"Which surface do you usually skip?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** Model the complete wash with the timer, then the complete handrub. *"What did the towel do after drying?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"You wore gloves. Do you still clean your hands?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"Which of the five moments happens in your work?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Alcohol, it's faster."** Fine when hands are not visibly dirty. Lea's are: soap and water.
- **"Twenty seconds."** The complete healthcare wash is 40–60 seconds.
- **Wipes wet handrub on clothes.** *"Rub until when?"*
- **"I had gloves on."** Gloves do not replace hand hygiene; clean hands after removing them.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Handrub cleans any hands, even dirty ones."** Handrub is quick and was everywhere in recent years. *"Can you see dirt? Then which method?"* Visible dirt calls for soap and water. Correct the idea, never the person.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Hand-hygiene return demonstration**, triads at stations, 70 minutes plus feedback.

- **Learner:** draws a card, names the method, demonstrates; then does the other card.
- **Partner:** timer-keeper; holds the method cards and times each procedure.
- **Observer:** marks every checklist row observed, retry or not observed.
- If anything is missed, the learner repeats the whole procedure. No running water: practise handrub now, handwashing later with the supervisor.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1 (visibly dirty hands):** **"Wash with soap and water, then dry."** Complete washing and appropriate drying.
  - "Handrub alone despite the visible dirt": visible dirt calls for soap and water.
  - "A quick rinse with water alone": soap, all-surface rubbing and drying are missing.
- **Check 2 (handrub applied, hands still wet):** **"Continue rubbing until dry."** Let hands dry while completing rubbing across all surfaces.
  - "Wipe it on clothing immediately to return to work": handrub should not be wiped off; hands may be recontaminated.
  - "Rinse with water before completing the handrub procedure": rinsing is not part of the handrub procedure.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Appropriate method; all surfaces of both hands; complete timing and drying; no recontamination.

- **Kaya na:** Completes both demonstrations and every checklist step without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Chooses the correct method but misses a surface or step; corrects it in a repeat demonstration.
- **Hindi pa:** Uses handrub on visibly dirty hands, misses essential surfaces or wipes off wet handrub.

Watch thumbs, fingertips and the tap. Write behavior (*"missed left thumb"*), not *"careless"*. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** name the missed surface; the learner repeats the whole procedure now.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model the method choice; then one method at a time while the partner reads the checklist. Re-observe on day two.
- Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
