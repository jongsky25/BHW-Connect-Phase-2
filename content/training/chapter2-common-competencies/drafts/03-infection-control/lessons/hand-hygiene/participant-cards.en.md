# 2.3.3 — Hand-hygiene return demonstration

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

BHW Lea prepares for simulated care. Her hands are visibly dirty. Soap, water, single-use towels and alcohol-based handrub are available. A quick rinse is insufficient. First choose an appropriate method.

## Learner card

Choose a method for two cards: visibly dirty and not visibly dirty. Demonstrate handwashing and handrub to the supervisor.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
