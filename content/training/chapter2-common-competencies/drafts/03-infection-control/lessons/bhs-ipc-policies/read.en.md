## [scene] The situation

BHW Nilo finds an IPC folder with some old pages and no exposure-reporting contact. It includes injection and sterilization documents. Finding them does not authorize him to perform those procedures. He needs the applicable guidance and his own role.

## [action] What to do and say

Locate each policy and its date or version. Confirm with the IPC contact that it is in use. Record the owner, relevant role and training needed. Include hand hygiene, precautions, triage, cleaning, waste, injection safety and worker protection. Report missing or conflicting guidance rather than inventing a procedure.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

For hand hygiene, Nilo records the location and confirms the version with the nurse. He records the return demonstration still needed. For sterilization, he identifies specialist staff. For exposure, he obtains the contact and immediate reporting process. He does not mark unfinished training as completed.

## [scope] Scope and support

Reading or attendance alone is not authorization. Tasks require appropriate instruction, training and permission. For a sharps or body-fluid exposure, promptly use the local reporting and clinical-assessment process. Do not prescribe or start medicines from this checklist. Do not assume BHWs need no IPC training.
