## [purpose] Purpose

As Ina, the BHW runs a short meeting that invites every voice, summarises differences and confirms two voluntary roles with the existing group.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 15 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 60-minute subchapter plan): opening 1, model 2, role-play 6, feedback and retry 3, debrief 2, next step 1. App Read: about 5 minutes, separate.
- **Materials:** *Participant cards* and *Worksheet 2.6.3 Short community meeting* per triad; *Observation sheet 2.6.3* (staff only); job aid; name tags for Ina, the leader and Sari.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Practise Ina's line; you demonstrate it first (F41).
- Scope: the BHW supports the process and does not own decisions. Never force agreement or appoint unwilling people. No real groups; no health advice.

## [opening] Opening

Read the scene aloud. Ask: *"Eva has said nothing. Does that mean she agrees?"*

## [steps] Steps

1. **"The situation"** (Read and slide). Ask: *"Who is speaking, and who is not?"*
2. **"What to do and say."** Ask: *"How can Ina invite Eva but still let her pass?"*
3. **"Choose the first response."** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **"Worked example."** Demonstrate Ina's line with a volunteer as Sari. Ask: *"Was Eva given a role without agreeing?"*
5. **"Scope and support."** Ask: *"If people disagree, what does Ina do?"* (Summarise; return to the purpose.) Then run the role-play.
6. **"Apply it to the situation."** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **"On Monday, try this."** Link: *"Willing people have roles. What will they do, and how will we know it helped?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Let the leader handle all updates."** Ask: *"Who else has an idea?"*
- **"Give Eva the invitations job."** Ask: *"Did Eva say yes?"*
- **"Ask Eva what she thinks."** Add: *"You may also pass."*
- **"Form a new group without the leader."** Ask: *"What do we lose without the existing group?"*

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Silence means agreement."** Nobody objected, so it feels settled. But Eva's silence may be nervousness, not a yes. Do not correct the speaker; ask the group: *"How would you know Eva agreed?"*

## [practice] Practice activity

**Short community meeting** (role-play). Triads: Ina (facilitator), the leader, and Sari (resident with another suggestion). Three rounds, about 2 minutes; everyone plays Ina once. Ina states the purpose and discussion agreement, invites each voice, summarises differences and records two voluntary roles. Staff rate only Ina. The other two give one strength and one next action; replay the weak moment. Fictional only. Low resource: the facilitator plays the leader; pairs take turns as Ina. Solo: write Ina's lines; arrange observation later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

1. **"Eva is quiet. What should Ina do?"** Correct: **"Invite her and allow her to pass"** — this creates an opportunity without coercion. "Say she does not care…": this judges her and can undermine trust. "Record that she agreed to everything…": silence is not clear agreement.
2. **"An association already exists. What should be clarified before forming a core group?"** Correct: **"Collaboration and voluntary roles"** — this considers the existing group and who willingly takes responsibility. "Immediately appoint all the BHW's friends…": this does not establish community participation and agreement. "Dissolve the association…": this removes community ownership of the process.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Invites contributions, summarizes differences and confirms voluntary roles while considering the existing group.

- **Kaya na:** Hears another suggestion and confirms willing members, roles and the next discussion without coercion.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Invites contributions but needs a prompt to confirm acceptance of a role.
- **Hindi pa:** Listens only to the leader or treats silence as agreement.

Look for volunteers restating their roles. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** one next action, *"Ask them to restate their roles,"* then replay the closing.
- **Hindi pa:** name what you saw calmly, re-demonstrate Ina's line, then retry.
- Re-observe at the 2–4-week follow-up with a changed fictional meeting. Triad feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
