# 2.6.3 — Short community meeting

Fictional practice. Rotate roles so everyone plays Ina once.

Purok Maligaya already has a residents’ group. At the meeting, its leader wants to control every update. Eva is quiet, and Sari has an idea for people without phones. Ina needs to open the discussion and clarify who is willing to help.

## Learner card

You play Ina. State the purpose and a respectful discussion agreement. Invite each voice, allowing people to pass. Summarize the different suggestions and confirm two voluntary roles.

## Partner card

Play the leader of the residents’ group who wants to control every update. Say once that chat is enough. Then respond to how Ina invites other views. Accept a role only if Ina asks and you are willing. Do not add personal details.

## Observer card

Play Sari, a resident with an idea for people without phones. Share it only when Ina invites you. Afterwards, give Ina one strength and one next action; replay the weak moment. Staff rate only Ina with the separate observation sheet.
