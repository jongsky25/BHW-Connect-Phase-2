# 2.6.3 — Short community meeting

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Purok Maligaya already has a residents’ group. At the meeting, its leader wants to control every update. Eva is quiet, and Sari has an idea for people without phones. Ina needs to open the discussion and clarify who is willing to help.

## Learner card

Play facilitator, leader and resident with another suggestion. Invite each voice and confirm two voluntary roles.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
