## [scene] The situation

Purok Maligaya already has a residents’ group. At the meeting, its leader wants to control every update. Eva is quiet, and Sari has an idea for people without phones. Ina needs to open the discussion and clarify who is willing to help.

## [action] What to do and say

Explain the purpose and agree on respectful discussion. Invite everyone to contribute, allowing people to pass. Summarize different suggestions. Consider working with the existing group. Identify willing members, clarify roles and ask them to restate the agreement.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

Ina: “We have heard about chat. Sari, would you like to share another approach?” Sari suggests personal invitations. The group chooses to work with the existing association. The leader agrees to receive confirmed updates, and Sari to propose options for people without phones. Quiet Eva is not assigned a role without agreement.

## [scope] Scope and support

The BHW supports the process rather than automatically owning decisions. Do not force agreement or appoint unwilling people. When disagreement arises, summarize the points and return to the shared purpose. If the group is not ready, record what needs another discussion.
