## [scene] The situation

The group agrees on personal invitations for people without phones. Someone calls the effort successful because many attended the meeting. It is not yet known whether updates reached people in time. The group needs clear actions and a way to review results.

## [action] What to do and say

Record the action, owner, resources and date. Confirm resources instead of assuming they exist. Agree on follow-up and a simple result measure. Ask residents whether the method helped. When a commitment is missed, discuss the barrier respectfully and adjust the plan together.

## [example] Worked example

Plan: Ina confirms the time with the BHS. Volunteers deliver invitations before the agreed day. At follow-up, they ask whether residents received and understood the update before the visit. Short of paper? Confirm another method first. A member who could not finish is not shamed.

## [scope] Scope and support

Do not promise unconfirmed funds, transport or services. Use combined counts and avoid publishing personal reasons for nonattendance. Measures should support improvement, not punishment. Ask the organizer to confirm required permissions and reporting arrangements.
