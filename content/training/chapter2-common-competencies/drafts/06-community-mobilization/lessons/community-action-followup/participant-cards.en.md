# 2.6.4 — Action plan and review

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

The group agrees on personal invitations for people without phones. Someone calls the effort successful because many attended the meeting. It is not yet known whether updates reached people in time. The group needs clear actions and a way to review results.

## Learner card

Complete the group’s action plan. Add a timely-update measure and a response to an unfinished commitment.

## Partner card

Play the other voice in the case. Ask how the plan meets your stated needs. You may disagree or ask for clarification. Do not add personal or clinical details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
