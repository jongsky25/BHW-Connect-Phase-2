## [purpose] Purpose

The BHW completes an action plan with owner, confirmed resources, date, follow-up and a result measure beyond attendance, and responds respectfully to a missed task.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **Time:** 15 minutes face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 60-minute subchapter plan): opening 1, model 2, practice 6, feedback and retry 3, debrief 2, next step 1. App Read: about 5 minutes, separate.
- **Materials:** *Participant cards* and *Worksheet 2.6.4 Action plan and review* per triad; *Observation sheet 2.6.4* (staff only); job aid, one per participant.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Confirm locally the resource contact, permissions and reporting channel.
- Scope: no unconfirmed funds, transport or services. Combined counts only; no personal reasons for non-attendance. Measures support improvement, not punishment. The plan is a training tool, not an official form.

## [opening] Opening

Read the scene aloud. Ask: *"Does a full meeting prove the update reached people in time?"*

## [steps] Steps

1. **"The situation"** (Read and slide). Ask: *"What would tell us the update arrived in time?"*
2. **"What to do and say."** Ask: *"Who confirms each resource?"*
3. **"Choose the first response."** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **"Worked example."** Model the plan. Ask: *"What will they ask residents at follow-up?"*
5. **"Scope and support."** Ask: *"Short of paper. Now what?"* Then run the practice.
6. **"Apply it to the situation."** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **"On Monday, try this."** Close the subchapter: *"Which step would you skip in a hurry, and what would go wrong?"* Hand out the job aid and explain the 2–4-week follow-up task.

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Many came, so it worked."** Ask: *"Did the people without phones get it in time?"*
- **"The barangay will lend a vehicle."** Ask: *"Who confirmed that?"*
- **"Name the volunteer who did not deliver."** Ask: *"What got in their way?"*
- **"Ask residents if they got the update."** Praise it.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"If many people came to the meeting, the mobilization worked."** An honest belief: attendance is easy to count, and turnout does show trust. Call no one wrong. Ask: *"Did the people without phones get the update in time? How would we know?"*

## [practice] Practice activity

**Action plan and review.** Triads: learner, partner, observer. Three rounds, about 2 minutes; everyone is learner once. The learner completes the plan with a timely-update measure. The partner reports an unfinished task; the learner responds. The observer notes the exact words, then one strength and one next action; the learner retries. Staff rate. Fictional only. Low resource: manila paper. Solo: complete it, ask a colleague to review; arrange observation later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

1. **"What is missing from 'The group will send updates someday'?"** Correct: **"Owner, resources, date and measure"** — this makes the agreement specific and reviewable. "A list of every resident's personal health conditions…": these details are unnecessary. "A promise of transport…": this adds an unconfirmed promise.
2. **"Some invitations were not delivered. What should happen at follow-up?"** Correct: **"Discuss the barrier and adjust the plan"** — this maintains accountability while addressing the actual problem. "Shame the member…": this can damage trust without clarifying the reason. "Declare success…": meeting attendance does not prove that updates reached people.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: Includes an action, owner, resource confirmation, date, measure and respectful community follow-up.

- **Kaya na:** Completes the plan, measures receipt of updates beyond attendance and adapts to barriers without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Names an owner and date but needs a prompt to confirm resources or add a measure.
- **Hindi pa:** Promises unconfirmed resources or shames someone who did not finish.

Look for a measure residents can answer. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** one next action, *"Confirm the resource first,"* then retry that line.
- **Hindi pa:** name what you saw calmly, re-model the missed-task response, then retry.
- Re-observe at the 2–4-week follow-up, when the BHW reviews a fictional plan with the organizer. Triad feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
