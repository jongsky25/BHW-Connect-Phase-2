# Community mobilization

## Identify the issue with the community

Hear affected people before choosing a solution. Separate reports from assumptions. Ask whose voice is missing. Together, state what is happening, who is affected and what evidence exists. Mark what remains unknown. Community members share decisions rather than simply receiving instructions.

## Invite people and address barriers

Identify affected residents, local leaders and others who can help. Ask who is trusted to invite them. Explore barriers involving time, location, language and speaking. Agree on options such as personal invitations, suitable timing or small conversations. Do not assume a leader represents everyone.

## Build trust and a core group

Explain the purpose and agree on respectful discussion. Invite everyone to contribute, allowing people to pass. Summarize different suggestions. Consider working with the existing group. Identify willing members, clarify roles and ask them to restate the agreement.

## Agree actions and review results

Record the action, owner, resources and date. Confirm resources instead of assuming they exist. Agree on follow-up and a simple result measure. Ask residents whether the method helped. When a commitment is missed, discuss the barrier respectfully and adjust the plan together.
