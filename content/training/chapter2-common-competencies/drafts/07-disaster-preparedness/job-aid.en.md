## Connect people to essential services

Identify and link four domains: medical/public health, nutrition, safe water/sanitation/hygiene (WASH), and mental health/psychosocial support (MHPSS). Alert assigned staff to urgent risk and interrupted services. For a distressed person, check safety, listen with consent, and link to practical help and qualified support.

## Practise coordinated disaster response

Check the official warning and local plan. Identify confirmed contacts for evacuation, clinical continuity, WASH and psychosocial support. Report immediate risk to the right team, confirm handover, and set follow-up. A responder should rest and seek support when it is unsafe to continue.

The group prioritizes safe evacuation and urgent referral. It routes medicine and water to the appropriate staff, records acknowledgement, and arranges follow-up and relief for the exhausted volunteer.
