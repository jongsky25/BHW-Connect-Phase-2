## [scene] The situation

Pio’s family is making a go-bag or e-balde. They have an infant and a member taking regular medicine; money is limited and the container must be easy to carry.

## [action] What to do and say

Use a current DOH and local checklist. Prioritize safe water, food, light, communication, basic hygiene, water-protected documents and first-aid supplies. Include personal medicines and infant needs under the family’s care plan; check expiry and portability.

## [example] Worked example

## [scope] Scope and support

Items are examples, not a mandatory universal list or permission to dispense medicines. Adapt to local hazards, household needs and medical plans.
