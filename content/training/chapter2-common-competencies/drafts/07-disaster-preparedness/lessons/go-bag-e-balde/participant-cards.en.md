# 2.7.3 — Practice: Prepare a go-bag or e-balde

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Pio’s family is making a go-bag or e-balde. They have an infant and a member taking regular medicine; money is limited and the container must be easy to carry.

## Learner card

Help Pio's family choose items for the bag. Say why each item matters, ask about access to regular medicine, and write a date to recheck.

## Partner card

You play Pio's family. Say: "We have a baby, and one of us takes regular medicine. We cannot spend much." Then ask: "Do we really need all of this?" Do not name a medicine or add health details.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.

## Item picture cards

Cut out and use with a light bag or bucket. Pictures only — no real medicines.
