# 2.7.2 — Practice: Confirm evacuation and warning arrangements

Fictional practice. Rotate learner, partner and observer.

Heavy rain is forecast. Nena’s family asks when and where to evacuate, but the printed directory is old.

## Learner card

Using the mock advisory, tell Nena's family the source and time, the confirmed action, and the contact you will verify. Fill in the worksheet.

## Partner card

You play Nena's family. Ask: "When do we leave, and where do we go?" If the learner sounds unsure, ask: "Is that number still right?" Accept "I will confirm and come back to you." Do not add health details or real places.

## Observer card

Notice the actual words and actions. Give one strength and one next action, then retry. Use the separate observation sheet with your facilitator.
