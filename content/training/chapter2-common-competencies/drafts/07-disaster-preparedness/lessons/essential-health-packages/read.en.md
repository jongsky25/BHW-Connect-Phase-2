## [scene] The situation

At a temporary shelter, a child may lack food, safe water is limited, a resident needs regular medicine, and another is distressed.

## [action] What to do and say

Identify and link four domains: medical/public health, nutrition, safe water/sanitation/hygiene (WASH), and mental health/psychosocial support (MHPSS). Alert assigned staff to urgent risk and interrupted services. For a distressed person, check safety, listen with consent, and link to practical help and qualified support.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read the explanation for each option.

## [example] Worked example

The BHW routes water to WASH, food to nutrition and medicines to a clinician. They speak calmly with the distressed person without demanding a story and offer referral.

## [scope] Scope and support

Recognizing a domain does not authorize prescribing, ration-setting, declaring water safe or providing therapy. Follow local clinical and service protocols.
