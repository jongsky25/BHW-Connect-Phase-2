## [purpose] Purpose

On a fictional map, the BHW marks a hazard, a support need and one capacity, and names who must confirm that capacity.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **10 minutes face-to-face** (share of the 60-minute subchapter plan): opening 1, model 1, practice 4, feedback and retry 2, debrief 1, next step 1. Read in the app: about 3 minutes.
- **Materials:** 2.7.1 cards, worksheet and observation sheet per triad; job aid; fictional Maligaya map with the school marked "not confirmed".

## [prepare] Prepare

- Confirm for debrief: whether an official hazard map exists, and who in the barangay DRRM office and health team verifies it.
- Scope: the practice map is not an official hazard map or evacuation order. Never mark real households or call a real building a shelter.

## [opening] Opening

Show the map and read the scene. Ask: *"What do we know here, and what must someone confirm?"* Write "Known" and "To confirm" on the board.

## [steps] Steps

Each step matches the Read section and slide of the same name.

1. **The situation.** Ask: *"Which is the hazard, the need, the possible capacity?"*
2. **What to do and say.** Ask: *"Where do we write what we are unsure of?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1 — let pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** Model the three layers and the "verify locally" label. Ask: *"Why is the family's house not on the map?"* Run the practice.
5. **Scope and support.** Ask: *"Can this map tell people where to evacuate?"*
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2 — let pairs answer before revealing.
7. **On Monday, try this.** Ask: *"Who will review your blank risk-map checklist?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"The school is the evacuation centre."** Thank them, then ask: *"Who confirmed it this year?"* Move it to "To confirm".
- **They name the family.** Say: *"You know who needs help. On the map, write the need, not the name."*
- **"River is the hazard, school is the capacity."** Correct. Ask who verifies the school, and when.

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"The school was a shelter last time, so it is a shelter now."** It worked before, and BHWs want to answer fast. Do not single anyone out. Read the option "Say the school is open without checking" and its feedback: this may harm or delay help. Ask what might have changed.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Risk map exercise.** Triads: learner, partner (a resident), observer. In 4 minutes the learner marks a hazard, a support need and a capacity, and fills "Who/when will verify". The observer notes actual words, one strength and one next action. Retry 2 minutes; rotate roles. Fictional details only. Low resource: map on the board. Solo: worksheet now, staff observation later.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

- **Check 1 (first safe step).** Correct: **"Separate hazard, support need and capacity; verify route and shelter"** — it ties action to a verified plan and scope. "Assume the old information is still correct…": information may be old or wrong. "Decide alone…": the correct authority and local context are needed.
- **Check 2 (safe next step).** Correct: **"Inform the lead, record handover, and follow up"** — it establishes clear ownership and next action. "Say the school is open without checking…": this may harm or delay help. "Nothing needs recording or follow-up…": accountability and continuity are lost.

## [observe] What to observe

Indicator: In a fictional case, on the fictional map, identify a hazard, a support need and one capacity needing confirmation.

- **Kaya na:** Completes action, boundary, handover and follow-up without prompts.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Chooses the first action but needs a prompt for verification or follow-up.
- **Hindi pa:** Uses an unverified plan or exceeds the role.

Look for a named verifier beside the capacity and no real location. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** re-model the verify column and retry now using the route.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model the worked example; give one hazard, one need, one capacity; pair with a peer rated Kaya na.
- Re-observe at the next supervised practice or transfer task with a changed map. Peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating.
