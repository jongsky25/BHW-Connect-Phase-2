# A respectful conversation

Pause and notice the setting. Introduce yourself and the purpose. Ask permission to talk. Say, “What would you like to clarify about the visit?” Listen before explaining. If the time or place is unsuitable, offer another opportunity.

Use a calm voice. Say, “I would like to hear what happened.” Offer a more private, safe place if she wants. Ask, “What concerns you most?” Listen without interrupting. Summarize what she said, then ask, “Have I understood correctly?”

Clarify the outcome the person wants. Ask whether they have another approach in mind. Offer only what you can do or confirm. Say, “I can ask about another schedule. Would you like that?” Confirm who will act and when you will update each other.

Separate the resident’s words from your assumptions. Share only necessary information with the authorized colleague. Include the action, agreement and support still needed. Then ask yourself: Did I listen? What helped? What one thing will I change next time?
