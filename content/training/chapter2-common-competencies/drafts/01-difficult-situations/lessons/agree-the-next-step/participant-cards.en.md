# 2.1.3 — Agree on the next step

Fictional practice case.

BHW Salma visits Ben in Marawi. He cannot go to the health center in the morning because of work. “I cannot lose a whole day,” he says. Salma does not yet know whether staff offer another schedule.

## BHW card (Salma)

Using the action card, offer Ben two realistic follow-up options. Offer only what you can do or confirm. Ask which he prefers. Confirm who will act and when you will update each other. Say clearly what is not confirmed yet.

## Ben card

You are Ben. You cannot go to the health center in the morning because of work.

- Start with: “I cannot lose a whole day.”
- You may ask: “Could it be in the afternoon?”
- Choose the option that fits your work, or decline one that does not.
- At the end, repeat the agreement in your own words.
- Respond only to what Salma actually says. Do not introduce a medical emergency in this exercise.

## Observer card

Watch Salma. Note the options she offers, Ben’s choice, the action, who will act and when, and what is still unconfirmed. Give one specific strength and one action to try again. Rotate roles.
