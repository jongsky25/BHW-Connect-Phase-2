# 2.1.3 — Agree on the next step

Fictional practice case.

BHW Salma visits Ben in Marawi. He cannot go to the health center in the morning because of work. “I cannot lose a whole day,” he says. Salma does not yet know whether staff offer another schedule.

## BHW card

Using the action card, offer Ben two realistic follow-up options. Ask which he prefers.

## Resident card

Ben: “Could it be in the afternoon?” Salma: “I will ask the health center. I can call you tomorrow at two if that works.” Ben: “Yes.” Salma: “The appointment is not confirmed yet; tomorrow’s call is an update.” The agreement and uncertainty are both clear.

Keep the same concern but use your own words. Respond to what the BHW actually says. You may ask for clarification or decline an offered option. Do not introduce a medical emergency in this exercise.

## Observer

Give one specific strength and one action to try again. Rotate roles.
