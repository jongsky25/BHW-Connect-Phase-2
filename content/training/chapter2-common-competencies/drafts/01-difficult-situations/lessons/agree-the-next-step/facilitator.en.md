## [purpose] Purpose

With Ben, the BHW offers realistic options, lets him choose, and confirms action, owner and time, naming what is unconfirmed.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

- **180 minutes** face-to-face (this lesson's share of the 600-minute subchapter plan): opening 10, model and Self-check 1 30, practice 90, feedback and retry 30, Self-check 2 and close 20. App Read: 5–10 minutes, separate.
- **Materials:** 2.1.3 participant cards and observation sheet, job aid *A respectful conversation*, blank action cards, manila paper.

## [prepare] Prepare

- Ask the midwife whom to ask about other schedules. In the role-play the schedule stays unconfirmed.
- Ben players may decline an option; no medical emergency.
- Not in scope: guaranteeing a schedule, booking appointments or forcing agreement. Urgent help comes first; confirm the route with the midwife, RHU or local protocol.

## [opening] Opening

Read Ben's line: *"I cannot lose a whole day."* Ask: *"What can Salma honestly offer? What not yet?"* List answers in two columns.

## [steps] Steps

1. **The situation (Read section = matching slide).** *"What is stopping Ben: interest or time?"*
2. **What to do and say.** Model it, saying the schedule is not yet confirmed. *"Which words offered only what Salma can do?"*
3. **Choose the first response.** Self-check 1; pairs answer before revealing.
4. **Worked example.** *"What is agreed, and what is still uncertain?"*
5. **Scope and support.** *"What if no option fits?"* Then practise.
6. **Apply it to the situation.** Self-check 2; pairs answer first.
7. **On Monday, try this.** *"Whom will you practise the blank action card with?"*

## [expected-answers] Expected answers and how to respond

- **"Tell him to come in the afternoon."** Ask: *"Is that schedule confirmed?"*
- **"He's not really interested."** Understandable. *"What did Ben actually say?"*
- **"I'll ask and call him."** Good. *"When exactly, and who acts?"*

## [misconception] Common misconception

**"Giving a date shows you are helpful."** It comes from wanting to help. Without naming anyone, ask: *"What is the difference between an update call and an appointment?"* Read Salma's line: tomorrow's call is an update, not an appointment.

## [practice] Practice activity

**Action card: Ben's follow-up.** Triads: Salma, Ben, observer. Three 30-minute rounds; everyone plays Salma once.

- 10 min: offer two realistic options; Ben chooses; fill in the action card; Ben repeats the agreement.
- 5 min: observer gives one strength, one next action.
- 15 min: repeat the weak part with new wording; rotate.

The observer notes options, choice, action/who/when and what is unconfirmed. Fictional details only. No printing: draw three boxes (action, who, when). Alone: record a response, then arrange staff observation.

## [answer-key] Self-check answer key

1. **What can Salma offer Ben?** Correct: "Ask the health center and update him at an agreed time." It is a concrete action without promising an appointment. "Promise staff will be available whenever he arrives": availability is not confirmed. "Assume Ben is not interested in the service": he described a time barrier, not a lack of interest.
2. **Which closing is clearest?** Correct: *"I will ask and call tomorrow at two."* It has an action, owner and time to confirm with Ben. *"All right, let us wait and see what happens."* assigns no action or update time. *"Just go tomorrow; staff will deal with it."* turns an unconfirmed discussion into an appointment.

## [observe] What to observe

Staff rate during the practice: Records the chosen action, responsible person and time; confirms Ben’s choice without presenting an update as an appointment.

- **Kaya na:** States all three elements and the unconfirmed schedule; Ben can restate the agreement without prompting.
- **Kailangan pa ng practice:** Has an action and owner but no time; adds it after being asked when the update will happen.
- **Hindi pa:** Promises an unconfirmed appointment or insists on an option Ben has declined.

Look for the words "not confirmed yet" said aloud. A correct self-check is not evidence of the skill.

## [support] If a BHW needs more support

- **Kailangan pa:** *"When will you update each other?"* Repeat the closing now.
- **Hindi pa:** re-model steps 2 and 4. Shorter case: Ben says *"Evenings are better."* Offer to ask, and name one update time.
- Pair with a Kaya na BHW; peer feedback is practice, not a staff rating. Re-observe the same day or at the midwife follow-up within 2–4 weeks.
