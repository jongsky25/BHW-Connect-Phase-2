## [purpose] Purpose

180 minutes: opening 10; model 15; triad practice 115; feedback/retry 20; debrief 10; workplace plan 10. Use Slides 1–7, role cards, paper and the observation sheet. Ask: What was the concern? Which words helped? What should happen next? Ready: States all three elements and the unconfirmed schedule; Ben can restate the agreement without prompting.

Given Ben’s situation, offer realistic options and confirm the chosen action, responsible person and follow-up time.

## [time-materials] Time and materials

This session is part of the 10-hour behavior competency. Digital reading time is separate. Use the participant cards, observer sheet and job aid. Paper and spoken instructions replace the projector and internet.

## [prepare] Prepare

One week before: confirm the local supervisor and emergency contacts. Print one card set per triad. For 30 participants, form 10 triads; rotate BHW, resident and observer over three rounds. Staff observe each learner before assigning a staff rating; use extra rounds when necessary.

## [opening] Opening

BHW Salma visits Ben in Marawi. He cannot go to the health center in the morning because of work. “I cannot lose a whole day,” he says. Salma does not yet know whether staff offer another schedule.

## [steps] Steps

Read the case and model the dialogue. Ask the first check before revealing feedback. Run the activity below, then the second check. Give a specific strength and one next action. Repeat the part needing improvement, with changed wording from the resident.

## [expected-answers] Expected response

Ben: “Could it be in the afternoon?” Salma: “I will ask the health center. I can call you tomorrow at two if that works.” Ben: “Yes.” Salma: “The appointment is not confirmed yet; tomorrow’s call is an update.” The agreement and uncertainty are both clear.

## [misconception] Common error

Promises an unconfirmed appointment or insists on an option Ben has declined.

## [practice] Activity you can run

Using the action card, offer Ben two realistic follow-up options. Ask which he prefers.

Suggested activity; suitable for observation. Use the supplied cards. Residents may decline; do not reward forced agreement. Alone: record a fictional response and self-check, then arrange staff observation. Participation alone is not competence.

## [answer-key] Check explanations

1: This is a concrete action without promising an appointment. / 2: Staff availability has not been confirmed. / 3: Ben described a time barrier, not a lack of interest.

1: No action or update time is assigned. / 2: This turns an unconfirmed discussion into an appointment. / 3: This has an action, owner and time to confirm with Ben.

## [observe] Observe

Records the chosen action, responsible person and time; confirms Ben’s choice without presenting an update as an appointment.

Ready: States all three elements and the unconfirmed schedule; Ben can restate the agreement without prompting.

Needs practice: Has an action and owner but no time; adds it after being asked when the update will happen.

Not yet: Promises an unconfirmed appointment or insists on an option Ben has declined.

## [support] Support and debrief

Allow a spoken or written response and the learner’s preferred language. Ask what helped, what was missed and what to try again. Record only performances you observed. Peer observations support practice and do not become staff ratings.

## [sources-review] Local resources

Use the listening/planning job aid, blank observation sheet and approved local contact directory. Confirm the reporting channel with the midwife. Keep these cards available for the follow-up practice session.
