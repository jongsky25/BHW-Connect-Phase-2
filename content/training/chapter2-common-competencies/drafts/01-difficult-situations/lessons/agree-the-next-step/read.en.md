## [scene] The situation

BHW Salma visits Ben in Marawi. He cannot go to the health center in the morning because of work. “I cannot lose a whole day,” he says. Salma does not yet know whether staff offer another schedule.

## [action] What to do and say

Clarify the outcome the person wants. Ask whether they have another approach in mind. Offer only what you can do or confirm. Say, “I can ask about another schedule. Would you like that?” Confirm who will act and when you will update each other.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read why each choice helps or does not help.

## [example] Worked example

Ben: “Could it be in the afternoon?” Salma: “I will ask the health center. I can call you tomorrow at two if that works.” Ben: “Yes.” Salma: “The appointment is not confirmed yet; tomorrow’s call is an update.” The agreement and uncertainty are both clear.

## [scope] Scope and support

The BHW helps coordinate; do not guarantee a schedule or force agreement. If no suitable option is available, ask the midwife or supervisor. If there is immediate danger, prioritize emergency services; routine follow-up must not replace urgent help.

## [check-transfer] Apply it to the situation

Return to the story. Choose the appropriate next step.
