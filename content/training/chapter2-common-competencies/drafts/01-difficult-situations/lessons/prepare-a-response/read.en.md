## [scene] The situation

BHW Amina arrives at Rosa’s home in Cotabato. “Why do you need our names again?” Rosa asks. Neighbors are outside. Amina has a schedule, but she does not yet know Rosa’s concern.

## [action] What to do and say

Pause and notice the setting. Introduce yourself and the purpose. Ask permission to talk. Say, “What would you like to clarify about the visit?” Listen before explaining. If the time or place is unsuitable, offer another opportunity.

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read why each choice helps or does not help.

## [example] Worked example

Amina: “May I explain the visit?” Rosa: “The neighbors can hear.” Amina: “Would you prefer another time?” Rosa chooses the afternoon. Amina does not write “uncooperative.” What she has learned is a concern about privacy.

## [scope] Scope and support

The BHW can listen, explain and plan a return visit. Do not force entry or promise services outside your control. Ask the midwife or assigned supervisor when the next step is unclear. If there is immediate danger, prioritize getting help safely through the local emergency service.

## [check-transfer] Apply it to the situation

Return to the story. Choose the appropriate next step.
