# 2.1.1 — Find out the concern

Fictional practice case.

BHW Amina arrives at Rosa’s home in Cotabato. “Why do you need our names again?” Rosa asks. Neighbors are outside. Amina has a schedule, but she does not yet know Rosa’s concern.

## BHW card (Amina)

Write the purpose, opening question, permission request and support contact for Rosa’s situation. Then try your plan with Rosa. Listen to her reply before explaining. If the time or place is unsuitable, offer another opportunity.

## Rosa card

You are Rosa. BHW Amina arrives at your home with her schedule. Neighbors are outside and can hear. Your concern is privacy; you are not refusing the visit.

- Start with: “Why do you need our names again?”
- If Amina asks what you would like to clarify, say: “The neighbors can hear.”
- If she offers another time, you may choose the afternoon.
- Respond only to what Amina actually says. You may ask for clarification or decline an offered option.
- Do not introduce a medical emergency in this exercise.

## Observer card

Watch Amina. Write down her exact words. Note whether she asks permission before mentioning the form. Give one specific strength and one action to try again. Rotate roles.
