## [scene] The situation

At a barangay in Iloilo, Liza meets BHW Jun. “You keep coming back. Nothing happens!” Her voice is raised. People in the queue are listening. Jun needs to find out what happened without arguing.

## [action] What to do and say

Use a calm voice. Say, “I would like to hear what happened.” Offer a more private, safe place if she wants. Ask, “What concerns you most?” Listen without interrupting. Summarize what she said, then ask, “Have I understood correctly?”

## [check-start] Choose the first response

Choose an answer first. Then read why each choice helps or does not help.

## [example] Worked example

Liza: “I went twice. I don’t know whom to speak to.” Jun: “You have been twice, but it is still unclear who can help. Is that right?” Liza: “Yes, that is the problem.” Jun clarifies the concern before suggesting a step.

## [scope] Scope and support

Calm listening is not a promise to grant every request. Clarify and connect the resident with the appropriate staff. Ask the midwife or supervisor for support when the situation is unsafe or beyond your role. If there is immediate danger, use the local emergency service from a safe place.

## [check-transfer] Apply it to the situation

Return to the story. Choose the appropriate next step.
