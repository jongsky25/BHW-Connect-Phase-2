# 2.1.2 — Listen and clarify calmly

Fictional practice case.

At a barangay in Iloilo, Liza meets BHW Jun. “You keep coming back. Nothing happens!” Her voice is raised. People in the queue are listening. Jun needs to find out what happened without arguing.

## BHW card (Jun)

Use a calm voice. Say, “I would like to hear what happened.” Offer a more private, safe place if Liza wants. Ask about her concern and listen without interrupting. Summarize what she said, then ask, “Have I understood correctly?” Do not promise an outcome.

## Liza card

You are Liza. You have gone twice and still do not know whom to speak to. People in the queue are listening.

- Start with a raised voice: “You keep coming back. Nothing happens!”
- If Jun offers a more private place, you may accept.
- When he asks about your concern, say: “I went twice. I don’t know whom to speak to.”
- If his summary is right, say so. If it is wrong, correct him.
- Keep the same concern but use your own words. Do not introduce a medical emergency in this exercise.

## Observer card

Watch Jun. Write his summary word for word. Note whether he lets Liza finish and checks the summary with her. Give one specific strength and one action to try again. Rotate roles.

## Listening checklist

Use this in practice with the midwife or a colleague. Tick each step you did.

- [ ] I used a calm voice.
- [ ] I said, “I would like to hear what happened.”
- [ ] I offered a more private, safe place.
- [ ] I asked, “What concerns you most?”
- [ ] I listened without interrupting.
- [ ] I summarized what the person said.
- [ ] I asked, “Have I understood correctly?”
- [ ] I did not promise an outcome.
